        my first playthrough, but I’ve got my sights set on unlocking all three
        endings, so 100%-ing it is definitely on my list. I’ve also been dipping
        my toes back into Hollow Knight, one of my fav games of all time. Trying
        to get my fix while we wait for any news of
        <strong>Silksong</strong> (cue me putting on clown makeup).

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Gaming should be for everyone! What games do you recommend newcomers
          to try?</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Best game for someone who is new to gaming? Any Soulsborne title…just
        kidding. I would obviously have to choose
        <strong>Animal Crossing</strong>. I’ve been playing it since the
        Gamecube release and it continues to be my comfort game to this day (I
        won’t tell you how many hours I have in New Horizons…) It’s cozy,
        relaxing, and there’s no really “beating” the game - so it’s perfect for
        folks who are newer to video games and want something low-key where they
        can pick up and play at their own pace. Also: my favorite AC villager?
        Roald &lt;3
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >The year is almost over! What games have you been trying to finish
          last month?&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        I’ve been busy continuing my Yakuza adventures with
        <strong>Yakuza Kiwami</strong>! When I’m not completing substories or
        saving up money for abilities, I’ve actually been returning to some
        older favorites. I’m still playing <strong>Pikmin Bloom</strong>, the
        mobile game that counts your steps and lets you grow Pikmin, but seeing
        all the cute Pikmin in my mobile game has made me return to playing a
        bit more of <strong>Pikmin 4</strong> too. At home, there’s been more
        than a few <strong>Call of Duty: Warzone</strong> sessions, and even a
        few big <strong>Among Us</strong> nights. For Among Us, [lately, I’m
        finding it] hard to lie about what tasks I’m doing as the imposter when
        I can’t remember what tasks even exist. Playing a few more rounds of the
        game will fix that, though.
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >Gaming should be for everyone! What games do you recommend newcomers
          to try?</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        I think my answer would change depending on which platforms they have
        available and what they like to do! For mobile, maybe something like
        <strong>Genshin Impact</strong> for the open world,
        <strong>MARVEL SNAP</strong> if they like card games<strong>, </strong
        >or <strong>Pokémon GO</strong> if they want something
        interactive<strong>. </strong>If they have a Nintendo Switch, I think
        games like <strong>Snipperclips </strong>for puzzles<strong
          >, Animal Crossing: New Horizons </strong
        >for relaxation and building<strong>, </strong>or
        <strong>Mario Kart 8 Deluxe </strong>for the races would be good
        recommendations - the games might have simpler game mechanics but are
        incredibly fun. I know I’ve put hundreds of hours into Animal Crossing
        games myself! Lastly, if they want a game (or many games) to play with
        friends, then I love <strong>The Jackbox Party Pack</strong> games. Each
        pack has a few different kinds of games in it, and your friends just
        need to be able to connect with their phones.
      </p>

      <h2>
        ‍<strong
          >The year is almost over! What games have you been trying to finish
          last month?&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Just like last month, I’m still working my way through
        <strong>Metaphor Re:Fantazio! </strong>Any JRPG or Persona fans should
        absolutely pick this one up - it’s only gotten better as I’ve moved
        through it. When I need a break from that, I’ve also been working on
        <strong>Rogue Galaxy!</strong> It’s a PS2 JRPG that I played a lot of
        when I was a kid, but never actually finished (if I remember correctly,
        the final boss was too hard and I got stuck). I’m hoping I can make it
        through this time around, and that it’s not like when I tried to go back
        and replay games on the Sega Genesis (Lion King, Aladdin, Toy Story) and
        realized they were <em>actually</em> hard.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >Gaming should be for everyone! What games do you recommend newcomers
          to try?</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Ooh, it depends! I do love giving recommendations, and I feel like you
        can find a video game for anyone based on what they like in other media
        (books, movies, TV). My wife wasn’t a gamer at all, but
        <strong>Ori and the Blind Forest</strong> really hooked her and has
        spiraled her into more games (and I’d recommend that game to anyone,
        it’s beautiful and is a blast)!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        But in general, I feel like mobile games are a great place to start. You
        can find card games like
        <strong>Marvel SNAP or Pokémon TCG Pocket</strong>, or even try out some
        puzzle games like <strong>Monument Valley</strong>. Mobile games can
        [be] great stepping stones to find other games you might like on PC or
        consoles!&nbsp;
      </p>
