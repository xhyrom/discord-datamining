            <p>Before you hop on Discord and start chillin’ with your friends, fostering deeper connections, and creating memories that last a lifetime, there’s something important you’ve got to set up: <strong>your profile and experience</strong>!</p>
            <p>So read along as we go through all the different ways you can customize your Discord. From tailoring your forward-facing presence to sprucing up the app itself, there are plenty of ways to make your experience feel unique to you.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div class="btn-wrapper w-condition-invisible"><a href="#" class="btn-blog w-dyn-bind-empty w-button"></a></div>

        <div class="blog-post-content w-richtext">
            <h2><strong>Give Your Discord Profile a Refresh<br></strong></h2>
            <p>Your profile is your home base where everyone can learn a little bit about you! Think of your Discord profile as one of those “Hi, My Name is ____” nametags, except with way more room for you to shove an entire paragraph about yourself instead of just your name.</p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd00f678511e6f607ea85_QmvaqbkeOIJgHHuoj5ClYGtAMg7WpkwtePN-FLIZyLLndrnj-040cmlyLXxM1qk6lYmYTDnBAfRpCB8J1ROVXG6LqXimIRFFI9n0h25xWlse43p_pxFgHbjZBDFAXefD5uxitsBLBIrrfD8R8XJfj7M.png" alt="A preview of the user Sunn’s Discord profile."></div>
            </figure>
            <p><strong>Everyone can edit and share the following information in their Discord profiles:</strong><br></p>
            <ul role="list">
                <li><strong>Display Name</strong>: Change what people should call you, at any time. Changing this won’t affect your actual username, so you can go crazy with it and people can still add you as a friend.</li>
                <li><strong>Avatar</strong>: You! Or a bird. Or your new OC, or your favorite food… whatever best reflects yourself or your current mood.</li>
                <li><strong>Pronouns</strong>: A small text field that lets everyone know how to refer to you.</li>
                <li><strong>About Me</strong>: A longer bio where you can explain what you’re all about. Use bold, italics, bullet lists, and emoji to format it just the right way!</li>
                <li><strong>Connected Accounts</strong>: Let others find you outside of Discord. Certain accounts even unlock special Discord features! Learn more about those here.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div id="heading-3" class="rich-wrapper">
        <div class="blog-post-content w-richtext">
            <h2><strong>Swap Between Light and Dark Mode<br></strong></h2>
            <p>Do you like your chats looking like they’re written on a piece of paper? Or do you prefer to keep things dark and moody for catching up on chat during the late hours of the night?</p>
            <p>Whichever is easier on your eyes, we’ve got a solution: head into <strong>User Settings &gt; Appearance</strong> to swap between Light and Dark themes at any time.</p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd09e08f8ef023a571267_c9DdRoRW6vpO1EzQQu58bRTnvEvd1jAJirhj87S73xZq2bcvDBLd4YqnqtGD_ee_r73byyPezYPmCXJyKUX2rO5EIoXEz7PH2fCbNy_v7Y8YApzkC3iqwBVaFJ2OkaVDxDon5SgwNCSNPzqjWVvDsUs.png" alt="The Appearance page in User Settings."></div>
            </figure>
            <p>Or, if you prefer to have your entire device in a specific mode, you can choose the <strong>Sync</strong> option to have Discord match up with your system’s Light or Dark theme to avoid any surprises — like being blinded by Light mode when your entire device is already in a Dark mood.</p>
            <p>You might see other Theme options right below Light and Dark mode, but we’ll talk about those later on in the article…</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div id="heading-4" class="rich-wrapper">
        <div class="blog-post-content w-richtext">
            <h2><strong>Set a Status Message to Tell Everyone What’s Up<br></strong></h2>
            <p>What are you doing right now? Whatever it is, you don’t have to message every single individual friend each time you’re doing something new to keep them in the know. Instead, set your Status!</p>
            <p><strong>Status Messages are shown right under your display name</strong> in places such as your profile, in DM conversations, and on a server’s Members list! At school working hard? Or hardly working? Either way, write a short message to keep everyone informed — you can even add an emoji to it!</p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd10e8af190848bdbdbbe_JBKDcvRMe3Si2f9ZF6_fC9hnJwQ8J2g12wgyHGv9Pumn7Uv-CRZ7eDP1RtPKYojeWq4ptU6fuModnpNtnM5GHAGG35gp4Uq1WlFA5exqDlAiswNGgtNsPvfbF7J1b8_3jdV_ZUZ5Ve3Omv8Wmv_Ho2c.png" alt="A user setting a status.&nbsp;"></div>
            </figure>
            <p>For those of us old enough to remember setting up “Away” messages on AOL Instant Messenger, this is nothing new… but if you’ve never set a status message before, try it out sometime.</p>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div id="heading-5" class="rich-wrapper">
        <div class="blog-post-content w-richtext">
            <h2><strong>Automatically Show What You’re Doing by Linking Accounts<br></strong></h2>
            <p>Sometimes, you don’t want to prep a message to explain what you’re up to. You just wanna get in and play a game or listen to music! Whatever your preference, we got ya covered there, too.</p>
            <p>By heading into <strong>User Settings &gt; Connections</strong>, you’ll be able to link a ton of different accounts to Discord. All of them let you display that account on your Discord profile, but a few of them have a unique feature that will <strong>automatically show your friends what you’re doing.</strong></p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:812pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd1ed2426600a6e3ed0da_KigjrEsTQKiXUKF3lb6g3p0VhnERMHymWqFi4hwlsEE9RBNqzT89U333U2F96qWgrxz68SnvKoVZJ2YLECU5P2v3kAcFpKfVCe_Xt9stB712L5BzfRJBNksaqP2orG5BmsRbQ1k4lVoxyWDIIxhVRKI.png" alt="A preview of a user’s Discord profile showing their Spotify activity.&nbsp;"></div>
            </figure>
            <p>Connecting one of the following accounts will display a unique activity status on that platform:</p>
            <ul role="list">
                <li><strong>Xbox</strong>: Display what Xbox Series X|S or Xbox One game you’re playing.</li>
                <li><strong>PlayStation</strong>: Show the PlayStation 5 game you’re currently playing.</li>
                <li><strong>Spotify</strong>: Display what song you’re currently listening to.</li>
                <li><strong>Crunchyroll</strong>: Show what anime episode you’re watching.</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </div>
    <div id="heading-6" class="rich-wrapper">
        <div class="blog-post-content w-richtext">
            <h2><strong>Shop for Ways to Make Your Profile POP<br></strong></h2>
            <p>You’ve got your display icon and a perfectly written bio of yourself all set up on your Discord profile, ready for the world to enjoy. Now, how are you going to tie it all together? With a bow? A “thank you for visiting my profile” message at the end of your bio? Or, do you want to make your profile shine above the others?</p>
            <p>We’ve got the perfect solution for you: <a href="https://discord.com/blog/avatar-decorations-collect-and-keep-the-newest-styles"><strong>Profile Effects &amp; Avatar Decorations</strong></a>! <strong>Avatar Decorations</strong> are shown everywhere your profile icon is seen, including next to your messages in conversations or on your profile page. <strong>Profile Effects</strong> are huuuge visual flourishes that play whenever someone opens up your profile, whether it’s the full profile or the little pop-up version.</p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:720pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd249ab9b86178bfed47a_Avatar%20Decorations%20Profile%20Effects.gif" loading="lazy" alt=""></div>
            </figure>
            <p>You can find all this in the Shop, located in <strong>User Settings &gt; Profiles &gt; Go to Shop</strong> on either the desktop or web apps! While you can’t purchase items from the Shop on mobile at this time, you can still apply any decoration you’ve previously purchased while on the go.</p>
            <p><em>Note: Currently, the Shop is in Nitro Early Access, meaning you’ll need to be a Nitro member to purchase. However, everyone can still browse the Shop and see what’s available. When the Shop is open to all, we’ll remove this note!</em></p>
        </div>
    </div>

            <h2><strong>With Nitro, Use a Different Avatar, Banner, and Bio on Each Server<br></strong></h2>
            <p>If you’re in a LOT of Discord servers for different friend groups, interests, and hobbies, having a single place to explain who you are to everyone may feel a bit limiting. You might think to yourself, “Well, my profile is like <em>‘my main space</em>,’ but I’m a multi-faceted person with many hobbies who shows up in different ways based on where I am.”</p>
            <p>First off, nice job using “multi-faceted” in that sentence, and second: did you know <strong>Nitro members can establish entirely different profiles in each server they’re in</strong>?</p>
            <figure class="w-richtext-figure-type-image w-richtext-align-fullwidth" style="max-width:1600pxpx">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/655bd2a72161f30e5ab469fb_99nOnTzlvQy-POBTwSgll-ktPt59PerAReyeE0GqPGAMBM9IOiBdf-lE5bgAVceeb50aQ4sykzlH9QyBxbPfQHrRqP_xn8JxZqMbRWhlEowa1eKH1s-z3wT81ZjfO7c6mw_XssjQ4iVdrqqA13wloRw.png" alt="A user named “Phibi” using three different profiles in three different servers."></div>
            </figure>
            <p>Everyone can set the profile customization options mentioned here. Nitro members can set those same customization options on different servers. Tailor your presence depending on the people that you’re spending time with, and yes… they can be different on <em>every single server</em> you’re in.</p>
        </div>
    </div>

            <h2><strong>With Nitro, Use More Themes!<br></strong></h2>
            <p>Purple wall. Purple chair. Purple headphones. Purple phone case. Purple keyboard.<br><br>…but just the standard Dark theme on Discord? No, we can’t have that… make it PURPLE.</p>
            <p><strong>Nitro members can choose between over 20 additional Themes</strong> on top of the existing Light and Dark themes! Decorate your Discord with darker Themes such as Chroma Glow or Midnight Blurple, or brighten up the mood with lighter-colored Themes like Citrus Sherbet.</p>
