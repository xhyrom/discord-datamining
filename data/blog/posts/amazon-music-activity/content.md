      </p>

        Activity works, and how you can try Amazon Music Unlimited for yourself
        by scoring a free three-month trial!
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Amazon Music Unlimited Customers Can Listen Together on Discord<br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        To listen together in the new Amazon Music Listening Party Activity,
        <strong
          >each participant will need their own Amazon Music Unlimited
          subscription</strong
        >, the subscription tier that lets you pick the exact song or podcast
        you want to listen to.
      </p>
      <p>
        <em
          >Not a member of the “Unlimited” tier? Scroll on over here to learn
          how to score a 3-month trial of Amazon Music Unlimited so you can vibe
          with your friends!&nbsp;&nbsp;</em
        >
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/66bf8be2f729b691bfabf401_AD_4nXf184e_X_aBpEfnVsdVgL4RpebTmabos4BfLN4wol9TRxKi2RoCWWDPBiBEQLJ7yXsYoyulnFmp4c8OuXT8ueg6qt1JFBFpaXQVkyaBEylXlCPBtQVdHmuKLUq7v16rpI9oKapVEd4-kZWePB5P444NZLE.jpeg"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="An image demonstrating how to access the Amazon Music Activity on both desktop and mobile."
          />
        </div>
        <figcaption>
          You can access the Activity on either desktop (left) or mobile
          (right)!
        </figcaption>
      </figure>
      <p>
        To get started,<strong> </strong>hop into any voice channel, text
        channel, or DM conversation.
        <strong
          >You can use the new Activity anywhere you can talk on
          Discord!&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>On desktop and web</strong>, use the Rocketship button in a
          voice call, or use the “+” button in a text conversation and pick
          “Start Activity.”&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>On mobile</strong>, open the App Launcher to find the
          Activity. It’s the icon with four shapes!&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        In the Activities list, you’ll see an option called “Amazon Music
        Listening Party.” You can probably figure out the rest:
        <strong>select “Amazon Music” from the list of Activities</strong>, link
        your Amazon Music account to Discord, and get to listening!
      </p>
      <p>
        On the top of the Activity, you’ll see some very important tabs:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Home</strong>, where you’ll receive recommendations from
          Amazon Music and can explore what’s popular in the world. You know,
          popular like those songs you hear at the grocery store.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Library</strong>, where you’ll find all the music and podcasts
          you’ve saved and collected.
        </li>
        <li><strong>Podcasts</strong>, the home of… uh, podcasts!&nbsp;</li>
      </ul>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1920pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/66c3d35ef1f4f250ae3b5b07_66c3d30b6e69308e1b95726e_AmazonMusic%2520Blog%2520UIStatic.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A screenshot of the “Amazon Music Listening Party” Activity."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        The left side of the app? That’s the queue, where you can see what’s
        next and who added it. Everyone can add their favorite tunes to the
        queue, then you can (lovingly) sneak in an episode of that podcast
        you’re obsessed with while you’ve got ‘em in a group.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Your saved music and playlists in the Activity will sync to your Amazon
        Music account, letting you bring your VC’s favorite playlist with you
        wherever you go! Or, if you find a great album or podcast you wanna
        share with your friends while using the Amazon Music app on web or
        mobile, save it to your Library and you’ll find it the next time you
        start the Activity on Discord.&nbsp;
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Enjoy Musical Bliss with 3 Months of Amazon Music Unlimited<br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        If you haven’t tried Amazon Music before, you might be asking yourself:
        “how will I know if Amazon Music will be <em>the one</em>?” You know,
        the streaming service that keeps your favorite tunes just a tap away,
        has your favorite podcasts, *<em>and*</em> lets you enjoy music with all
        your friends while hanging out on Discord.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Well, you’re in luck: <strong>qualifying</strong>
        <strong
          >Discord users can redeem a three-month trial of Amazon Music
          Unlimited</strong
        >
        by linking their Amazon account to Discord from within the Amazon Music
        Listening Party Activity!
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1920pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/66c3d35ef1f4f250ae3b5b0b_66c3d31af72e061da7693789_Amazon%2520Music%2520Offer.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="The same playful, bubbly depictions of headphones, megaphones, turntables from the first image. The message now reads: “Try 3 months of Amazon Music Unlimited. Starts at $10.99/month after. New subscribers only. Terms apply.”"
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        To see if you qualify for a three-month trial, follow the steps below
        (if you haven’t already from checking out the Listening Party
        Activity!):&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          Open the Amazon Music Listening Party Activity in any voice channel or
          text conversation.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          If it’s your first time, you’ll be prompted to link your Amazon
          account to Discord. Do that!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          If you link your account, don’t already have an existing Music
          Unlimited subscription, and qualify, you’ll be offered a three-month
          free trial of Amazon Music Unlimited!&nbsp;&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        The free trial will let you check out all the “Amazon Music Listening
        Party” Activity has to offer — along with, of course, everything else
        that Amazon Music Unlimited offers on their desktop and mobile apps! See
        what Amazon Music has in store
        <a href="https://www.amazon.com/music/unlimited">riiight over here</a>.
        Additional terms apply. Learn more details about the trial in the
        Activity.
      </p>
