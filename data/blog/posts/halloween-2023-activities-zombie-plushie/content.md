            <h2><strong>Apparition-Infected Activities and Discord Apps</strong>‍</h2>
            <p>Who’s up for some ghostly games?&nbsp;</p>
            <p>A few activities have received a monstrous makeover: <strong>Color Together</strong> gets new Halloween-themed drawing for you to fill in with your friends, and <strong>Sketch Heads</strong> will have a special Halloween-themed category for your friends to draw fiendish creatures… or at least, innocent things that your not-so-artistic friends draw that end up looking like fiendish creatures.</p>
            <p>Not sure which you’ll play first? Try out some <strong>SpellCast</strong> instead first, our original witchy word game, or any of the available Activities <a href="https://discord.com/blog/server-activities-games-voice-watch-together#heading-3">listed on our blog</a>.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>The Halloween hauntings have the ability to sneak into chat this year… point your lantern towards the <a href="https://discord.com/application-directory">App Directory</a> and you’ll find a curated collection of third-party Halloween-themed Apps to add to your server, including:&nbsp;</p>
            <ul role="list">
                <li><strong>Hallowstreat</strong>: Trick-or-treat with everyone in your Discord server! Collect and gift over 600 varieties of candy, and even steal candy (in a friendly manner) between you and your friends. Plus… bosses?? And powerups?? And SCARECROWS??&nbsp;</li>
                <li><strong>Chucky</strong>: Play a wide assortment of Halloween-themed minigames, including “Find the Ghost,” “Potion Brewer,” and “Zombie Says.” <em>Spoiler: The zombie says “brrrnggn braaains.”</em>&nbsp;</li>
                <li><strong>Cabot</strong>: Enter the world of divination when Cabot brings Tarot cards, Runes, Crystals, and Coins to your server. Or, start your research using Cabot’s extensive resources for botanicals and use the app’s… <em>Moon</em> Calculator? As if real math wasn’t hard enough.</li>
            </ul>
            <p>These apps and many more are available in the App Directory. Explore ‘em all… <em>if you dare.</em>&nbsp;</p>
