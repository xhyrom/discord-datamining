      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Join Discord Server Voice Channels From PS5 console</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        If you’ve already linked your account for PlayStation Network™ to
        Discord in the past, you’re all set! No need to reconnect the two again.
        Haven’t linked your account for PlayStation Network to Discord?
        <a
          href="https://discord.com/blog/playstation-5-voice-integration-announcement#heading-2"
          >See how to do it here</a
        >!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Once you’re all linked up, joining a Discord call from your PS5 console
        is done entirely within the system menus. Toss your mobile device across
        the room and onto a soft pillow, you won’t need it to join!&nbsp;<br />To
        join a Discord call directly from your PS5 console, grab your DualSense
        wireless controller and follow the steps below:<br />
      </p>
      <p>
        <strong
          >First, press the PlayStation button on your controller to open up the
          Control Center and navigate to the Game Base</strong
        >.
        <em
          >If you hid the Game Base from your Control Center in the past, you
          can press the Options button to customize the Control Center and add
          it back in.</em
        >&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/66678785bb453c074c09c1ee_AD_4nXeeHljbeYx3KecYX9ELeLb77a-WISikbUB5S94p3aaF45h94PB_sfeov2GDw6YMhysujhHmbieQlBIByX6narboIKTMpWw5Wp2_WSu4PHjvrlHym3mWBR4eRBZFlkZ3uKx3oi9C8YZeAFDGbiE7diuA7RY.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of the PS5 User Interface. The “Game Base” button is highlighted at the bottom of the screenshot."
          />
        </div>

      <p>
        <strong>Next,</strong>
        <strong>pick the new “Discord” option in the Game Base</strong>! You
        should know what the icon looks like… right? Once you select it, you’ll
        see a list of alllll the servers you’re in, and even an option to look
        through your DMs and Group DMs!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"

      </p>

        Way easier, and no extra device is necessary! If you still prefer the
        old-school method of joining a call by transferring it from Discord on
        desktop or mobile as
        <a
          href="https://discord.com/blog/playstation-5-voice-integration-announcement#heading-3"
          >mentioned here</a
        >, that option will still be available. Who knows, maybe you’ll see the
        button to transfer your call in Discord and go “OH YEAH, I can do that,
        huh?!” Options are always good, ya’know?
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Join Discord Voice in a DM or Group DM From Your PS5
          console</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Joining a Discord DM call from your PS5 console is similar to joining a
        server’s voice channel, but with one more button press that we’ll bold
        below. Remember, you’ll need to link your account for PlayStation
        Network to Discord beforehand.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          Press the “PlayStation” button on your DualSense wireless controller
          to open the Control Center and navigate to the Game Base.
        </li>
        <li>
          In the Game Base, select the “Discord” option. This will open up all
          your available servers AND include an option to browse through DMs.
        </li>
      </ul>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/666787fe3824efced1cbfe7f_AD_4nXeqKz-xl6dfaxDY75A5kqzc9gHiMxIxI_gb2p4x8nOmhI8rK-c3LTE6V5Zjzeanp-DeH59M1WedmhwO1kyhLWw0LTHvOTYD1VLxZOM-8ZRUxWaEfWfWhJcyL85AyCOYmRhUM8bUrjqkRf6V97ceODO2m3qo.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="One final demonstration of the Discord experience on PS5 console. Here, the “Direct Calls” button is prominently displayed.‍"
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong
            >In the Discord menu, select “Direct Voice Chats” to open up a list
            of your DMs and Group DMs, </strong
          >organized by the last time the conversation was active.
        </li>
        <li>Lastly, press Join to connect and start chattin’ away!&nbsp;</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        See? Easy peasy. If you’d like to use your phone to open a Help Center

      <h2><strong>WHEN Can I Join from my PS5 console?</strong></h2>
      <p>
        Soon! VERY Soon, in fact! This feature will be gradually rolling out to
        different regions over the coming weeks. Once the rollout ends, everyone
        who has updated their PS5 console to the latest system update and has
        linked their Discord and PlayStation Network accounts will be able to
        call directly from their PS5 console. We’ll update this article’s title
        when it’s finally out for everyone.
      </p>
      <p>
        <strong
          >Get ahead of the curve and link your PlayStation Network and Discord
          accounts by heading into User Settings &gt; Connections.</strong
        >
        Along with talking with your Discord friends on your PS5 console, you
        can also show off what game you’re playing on Discord,
        <a
          href="https://discord.com/blog/playstation-discord-account-connection-linking-game-status"
          >like this</a
        >!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Need help? We got ya. Shoot us a message to our lovely support team
        <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us">here</a> and we’ll get
        you set up and using Discord on your PS5 console in no time.
      </p>
