      <h2>
        <strong>How to Add Apps to Your Discord Server<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        If you find something you like in the Discord App Directory or see an
        app in your friend’s server that you really like, it’s easy to add it to
        your own server! <strong>Just press the giant “Add App” button</strong>,
        shown on both the App Directory and on an app’s profile.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600px"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6721515c9d16d42552fba317_AD_4nXfe6XIvj6a9gZDWDNVLaJ2y9Ges3_hYspI1ZkenSHyC0z6mlovV0ZKQjKuMXFu_z8cVM87ma_0cJX3FCAW3DezCY65FOEBti3D0ZJyu-PE0MtPUYxsuunRuovsZhXaS9AMqLghgU-949DeDZmw4GW0zlj0.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="An app’s profile pop-up, with the “Add App” button highlighted."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        But why would you wanna do that in the first place? The Discord
        Developer community makes apps for all sorts of use cases — if you’ve
        thought of a reason for an app to exist, there’s a good chance someone
        made it.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Some of the reasons you may want to add an app to your Discord server
        include…
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          Some <strong>additional moderation support</strong>! Our friend
          <a
            href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/4421269296535-AutoMod-FAQ"
            >AutoMod</a
          >
          will love their new bot buddy.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          Some moderation apps allow what’s called “<strong
            >reaction roles</strong
          >,” where people can give themselves a role for your server by
          reacting to a certain message. Give a Thumbs, get a role!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          Add cute lil’ <strong>text-based games</strong> to your server, such
          as farming or fishing games.
        </li>
        <li>
          Let everyone easily access and post cat pictures, on-demand. Limitless
          cat pictures. The cat pictures will never stop. Is this punishment, or
          salvation?
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        <em
          >Pro tip: If you’re looking for features like Reaction Roles and
          additional Moderation support built directly in Discord, consider
          using </em
        ><a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851"
          ><em>our Community server features</em></a
        ><em> that offer similar features!&nbsp;</em>
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Use Discord Apps in ANY chat <br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Back in the early days of apps on Discord, most were bots that could
        only be added to servers. But now, in today’s wondrous new world, you
        can add apps to… YOUR OWN ACCOUNT! It’s as if your pocket protector held
        apps instead of pens, making your pocket protector actually cool for
        once.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6721517828d02f853fec5b08_AD_4nXfrZK7NW_qGcc_k0XL8Dz73TTfA01N61PJitvWCFISTnMN0T9vPB6fb9-ptPRykdotGvStXwLnYpB04i-uGALv_IBoIpqr4RfPLV0F7n0T4fzDnVOkQCJmy9FAWfGubg-oVKK_BDfHod7jqVMaDqkjZSlBh.png"

        when you press the “Add App” button. Click that, then approve all the
        necessary account permissions. Now, you’ll find that app waiting for you
        in the App Launcher.
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600px"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"

        >, letting you either type out a command or click through your list and
        find the right one.
      </p>
      <p>
        The possibilities are <em>endless</em>!… but yeah yeah, go ahead and
        spam those cats first if you must — get it outta your system.&nbsp;

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Why Can’t I Launch an App in a Discord Server I’m In?<br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Apps on Discord can bring all sorts of new capabilities to servers on
        Discord. While these can liven up many of the chats you’re in, some
        servers and their moderation teams might run a tight ship and may not
        allow you to use any apps they haven’t added to the server
        themselves.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure class="w-richtext-align-center w-richtext-figure-type-image">
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/67215c5542670b2289604ba0_AD_4nXe2P3ITEXqrw6XBSRTV573bFY-pWay1p-lxjJ_tpNxaXnKa38rbz-VXJtwhtABerh_9-izMqUNe9KKSDEIM18os88rD22nMLczLem3GxDFqblvU-HaVLyMGe5ruvYnxm76IaAejvmHStWBdhZQC7o897PPP.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A message posted on Discord with an Activity embed."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        2If you’re unable to use an app or play an Activity on a server,
        <strong
          >double-check your roles or permissions to see if you have either the
          “External Apps” or “Activities” permissions toggled on</strong
        >. The server may also restrict using either of these to specific
        channels in their server, so take a peek around and see if there’s a
        space dedicated to using apps freely!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        And of course, you can always hop into a DM or a different server
        instead!
      </p>
