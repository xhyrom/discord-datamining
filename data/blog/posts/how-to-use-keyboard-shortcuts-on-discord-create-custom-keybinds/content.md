      <p>
        Need to mute your mic? Answer that sudden call ASAP? Quickly edit the
        last message to fix that spelling mistake that no one would
        <em>really</em> notice, but <em>you</em> would? All doable with just a
        few key presses — no mouse needed!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Keybinds &amp; Keyboard shortcuts let you quickly access different
        functionality on the desktop app by pressing a few buttons at once! In
        this article, we’ll go over helpful built-in shortcuts for text and
        voice chat, how to make your own keybinds, and how one
        <em>really</em> helpful shortcut lets you breeze through Discord.
      </p>
      <p>
        If you’re using the desktop client, you’ll be able to access these
        shortcuts even when Discord isn’t in focus. You can also use most of
        these on our web app using a desktop browser, but they won’t work if
        your web browser isn’t in focus.&nbsp;
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Helpful Keyboard Shortcuts for Text Chat on Discord<br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        You type all your messages and replies with that new clicky keyboard of
        yours, so why should you use your mouse to go and edit it? Just keep
        using that keyboard and help break it in a bit more!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 800pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6737a57aad1bc8b2844e6f36_AD_4nXdSBFwJbmfx_UfiWjA4-h4vC0GKBa5_OJtNgAKJBLnM1cyVFAD3fQdZSSqNq15tZ-BdJ3WS2IdCURzGCQPP5Kqus7SGKGDyVJbii6RGl_es-mxuHvvVoEidmoHEJDAKhlywNG7W8DOOdq4xG9AYj0CRW8k.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of interacting with messages using only a keyboard."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        These keyboard shortcuts can help you quickly access ways to edit or
        reply to a message, add emojis and stickers, or pin a message to
        reference later on.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4>
        <strong
          >Quickly attach emojis, stickers, and GIFs to your Discord
          message:&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + E</strong>: Open the Emoji Picker&nbsp;</li>
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + S</strong>: Open the Sticker picker&nbsp;</li>
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + G</strong>: Open the GIF picker&nbsp;</li>
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + U: </strong>Attach a File to your message
        </li>
      </ul>
      <h4>
        <strong
          >Press Tab, use your Arrow keys to highlight a message, then
          press…&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li><strong>E or Up</strong>: Edit Last Message (E or Up)</li>
        <li><strong>+</strong>: Add Reaction</li>
        <li><strong>Backspace</strong>: Delete the message</li>
        <li><strong>R</strong>: Reply to the message</li>
        <li><strong>P</strong>: Pin the message</li>
        <li><strong>F</strong>: Forward the selected message</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Lastly,
        <strong
          >pressing the Up Arrow Key lets you quickly edit the last message you
          sent</strong
        >. Spelling error? idk what you mean.
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >Helpful Keyboard Shortcuts for Voice &amp; Video Chat on Discord<br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Look — we love hearing your beautiful voice, but you’ve GOT to deal with
        how loud that clicky new keyboard of yours is. We’re <em>sure</em> it’s
        great for text chat, but <em>not</em> while in voice.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 960pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6737a5b1c5173e2395ed9681_AD_4nXfw0jBAY1WOfgY8nVBIGRiVNmUz-ad-qFutgO3wnn72st0ALO77kGamaS9Z088xxIiGgZ49P3FKqYqX7b9Q7tLfeFrQUwgjp2p9xlt0rIMNtWnGiTrb6e8fPbXlgv7suadN7OoeVZJZ27buADMQc0iU9eqt.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="An ongoing Discord call with four participants."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Thanks to a handful of voice-specific keyboard shortcuts built into
        Discord, you can mute your mic, deafen the audio of the call, or quickly
        answer a call at the press of a button. A few of them even let you
        access certain features without having Discord focused!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        The shortcuts below will always work on the desktop app, so if you ever
        have to use the Discord desktop app on a different computer, you’ll have
        something already familiar that you can use.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4><strong>While Discord is in the background:&nbsp;</strong></h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + M</strong>: Toggle Mute</li>
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + D</strong>: Toggle Deafen</li>
      </ul>
      <h4><strong>When you have Discord open:&nbsp;</strong></h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li><strong>CTRL/CMD + Return</strong>: Answer an Incoming Call</li>
        <li><strong>ESC</strong>: Decline Call, in fear</li>
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL + ’: </strong>Call the DM you’re currently looking at.
        </li>
      </ul>
      <h4>
        <strong
          >When you have Discord open AND you’re currently in a voice
          call</strong
        >:&nbsp;
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + Opt + V</strong>: Jump back to your call,
          wherever you are in-app.&nbsp;&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + B</strong>: Open the Soundboard&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Your friends who play games late into the night might know some of these
        shortcuts like the back of their mousepads (actually, don’t look under
        those mousepads). But, if these are new to you, now <em>you’ll</em> be
        the one who bestows knowledge to your friends! Just… be sure to unmute
        your mic first.
      </p>
    </div>

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Set Your Own Custom Keybinds on Discord<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Our built-in shortcuts are a great start to mastering the ways of the
        keyboard, but you can also set up keybinds tailored to your setup. Do
        you have one of those funky vertical split-keyboards or a really ᶦᵗᵗʸ
        ᵇᶦᵗᵗʸ ᵏᵉʸᵇᵒᵃʳᵈ that makes it tough to reach one of our built-in
        shortcuts? Set up your own!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        By heading into <strong>User Settings &gt; Keybinds</strong>, you can
        set up custom your own keyboard shortcuts for a bunch of voice-focused
        actions, along with some navigation actions.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600px"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6737a8ab73cfad57dd0132b6_AD_4nXd1LrXsS4uFF0Tk4Q1scbMuhBI1XIT-XBhgM2smLYTqDJB4bRNpqsNSonoNLJYlcAlyqch3lNrZjOFdWLaNPtm1cl38k0eBLUR_wvI_18UbNLaQFt6Xe8QNA5be1HCk6kJfwlLn0JGxnyliwSvRY8_pvnUu.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="Keybinds Settings page."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Some require you to just press your shortcut once, while others require
        you to hold the button down to do your action. We’ll organize ‘em by
        which ones are just a single press, and which ones are a hold!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4><strong>Toggle Keybinds:</strong></h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Toggle Mute</strong>: Mutes your mic (duh). Press it again to
          unmute your mic!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Toggle Deafen</strong>: Silences ALL your call’s sounds and
          mutes your mic. Useful for when stepping away from your desk to fiddle
          with your clicky keyboard.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Toggle VAD (Voice Activation Detection)</strong>: Quickly
          switch between “Push to Talk” and “Voice Activation” modes while
          you’re on a voice call.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Toggle Streamer Mode: </strong>Hides personal info from being
          visible on your client, such as usernames. Helpful when you’re sharing
          your screen with friends (or to the world!).&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Navigate Forward/Navigate Back</strong>: These act like your
          web browser’s “Back” and “Forward” buttons. If you forgot where you
          were just at, just press this keybind!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Switch to Voice Channel</strong>:<strong> </strong>Switch your
          desktop client to view the voice channel you’re in, no matter what
          part of Discord you’re currently looking at.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Disconnect from Voice Channel: </strong>Bye! <em>*click*</em>
        </li>
      </ul>
      <h4><strong>Hold Keybinds:&nbsp;</strong></h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Push to Talk: </strong>Your mic will only send your voice when
          holding this shortcut down.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Push to Mute</strong>: Your mic will be <em>muted</em> while
          you hold this shortcut down.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Priority Push to Talk</strong>: Lowers the volume of others in
          the call as you talk. Use responsibly!&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Trying to figure out which ones to set up first? We’ve got a few ideas
        for you… check out this quick clip below to see what we think are some
        of the best to get set up:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        ‍<a href="https://youtube.com/shorts/_zL2MehaD_g?si=0wnZRsBz8b3nswGU"
          >Keybinds make Discord so much more POWERFUL. - YouTube</a
        >
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Easily Speed Through Discord with the Quick Switcher <br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Keybinds and keyboard shortcuts help you quickly access important
        functions without lifting your hands off your keyboard, but you can
        <em>also</em> navigate to any of your conversations with just your
        keyboard as well?!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        By pressing <strong>CTRL/CMD + K, </strong>you’ll see a text box pop up
        with a simple question: <em>Where would you like to go?</em>&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        No, this isn’t some chatbot (thankfully) — this is the
        <strong>Quick Switcher</strong>. It’s a search bar for looking through
        nearly every chat you can access. From server channels to DMs and
        friends, type in ANYTHING and you’ll see the results for every server
        and conversation you’re in!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6737aeb3dc265ae807d83b93_AD_4nXf_qWdYTKjbBIL1Py4qg0WaI7kMEJbzgaueCNR3yqYDGw0lbvKrCzx4xV7IzSChrORIAAWAsIW0Pmp3dCxX-VlNLAp14hPe9a6vIaYIiErw-XBEERNsPcrzfZwyZvxKWR6KjZ8XNlFBquisaxqUCNWVnoZt.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="The Quick Switcher."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        The text field is even pre-selected for you when you summon the Quick
        Switcher, so you can immediately start typing the name of where you want
        to go!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        If you have a specific server channel or DM you have in mind, you can
        add one of the following characters to the beginning of your search
        query and you’ll be able to narrow down to certain types of
        results:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>@</strong> -&nbsp; search usernames of friends and mutual
          servers. (@Wumpus)
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong># </strong>-<strong> </strong>search through the text channels
          in your servers. (#general-chat)
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>!</strong> - search through your server’s voice channels.
          (!voice-chat)<strong>‍</strong>
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>* - </strong>search through your servers themselves. (*cool
          server)
        </li>
      </ul>
