      <p>
        Turn the volume up and listen in:
        <strong
          >Amazon Music Unlimited customers can hop into the new “Amazon Music
          Listening Party” Activity</strong
        >
        in voice calls or text chats and listen along to their favorite music or
        podcasts with their friends!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Never tried Amazon Music before? Qualifying users can launch the
        Activity to redeem a three-month trial to try it out. Tune in to our
        <a href="https://discord.com/blog/amazon-music-activity">blog</a> to see
        how it all works. Available in select markets. Terms apply.
      </p>
      <p>
        Along with the new Amazon Music Activity, we’ve got plenty more to share
        this month, includinggg:
      </p>
      <h3><strong>What’s Happening? Dungeon Crawling.</strong></h3>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong
            >Server member lists on desktop now showcase a member’s recent
            activity</strong
          >, including the games they’ve played and any jams they’ve listened
          to. Now you’ll know when your friend relaunches your favorite coop
          game for the first time in ages!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Discord on Xbox is getting a big ol’ buff. </strong>Now you
          can see any active server voice channels or the games your Discord
          friends are playing on your Xbox friends list, directly call a Discord
          buddy, and watch someone’s Discord stream from your console! See how
          it works
          <a
            href="https://discord.com/blog/see-whats-happening-on-discord-directly-from-your-xbox"
            >here</a
          >.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Prepare for adventure in Roll20’s new Activity.</strong>
          Embark on a Dungeons &amp; Dragons campaign or explore other popular
          RPG systems spanning multiple genres and settings with your friends in
          voice. Link your Roll20 account to access your existing characters and
          games in Discord!&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <h3><strong>Make it BIG. Keep Things Mindful.&nbsp;</strong></h3>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong
            >You can <em>finally</em> resize the picture-in-picture window on
            desktop. </strong
          >This one has been a highly requested feature. Now, you can make your
          video call as <strong>BIG</strong> or as <em>small</em> as you want
          while exploring your servers and chats across Discord.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Speaking of mindful, don’t miss our Teen Charter!</strong> We
          spoke directly with our teen users globally about what’s important for
          them to help keep their experiences on Discord comfy. Together,
          <a href="https://discord.com/safety-teen-charter"
            >we’ve co-created our Teen Charter</a
          >, a set of principles that matters most to teens: authenticity,
          inclusivity, privacy, and transparency.&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
