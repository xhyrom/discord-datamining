      <h2>
        How to participate in the Discord Quest and earn the exclusive “The
        Original is Back”-Pack for PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS
      </h2>
      <p>
        <strong
          >From May 14th to May 28th, you’ll have a chance to claim the
          exclusive “The Original Is Back”-Pack</strong
        >. Here’s how you can join in and claim your exclusive rewards:
      </p>
      <ol role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Launch PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS</strong>: Before diving into the
          Discord Quest, start the game on your device. You can’t stream the
          game without the game itself, ya’know!&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Accept the Quest: </strong>Use either the Quest bar displayed
          in Discord when you launch PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS or accept the Quest in
          User Settings &gt; Gift Inventory
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Head to a Voice Channel or DM on Discord</strong>: Open up
          Discord’s desktop app and go to a DM, Group DM, or a server where you
          feel most at home.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Go Live with PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS</strong>: Start a voice call
          or join a voice channel and click the Screen Share button to stream
          PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS to a friend. Make sure to select PUBG:
          BATTLEGROUNDS as the application to share; general screen sharing
          won’t count towards your Quest.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Stream for Success</strong>: Stream at least 15 minutes of
          your gameplay to at least one viewer. You can track your progress on
          the bar shown in User Settings &gt; Gift Inventory.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Claim Your Rewards:</strong> Once you’ve reached the
          requirements for streaming, you’ll be able to redeem your exclusive
          “The Original is Back”-Pack in your Gift Inventory.
        </li>
      </ol>
      <p>
        <em
          >Psst... Special Streaming Channel Alert! Want to show off your
          strategic chops? Stream on our </em
        ><a href="https://discord.gg/EqcajdpVzw"
          ><em>PUBG Discord Channel</em></a
        ><em
          >. We've set up a dedicated streaming channel just for the Quest. It’s
          a great place to display your gameplay and connect with like-minded
          players. Come over, have fun, and you might just find some new
          squadmates!</em
        >
      </p>
      <p>
        If you missed the Quest the first time it popped up in-app, no worries —
        you can also find the Quest in
        <strong>User Settings &gt; Gift Inventory</strong>.&nbsp;
      </p>
    </div>

      <h2>Discord – Our Friend and Helper in Staying Connected with YOU!</h2>
      <p>
        Now that you're all set with the how-tos, let’s dive into why Discord is
        more than just a platform for us.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Since the early days of PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS in 2017, Discord has played
        an essential role in how we interact with our community. It’s been more
        than just a platform; it's been a hub for real-time feedback, lively
        discussions, and a meeting place for players around the world.
      </p>
      <p>
        Our Discord server acts as a central gathering place where players
        discuss tactics, share epic game moments, and participate in community
        polls. These interactions help us gather invaluable insights into what
        our players think about our updates.
      </p>
      <p>
        We regularly host exclusive Discord-only events that often involve
        community challenges, trivia, and tournaments that are open to all skill
        levels, promoting a fun and engaging environment.
      </p>
      <p>
        In essence, Discord is not just a tool; it's an extension of the
        battleground itself! Whether you're sharing a recent victory,
        coordinating team play, or just hanging out, our
        <a href="https://discord.gg/EqcajdpVzw">official PUBG Discord server</a>
        is your go-to place for all things related to PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS.
      </p>
      <p>See you on the Battlegrounds, players!</p>
