            <h2><strong>Now In Nitro: Three Features for Members to Try<br></strong></h2>
            <p>Hey, @everyone! It’s been a hot minute since we’ve given an update on the latest and greatest that Nitro has to offer.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>Today, we’re giving the lowdown on three of the newest Nitro benefits: the ability to <strong>share Nitro with friends</strong>, adding <strong>four new App Icons and a new color Theme</strong>, and the power to <strong>assign Shop items to a particular Server Profile</strong>.&nbsp;</p>
            <p><em>Wondering what Nitro even IS? </em><a href="https://discord.com/nitro"><em>Discord Nitro</em></a><em> unlocks a TON of premium perks and powers to your Discord experience. Express yourself better, share better, show what you’re up to better… if you’ve tried something on Discord, there’s a good chance Nitro enhances it in some way.&nbsp;</em></p>
            <p>All caught up on Nitro? Let’s get started! </p>

            <h2><strong>Invite Your Friends: Give ‘Em&nbsp; 2-Week Nitro Passes&nbsp;</strong></h2>
            <p>Picture this: You’re streaming some crystal-clear Jackbox on your server thanks to your Nitro perks. During the stream, one of your friends wants to show off a different game they’re into! When they go to screen share, you bestow upon them <em>streaming</em> <em>superpowers</em> by giving some Nitro on the spot — now they’re streaming clearer than ever thanks to you!</p>
            <p>Crazy, right? So crazy in fact, we made it real. Now, <strong>Nitro members receive three friend passes they can share with a Nitroless friend,</strong> so you can share your snazzy Nitro perks and powers with your friends!</p>
            <figure style="max-width:1600pxpx" class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/666cb6627d327f1342f7f6e5_AD_4nXcCvuUZl-oPPxB43BIjdsJHhl06Hk5-tUhAegozAuf66Qo4fkZCb7gxejxvb_FXDpV9bqXUg_gWL4INzQyRq4UxQHIvZOoFJuLbQSkqcX5ZftBgGpeVqBLY66B4vfB0Quw8tATAjyJVISRyN7RFPGk_saPQ.png" alt="The “Share Nitro with Friends” menu within the Nitro tab."></div>
            </figure>
            <p>Each friend pass is a 2-week trial of Nitro. Members can share a pass by going into <strong>DMs &gt; Nitro &gt; Share Nitro</strong> or User Settings &gt; Nitro<strong> </strong>on either the desktop or web apps. Once you’re there, press “<strong>Select Friends</strong>” and you’ll see exactly who on your friends list qualifies for one of your friend passes.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>For two whole weeks, your buddy can try everything Nitro has to offer. Show ‘em how to use those custom emojis, sounds, and stickers you saved on your server. Or, pair your colorful profiles together like wine and questionably edible cheese!&nbsp;</p>
            <p>After you’ve given out those friend passes, don’t fret: watch for future refills of Nitro friend passes to dish out to more buddies. For the lowdown on how sharing Nitro with your friends works, including details on who qualifies to send and receive Nitro passes, *<em>pass</em>* by our Help Center <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/22567595858327-Share-Nitro">here</a>.</p>
        </div>

            <h2><strong>Try Four New App Icons and a New Color Theme</strong></h2>
            <p>Your metaphorical face isn’t the only thing that can sport a new style when you’re a Nitro member! <strong>Custom app icons</strong> and <strong>color app themes</strong> make Discord look the way you feel, both while you use Discord AND when you receive a notification on your phone.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>‍</p>
            <figure style="max-width:738pxpx" class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/666cbad6573395c1d2878ad5_AD_4nXcJyVAmg6mzjLDnHUpZQY4IV60t6pSB_M4MNVPyvHPm1hdnCJnvRtdfZE1EGW9JmXgU4vnZezIizRmOU55E9UL2_AqizVr9eq7KdgXn8XHsrtpRigV6s2Z7kqR4b1CppwrtxOhdTYCxYWQK1ahTXjnrN-s_.png" alt="A preview of the four new App Icons."></div>
            </figure>
            <p>Now, we’ve got <strong>four new app icons</strong> and <strong>a new color theme</strong>! “<strong>Color Wave</strong>,” “<strong>Midnight Prism</strong>,” and “<strong>In Rainbows</strong>” join the list of available app icons, while “<strong>Blurple Twilight</strong>” gets to be both an app icon AND a new color theme, inspired by the new looks we’re sporting on our website. (<a href="https://discord.com/">Did you see it yet!?</a>)&nbsp;</p>
            <figure style="max-width:1600pxpx" class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image">

            </figure>
            <p>This *<em>technically*</em> means that we added a theme called “Twilight” <em>after</em> adding a theme called “Midnight,” which is the wrong order in the real world — apologies to the savvy astronomers out there.</p>
            <p>Nitro members (and their friends currently using a friend pass) can use these new customization options in <strong>User Settings &gt; Appearance </strong>on both desktop and mobile.</p>
