      <h2>
        <strong>How to Bold Your Text on Discord<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        <strong>BOOM. </strong>You just gotta get
        <strong>LOUD</strong> sometimes. Or just do a BIG ol’ scream like
        <strong>AUUGHHGHGMBFUGHBGGHHHH</strong> or something like that.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        If you wanna make your words <strong>BIG</strong> and
        <strong>BOLD</strong>, just add two asterisks before and after the
        section you want to make bold.
      </p>
      <p>
        For example, if you want to really go <strong>WOAH</strong>, type your
        message like the one below:
      </p>
      <pre
        contenteditable="false"
        class="w-code-block"
        style="

          overflow-x: auto;
          background: #2b2b2b;
          color: #f8f8f2;
          padding: 0.5em;
        "
      ><code class="language-markdown" style="white-space:pre"><span style="font-weight:bold">**WHAT DO YOU MEAN**</span><span> you don't like asparagus??</span></code></pre>
      <p>
        ‍<br />Then, your message will go <strong>BOLD</strong> like this:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 836pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"

        On desktop or web, you can also highlight your message and press
        “<strong>CTRL/CMD + B</strong>” to instantly bold the words you have
        highlighted!&nbsp;

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Italicize Your Text on Discord<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Sometimes you need to <em>emphasize</em> something in your message,
        without YELLING it. <em>Or whisper…</em>
      </p>
      <p>
        Italics can help imply a specific enunciation of your words (“ohh, is
        <em>that</em> so, <em>huuuh</em>?”). They can also be used when
        referring to the title of something, such as the comic mini-series
        <em>Wumpus Wonderventures</em>. (<a
          href="https://www.webtoons.com/en/fantasy/wumpus-wonderventures-discord-webcomic/list?title_no=5291"
          >Yes, that’s real.</a
        >)&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        To make your words italic on Discord, add one asterisk before and after
        your message, like this:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <pre
        contenteditable="false"
        class="w-code-block"
        style="
          display: block;
          overflow-x: auto;
          background: #2b2b2b;
          color: #f8f8f2;
          padding: 0.5em;
        "
      ><code class="language-markdown" style="white-space:pre"><span style="font-style:italic">*Fancy.*</span></code></pre>
      <p>‍</p>
      <p>
        Then your message will look <em>sleek</em> and <em>stylish</em>, like
        this:&nbsp;

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Underline in Your Text on Discord<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Just like <strong>bolding</strong> and <em>italicizing</em>, underlining
        is another way you can add emphasis to a particular set of words in your
        message. You can use it to point out a few words, or you can add it to
        the top of a long message to give your post a “title” of sorts! At
        least, my boss always uses it like that on me…&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        To underline some text, add two “_” symbols before and after the words
        you want to underline. Think of it as adding lines around your
        words!&nbsp;
      </p>

      </p>
      <p>
        You can make any line a Header on Discord by placing a particular amount
        of # symbols in front of it, such as:&nbsp;

      <h2>
        <strong>How to make a list in your Discord message<br /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        Eggsfloursugarsodapopcornbreadcheese. Did you get that? Okay, maybe
        that’s a lot.
      </p>
      <p>
        Instead of writing out an important list in a single sentence, use a
        bulleted list! Simply add “-” before each line in your message and it’ll
        be intended with a dot! Any line in your message that starts with a dash
        will be a list.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <pre
        contenteditable="false"
        class="w-code-block"
        style="

</span><span></span><span style="color:#abe338">-</span><span> The second thing
</span><span></span><span style="color:#abe338">-</span><span> The third thing</span></code></pre>
      <p>
        <br />Now, instead of a single sentence saying everything, it’ll be easy
