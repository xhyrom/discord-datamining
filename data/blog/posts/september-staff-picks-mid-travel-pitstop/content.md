      <h2><strong>You’re new here! Who are ya?!&nbsp;</strong></h2>
      <p>
        Heyo! I’m Armando and I’m on Discord’s Integrated Marketing team!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >Fall has finally started. What games have fallen on your plate in the
          last month?</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Two games have taken up a lot of my time lately:
        <strong>Astro Bot</strong> and <strong>Into the Breach</strong>.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Astro Bot had absolutely no business being so amazing. I thought Astro’s
        Playroom was a fun platformer back when it came out, but Astro Bot dials
        it up to 11. I platinumed it in just a weekend and had fun every second
        I was doing it. The creativity in the level design and pure joy in the
        little moments are things I haven’t seen since I first played Super
        Mario Galaxy. I truly think Astro Bot has a fair shot for GOTY.&nbsp;
      </p>

      <p>
        Into the Breach came out in 2018 but I’m just getting around to picking
        it up. It’s great on my Switch, and to me, it plays like a mix of
        mini-chess and a roguelite. It’s a turn-based strategy game where you
        command giant mechs to protect your people from the dastardly Vek
        creatures! My joy-cons have definitely been sweaty with how tense this
        game gets…excuse me while I go play it again right now.
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >When you’re traveling out of town or across the world, what are your
          go-to games for when you’re on the move?&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        When I’m going I’m still gaming! My Switch is with me quite often,
        especially when I’m in planes, trains, and automobiles. If it’s not a
        new game I’m playing, I tend to go back to some games that feel timeless
        to me. Usually, that means I’ll discover some new secret in
        <strong>The Legend of Zelda: Breath of the Wild</strong> that I missed
        in my first 150 hours (pff amateur!!). <strong>Dead Cells </strong>is
        another go-to for me, and I think <strong>Balatro </strong>will be, too.
        Even though Balatro’s newer, it got to the point where I’d see those
        floating cards in my sleep so, yeah, that’s a good travel pick.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        I don’t want to leave out my trusty mobile puzzle games, either! I’ve
        been trying to beat my friend’s high score of <strong>1010!</strong> for
        years. Also, a lot of airplane seat screens now have
        <strong>2048</strong> built into them, so it’s great for plane rides but
        maybe not-so-great for the person sitting in front of me who just feels
        my finger swiping on their seat for an hour.&nbsp;
      </p>

        to run on many devices (like my Steam Deck), and the best part for me:
        optional tank controls.

        <strong
          >What are your go-to games for when you’re traveling?&nbsp;</strong

        I will NOT say Marvel SNAP again, I will NOT say Marvel SNAP aga-...

      <p>
        But I swear it’s not just that! When I know I’ll be gone for quite a
        while, I’ll bring my Steam Deck with me and load up on mulitplayer games
        that are easy to pick up, play a few rounds, and get back to what I was
        doing. <br /><br />These incluuuude: Dead By Daylight, The Texas Chain
        Saw Massacre, Halo Infinite, Street Fighter 6, and Fortnite (via GeForce

        down and put my full focus on it, or I’ll lose track of any storylines
        or important mission tidbits.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        With pick-up-and-play multiplayer games, I can play one match or two,
        and then not have to worry about what happened until I get back. Plus,
