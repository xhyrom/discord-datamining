      <p>
        Discord is where anyone can connect and talk while playing their
        favorite games, or just doin’ whatever. We’re always doing our best to
        make the app easy to navigate, no matter what platform you use Discord
        on or how you like to breeze through your conversations.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        One of those methods for navigating through Discord doesn’t involve a
        mouse or trackpad: <strong>Keyboard Navigation</strong>! Keyboard
        Navigation lets you get to wherever you want on Discord without using
        another device.
      </p>
      <p>
        Interested in using your keyboard as your primary input method? Just
        wanna learn a few tricks to accessing different features without using a
        mouse, stick around for a bit as we give the lowdown on how Keyboard
        Navigation works.&nbsp;
      </p>
    </div>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Use Buttons on Discord with Only a Keyboard <br
        /></strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        First, open Discord (duh). Then, press the <strong>Tab</strong> button
        on your keyboard.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Look: a box appeared on your screen! This box is your gateway to
        navigating Discord with just your keyboard.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 800pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/673fdc7446c127c49923eb9a_AD_4nXcGEbR2cxwZT0BenWMzWT6aI0qjq7suPMzQM0BdguZv1d1IlzH-OaJ9zMi7-7mE0r4JhP7-6-WME71o3z_XQIkjR7vypsEEqA0qPBT3SGLMX-NokPvJ4HQfmlFPDGHrkkEsb0etAA.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of Keyboard Navigation."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Think of this box as your “cursor” as you navigate with your keyboard
        —<strong> press Tab to move through interactive elements</strong> in the
        app, such as buttons and icons.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Once you’ve highlighted the button you’d like to press,
        <strong>press Enter/Return to “click”</strong> on it, just like you
        would a mouse. If your “cursor” is on a list, such as your DMs or a
        server’s channel list, you can
        <strong>use the Arrow Keys to move up and down lists</strong>.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        When you highlight a message, you can interact with it directly! What
        sorts of things can you do?...
      </p>
    </div>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Interact with Messages using Keyboard Navigation on
          Discord</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Many of the actions you would normally right-click on a message for can
        be accessed via Keyboard Navigation! No need to use your mouse or
        trackpad, and you won’t have to scroll down the whole list of items
        using the Tab button.&nbsp;&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 800pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/6737a57aad1bc8b2844e6f36_AD_4nXdSBFwJbmfx_UfiWjA4-h4vC0GKBa5_OJtNgAKJBLnM1cyVFAD3fQdZSSqNq15tZ-BdJ3WS2IdCURzGCQPP5Kqus7SGKGDyVJbii6RGl_es-mxuHvvVoEidmoHEJDAKhlywNG7W8DOOdq4xG9AYj0CRW8k.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of replying to a message using Keyboard Navigation."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Some of these shortcuts below were referenced in our
        <a
          href="https://discord.com/blog/how-to-use-keyboard-shortcuts-on-discord-create-custom-keybinds"
          >Keyboard Shortcuts blog post</a
        >. In this article, you’ll find an expanded list of shortcuts and how to
        use ‘em!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4>
        <strong
          >While using Keyboard Navigation, highlight a message, then
          press…&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li><strong>E or Up</strong>: Edit Last Message.</li>
        <li><strong>+</strong>: Add Reaction.</li>
        <li><strong>Backspace</strong>: Delete the message.</li>
        <li><strong>R</strong>: Reply to the message.</li>
        <li><strong>P</strong>: Pin the message.</li>
        <li><strong>F</strong>: Forward the selected message.</li>
        <li>
          <strong>ESC</strong>: Move your cursor to the chat input. If there’s a
          menu or modal open, it will close that first.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Alt/Option + Enter/Return</strong>: Mark the message as
          unread.
        </li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Interact with Servers &amp; DMs using Keyboard Navigation on
          Discord</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Trying to get to a specific place in one of your Discord servers? Or
        maybe you don’t really know <em>what</em> you’re looking for, you just
        wanna walk around virtually and see what @everyone is talking about.
        Understandable — the best stuff happens on a whim, ya’know?&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 800pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/673fdc7403c04b085b1f73e2_AD_4nXckLrZUtdwR4z4830wXXAMRnV2eFD-YNUHMhU0s0tkS6v2x2cLQfPf8vEMRKteSR6U7IUed7BjHFTkDo93xQnZSvvGubqnXAf_nCUPvrqT4o8smhJgq9npmXdZP62WEgsjjKO_oPg.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of navigating through a Discord server using Keyboard Navigation.&nbsp;"
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        You can use the following shortcuts to navigate through your servers and
        DMs, see what’s up, or jump straight to a particular message that may be
        important to you.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4>
        At any time, use the following shortcuts to navigate through your
        conversations:&nbsp;
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Opt + ⬆/⬇</strong>: Scroll through your server
          list.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Opt + ⬆/⬇</strong>: Scroll through your channel or DM
          list.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Opt + Shift + ⬆/⬇</strong>: Scroll through unread channels
          or DMs. See the newest stuff fast!&nbsp;&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + Opt + ⬆/⬇: </strong>Scroll through unread
          channels or DMs where you have been mentioned.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>CTRL/CMD + Opt + ➡: </strong>Swap between the last server you
          viewed and your DM tab.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Shift + Page Up</strong>: Jump straight to the oldest unread
          in a text channel. (Instead of scrolling up for ages until you find
          it.)
        </li>
      </ul>
    </div>

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How to Interact with Voice &amp; Video Calls using Keyboard
          Navigation on Discord</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        Mice? Clicky. Keyboards? Well…. also clicky. Sometimes clickier. But
        your keyboard can help you control your voice calls better, removing one
        clicky item from the auditory equation.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1440pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/673fdc73d566f762641fe60a_AD_4nXe6mc17yXm3yGCxmm5tIjcKGAYkjFvMrg2XenZuUsmdh_O9uSo76GMploo2W6CesiYRBoqoeAkTgi3dQOew0-tALuUQnoXyWnY_hj5vIoRKM_oTotdG9OB8WtuDul3-h8K4yb0v6g.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A group of eight in a Discord call.‍"
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        The following shortcuts are here to help make your voice and video calls
        easier!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h4>
        Before or during a voice call, use the following shortcuts to access
        specific call functions:&nbsp;
      </h4>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Before the call:&nbsp;</strong>some text
          <ul role="list">
            <li>
              <strong>CTRL + ‘: </strong>Start a call in the DM you’re looking
              at. Works in personal and group DMs!&nbsp;
            </li>
            <li>
              <strong>CTRL/CMD + Enter/Return</strong>: Answer a direct call
              that’s actively ringing you.&nbsp;&nbsp;
            </li>
            <li>
              <strong>ESC: </strong>Decline a direct call. You might’ve
              instinctively pressed this by accident you’ve been jumpscared by
              calls before.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
            </li>
          </ul>
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>During a call:&nbsp;</strong>some text
          <ul role="list">
            <li>
              <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + M</strong>: Mute your voice call.&nbsp;
            </li>
            <li>
              <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + D</strong>: Deafen your voice call,
              which both mutes your mic and the call itself.&nbsp;
            </li>
            <li>
              <strong>CTRL/CMD + Shift + Alt/Opt + V</strong>: Move to view the
              call you’re currently in, in case you got lost!&nbsp;
            </li>
          </ul>
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        At the time of writing, there isn’t a built-in keyboard shortcut for
        “Disconnecting from a Voice Channel”, but you can make one yourself by
        <a
          href="https://discord.com/blog/how-to-use-keyboard-shortcuts-on-discord-create-custom-keybinds#heading-4"
          >creating a Keybind</a
        >!&nbsp;
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Drag &amp; Drop using a Keyboard on Discord</strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        If your primary input method is a keyboard and you like to join a lot of
        Discord communities, the number of servers you’re in might start piling
        up. While you know Server Folders are a thing, you might’ve only seen it
        in the context of dragging a server icon over another with a mouse to
        make a server.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        But that’s not the only way you can make a server folder!
        <strong
          >You can Drag &amp; Drop certain items on Discord using just your
          keyboard!</strong
        >
        This includes servers in your sidebar, and channels in servers that you
        either own or have permission to move them.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/673fdc74df87546837053fcf_AD_4nXej-EP3MJ7fOrbgy-833hM1-7VdHteWJxuwVvlh8nb7DJ5gxshuzFpM9cw-xLOuWNHePDIyE_Gg28hrOFdaU59N5FwfA3807PUsVNiozRaO6ldjIZRwHebNs9WgEtwX95sY6HiA4w.gif"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="A demonstration of keyboard-based Drag &amp; Drop functionality on Discord."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        The example above demonstrates someone dragging a server icon and
        dropping it using just a keyboard! Wanna try it yourself? Follow the
        steps below on your own servers to learn how Drag &amp; Drop
        works:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          Use Keyboard Navigation to highlight the server you want to
          move.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>Press <strong>CTRL/CMD + D to “pick it up.”</strong></li>
        <li>
          Use the <strong>Arrow Keys</strong> to move the server you’ve picked
          up to where you want it to be.
        </li>
        <li>
          Press <strong>Enter/Return</strong>
          <strong>to “drop” it down! </strong>Now things look
          ✨<em>perfect✨</em>.
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        When using your keyboard, dragging a server on top of another server and
        hitting Enter/Return will make a server folder! If you want to move a
        server into an existing folder, use the Arrow Keys to move the server
        over the folder, hit Enter/Return, and the server will open up! You can
        continue to Drag the server into the perfect spot in your folder and
        Drop it.&nbsp;
      </p>

      <h2>
        <strong>How to Navigate Discord Using a Keyboard EVEN FASTER</strong>
      </h2>
      <p>
        As you’re pressing Tab and the Arrow Keys to move through Discord, you
        might be thinking to yourself: “wow this is a lot of Tab pressing, and
        my pinky finger hurts… can’t I speed things up a bit?
      </p>
      <p>
        YES, you can! While in Keyboard Navigation, you can
        <strong>press F6 to move from left to right in the app</strong> instead
        of being restricted to only going up and down!
        <em
          >If you’re on a Mac, you’ll also need to hold down the Globe button
          while pressing F6.&nbsp;</em
        >
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600px"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/673fdc73f8aba96ec2c48a5c_AD_4nXeZx5OpdeaK0nNUYK4WvXFgm8kAVFARjxzHR5J0AuaxUkQ1D3eHkuq35nNEWVsQk3EUpHy9klgfpOHm14fMdoNOzQJfVJxGAACdnNgEZHqS0t1_V6deQ0Ki2yZZ5nMw_tHkS9ku-A.png"
            loading="lazy"
            alt="The four sections of the desktop app users can jump between using F6."
          />
        </div>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Pressing F6 will move between four distinct sections of the app, from
        left to right:&nbsp;
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>Your server list and the Direct Messages button.&nbsp;</li>
        <li>Your conversation list (either DMs or server channels)</li>
        <li>The text conversation itself.&nbsp;</li>
        <li>The members of that conversation.&nbsp;</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        You’ll loop <em>back</em> to the server list if you press F6 while
        highlighting your member list. If you need to scroll down a particular
        list fast,
        <strong>you can hold down Tab to SPEED through menu items</strong>.
        <em>Wanna see me do it again?&nbsp;</em>
      </p>
      <p>
        Once you’ve got a handle on using a mix of Tab, the Arrow Keys, and F6,
        you’ll be able to reach for whatever you need without moving away from
        your keyboard. Just be sure to take a break from your computer once in a
        while!
      </p>
