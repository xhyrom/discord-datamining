      <h1>🫵 HEY YOU. Do you like games?&nbsp;</h1>
      <p>
        Yeah, well, WE DO TOO! In fact, we play them so often that we even talk
        about them on this here blog! In case been a while or you’re new to this
        series, <strong>Staff Picks</strong> is where a couple of Discord
        employees come together to summarize what they’ve been playing over the
        last month.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        This month, we’ve got Emi, Cody, Matt, and Mark chattin’ about what
        games they’ve been playing and what games they like to screen share with
        their friends on Discord. See which Staff members vibe with your play
        style, or discover something new to check out and play with your group
        of Discord buddies!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        🥁🥁🥁🥁 dudududududu… 🥁🥁🥁🥁 Without further ado, let’s start with
        Emi!&nbsp;

        <strong
          >How is it ALREADY July?! What have you been playing in the last
          month?</strong

      </h2>
      <p>
        In July I’ve found myself once again wishing to be the very best, like
        no one ever was. And by that, I mean I’ve been spending quite a few
        hours playing <strong>Pokémon Snap</strong>. I don’t turn on my N64 too
        often, although I’d definitely like to use it more, but I’ve been trying
        to play it more this month. The weather has been pretty nice recently
        though, which means I’ve been out of the house more, which
        <em>also </em>means I had time to play
        <strong>New Pokémon Snap</strong> and throw apples to even
        <em>more</em> Pokémon. Basically, I’ve been on the hunt for the perfect
        pictures.

          >You’re about to stream a game to your Discord friends for game night.

        chat and hang out. I usually end up streaming whatever I’m playing on
        most game nights just so folks that drop by can laugh (or suffer defeat)

      <h2>
        <strong
          >How is it ALREADY July?! What have you been playing in the last
          month?</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        I’ve been traveling around a LOT the last few weeks, so I’ve been mostly
        sticking to my phone for my gaming needs.
        <strong>Marvel SNAP</strong> has been a daily play for me for… nearly
        two years now… which is crazy to think about as I write this down.
      </p>
      <p>
        In other card games, it ends up being a pretty big barrier to entry for
        me since&nbsp; In Marvel SNAP, the matches are like 3 minutes tops, your
        deck is only 12 cards max total, and the goal is to have the bigger
        “Power” number in at least two of the three “Locations” on the board. If
        things aren’t going your way, you’re encouraged to cut your losses and
        skedaddle before things get too bad — or go all-in if you’re feeling
        confident!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <figure
        style="max-width: 1600pxpx"
        class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image"
      >
        <div>
          <img
            src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/66a90a08658a31339d17b3e6_AD_4nXfzukrFj9kXaZn8fXhtY9plOewbsxELLdwgm5VooHMrjjUZSEPcPWeMdltpS0gENieML0qfxvH5y8BqaydP9jHUcbx7GvHYBpyFSbmyTX-oRo_tJiIzql_mrkKLKViexdiP7yAveN4ogjaLXiebKlIyX5Q.png"

          />
        </div>
        <figcaption>
          Marvel SNAP (<a href="https://www.marvelsnap.com/">Official Website</a
          >)
        </figcaption>
      </figure>
      <p>
        Since it’s a digital game, it can do some CRAZY stuff: You can receive a
        random card in your hand that you may not even own yet. One card,
        Mysterio, adds “fake” duplicates to the rest of your board, deceiving
        your opponent into which one is the real one. And one of the random
        Locations, Ego, takes full control of both you and your opponent’s plays
        and you both get to watch it play cards wherever it wants to, whether or
        not it’s a good choice.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        It’s fast, fun, and adds new content monthly. I’ve laughed, I’ve
        screamed, and I’ve pulled some sick plays on a DAILY basis for nearly
        TWO years so far. It’s incredible both how long I’ve stuck with it and
        how I still love playing.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Plus, I have the snazzy in-game title that says “I’m In The Discord”
        under my name. Gotta rep, ya’know?&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >You’re about to stream a game to your Discord friends for game night.
          What do you play?&nbsp;</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        I like to think I’m pretty cognizant about streaming games that people
        would find enjoyable. My first thought would be to stream something like
        (sorry, here I go again with this game)
        <strong>Fortnite Festival</strong> or any sort of rhythm game, where
        whether or not you’re paying attention to the gameplay, you can still at
        least enjoy the music in the background.
      </p>
      <p>
        Alternatively, I would pick something that watchers could easily
        understand. A simple shooter, a puzzle game, or a heavily story-based
        game where cutscenes are engaging enough to watch.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        When I was way younger, I’d watch E3 press conferences where the person
        demoing the game would intentionally move their first-person camera
        slowly and smoothly, or look around to very prominent story beats so the
        audience goes “Wooaaahhh!” and I’ve kinda picked up that habit when
        someone else watches what I play. Gotta make it enjoyable for
        everyone!&nbsp;
      </p>
