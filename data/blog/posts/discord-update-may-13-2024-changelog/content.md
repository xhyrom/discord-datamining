            <p>Discord turns 9 this May! And birthdays mean presents… but for YOU. 🫵&nbsp;</p>
            <p>Over the next nine days, we’ll be giving away one-of-a-kind prizes and stuff that makes gaming with your friends on Discord way more fun. Keep an eye out on our social media accounts such as X (<a href="https://twitter.com/discord">US</a>, <a href="https://twitter.com/discord_JP">Japan</a>, <a href="https://twitter.com/discord_FR">France</a>) and <a href="https://www.instagram.com/discord/">Instagram</a>!</p>
            <p>We also brought you gifts *<em>right here, right now*</em> in the form of new ways to spend time with your friends and bug fixes to make that time as frictionless as possible. Read on to see what’s new!&nbsp;</p>

            <h3><strong>More Fun, During and After The Call&nbsp;</strong></h3>
            <ul role="list">
                <li><strong>You can now play an Activity within text channels in servers. </strong>If you’re not feeling like being in Voice but still want to get a few rounds of Blazing 8s in with your friends, we’ve got you covered. Desktop users can use the “+” button in text channels, and mobile peeps can press the shiny new button that we’re about to shout out below (<em>get ready for it</em>):&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</li>
                <li><strong>Access Apps on mobile with an all-new “Shapes” button.</strong> It’s your one-stop-shop for all your Apps and Activities, right on your mobile chat bar. Easily use App commands with taps instead of typing — no need to worry about which shape goes in the square hole. <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/21334461140375-Using-Apps-on-Discord">Learn more here</a>.<strong>‍</strong></li>
                <li><strong>Ask what @everyone thinks with Polls.</strong> Use Polls to decide what to play, ask your burning and benign questions, or decide if you should chomp an entire jalapeno in one bite! Use the previously-mentioned “+” button to create Polls. +1 to the Plus Button.<strong>‍</strong></li>
                <li><strong>On desktop, pop-up profiles now display someone’s mutual servers and friends. </strong>Now it’s just a single click to see which mutuals you share with someone, helping make new connections easier. Also helpful for determining which server someone’s asking for mod help in.&nbsp;</li>
            </ul>
        </div>
    </div>

            <h3><strong>More Fixes, Less Crashes</strong></h3>
            <ul role="list">
                <li><strong>Bluetooth devices now connect properly when you join a call. </strong>Previously, if you joined a call and turned on your Bluetooth headphones, your call wouldn't swap to your newly connected device properly. This now just works, meaning less talking into a device that wasn’t even working.&nbsp;</li>
                <li><strong>Permission to Poll: Granted. </strong>&nbsp;Tons of work goes into managing servers. It’s important to have the right tools—and permissions—to do so. Creating Polls in servers is now tied to a new “Create Polls” permission. Save it for just moderators, or let everyone use ‘em!<strong>‍</strong></li>
                <li><strong>Mobile is better at not using battery when it doesn’t need to.</strong> Mobile now knows to conserve resources when the app isn’t detecting anyone talking or you’re deafened in a Voice call, letting the app save on battery. Rejoice, VC sleepers!&nbsp;<strong>‍</strong></li>
                <li><strong>Crashes are a bummer. Android does that less now.</strong> Our expert dev team resolved a few long-standing Android crashes that were particularly tricky to track down. These fixes reduced the platform's crash rate by over a third — 37% to be precise! <em>/adjusts glasses</em></li>
            </ul>
            <p>P.S. If you love reading about stuff like Electron updates, unicode rendering fixes, and tangible reductions in crash rates shown in percentages, you’re in luck: our Engineering team has collected the latest technical fixes to show how they’re always making Discord better.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>Check out their Patch Notes <a href="http://dis.gd/patch-notes-april-2024">here</a>!&nbsp;</p>
