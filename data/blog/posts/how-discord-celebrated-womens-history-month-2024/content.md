      <p>
        Hey, everyone! We’re closing out the month of March and celebrating
        Women’s History Month with a spotlight on Discord’s own Women in Tech
        ERG: a community for inspiration, support, and opportunities for Discord
        employees who identify as women.
      </p>
      <p>
        Internally at Discord, we kicked off Women’s History Month with a panel
        entitled, “How We Rise Together” led by our very own Kelly Liang, SVP of
        Global Partnerships. Kelly welcomed an ensemble of female executive
        leaders from across the Gaming, Tech, and Advertising industries, who
        shared inspiring, powerful insights for all that participated.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        We also hosted several Career Roundtables throughout the month, hosted
        by women and non-binary leaders at Discord. Members of the Women in Tech
        ERG and allies alike came together to have open conversations about the
        realities, challenges, and opportunities for women and non-binary folks
        in the workplace and discussed ways to support one another.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        As Discord is committed to building an inclusive and representative
        workforce – advocating for gender parity, equity, and inclusion in all
        that we do – this work doesn’t stop here. The Women In Tech ERG also
        runs ongoing sessions for community building, mentorship, career
        development, and nonprofit partnerships year-round.
      </p>
      <p>
        Outside of our doors, there are countless communities of women and
        nonbinary folks online who are creating amazing content and revitalizing
        the gaming space. This month, our Women in Tech ERG and its allies would
        love to share a handful of their favorite vibrant gaming communities and
        content creators led by women. <br />
      </p>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <a href="https://thegamehers.com/">the *gamehers</a>: “The first and
          largest media platform and social networking community for women in
          gaming.”
        </li>
        <li>
          The <a href="https://www.youtube.com/galorants">Galorants:</a> A
          VALORANT community whose goal is to “make a positive community for
          marginalized genders.”
        </li>
        <li>
          <a href="https://www.anykey.org/">AnyKey</a>: A non-profit
          organization that “fosters change in esports, competitive gaming, and
          live streaming.”
        </li>
        <li>
          <a href="https://www.twitch.tv/omgitspotter">Omgitspotter</a>: Twitch
          Streamer and “the captain and IGL of EG Valorant.”&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <a href="https://raidiant.gg/">Raidiant</a>: A “one-of-a-kind
          broadcast, events, and content platform that exists to improve
          conditions for women in gaming and esports.”
        </li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        In honor of Women’s History Month, Discord is raising funds for
        <a href="https://girlswhocode.com/">Girls Who Code</a>, an organization
        on a mission to close the gender gap in technology and change the image
        of what a programmer looks like and does. Discord is proud to honor
        communities online that are making the world a more thriving, inclusive
        place for women, girls, and nonbinary folks.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        If you’d like to be a part of the Women in Tech ERGs’ journey and
        Discord’s culture, we’d love to have you —
        <a href="https://discord.com/careers"
          >consider taking a peek at our jobs page from time to time</a
        >!&nbsp;&nbsp;
      </p>
