      <p>
        Looking for something fun to do? Tap into apps anywhere on Discord and
        discover new ways to play games, listen to music, and watch videos
        together!
      </p>
      <p>
        The App Launcher, now available on desktop and mobile, lets you easily
        bring more fun to any of your chats and voice calls. Search through
        thousands of apps directly within the App Launcher, or browse curated
        collections. If you find one you like, you can add it to your account
        for easy access!&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Hop in one of your conversations and press the App Launcher button to
        try them for yourself! Learn about what’s new with apps over at our
        <a href="https://dis.gd/TryApps">blog</a>.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <h3><strong>Test Drive That New App Launcher&nbsp;</strong></h3>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>FOUR new Activities to check out for any occasion.</strong>
          Battle other servers in “Arena Kingdoms,” solve daily tricky Chess
          puzzles in “Echo Chess,” sink your opponent’s ships in the
          Viking-themed PVP game “Battletabs,” and learn more about your server
          buddies in “Magic Circle.” Find ‘em in the App Launcher!
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Use Apps to edit images directly in chat. </strong>The next
          time you hover over an image, tap the App Launcher on the top-right of
          it to make your photo bounce, add a caption, and more. Try using
          Viggle’s “Animate” command on a pic of your friend and watch as they
          show off their new dance moves directly in chat! Who knew they had it
          in ’em?
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong
            >Can’t find the perfect Activity? Why not build your own? </strong
          ><a href="https://dis.gd/BuildWhereTheWorldPlays"
            >Developers can now build, launch, and monetize their own eligible
            Activities</a
          >
          directly on Discord. They can even opt-in to be discoverable by anyone
          to use or play via the App Launcher!&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <h3><strong>Enhancing Privacy &amp; Account Security</strong></h3>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong
            >End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) is coming to Audio &amp; Video calls. </strong
          ><a href="http://discord.com/safety/more-private-and-secure"
            >E2EE A/V</a
          >
          helps ensure your voice and video calls in DMs, Group DMs, server
          voice channels, and Go Live streams are only accessible by those in
          the call. During the call, you can check to
          <a
            href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/25968222946071/"
            >make sure your connection is encrypted</a
          >.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>With Passkeys, your noggin is now your login.</strong> Now you
          can
          <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/25966860846231"
            >set up a Passkey</a
          >
          to use your device’s biometric features (like Face ID or Touch ID) to
          sign in instantly, instead of typing in a password like
          “st!nkUmz-r0kz.” Passkeys live on your device, and not even Discord
          can access your biometric data or Passkey.&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
