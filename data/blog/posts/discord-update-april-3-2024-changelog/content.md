      <h2>Watch stuff. Hunt stuff. Favorite LOTS of stuff.</h2>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong>Watch Together gets even better. </strong>Tons of
          quality-of-life fixes were added, including saving your recent
          searches for easy rediscovery (only you can see your history). Plus,
          server admins can now revoke a host’s control if they queued up
          “rainbow bunchie 10 hours” ten times in a row.&nbsp;
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong
            >Prepare to hunt with a Monster Hunter Now reward bundle for Nitro
            members.</strong
          >
          If you’ve got Nitro, head into your Gift Inventory from now until
          April 18th to receive a reward bundle! Use it to adventure in the city
          with your friends while hunting a Rathalos or two. More deets
          <a
            href="https://discord.com/blog/monster-hunter-now-reward-bundle-for-nitro-members"
            >here</a
          >.<strong>‍</strong>
        </li>

          <strong
            >We fixed a <em>really</em> niche bug related to saving
            <em>way</em> too many GIFs.</strong
          >
          Previously, if you favorited an <em>obscene</em> amount of GIFs, the
          GIF picker would eventually lock up and prevent you from adding or
