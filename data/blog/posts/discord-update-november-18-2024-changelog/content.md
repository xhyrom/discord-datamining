      <h3><strong>Forwarding and… Fish&nbsp;</strong></h3>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>
          <strong
            >Wanna share something to another chat? Just Forward it! </strong
          >Stop stockpiling screenshots of messages you want to share — when
          hovering over a message, click the new “Forward” button, choose where
          to send it, and POOF! Off it goes… your storage space will thank
          you.&nbsp;
        </li>
      </ul>
      <ul role="list">
        <li>

            >clear a game’s play history</a
          >
          if you don’t want others to know you spent <em>way</em> too much time
          3-ticking your Fishing to 99 in OSRS.
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>What fish weighs up to 2.5 tons?</strong> If you know the
          answer, you’ll be a pro at “Quiz Planet,” one of the latest Activities
          to hit the App Directory! Play with your friends and see which of you
          knows an unusual amount of info on some indie band no one’s heard of
          (except YOU apparently).&nbsp;
        </li>
