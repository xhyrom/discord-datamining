            <h2><strong>How Can I Get a Two-Week Trial of Nitro?<br></strong></h2>
            <p>If you don’t have Nitro currently or it’s been a moment since you’ve last had Nitro, it’s very likely that YES, you can grab a two-week trial of Nitro and try out everything it’s got to offer.&nbsp;</p>
            <p>As long as you meet the following requirements, there’s a good chance you qualify for a two-week trial:</p>
            <ul role="list">
                <li>You haven’t subscribed to Nitro in the last 30 days.&nbsp;</li>
                <li>You aren’t currently using a Nitro membership that was gifted to you.</li>
                <li>You haven’t already been offered a Nitro trial within the last 45 days.&nbsp;</li>
                <li>You’re not already a Discord Nitro or Nitro Basic member (in which case, you should share this blog with a friend!).&nbsp;</li>
                <li>You live in one of the many corners of the world where Nitro is available for purchase.&nbsp;</li>
                <li>Your Discord account is at least 30 days old.&nbsp;</li>
            </ul>
            <p>If this sounds like YOU, head into <strong>User Settings &gt; Nitro</strong> in-app and you should see a banner that looks like this:</p>

                <li><a href="https://discord.com/nitro"><strong>Nitro members</strong></a><strong> get early access to some of the latest features</strong>, including <a href="https://discord.com/blog/meme-up-some-fun-with-remix">Remix</a>, Clips, and the <a href="https://discord.com/blog/avatar-decorations-collect-and-keep-the-newest-styles#heading-2">Shop</a>.
