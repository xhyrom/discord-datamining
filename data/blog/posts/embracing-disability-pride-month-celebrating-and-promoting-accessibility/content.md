      <p>
        <strong>Disability Pride Month</strong> is a time to celebrate the
        achievements, culture, and contributions of people with disabilities.
        Every July, we take this opportunity to collectively promote visibility,
        raise awareness about the challenges and triumphs of the disabled
        community, and advocate for the dignity, respect, and inclusion for all.
      </p>
      <p>
        This year’s celebration coincides with the anniversary of the Americans
        with Disabilities Act (ADA), which was signed into law on July 26, 1990.
        This landmark legislation has been instrumental in ensuring the civil
        rights of disabled individuals, aiming to prohibit the discrimination of
        those with disabilities and help promote accessibility in all areas of
        public life.
      </p>
      <h2>
        <strong
          >Breaking the Stigma: Why Disability Pride Matters for
          Everyone</strong
        >
      </h2>
      <p>
        This month is not just about acknowledging progress. While its origins
        are from a piece of US legislation, it’s now a worldwide initiative
        about affirming the pride and identity of individuals with disabilities.
        We shine a light on the social, economic, and cultural disparities that
        <em>still</em> exist for the disabled community — simply put, it's about
        <em>re-branding</em> the word "disability.”&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        Discord’s Brain &amp; Body Works Employee Resource Group (previously
        known as the DisAbilities ERG)&nbsp; is committed to building belonging
        for all kinds of bodies and minds. Disability Pride Month encourages us
        to see disability as a natural part of human diversity, and the Brain
        &amp; Body Works ERG was created to build intentional safe spaces for
        people, regardless of ability status, and to advocate for the needs of
        physically disabled folks and neurodivergent individuals.&nbsp;
      </p>
      <p>
        In honor of Disability Pride month, Discord and the Brain &amp; Body
        Works ERG will be donating to three important organizations that
        support, advocate for, and help to create a more inclusive and equitable
        world for disabled communities globally:
        <a href="https://canine.org/">Canine Companions</a>,
        <a href="https://ablegamers.org/">Able Gamers</a>, and the
        <a href="https://www.nimh.nih.gov/"
          >National Institute of Mental Health and Technology</a
        >.
      </p>
