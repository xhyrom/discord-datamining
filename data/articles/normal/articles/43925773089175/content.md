<h1 id="h_nep_what_are">What are Nitro Emoji Packs?</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Nitro Emoji Packs</strong> are 5 collections of exclusive emojis that
  come with Nitro. Each pack has 25 emojis with its own personality, so you can
  pick the vibe that matches yours. These emojis are made just for Nitro, and
  you can't find them anywhere else on Discord.
</p>
<p>The 5 packs are:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef87c38c7f0466033368787618c5aa8c5">Cattos</li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec106ba51369061a3c30385893a77be5f">Lil Guys</li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb2e4d137f6806154d5a6ca7dd963fa0a">Mochi</li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee98164bfb7ff29346c0e6c51f9ec3ed8">Toebi</li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7495c4f93082a37b8c2356a70384980d">Skelly</li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_nep_who_can_use">Who Can Use Nitro Emoji Packs?</h1>
<div class="exp-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Nitro Emoji Packs are currently an experiment and

<h1 id="h_nep_how_to_use">How to Use Nitro Emoji Packs</h1>
<p>
  During the experiment, you can send and react with Nitro Emoji Packs on
  <strong>desktop</strong>. On <strong>mobile</strong>, you can see these emojis
  when others use them, but you can't select them yourself.
</p>
<h2 id="h_nep_desktop">Desktop</h2>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>Send an Emoji in a Message</strong></span
  >
</p>
<p>
  1. Select the <strong>emoji icon</strong> on the right side of the message bar
  to open the <strong>emoji picker</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  2. Select the <strong>plus icon</strong> at the top of the emoji picker's left
  sidebar. When you hover over it, you'll see
  <strong>View Nitro Emojis</strong>. This opens the
  <strong>Nitro Emoji Packs</strong> view, where you can preview each pack.
</p>
<p>
  3. To see every emoji in a pack, select the <strong>arrow</strong> next to the
  pack's name.
</p>
<p>
  4. Choose an emoji to add it to your message, then send your message as usual.
</p>

</h4>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>React to a Message</strong></span
  >
</p>
<p>
  1. Hover over the message you want to react to, then select the
  <strong>Add Reaction</strong> icon (smiley face) in the menu that appears on
  the right side of the message.
</p>
<p>
  2. Select the <strong>plus icon</strong> at the top of the reaction picker's
  left sidebar to open the <strong>Nitro Emoji Packs</strong> view.
</p>
<p>
  3. Choose an emoji from any pack. Your reaction appears under the message
  right away.
</p>

<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>Add or Remove a Pack from Your Emoji Picker</strong></span
  >
</p>
<p>
  Adding a pack puts its emojis in your main emoji picker, so you can find them
  without opening the <strong>Nitro Emoji Packs</strong> view each time.
</p>
<p>
  1. Open the <strong>emoji picker</strong> and select the
  <strong>plus icon</strong> at the top of the left sidebar to open the
  <strong>Nitro Emoji Packs</strong> view.
</p>
<p>
  2. Select the <strong>plus button</strong> (<strong>Add to picker</strong>)
  next to the pack you want to add. The button changes to a
  <strong>minus button</strong> once the pack is added.
</p>
<p>3. To remove a pack, select the <strong>minus button</strong> next to it.</p>

<h1 id="h_nep_faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Do I need to buy Nitro Emoji Packs separately?</strong></p>
<p>A: No. All 5 packs come with your Nitro subscription at no extra cost.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Where can I use Nitro Emoji Packs?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: You can use Nitro Emoji Packs in messages and as reactions, just like other
  custom emojis that come with Nitro.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Will I automatically get all the Nitro Emoji Packs added to my emoji
    picker?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No, adding and removing individual Nitro Emoji Packs (5 total) are opt-in,
  so you can decide to add or remove each pack to your emoji picker. See
  <a href="#h_nep_desktop"
    ><strong>Add or remove a pack from your emoji picker</strong></a
  >
  above for steps.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I use Nitro Emoji Packs on mobile?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Not yet. During the experiment, you can send and react with these emojis on
  desktop. On mobile, you can see them when others use them.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can my friends without Nitro see the emojis I send?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Everyone in the conversation can see Nitro Emoji Packs, whether they
  have Nitro or not.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens to my Nitro Emoji Packs if my Nitro subscription
    ends?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: You will no longer have access to the Nitro Emoji Packs. Any emoji
  previously sent from the packs will still be shown.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Were these emojis made with AI?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Every emoji in Nitro Emoji Packs was designed by hand, either by
  Discord's in-house artists or by the creative partners we worked with.
</p>
