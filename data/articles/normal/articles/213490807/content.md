<h1 id="h_01KK08XK3SMCQMQJ45PMDW8SR3">
  How to Submit Feedback Through the Feedback Form
</h1>
<div class="book-box">
  If you need support signing into your Discord Support Account, check out
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/35714463749015"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >this article here</a
  >.
</div>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3a9bd7d9752e791906cff9bcafda295c">
    Sign into your Discord Support Account.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eed308832c7746d0e2d9b09faf95c6512">
    Navigate to the Discord Feedback Form at
    <a href="http://dis.gd/feedback" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><strong>dis.gd/feedback</strong></a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e99b4dc5b24724702edb01ec127f715ed">
    Select the category that best describes your feedback (e.g., Text Chat,
    Voice and Video, etc.).
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea14b6296abe58c70d2ce48ad1c840d7f">
    Fill in the feedback fields with as much detail as you'd like to share.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebb16e76367908abca4fc14f6b25fd460">
    Check the acknowledgment box confirming you understand this form is for
    feedback only and does not receive support replies.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2973de1ef40144f2c40b350b7c93e5e3">
    Select <strong>Submit</strong>.
  </li>
</ol>
<p>
  Once your form has been submitted, you'll receive a confirmation email letting
  you know your feedback was received.
</p>

<h2 id="h_01KK08XK3VT5Z880CJQ1F8Q0D0">
  What Happens After You Submit Feedback?
</h2>
<p>
  Once you submit your feedback, it's forwarded to the appropriate internal team
  at Discord — such as Product, Engineering, or Design — for review.
</p>
<p>Here are a few things to keep in mind:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed58bed68ba7c83b52299dbdd28ce370c">
    <strong>You will not receive a personal reply.</strong> Our Support team
    does not respond to submissions in the Feedback Form.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec47acc8daa719dc00f28e65a09a12dff">
    <strong>We can't guarantee implementation.</strong> While all feedback is
    read and valued, we're unable to provide an ETA on when — or whether — a
    suggestion will be acted on.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec0a0b444e41d8abd18834963be745b06">
    <strong>Your feedback matters.</strong> Even without a reply, your
    submission helps shape Discord's product decisions and roadmap.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01KK08XK3WFXP2VX13EMHTBGM8">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Can I submit a bug report through the Feedback Form?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you've found a bug, the best way to get it resolved is to submit a
  support ticket at
  <a href="https://dis.gd/contact" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >dis.gd/contact</a
  >
  so our team can investigate. You're welcome to share broader product feedback
  about the experience, but bug reports requiring investigation should go
  through Support.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Will Discord reply to my feedback in the Feedback Form?</strong> 
</p>
<p>
  A: No. The Feedback Form is a one-way channel used to collect community input.
  While we cannot reply to individual submissions, we do review feedback
  internally and share it with the appropriate teams.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: What if I submitted a feedback request in the Support Form?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Our Support team will redirect you to the Feedback Form so we can properly
  track this feedback.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I submitted a legal inquiry or formal request through this Feedback Form
    — what should I do?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: The Feedback Form is not the appropriate channel for legal inquiries,
  notices, or demands, or regulatory complaints. Submissions to this form are
  not reviewed by Discord's legal team. Please refer to
  <a href="https://discord.com/terms" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >discord.com/terms</a
  >
  or contact Discord through the appropriate legal channels.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I submitted feedback but want to follow up or add more context. What
    should I do?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: You're welcome to submit a new feedback entry to the Feedback Form at any
  time. Since we don't reply to individual submissions, there's no way to expand
  to an existing submission — but every entry is reviewed.
</p>
