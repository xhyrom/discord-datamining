<h1 id="h_01KTCJKKZ6MQVKG2CP61BCKWWV">What Are Profile Frames?</h1>
<p>
  Profile Frames are decorative borders that wrap around your Discord profile.
  They're a new way to add personality and style to your profile across Discord
  or per-server.
</p>
<p>
  Profile Frames are available in the Discord Shop alongside other collectibles
  like Avatar Decorations, Nameplates, and Profile Effects.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KTCKEWE0E42K94PGEWZ5XQDZ">
  How do I apply or change my Profile Frame?
</h1>
<h2 id="h_01KTCKENYTZTXGFS0YT587SZYR">Desktop/Browser</h2>
<p>
  1. On the desktop app or browser, head into your
  <strong>User Settings</strong> by selecting the
  <strong>cogwheel</strong> [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/41920250285719"
    width="20"
    height="20"
  />]. 
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><img
      src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/41920245739415"

<h1 id="h_01KTCKW5E113KE1KQW869K91RS">Where do Profile Frames appear?</h1>
<p>
  Profile Frames appear on your full profile, including your profile pop-out,
  the full profile card, and anywhere your profile is seen by others across
  Discord.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    class="wysiwyg-image-resized"

  />.  <img
    class="wysiwyg-image-resized"
    style="aspect-ratio: 362/424; width: 46.63%"
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/41920245742359"
    width="362"
    height="424"
  />
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01KTCKXC6T167V8EF222V40TPJ">
  Profile Frames as seen in pop-out profile cards.
</h4>
<p>
  Profile Frames are built to look great across different platforms and profile
  layouts, but you may notice that a Frame looks slightly different depending on
  where it's being viewed. This is intentional—some Frames may look different
  depending on whether you're viewing them on desktop, mobile, or in different
  profile layouts.
</p>
<p>
  Some Frames also include additional decorative elements that appear based on
  the size of your profile, meaning adding more to your profile (like bio text
  or widgets) can unlock more of a Frame's full design. The best way to see how
  a Frame's full design will look on your device is in the Shop preview before
  you buy.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KTCKYB0FTRZM0382NCHV7S3Q">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Are Profile Frames available to everyone?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Profile Frames are currently in Nitro Early Access, meaning Nitro
  subscribers get to shop the collection first, before it opens to all Discord
  users.
</p>
<p>
  If you're not a Nitro subscriber, you can still see Frames on other users'
  profiles.
</p>
<div class="book-box">
  Want early access?
  <a href="https://discord.com/nitro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Subscribe to Nitro</a
  >
  to shop Profile Frames now.
</div>
<p><strong>Q: When can I buy Profile Frames with Orbs?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Profile Frames are not currently eligible for Orbs purchases. Orbs
  purchasing will be rolling out over the next 2 months.
</p>
<p>
  In the meantime, Profile Frames can be purchased with USD and other supported
  currencies through the Shop.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can everyone see my Profile Frame or just me?</strong></p>
<p>A: Everyone can see your Profile Frame.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Do Nitro subscribers get discounts on Profile Frames?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes, Nitro subscribers get discounts across the Discord Shop, including on
  Profile Frames. Your discount is applied automatically at checkout—no code
  needed.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Are Profile Frames available to purchase and edit on mobile?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, Profile Frames can be purchased and edited on the Discord mobile app.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I get a refund for my Profile Frame purchase? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: Profile Frames are non-refundable, as stated in our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/360012668071"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Refund Policy</a
  >.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I refund Nitro after buying a Profile Frame?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, if you’ve purchased a Profile Frame within 5 days of starting or
  renewing your Nitro subscription, you will not be eligible to refund your
  Nitro subscription.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How do Profile Frames interact with Profile Effects?</strong></p>
<p>
  A:
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/17828465914263"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Profile Effects</a
  >
  are another type of Discord profile decoration that can be purchased in the
  Shop. While the Profile Frames wrap around your Discord profile, Profile
  Effects render on the profile itself.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I purchased and applied a Profile Frame to my profile but I don’t see
    it.</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Make sure your Discord app is updated to the latest version. If you can’t
  see it, go to your <strong>User Settings &gt; Edit Profile</strong> to confirm
  the Profile Frame is selected. And if you still don’t see it, restart your
  app.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Is localized pricing available for profile frames?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Localized pricing is available for some profile frame purchases and gifts.
  You should see at checkout whether localized pricing is available. Otherwise,
  the purchase will default to USD ($). You can learn more about localized
  pricing
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/4407269525911"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >here</a
  >.
</p>
