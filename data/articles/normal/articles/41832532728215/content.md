<h1 id="h_01KXDZGFK4VY07RAVSVWGAKDXM">What is an LFG Channel?</h1>
<p>
  <strong>LFG Channels</strong> are a forum-style channel where players in
  eligible servers can post and browse live game invites for select supported
  games. Instead of sending invites directly to friends where they can get
  buried in chat, you can post your session to the channel so other players in
  the server can find and join it.
</p>
<p>
  Each LFG Channel post includes its own thread, so players can chat and connect
  to voice together before joining.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KXDZGFK5P1D1ACE08JDM9N3B">Eligibility</h1>
<p>
   LFG Channels are currently being tested with a limited group of servers and
  players.
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8ff9d1e4751043abd6d78e200414011b">
    It's currently available in the official ARC Raiders server. Support for
    other games is coming soon.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e58b5421e4d01ce0543c9a950fcb55f17">
    It's available to members of those servers who have the corresponding game
    installed.
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note</strong>: Want other players to be able to join you? Set your
    privacy or activity-sharing settings to public on the platform you play on
    (e.g. Steam, Epic, Xbox, and etc.).</span
  >
</div>

<h1 id="h_01KXDZGFKB764PFC7ZPSJMYJ1F">How to Join an LFG Invite</h1>
<p>
  You can join another player's posted invite directly from the LFG Channel.
</p>
<p>1. Browse the LFG Channel to find a session you'd like to join.</p>
<p>2. Open the post and select <strong>Join</strong>.</p>
<p>
  Depending on the game, this either sends a request that the post creator
  accepts in-game, or joins you directly if the session is open to anyone.
</p>
<div class="tip-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Tip</strong>: Chat in the thread before joining to confirm details
    like play style, mic preference, and what the poster is looking for.</span
  >
</div>
<h1 id="h_01KXDZGFKCFERM253HWYE4R318">Linking Your Discord Account</h1>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/32330173689623-Account-Connections-on-Discord-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Linking your account</span></a
  >
  connects your Discord account to your in-game account for a supported game.
</p>
<p>
  Linking your account is optional, but it's the easiest way to show your
  squad's open spots and get others to join your game seamlessly. Once linked,
  your post shows a live invite embed so other players can join you directly
  from Discord. Without it, you can still create posts and join other players'
  games. 
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KXDZGFKD9PZPC1K9SWFY6DFJ">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see the LFG Channel in my server?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: This feature is part of a limited experiment and is only visible in select
  servers for supported games. If you don't see it, your server may not
  currently be part of the experiment.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I request access to the LFG Channel experiment?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: This experiment is currently limited to a small group of servers, and
  access can't be requested or manually granted at this time.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Do I need to link my account to use the LFG Channel?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, linking is optional. If you link your account, your post will show a
  live session card so other players can join you directly. 
</p>
<p>
  If you stay unlinked, you can still create posts and join other players'
  games, you just won't have that live session card.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why am I unable to accept a game invite? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: This can happen if you're trying to join from a different platform than
  where the game is installed. 
</p>
<p>
  Make sure you're using the same platform where the game is installed (e.g.
  Steam, Epic, Xbox, and etc.) to create or join an LFG post. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see a live invite embed on my post?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: A live invite embed only shows when all of the following are true: your
  account is linked, your game is running, and your privacy or activity-sharing
  settings are set to public on the platform you play on (e.g. Steam, Epic,
  Xbox, and etc.), and you've turned on activity sharing both in your Discord
  User Settings and for the official ARC Raiders server. 
</p>
<p>
  Make sure your account is linked and your game is already running before you
  create your post. The embed won't appear if you complete these steps
  afterward.
</p>
