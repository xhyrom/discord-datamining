  separate from the desktop app. This article covers how to manage both global
  and server-specific notifications on each platform.

<h1 id="h_01KYNGGVV7NJF96CC8YGH0NDCD">In-App Notifications Settings</h1>
<p>
  Discord's in-app Notification Settings screen controls whether Discord sends
  you a notification at all. Once Discord decides to notify you, your device's
  OS-level settings (covered separately below for Android and iOS) control how
  that notification actually appears on your phone, whether as a banner, sound,
  badge, and so on.
</p>
<p>You can reach this screen two different ways:</p>
<h2 id="h_01KYNGGVV711G4PMNQVE4T6M7C">Notifications Page</h2>
<p>
  1. Tap the <strong>Notifications</strong> icon to the right of your profile.
</p>
<p>
  2. Tap the <strong>ellipsis</strong> in the top-right corner of Notifications
  and select <strong>Notification Settings</strong>.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KYNGGVVDV0K04VMPGQPW8WYK">Android</h1>
<h2 id="h_01KYNGGVVEBE9G1HH70N4KYE3K">App Notifications Settings</h2>
<p>
  This menu controls which activity sends you a notification, like new messages
  or a friend going live. Similar to iOS, Android manages these settings through
  your device's settings rather than from within Discord.
</p>
<p>
  1. Open your device's <strong>Settings</strong> app and tap
  <strong>Notifications</strong>.
</p>
<p>2. Tap <strong>App notifications</strong>.</p>
<p>3. Tap <strong>Discord</strong> from your list of apps.</p>
<p>
  This opens the Discord notification settings screen, where you can manage
  global notification behavior for the app.
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e58e2d6ab3c9abc0255066f7f4622ca3f">
    <strong>All Discord notifications</strong>: This acts as your master
    notification control. Disabling it means Discord won't send any push or
    banner notifications when you receive activity.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee6e7c6353ed77df8151c939575fcfffa">
    <strong>Notification organizer</strong>: When enabled, Android automatically
    bundles and silences lower-priority Discord notifications.
  </li>
</ul>

<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01KYNGGVVF9PHN2Q3HJN0JKKFV">
  <span style="color: #666666">Discord Notification Settings on Android</span>
</h4>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>Notification Categories</strong></span
  >
</p>
<p>
  Tap a category to expand it and view or adjust the individual notification
  types inside. Each category also has an
  <strong>All [category] notifications</strong> toggle that turns every
  notification type within it on or off at once.
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e99a24f5171158d647958d165ab6593d1">
    <strong>Real-time</strong>: Notifications for live activity.
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e115c977e729321d0ec4e055fe4b66233">
        Voice connected: Notifies you when you connect to a voice channel.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e60e999838fb07f61eec25bcc3c270101">
    <strong>Server</strong>: Notifications from your servers.
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e76065f97f41807e67236e2954b5a50a6">
        Forum notifications: Notifies you about new posts in forum channels.
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e477b1c7de935315fb2f31bf9ca08fabe">
        Messages: Notifies you about new messages in your servers.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ede9f29462c6e9715674ca4332c171496">
    <strong>Social</strong>: Notifications for your friends and direct
    interactions.
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e73f5c140b566e54ede47eae3b94f186c">
        Direct messages: Notifies you about new DMs.
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="eb9cd8129624e502d295e6190d99e8e96">
        Friend requests: Notifies you about new friend requests.
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="efd4dd6ca31b2408aab1c22ae08a13238">
        Reaction Notifications: Notifies you when someone reacts to your
        messages.
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e2b37d1db65412360ae7dfe3b2ad50278">
        Friend Activity: Notifies you about your friends' activity, like the
        games they're playing.
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  Tap <strong>Show unused categories</strong> to reveal additional notification
  types that aren't currently active.
</p>

<h2 id="h_01KYNGGVVPFWFEZGMPB05ZEYRC">Server Notification Settings</h2>
<p>
  <strong>All Messages</strong>: Any message posted in this server triggers a
  notification, based on your app notification settings.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Only @mentions</strong>: Only specific @mentions will trigger a
  notification. This includes direct @username, @role, @everyone, and @here.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Nothing</strong>: No messages in this server will trigger a device
  notification.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Suppress @everyone and @here</strong>: Enabling this option prevents
  both types of mentions from triggering a notification.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Suppress All Role @mentions</strong>: When enabled, this prevents role
  mentions (like @admins or @moderators) from triggering a notification.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Suppress Highlights</strong>: Suppresses notifications for noteworthy
  content like messages, friend activity, events, and more.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Mute New Events</strong>: When enabled, this mutes notifications for
  new events in the server.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Mobile Push Notifications</strong>: When enabled, push notifications
  for this server come through to your mobile device.
</p>
