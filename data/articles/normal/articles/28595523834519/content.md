<h1 id="h_01JFARPE9PYW6NYTJ02328X123">Promotion Terms</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Quest Game: Delta Force</strong><br /><strong
    >Quest Name: Gareena Pioneer Quest</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Offer Details: </strong>Play 15 minutes of
  <strong>Delta Force </strong>on PC to receive a
  <strong>P90 - Pioneer Blue</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Who is Eligible:</strong> Available to players on PC where both
  <strong>Discord</strong> and<strong> Delta Force</strong> are available.
  Discord’s premium services are restricted in some regions. 
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>NOTE</strong>: Players can play Delta Force on PC through either Steam
  or downloading the game pack on the
  <a
    href="https://deltaforce.garena.com/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >official website</a
  >.
</div>
<p>
  <strong>Availability Period: </strong>The
  <strong>Gareena Pioneer Quest</strong> can be accepted on Discord from
  <strong>December 17, 2024 </strong>until
  <strong>December 23, 2024 (11:59PM UTC)</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  Quest requirements must be completed during this period to receive the
  reward. 
</p>
<p><strong>Other Terms:</strong> Limit of one reward redemption per account.</p>
<p>
  For more information on Discord Quests and to view the currently active
  quests, visit our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/22225719947543"

    >Discord Quest Help Center</a
  >. 
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JFARPE9QEQT04PR8GRANXSGK">How to Complete the Quest</h1>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>NOTE</strong>: After accepting the <strong>Gareena Pioneer</strong
  ><strong> Quest</strong> on PC, you must play Delta Force on PC with the
  <span class="wysiwyg-underline">Discord desktop app</span> running
  simultaneously. 
</div>
<p>
  In order to successfully complete the <strong>Gareena Pioneer Quest</strong>,
  players who meet the eligibility criteria must play
  <strong>Delta Force </strong>for a minimum duration of 15 minutes.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JFARPE9Q2QN1GAW27E0WQFHA">Redemption Instructions</h1>
<p>
  Once you have completed the Quest by playing 15 minutes of
  <strong>Delta Force</strong>, you will receive a reward code in
  <strong>Quest Home </strong>(<strong>Discovery &gt; Quests</strong>). For
  detailed instructions on how to claim your reward code, please refer to our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/22225719947543-Discord-Quests-FAQ#h_01HVPBZR5FKKSQ3R1JXB9E4DM3"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Discord Quests FAQ</a
  >.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  Once you select <strong>Claim Reward</strong>, the reward code will also be
  sent to the email address associated with your Discord account.
</div>
<p>
  After you have a reward code, head over the
  <a
    href="https://redeem.df.garena.sg/cdkgarena.html"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Delta Force’s Official Redemption Website</a
  >
  to redeem it.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01JFARPE9QKW1BE9AX38215MV0">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is there a limit on how many Quests I can accept on my Discord
    account?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Eligible users can accept one of each of the available Quest campaigns on
  their Discord account. 
</p>
<p>
  For more information on Discord Quests and to view the currently active
  quests, visit our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/22225719947543"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Discord Quest Help Center</a
  >.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Is this feature/promotion available in my region?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Some Quests will not be available in all regions based on terms and
  conditions. If you are in an ineligible region for a Quest, you will not be
  able to complete it.<br />Additionally, Discord’s premium services are
  restricted in some regions. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Why am I unable to see Quests on the Discord mobile app?</strong>
</p>
<p>A: This feature is only available on the desktop and browser app.</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I have concerns or questions regarding privacy and Quests. </strong

</p>

  A: ​​We encourage you to review

</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why am I unable to complete Quests while using Linux?</strong></p>
<p>A: Currently, Quest rewards are unable to be earned on Linux devices. </p>
<p>
  However, our team is looking into a resolution for this issue so that earning
  Quest rewards is possible on Linux in the future. For now, we recommend
  completing the requirement on a different operating system, like Windows. 
</p>
<p>
  Our support team does not have an exact ETA for when a solution will be
  available, and won't be able to provide any status updates on this specific
  issue.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: I no longer see a Quest. Where did it go?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Quests are typically only available for a limited amount of time. If you no
  longer see a Quest in the <strong>Quest</strong>
  <strong>Home</strong> (<strong>Discover &gt; Quests</strong>) or in-app
  notifications about the quest, the period to complete and claim the reward for
  the Quest is over.
</p>
