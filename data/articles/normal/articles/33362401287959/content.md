<p>
  The
  <strong>United Kingdom's Online Safety Act</strong> (“<strong>OSA</strong>”)
  introduces new responsibilities for online platforms to reduce safety risks
  and provide age-appropriate experiences for users, especially teens. In
  compliance with these regulations, all new and existing UK users will be
  assigned updated default settings. 
</p>
<p>
  Although Discord has not been classified as an Age-Restricted Social Media
  Platform under <strong>Australia’s </strong
  ><a
    href="https://www.esafety.gov.au/young-people/social-media-age-restrictions#quick-facts"
    ><strong
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
        >Social Media Minimum Age Act</span
      ></strong
    ></a
  >, we have decided to apply this same experience to new and existing users in
  Australia starting <strong>December 9th, 2025</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  These defaults include automatic content filtering (which is already enabled
  for teens everywhere) to reduce the likelihood that teens encounter certain
  types of potentially harmful content, as well as different social settings. UK
  and Australian users who wish to access content flagged by our filters or
  customize those settings can do so only after verifying that they are 18 or
  older through our new privacy-forward age assurance experience. 
</p>
<p>
  Our new privacy-forward age assurance experience is required in specific
  scenarios (more details below), while building on our commitment to fostering
  genuine connections and a positive online experience. Check out this
  <a
    href="http://discord.com/safety/adapting-discord-for-the-uk-online-safety-act"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">article</span></a
  >
  to learn more about why we are making these changes in the UK and this
  <a
    href="http://discord.com/safety/introducing-new-teen-by-default-experiences-for-australian-users"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">article</span></a
  >
  for Australia.
</p>
<p>
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Age assurance </span></a
  >is only available to UK and Australian users and is a one-time process. Once
  completed, you won't need to verify your age again.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KBKHG0AXD0XK806RNTYHM2W9">Privacy-Forward Age Assurance</h1>
<p>
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Age assurance</span
    ><span style="color: #1155cc"> </span></a
  >is the backbone of this new experience for all new and existing UK and
  Australian users.  
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee5932033a8c89b4b2cab4b1aa526f27d">
    <strong>Privacy-protecting process. </strong>Discord and k-ID do not
    permanently store personal identity documents or your video selfies. Images
    of your identity documents and ID match selfies are deleted directly after
    your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used for facial age
    estimation never leaves your device.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eba5ed446192d9258e893c7d8275ddf1a">
    <strong>Verify once.</strong> After completing the age assurance process,
    your Discord experience is tailored to your verified age group, and you
    won't be prompted again.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4ad012c7449cd05c091b08bee152495e">
    <strong>You can always re-verify. </strong>If you believe you’ve been
    incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you can attempt to re-verify. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5ee3573dc4c9b6848b95f4f361973925">
    <strong>Visible only to you.</strong> Other users can’t see your
    verification status or verified age group. That information is private to
    you.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  If you have not already verified your age group, you will be prompted to age
  verify if you attempt to:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e38e156457202501446d5073b671c5cd3">
    Unblur sensitive content flagged by our
    <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/18210995019671/"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Sensitive Content Filter</span></a
    >. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9971a584901de0da6663a51046480f0a">
    Change any of the default
    <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/18210995019671/"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
        >Sensitive Content Filter settings</span
      ></a
    >
    to “<strong>Show</strong>” sensitive content that’s blurred by the Sensitive
    Content Filter.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e6dc4686d87c19b0aee8483431542c13b">
    Turn off
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/7924992471191-Message-Requests"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Message Requests</span> (</a
    >they are enabled by default to help you screen out unwanted DMs from your
    DM list).
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e01f15decca060be7ba8fdedf1bb0aac0">
    Access
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age-restricted (18+) channels</span></a
    >. 
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="book-box">
  To learn more about how to verify your age, go to our
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Assurance on Discord</span
    ></a
  >
</div>
<h1 id="h_01KBKHG0B2D0MG2RZXBR6MKR1P">Default Safety Settings</h1>
<p>
  All new and existing UK and Australian users will have enhanced default safety
  settings. Age assurance may be required when you try to change certain
  settings or access specific content.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01KBKHG0B2QNMK6M7QH286MXBH">Content Default Settings Changes</h2>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e040964b41eed163df3f9f856605b0ade">
    Like before, the
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/18210995019671-Discord-Sensitive-Content-Filters"
      ><strong
        ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Sensitive Content Filter</span></strong
      ></a
    >
    is enabled by default to help protect you from seeing unwanted sensitive and
    mature content. The default settings for this feature will blur content
    flagged in DMs with friends and messages in server channels, but it will
    also block content flagged in DMs with strangers. What’s changed  is that
    modifying  these default content settings to <strong>“Show”</strong> will
    now require age assurance.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01KBKHG0B9RCRRDR9NKG9Z26V8">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why is age assurance only required for UK and Australian users?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: We have been working hard to meet our obligations under the UK Online
  Safety Act and how best to deliver age-appropriate experiences on Discord in
  light of the Australian
  <a
    href="https://www.esafety.gov.au/young-people/social-media-age-restrictions#quick-facts"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Social Media Minimum Age Act</span></a
  >. UK and Australian users will experience new default settings designed to
  create a teen-appropriate environment, while supporting positive engagement on
  Discord.
</p>
<p>
  While these changes only apply to UK and Australian users, they build on our
  existing safety architecture and represent our ongoing commitment to
  age-appropriate experiences worldwide.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do these changes apply to all UK and Australian Discord users?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The features and experiences described above apply to all new and existing
  UK and Australian accounts.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What age assurance methods are available?</strong></p>
<p>A: There are two primary assurance methods available:</p>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="e499c4b39ce556fb15bcac83f27af3e39">
    Face Scan: Uses your device's camera to verify your age group
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e6447721ba3102557744e5821b64b6ca5">
    ID Scan: Allows you to scan an identification document
  </li>
</ol>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note: </strong>You may need to verify using both methods if Discord
  isn't fully confident in the facial age estimation alone.
</div>
<div class="book-box">
  For detailed instructions on each age assurance method, please see our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343-How-to-Verify-Age-Group"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Assurance on Discord</span
    ></a
  ><span style="color: #434343"> article.</span>
</div>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is my personal data stored during the age assurance process?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or
  your video selfies. Images of your identity documents and ID match selfies are
  deleted directly after your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used
  for facial age estimation never leaves your device.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: What if my age assurance fails or shows incorrect results?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: If the age assurance process fails or shows incorrect results, retry the
  verification through Discord system messages by selecting
  "<strong>Retry</strong>". 
</p>
<p>
  If you were incorrectly identified as under
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040724612"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">minimum age requirement</span></a
  >
  and want to appeal, please follow instructions
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932-Help-I-m-old-enough-to-use-Discord-in-my-country-but-I-got-locked-out"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >
  to use the ID scan option to verify your date of birth.
</p>
<div class="book-box">
  For more information on handling age assurance issues, visit our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343-How-to-Verify-Age-Group"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Assurance on Discord</span
    ></a
  ><span style="color: #434343"> article.</span>
</div>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age every time I access age-restricted content or
    attempt to update a default setting?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. Age assurance is a one-time process. Once your age group is confirmed,
  you won't need to verify again. If you are verified into the teen age group
  (13-17 years old), you will not be able to adjust the settings described above
  until you verify your age group to be at least 18 years old. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What happens if I'm verified as under 18?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you're verified as under 18, certain default safety settings will remain
  in place that cannot be changed. These include sensitive content filters,
  message request settings and restrictions when attempting to access
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age-restricted channels</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I’m verified as too young to be on Discord?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Your account will be banned if you are under the minimum age requirement to
  be on Discord. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I appeal if I believe my account was incorrectly age-restricted or
    banned?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. If you believe you were incorrectly verified and want to appeal, you
  may try the verification process using the ID scan option. 
</p>
<p>
  Please follow the instructions
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932-Help-I-m-old-enough-to-use-Discord-in-my-country-but-I-got-locked-out"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >
  to use the ID scan option to verify your date of birth.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Are there plans to add these changes to other regions in the
    world?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Supporting our users’ well-being is at the heart of our work here at
  Discord. While this update is focused on UK and Australian users, we continue
  to invest in features that create a more age-appropriate experience for our
  global community as we work with key stakeholders across the industry, as well
  as parents, digital wellness experts and regulators worldwide. Every region
  has different needs, and we’re committed to building experiences that empower
  users as they continue to use our platform.
</p>
