<p>
  The
  <strong>United Kingdom's Online Safety Act</strong> (“<strong>OSA</strong>”)
  introduces new responsibilities for online platforms to reduce safety risks
  and provide age-appropriate experiences for users, especially teens. In
  compliance with the UK OSA, all new and existing UK users will be assigned
  updated default settings. 
</p>
<p>
  These defaults include automatic content filtering (which is already enabled
  for teens everywhere) to reduce the likelihood that teens encounter certain
  types of potentially harmful content, as well as different social settings. UK
  users who wish to access content flagged by our filters or customize those
  settings can do so only after verifying that they are 18 or older through our
  new privacy-forward age verification experience. 
</p>
<p>
  Our new privacy-forward age verification experience is required in specific
  scenarios that meet the OSA’s requirements (more details below), while
  building on our commitment to fostering genuine connections and a positive
  online experience. Check out our
  <a
    href="http://discord.com/safety/adapting-discord-for-the-uk-online-safety-act"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Safety Center article</span></a
  >
  to learn more about why we are making these changes due to the UK OSA.
</p>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Age verification</span></a
  >
  is only available to UK users and is a one-time process. Once completed, you
  won't need to verify again.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01K0FX0JKDCJ8NS9FZSEP4CQE0">Privacy-Forward Age Verification</h1>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Age verification</span></a
  >
  is the backbone of this new experience for all new and existing UK users.  
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Privacy-protecting process. </strong>Discord and k-ID do not
    permanently store personal identity documents or your video selfies. Images
    of your identity documents and ID match selfies are deleted directly after
    your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used for facial age
    estimation never leaves your device.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Verify once.</strong> After completing verification, your Discord
    experience is tailored to your verified age group, and you won't be prompted
    again.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>You can always re-verify. </strong>If you believe you’ve been
    incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you can attempt to re-verify.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Visible only to you.</strong> Other users can’t see your
    verification status or verified age group. That information is private to
    you.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  If you have not already verified your age group, you will be prompted to age
  verify if you attempt to:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    Unblur sensitive content flagged by our
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/18210995019671/"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Sensitive Content Filter</span></a
    >.
  </li>
  <li>
    Change any of the default
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/18210995019671/"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
        >Sensitive Content Filter settings</span
      ></a
    >
    to “<strong>Show</strong>” sensitive content that’s blurred by the Sensitive
    Content Filter.
  </li>
  <li>
    Turn off
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/7924992471191-Message-Requests"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Message Requests</span> (</a
    >they are enabled by default to help you screen out unwanted DMs from your
    DM list).
  </li>
  <li>
    Access
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age-restricted (18+) channels</span></a
    >. 
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="book-box">
  To learn more about how to age verify, go to our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Verification on Discord article.</span

<h1 id="h_01K0FX0JKJG6BHXBJ3BZGDB5FC">Default Safety Settings</h1>
<p>
  All new and existing UK users will have enhanced default safety settings. Age
  verification may be required when you try to change certain settings or access
  specific content.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01K0FX0JKKY7P5B5NCJ2AQ1N24">Content Default Settings Changes</h2>
<ul>
  <li>
    Like before, the
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/18210995019671-Discord-Sensitive-Content-Filters"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><strong
        ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Sensitive Content Filter</span></strong
      ></a
    >
    is enabled by default to help protect you from seeing unwanted sensitive and
    mature content. The default settings for this feature will blur content
    flagged in DMs with friends and messages in server channels, but it will
    block content flagged in DMs with strangers. Attempting to change these
    default settings to <strong>“Show”</strong> will require age verification.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01K0FX0JKT59GVYYQ1NSVT1DRQ">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Why is age verification only required for UK users?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: We have been working hard to meet our obligations under the UK Online
  Safety Act. As this legislation comes into effect, UK users will experience
  new default settings designed to create a teen-appropriate experience by
  default, while maintaining a positive experience using the service.
</p>
<p>
  While the Online Safety Act introduces new requirements specifically for UK
  users, these changes build on our existing safety architecture and represent
  our ongoing commitment to age-appropriate experiences worldwide.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Does this apply to all UK Discord users?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The features and experiences described above apply to all new and existing
  UK accounts.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What age verification methods are available?</strong></p>
<p>A: There are two primary verification methods available:</p>
<ol>
  <li>Face Scan: Uses your device's camera to verify your age group</li>
  <li>ID Scan: Allows you to scan an identification document</li>
</ol>
<p>
  You can also request a manual review with our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000029731"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Trust &amp; Safety team</a
  >.
</p>
<div class="book-box">
  For detailed instructions on each method, please see our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343-How-to-Verify-Age-Group"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Verification on Discord</span
    ></a
  >
  article.
</div>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is my personal data stored during the verification process?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or
  your video selfies. Images of your identity documents and ID match selfies are
  deleted directly after your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used
  for facial age estimation never leaves your device.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What if my age verification fails or shows incorrect results?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If verification fails or shows incorrect results, you have two options:
</p>
<ol>
  <li>
    Retry the verification through Discord system messages by selecting
    "<strong>Retry</strong>".
  </li>
  <li>Request a manual review through our support system.</li>
</ol>
<div class="book-box">
  For more information on handling verification issues, visit our
  <a

    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Complete Age Verification on Discord</span

</div>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age every time I access age-restricted content or
    attempt to update a default setting?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. Age verification is a one-time process. Once your age group is
  confirmed, you won't need to verify again. If you are verified into the teen
  age group (13-17 years old), you will not be able to adjust the settings
  described above until you verify as at least 18 years old. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What happens if I'm verified as under 18?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you're verified as under 18, certain default safety settings will remain
  in place that cannot be changed. These include sensitive content filters,
  message request settings and restrictions when attempting to access
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age-restricted channels</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I appeal if I believe my account was incorrectly
    age-restricted?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. If you believe you were incorrectly verified into the teen age group,
  you may retry the verification process or submit an
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932-Help-I-m-old-enough-to-use-Discord-in-my-country-but-I-got-locked-out#:~:text=Submit%20your%20photo%20to%20our,team%20assist%20you%20more%20effectively."
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">appeal</span></a
  >
  for a manual review. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I’m verified as too young to be on Discord?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Your account will be banned if you are under 13 years of age. If you
  believe we made a mistake, you can
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932-Help-I-m-old-enough-to-use-Discord-in-my-country-but-I-got-locked-out#:~:text=Submit%20your%20photo%20to%20our,team%20assist%20you%20more%20effectively."
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">make an appeal</span></a
  >.
</p>

<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Are there plans to add these changes to other regions in the
    world?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Supporting our users’ well-being is at the heart of our work here at
  Discord. While this update is focused on UK users, we continue to invest in
  features that create age-appropriate experiences for our global community as
  we work with key stakeholders across the industry, as well as parents, digital
  wellness experts and regulators worldwide. Every region has different needs,
  and we’re committed to building experiences that empower users as they
  continue to use our platform.
</p>
