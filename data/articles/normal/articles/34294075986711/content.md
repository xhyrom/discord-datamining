<h1 id="h_01K31JTQCXZM7KSJ5NK8PQ159E">What is the Shop Sale?</h1>
<p>
  Starting <strong>August 27, 2025</strong> until
  <strong>September 3, 2025 (5:00PM UTC)</strong>, select users will be able to
  enjoy a discounted Shop item for a limited time. 
</p>
<h1 id="h_01K31JTQCY0BZCDX317J5F2ZBW">Who Can Access the Discord Shop Sale?</h1>
<p>
  This promotion is available to select Discord users on the desktop app only.
  Eligible users will receive an exclusive discount on their first qualifying
  Shop item purchase. 
</p>
<p><strong>How to know if you're eligible:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>You'll see discount percentages displayed on eligible items</li>
  <li>A promotional banner will appear in the Shop tab</li>
  <li>
    A special "<strong>OFFER</strong>" badge will be visible on the Shop tab
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01K31JTQD5GS1D6XN3ZQ26GFGZ">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Is the Shop discount available on multiple purchases?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, eligible users will receive an exclusive discount on their first
  qualifying Shop item purchase only. The discount cannot be applied to
  subsequent purchases.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see the Shop Sale discounts?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: This sale is available to select users only. If you don't see promotional
  banners, discount labels, or countdown timers in your Shop, you're not part of
  this particular sale. Discord will be running future promotions in the Shop,
  so stay tuned for more! 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I use my discount on mobile? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, the Shop sale is only available on desktop. You'll need to use
  Discord's desktop app or web version to access the discounted pricing.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I combine Orbs with my Shop Sale discount?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Unfortunately, items purchased with Orbs are not eligible for the Shop
  sale. You'll need to use a credit card, PayPal, or other standard payment
  method.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What happens if I don't use my discount? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: Your discount will expire when the promotion ends on
  <strong>September 3, 2025</strong> <strong>(5:00PM UTC)</strong>, regardless
  of when you first saw it. Make sure to complete your purchase before the
  deadline!
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I gift discounted items to friends during the Shop Sale?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes! Gifting is fully supported during the Discord Shop Sale. However, the
  discount only applies to your first qualifying purchase. If you've already
  used your discount, it won't apply when gifting items later.
</p>
