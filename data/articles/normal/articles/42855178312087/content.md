  Discord to use age signals shared from the
  <a
    href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/127462"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple App Store</span></a
  >
  or
  <a
    href="https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2026/07/google-play-age-signals-api-safer-experiences.html"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google Play Store</span></a
  >
  to confirm your age group. In compliance with this law, Discord will receive
  your age range associated with your device’s app store. 
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H1F42BAA5YVWBANHEZMSAW">
  How the App Store Age Check Works in Texas
</h1>
<p>
  For Texas users, Discord receives the age range associated with your device’s
  app store automatically when you open the app. This is different from how age
  assurance on Discord works in most other regions, where you’re only prompted
  to confirm your age if you try to access age-restricted content, spaces, or
  settings. <br />Here’s how the app store age range check works: 
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8beb8dc96a1fe1afdef8d540a8e3ffb2">
    Discord checks the account settings for your device’s app store account
    (such as your Apple or Google account) for an existing age range. You can
    refer to
    <a
      href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple</span></a
    >
    or
    <a
      href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27442?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&amp;oco=1#zippy=%2Cbirthday"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google’s</span></a
    >
    help pages for details on how to ensure your age is accurately reflected in
    your account.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb268e0cc6e0413c845408865ed8cde60">
    <strong>If your app store account shows that you are 18 or older:</strong>
    you are placed in the adult age group and can access age-restricted content,
    spaces, and settings on Discord. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e1618f3e2c55ded6ef73fe4a354c244e0">
    <strong
      >If your app store account shows that you are between 13 and 17:</strong
    >
    you will be placed into the teen age group, and will not be able to access
    age-restricted content and spaces, or change certain
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42178056096663"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">settings</span></a
    >
    (for example, you cannot disable Sensitive Content Filters). Messaging
    friends, joining voice calls, and accessing non age-restricted channels and
    servers all continue to work as normal. 
  </li>
</ul>
<p class="wysiwyg-indent1">
  If your app store age range incorrectly shows that you’re a teen, you can
  refer to
  <a
    href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple</span></a
  >
  or
  <a
    href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27442?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&amp;oco=1#zippy=%2Cbirthday"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google’s</span></a
  >
  help pages for details on how to update the age that is associated with your
  account. You can also confirm your age through a different method, see
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Confirm Your Age Group on Discord</span
    ></a
  >
  for instructions. 
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e03698bba1c9266ea67420c088642eb1e">
    <strong>If your app store account shows that you are under 13:</strong> your
    account will be disabled because you must be at least 13 years old to use
    Discord.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01M2H1F42G8JFBXBZP4B7KJ09H">
  What Happens If the Age Check Doesn’t Return a Result
</h1>
<p>
  If the check via the app store doesn’t return a result, you can continue using
  Discord as usual. You just won’t be able to access age-restricted content,
  spaces, or settings until you confirm you’re an adult, whether it’s through
  having your app store age range shared in the future, or through one of our
  other
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age assurance methods</span></a
  >. 
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M2H1F42HZK5B9CZTSW2VPEVK">Parental Controls and Account Access</h1>
<p>
  Texas law also gives parents and guardians control over whether their teen can
  use apps like Discord at all, separate from the age assurance process. 
</p>
<p>
  If your parent or guardian removes your permission to use Discord through your
  device’s app store parental controls, your account will be blocked from
  accessing Discord until your parent or guardian restores permission. There is
  no appeal process through Discord for this type of block, and access is
  restored only when your parent or guardian reverses the change in your
  device’s parental controls. If your account is blocked in this way; your
  servers, friends list, and message history will stay intact and will be
  restored if access is reinstated within our standard retention window of 2
  years. Beyond that, your account will be subject to
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/5106714856215-Inactive-Account-Deletion"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">deletion</span></a
  >
  like any other inactive Discord account. 
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note</strong>: If you're a parent or guardian, here’s how to </span
  ><a
    href="https://support.apple.com/125399"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">restore access on Apple devices</span></a
  ><span style="color: #434343">.</span>
</div>
<p>
  If you previously removed permissions for your teen to use Discord and want to
  allow it again:
</p>
<p>
  1. Your teen will need to initiate an exception request. When trying to open
  an app with an age rating higher than the app age-rating restriction that you
  set for them, your teen will see a message that says content restrictions are
  enabled and can ask for an exception.
</p>
<p>
  2. If they ask for an exception, you'll receive a message in Apple’s Messages
  app. You can approve or decline the request right from the message
  notification or in the Messages app.
</p>
<p>
  3. On your device: When you tap <strong>Review the request</strong>, the app's
  page opens. You can approve or decline the exception, or view the app's Age
  Rating Details.
</p>
<p>
  4. Discord will detect the restored permission and lift the account block
  automatically. No Discord support ticket or appeal is needed.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H1F42S8V2ETQNRQ0ARV1Y6">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Do these changes apply to all Discord users in Texas?</strong> 
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, the automatic app store age range check applies to all new and
  existing Discord accounts in Texas when using Discord on their iOS or Android
  devices. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why is my age range shared automatically, when users elsewhere are only
    asked to confirm their age when they try to access something
    age-restricted?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Texas's App Store Accountability Act requires app stores to share age
  signals automatically with developers like Discord, rather than checking when
  someone tries to access age-restricted content. That's why Texas users will
  have their age ranges shared automatically when opening Discord on an iOS or
  Android device. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What information does Discord receive from the app store check?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord only receives your age range (for example, whether you're 18 or
  older), not your name, Apple or Google ID, birthdate, or any other account
  information.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: What if I don't want to share my age from the app store?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Texas's App Store Accountability Act requires app stores to share age
  signals with developers like Discord for users in Texas, when opening Discord
  on an iOS or Android device. More details can be found in the
  <a
    href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/127462"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple</span></a
  >
  and
  <a
    href="https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16569691?hl=en#zippy=%2Cwhen-is-the-age-signal-available-for-example-on-install-first-launch-or-on-demand%2Cwhich-users-will-be-required-to-be-verified"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google</span></a
  >
  help pages. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if my app store account says I'm under 18 but I'm actually
    an adult?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If your app store age range is incorrect, you can refer to
  <a
    href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple</span></a
  >
  or
  <a
    href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27442?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&amp;oco=1#zippy=%2Cbirthday"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google’s</span></a
  >
  help pages for details on how to update the age that is associated with your
  account. You can also confirm your age through a different method, the same
  way any user can if they are initially placed in the wrong age group. See
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >How to Confirm Your Age Group on Discord</span
    ></a
  >
  for instructions.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If my age is shared through the app store once, will I need to confirm
    it again?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: In most cases, no. However, if the age range from your app store account
  later changes (for example, if a parent or guardian updates or removes it),
  you may need to reconfirm your age. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can a parent or guardian control my access to Discord separately from
    age assurance?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, if a parent or guardian removes permission for you to use Discord
  through their app store parental controls you will be blocked from accessing
  Discord until they restore that permission. See
  <a href="#h_01M2H1F42HZK5B9CZTSW2VPEVK"
    >Parental Controls and Account Access above</a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I appeal if I believe my account was incorrectly restricted?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If your app store age range that was shared is incorrect, you can refer to
  <a
    href="https://support.apple.com/en-us/102473"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Apple</span></a
  >
  or
  <a
    href="https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/27442?co=GENIE.Platform%3DAndroid&amp;oco=1#zippy=%2Cbirthday"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Google’s</span></a
  >
  help pages for details on how to update the age that is associated with your
  account, or retry
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">confirming your age</span></a
  >. If your account was blocked because a parent or guardian removed app
  permissions (see
  <a href="#h_01M2H1F42HZK5B9CZTSW2VPEVK"
    >Parental Controls and Account Access</a
  >), those are separate permissions that can only be changed by your parent or
  guardian, not by retrying to confirm your age.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If my Discord account was blocked via app store parental controls by my
    parent or guardian, can I unblock it once I turn 18?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, assuming you turn 18 within our standard 2 year retention period for
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/5106714856215-Inactive-Account-Deletion"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">inactive accounts</span></a
  >, you can end parental controls on your app store account. After, when you
  launch the Discord app your account will automatically unlock.
</p>
