<h2 id="h_01M5B09PE0F12R934RRW1QEGZJ">Account Age Badge</h2>
<figure class="wysiwyg-image">
  <img
    style="aspect-ratio: 992/302"
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44370048467351"
    alt="Example of Account Age Badge"
    width="992"
    height="302"
  />
  <figcaption><em>Example of Account Age Badge</em></figcaption>
</figure>
<p>
  The <strong>Account Age</strong> badge reflects how long your account has
  existed. It unlocks once your account is a year old and evolves as your
  account gets older. Once you unlock a level, you won't lose it or drop back to
  a lower level. The badge evolves as time goes on.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M5B09PE16ZXEK11WNNYDA4W3">Game Time Badge</h2>
<figure class="wysiwyg-image">
  <img
    style="aspect-ratio: 1018/318"
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44370063946007"
    alt="Example of Game Time Badge"
    width="1018"
    height="318"
  />
  <figcaption>
    <em><span data-token-index="0">Example of Game Time Badge</span></em>
  </figcaption>
</figure>
<p>
  The <strong>Game Time</strong> badge reflects the total time you've spent
  playing games that Discord can detect. Time across all detected games counts
  toward the same total, and the badge evolves as that total grows.
  Unfortunately, games you play on consoles, like Xbox or PlayStation, don't
  count toward the Game Time badge, since only the Discord desktop app can
  detect games. Game Time includes time played on or after December 8, 2025.
  Badge progress may take some time to reflect your recent activity.
</p>
<p>
  To count progress toward these badges, turn on
  <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong>. Check
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21865322754327"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong>Data Used to Personalize Discord</strong></a
  >
  for details on how to enable this setting.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M5B09PE1KGVRVQCD136FN6YB">Game Variety Badge</h2>
<figure class="wysiwyg-image">
  <img
    style="aspect-ratio: 1020/306"
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/44370063946391"
    alt=" Example of Game Variety Badge"
    width="1020"
    height="306"
  />
  <figcaption>
    <em><span data-token-index="0">Example of Game Variety Badge</span></em>
  </figcaption>
</figure>
<p>
  The <strong>Game Variety</strong> badge reflects how many different games
  you've played that Discord recognizes. The badge evolves as that number grows.
</p>
<p>
  Discord recognizes most desktop games automatically, though some very small
  games may not be detected. Some platforms are tracked as a single game. For
  example, playing different experiences in Roblox or Fortnite counts as one
  game.
</p>
<p>
  Games you play on consoles, like Xbox or PlayStation, don't count toward the
  Game Variety badge. This badge only tracks games detected by the Discord
  desktop app. Badge progress may take some time to reflect your recent
  activity.
</p>
<p>
  To count progress toward these badges, turn on
  <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong>. Check
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21865322754327"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong>Data Used to Personalize Discord</strong></a
  >
  for details on how to enable this setting.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M5B09PEAGRM9KHFBZJ9HVYYT">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do console games count toward the Game Time or Game Variety
    badges?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Not at this time. These badges only count games detected by the Discord
  desktop app, so games played on consoles like Xbox or PlayStation aren't
  included.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I earn the Game Time, Game Variety, or Streaming badges on
    mobile?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. These badges can only be unlocked and leveled up using the Discord
  desktop app.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why isn't my badge progress updating?</strong></p>
<p dir="ltr">
  A: Badge progress may take some time to reflect your recent activity. If your
  progress still does not update, confirm that you are using the Discord desktop
  app and that the
  <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong> setting is
  enabled. For instructions on enabling this setting, see
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21865322754327"
    >Data Used to Personalize Discord</a
  >.
</p>
<p dir="ltr">
  Once you earn a badge, it appears in your Badge Directory along with details
  about how it was earned. To learn how to hide or reorder the badges on your
  profile, see
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/44393096706583"
    >How to Manage Your Discord Profile Badges</a
  >.
</p>
