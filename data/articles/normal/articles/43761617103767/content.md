<h1 id="h_01M3N49TZ4REXHVCK1TD1DJ2NR">
  <strong>What is a Riot Cash Code?</strong>
</h1>
<p>
  A Riot Cash Code is a $10 code redeemable for in-game currency in select Riot
  Games titles, including League of Legends and VALORANT. When you subscribe to
  Nitro for the first time, you can unlock a Riot Cash Code as a welcome
  incentive.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M3N49TZ46MN56GZ83NMT37W0">
  <strong>Eligibility Requirements</strong>
</h1>
<p><strong>You are eligible if:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebc37182f49743331d1c6b61e1d36d53b">
    You subscribe to Nitro (not Nitro Basic) as a new subscriber. Subscribing
    unlocks your Riot Cash Code. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e35e126cc67dc0d596ea946d915e04417">
    You have an active League of Legends and/or VALORANT account that you've
    played on within the last 180 days.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ecb562d675aab97ebab47d2100b9faf3c">
    You are located in the United States.
  </li>
</ul>
<p><strong>You are not eligible if:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e54d1a743b7c996e9aa10f6fb47f6b05a">
    You already have an active Discord Nitro subscription at the time of this
    offer.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec5c72058f70c4779e10f3e2562402edb">
    You have Nitro Basic, Nitro Classic, or are currently using Nitro credits.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eca039e4ff98637a3034fef4d5a556ae6">
    You have already received a Riot Cash Code through this offer. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e366f7283affdd5adbc147861085f6844">
    You have not played League of Legends or Valorant in the last 6 months
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01M3N49TZM8X57QR0DPNAG36KY">
  <strong>Frequently Asked Questions</strong>
</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Do I need a Nitro subscription to get a Riot Cash Code?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. This offer is available to certain new Nitro subscribers. Refer to our
  Eligibility Requirements above for more details. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I already have an active Nitro subscription. Can I still get a Riot Cash
    Code?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No, this offer is available to new subscribers only. If you're already
  subscribed to Nitro, you're not eligible for this offer.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Where do I redeem my Riot Cash Code?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: You can redeem your code at
  <a href="http://shop.riotgames.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">shop.riotgames.com</span></a
  >
  while logged into your Riot Games account, or through the prepaid codes and
  cards section at checkout in participating Riot titles.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What if codes for this offer run out?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Codes are limited. If none are available when you check, you'll see a
  message letting you know more are on the way, and you can check back later.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Does my Riot Cash Code expire?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Your code doesn't expire once you've claimed it, but you must claim it in
  Nitro Home by <strong>October 28, 2026</strong>. If you don't claim it by
  then, you won't be able to get your code.
</p>
