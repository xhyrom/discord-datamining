<h1 id="h_01KXENYB4BHHJBHVBRTY3T7PKN">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Q: I don’t see the promotion details on my Nitro tab. Why?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: If you don’t see any promotion details on your Nitro tab on the desktop,
  browser, or Android app, it is because you do not meet the eligibility
  criteria for this promotion. Note that this promotion is not available on the
  iOS Discord mobile app.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If I upgrade my subscription from Nitro Basic or Nitro Classic to Nitro,
    can I receive this promotion?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, this promotion applies for upgrades from Nitro Basic or Classic to
  Nitro.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: How much will I be charged if I upgrade my plan to Nitro?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: We prorate the amount you’ll be charged for upgrading to Nitro, which
  depends on:
</p>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="e94dea23e235227875a75f60cd08775b9">
    Whether you’re upgrading from Basic or Classic
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee4ffb3e2d755bcf486b36af70936966f">
    The amount of your existing subscription plan you haven’t used
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e07a3b8622d7b4489ca8ef32b03e76478">
    The remaining amount of your new subscription plan you will use after
    upgrading
  </li>
</ol>
<p>
  For example, if you’ve used Nitro Basic for only 10 days this month, you’ll
  only be charged for those 10 days at Basic’s price. Then, if you upgrade to
  Nitro mid-month and have 20 days left, you’ll only pay for those 20 days at
  Nitro’s price, rather than for the entire month.
</p>
<p>
  You can find more details
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017693772-Payments-and-Billing-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I claimed and redeemed a different trial for Discord Nitro already. Can
    I access this promo?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Unfortunately, if you are currently on a free trial, discounted plan or
  have been in the last 8 days before attempting to claim, you are not eligible
  for this promotion. Have no fear, there will be more of these promotions down
  the road!
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If I have Nitro credit in my account, can I apply it to this
    promo?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you have credit in your account from a previous Nitro subscription, this
  credit will be automatically applied to the purchase total when you check out.
</p>
<p>
  If you have credit in your account that is associated with a different Nitro
  subscription type (ex: Nitro Classic), it will not apply to this promotion.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I’m currently using Nitro credit and claim this
    promotional offer?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: On desktop, Nitro credit users can purchase and claim this promotion.
  Here’s how it works:
</p>
<p>
  When you claim the offer while on Nitro credit, your paid subscription will
  begin immediately. Any remaining Nitro credit will be applied after your first
  paid month ends, followed by your extra month of Nitro, and then your regular
  subscription will continue.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Example:</strong> If you have 4 days of Nitro credit remaining when
  you claim the offer:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec4e0238581d2c6037dce38346f7b65f2">
    Your first paid month starts immediately.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efe384fa4aa85949d6a8ef4d6d3e0f0eb">
    After 30 days, your remaining 4 days of Nitro credit activates.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e411cfe753a93ada9deb7ffc7e406303f">
    Once those 4 days end, your extra month of Nitro begins.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea36e772c3912f9d0bef3ae519f314d2b">
    After that, your regular monthly subscription resumes.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I see a notification saying “You’re ineligible for this offer.”
    Why?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you encounter this notification, it is because you do not meet the
  eligibility criteria outlined above.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Will my subscription renew automatically after the promotion
    ends?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes your subscription will renew automatically after the promotion ends. If
  you’d like to make changes to your subscription, including cancel, you can
  find more information
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/19580873036695-How-to-Cancel-your-Nitro-Server-Boosting-or-other-Discord-Subscriptions"

    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I gift my extra month of Nitro to someone else?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, this month of Nitro is yours to keep and will be automatically applied
  to your account. It can’t be gifted or transferred to another user. Note that
  gifted Nitro subscriptions are not eligible for this promotion.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Is this promotion available on iOS?</strong></p>
<p>A: This promotion is not available on the Discord iOS mobile app. </p>

    >Q: I purchased a Nitro subscription during the promotion period, but I’d
