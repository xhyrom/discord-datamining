<h1 id="h_01KBDWQWVGND8E66PZBXVWWC8N">What is the Marvel Rivals Shop?</h1>
<p>
  The Marvel Rivals Shop is a shop in the Marvel Rivals server that lets you
  purchase Marvel Rivals in-game items directly through Discord. 
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> All prices in the Marvel Rivals Shop are shown in U.S.
  dollars (USD). Your selected payment method will automatically handle any
  necessary currency conversion.
</div>
<h1 id="h_01KBDWQWVJH5YH3WB1KW0EZ6VS">How to Access the Marvel Rivals Shop</h1>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> The Marvel Rivals Shop is currently available on the
  Discord desktop app only, within the official Marvel Rivals server. Items
  purchased through the Marvel Rivals Shop can only be redeemed in Marvel Rivals
  on PC or Xbox.
</div>
<p>
  You can access the Marvel Rivals Shop from the official
  <a href="https://discord.com/channels/1193841000108531764/game-shop"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Marvel Rivals Discord server</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>1. Join the Marvel Rivals Discord server (if you haven't already).</p>
<p>
  2. Select the <strong>Marvel Rivals Shop</strong> tab towards the top of the
  server’s channel list
</p>
<p>3. You can now browse all available Marvel Shop items.</p>

<h1 id="h_01KBDWQWVZ8KCGHCE2KCV433WF">Gifting Marvel Rivals Cosmetics </h1>
<p>
  Want to surprise a friend with a Marvel Rivals cosmetic? You can gift items
  directly through the Marvel Rivals Shop!
</p>
<p>1. Browse the Marvel Rivals Shop and select an item you'd like to gift.</p>
<p>2. Select the <strong>Gift Item</strong> button.</p>
<p>3. You can personalize your gift with a custom message </p>
<p>
  4. Once you’re ready, complete your purchase by following the prompts and send
  your gift.

<h1 id="h_01KBDWQWW4296MHY13MZWQ87TP">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Can I refund a Marvel Rivals purchase?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Generally, purchases in the Marvel Rivals shop are non-refundable. However,
  there are a few cases where Discord would provide a refund for your Marvel
  Rivals in-game item purchase: 
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e63a37379b7f175976a0813668fa59352">
    If the in-game item is already owned by the user within Marvel Rivals 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8f6874083ed5a473930fb633676b23f9">
    If the user is unable to claim the in-game item on their PC or Xbox
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  In these instances, if the user attempts to claim the item within the 14-day
  claim period, Discord will attempt to automatically process a refund. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Are Marvel Rivals Shop items available in all regions?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: The Marvel Rivals Shop items are available in the United States, Canada,
  UK, Ireland, and Oceania.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What currency does the Marvel Rivals Shop support?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: All prices in the Marvel Rivals Shop are shown in U.S. dollars (USD). Your
  selected payment method will automatically handle any necessary currency
  conversion.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How do I see what items I've purchased?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: You can see any purchases on your Billing tab in settings in the
  ‘Transaction History’ section.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I gift items to someone who doesn't play Marvel Rivals?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, you can gift Marvel Rivals cosmetics to any Discord user. If they
  don’t play Marvel Rivals, they will need to create an account and link that
  account with Discord in order to accept the gift. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Which platform can I redeem the Marvel Rivals Shop items on?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Currently only supported on desktop platforms (Steam, Epic, Loading Bay).
  If your connected account is on PlayStation therefore unable to receive the
  item, we may offer a refund, provided that you or the gift recipient attempted
  to claim the item within 14 days of purchase.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I purchase or receive a gift of an item I already own in
    the game?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Where a user purchases an item that they already own in Rivals, we will
  issue a refund for duplicate items that can’t be claimed in-game. If you
  attempt to claim a gifted item that you already own in-game, the gift sender
  will be refunded. Note that purchasers and gift receivers must attempt to
  claim items within 14 days to ensure Discord can issue any eligible refunds. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I purchase from the Marvel Rivals Shop without linking my
    account?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, you can purchase from the shop without linking your account, but you
  will need to link your account to claim the item. You can find the account
  linking entrance in <strong>Settings</strong> <strong>&gt;</strong>
  <strong>Billing</strong> <strong>&gt;</strong> navigate to the item you
  purchased <strong>&gt;</strong> <strong>Connect Account</strong>.
</p>
