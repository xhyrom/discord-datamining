<h1 id="h_01M74041ZRCT3K5MP26DFVQ8HT">Filing a Grievance</h1>
<p>
  If you are in India and would like to file a complaint under applicable local
  laws regarding content on Discord, please submit your complaint to Discord’s
  Grievance Officer, whose contact details are set out below.
</p>

  <a

<h1 id="h_01M74041ZSJ4TCZCFHS7DWZA0W">
  Publishers of News and Current Affairs Content
</h1>
<p>
  If you are a publisher of news and current affairs content under the
  Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code)
  Rules, 2021, Discord is required to inform you that you must provide details
  of your Discord accounts to the Ministry of Information and Broadcasting,
  Government of India as required under Rule 18.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M74041ZS4JBHC6TRBC1RZSSF">
  Mandatory reporting of unlawful content
</h1>
<p>
  Under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media
  Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, Discord may report users disseminating content that
  violates laws such as the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023, or the
  POCSO Act, 2012, to the appropriate authorities.
</p>
<p>
  Sharing or hosting unlawful Synthetically Generated Information as defined by
  the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics
  Code) Rules, 2021 may lead to removal of content, account suspension, and
  punishment under applicable laws.
</p>
