<h1 id="h_01JSYQQDEH4R811QPDFRJRDE0H">Discord Voice Troubleshooting</h1>
<p>
  If you're experiencing issues with voice chat on Discord, follow this
  step-by-step guide to help diagnose and resolve common problems.
</p>
<p><strong>1. Check Your Microphone and Headset</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebe377749793a7451f5a4cc93f5ada9f7">
    Ensure your microphone or headset is properly connected to your device.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="edf208a4305f0f99c58c8df68c76028b7">
    If using a USB microphone, try plugging it into a different port.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef0bf37ba56310ff0eadc0acf88dd813f">
    Test your microphone with another app to confirm it’s working.
  </li>
</ul>
<p><strong>2. Verify Input and Output Devices in Discord</strong></p>
<p>
  2a. To check your input devices through the desktop/browser app, tap on the
  <strong>cogwheel </strong>icon [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721231846295"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />] in the lower-left corner of the app to access your
  <strong>User Settings</strong>.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      ><img
        src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721246945815"
        width="424"
        height="94" /></span
  ></span>
</p>

  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Cogwheel icon on Discord desktop/drowser</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  2b. Tap on the <strong>Voice &amp; Video</strong> tab. Make sure the correct
  <strong>Input Device</strong> and <strong>Output Device</strong> are selected.
  If you’re having trouble with your microphone not working while using the
  Discord app on desktop/browser, you can also run a
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360020641332"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Mic Test</a
  >.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721231848215"
    width="589"
    height="456"
  />
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01JSYQQDEHYMZJ98N3CGC3XFAR">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Example of the Input and Outside Device settings under the Voice &amp;
      Video tab</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  2c. On the Discord mobile app, tap on your avatar in the bottom-right corner.
  Then tap on the cogwheel in the top-right corner to access
  <strong>User Settings</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  2d. While under <strong>User Settings, </strong>scroll down and tap on
  <strong>Voice</strong> under <strong>App Settings</strong>. Here you can check
  your Input settings to ensure the correct <strong>Input Mode</strong> is
  selected (<strong>Voice Activity</strong> or P<strong>ush to Talk</strong>).
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img

    height="809"

</p>

  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Where to find Voice settings on mobile</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  2e. On the mobile app, you can adjust the Output volume and see if enabling or
  disabled additional settings helps.
</p>
<p><strong>3. Make sure you’re not Muted or Deafened</strong></p>
<p>
  Double-check to ensure you haven’t accidentally muted or deafened yourself.
  You can check this on desktop/browser by looking to the right-hand side of
  your avatar in the bottom-left corner of the app.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">

      >Example of what it looks like when you are muted and deafened in Discord
      on the desktop app/browser</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p><strong>4. Check for any Discord app updates</strong></p>
<p>
  Ensure you're using the latest version of the app, whether on desktop or
  mobile, to maintain compatibility and access the latest features.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01JSYQQDEH40MX3ZXZQS8HM0EJ">Discord Video Troubleshooting</h1>
<p>
  If you're experiencing issues with sharing your video or viewing someone
  else's on Discord, this guide will help you get started with some
  troubleshooting steps.
</p>
<p><strong>1. Check Video device settings</strong></p>
<p>
  1a. If your video is not displaying for others, the first thing you want to
  check is that the correct camera device is selected. 
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721231849879"
    width="624"
    height="481"
  />
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01JSYQXDZYMGDAP9WY0JKMTEY1">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Where to locate your camera settings under Voice &amp; Video tab</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p>1b. If the correct device is selected, please double-check the following:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9c31635be462423f36c2a0f818a4d624">
    Ensure your camera is properly connected to your device and try another
    port.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e099314e8fefea725d1a684f071bcc7ee">
    If using an external webcam, try unplugging and reconnecting it.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>Test the camera in another application to confirm it’s working.</p>
<p><strong>2. Check for any updates</strong></p>
<p>
  Ensure you're using the latest version of the app, whether on desktop or
  mobile, to maintain compatibility and access the latest features.
</p>
<p>
  If you have software installed for your webcam, check to make sure it doesn’t
  have any outstanding updates as well.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01JSYQQDEHK9ZN2BVARSNM4HEH">
  Discord Streaming &amp; Screen Share Troubleshooting
</h1>
<p>
  If you’re running into any issues with screen sharing or streaming, here are
  some settings to check.
</p>
<p><strong>1. Check your Internet Connection</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed254978ac9270c9634fe8c81dc3f94b7">
    Ensure you have a stable internet connection to prevent lag or
    disconnections.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e663c9f85659b4a7f4564ddd585489cf1">
    If possible, use a wired connection for better stability.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  <strong>2. Restart Discord and Your Device &amp; Check for Updates</strong>
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb56fc518093dd41e92377e1225e0bd99">
    Close and reopen Discord to refresh the app.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0b3cb2bb1f7e699485af559bad1f6b70">
    If the issue persists, restart your computer or mobile device.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb3fe564710bd90ab320b4230e1c98bd6">
    Make sure you’re also running the latest version of the Discord app.
  </li>
</ul>
<p><strong>3. Disable Hardware Acceleration</strong></p>
<p>
  If you have <strong>Hardware Acceleration</strong> on, and you find your
  stream is lagging, please try disabling this setting to see if it helps. 
</p>
<p>
  3a. Tap on the <strong>cogwheel </strong>icon [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721231846295"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />] in the lower-left corner of the app to access your
  <strong>User Settings</strong> in the desktop/browser app.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      ><img
        src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721246945815"
        width="424"
        height="94" /></span
  ></span>
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01JSYQQDEHP23QQ94E9BJS50PA">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Cogwheel icon on Discord desktop/drowser</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  3b. Press on <strong>Voice &amp; Video </strong>then tap on the
  <strong>Video</strong> tab. While here, scroll down until you see the
  <strong>Hardware Acceleration</strong> setting. If enabled, tap on the check
  mark to disable. 
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31721231850263"
    width="624"
    height="476"
  />
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01JSYQQDEHY2SZD2N2F7WPZ1AW">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Example of where the Hardware Acceleration setting is located under the
      Voice &amp; Video tab</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p><strong>4. Grant Necessary Permissions</strong></p>
<p>
  Make sure that your desktop device has all of the necessary permissions to be
  able to screen share in Discord.
</p>
<p>Windows:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed12dadd17b40966419cde66725e25f88">
    Run Discord as an administrator by right-clicking the Discord icon and
    selecting <strong>Run as administrator</strong>.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2caac0716ac82d676e32ac37d6aef64f">
    Check <strong>Settings &gt; Privacy &gt; Screen Recording</strong> to ensure
    Discord has permission to record the screen.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>macOS:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="efc7ca09d9847145a5363c7191ced580e">
    Go to
    <strong>System Preferences &gt; Security &amp; Privacy &gt; Privacy</strong
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e52f8a553893d768edf30b6932f152c26">
    Under <strong>Screen Recording</strong>, ensure Discord is checked.
  </li>
</ul>
