<h1 id="h_01JFGE70ZKFMH45WD5C3KYSQ4N">
  How to Verify Your Age to Unlock Your Account
</h1>
<p>
  Discord is experimenting with new methods that you can choose from to verify
  your age, to unlock your account.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01JFGEDJSB8FYFQW9Q22ZVW0BQ">Unlock Your Account with Photo ID </h2>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color="">1.  </span
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Take a photo of yourself that includes yourself holding the following
      items:</span
    ></span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e12bd28ce83b38d068b7f44f381c86a15">
    <span style="background-color: #ffffff; color: #2e3338">A </span
    ><span style="background-color: #ffffff"><strong>photo ID</strong></span
    ><span style="background-color: #ffffff; color: #2e3338">
      showing your date of birth</span
    >
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2942f530ab6c240e3ce3a94315eb2113">
    <span style="background-color: #ffffff; color: #2e3338"
      >A piece of paper with your </span
    ><span style="background-color: #ffffff"
      >full <strong>Discord Username</strong></span
    >
    <span style="background-color: #ffffff; color: #2e3338"
      ><em>(ex: Wumpus)</em></span
    >
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color="">2. </span
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Submit your photo to our Trust &amp; Safety team at:

<h1 id="h_01JFGEWWVRA7ZD4HVBAJJ9B182">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Does Discord or k-ID keep my selfie data? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: Discord only logs the k-ID age verification results used to unlock your
  account—it doesn’t save your selfie image. For questions about k-ID’s
  processes, please contact k-ID. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What if the k-ID age verification fails? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: After the verification attempt, you’ll see
  <strong>Age Check Complete</strong> at the top of your
  <strong>Active Violations</strong> screen. You can try k-ID certification
  again but we may limit how many times you can use this method for appeal, or
  proceed with the photo ID verification method instead.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: When unlocking my account with Photo ID, do I need to take multiple
    photos for verification?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No, all required information (your photo ID and username paper) must be
  visible and readable in a single photo.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Which email should I use to submit my verification?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: You must submit your verification photo using the email address that's
  associated with your Discord account.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How will Discord use my verification information?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The information you provide will be used solely for age verification
  purposes and will not be used for anything else.
</p>
