<h2 id="h_01KX1DVQ4Z1BJ8D9PWT710VHPV">
  Payment Info and Subscription Questions
</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Why do I need to submit my payment info to redeem Nitro?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Because Nitro is a subscription plan, at the end of your 1 month trial, we
  will start your first billing cycle so you can continue to enjoy Nitro on
  Discord.
</p>
<p>
  However, you can cancel the Nitro subscription at any time before the end of
  the 1 month.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why do I see a temporary hold/charge on my account when this is supposed
    to be a free promotion?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Just to clarify, this Nitro promotion is free, however, there will be a
  <strong>temporary hold</strong> placed on the payment method upon activating
  the promotion. This is to help Discord confirm the payment method works as
  expected and to prevent the use of fraudulent payment methods. This charge
  will appear in your Discord transaction history, and it may appear as a hold
  on your payment method.
</p>
<p>
  We immediately reverse the temporary hold, and you’ll see that reflected in
  your Discord transaction history. Your bank may take longer to remove the hold
  from your payment method.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note:</strong> Banks will usually remove the temporary hold from
    your account within a few business days.</span
  >
</div>
<p>
  If your Discord transaction history does not show the hold released after 5
  business days, please let us know at<a
    href="https://dis.gd/contact"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
  >
    <span class="wysiwyg-underline">https://dis.gd/contact</span></a
  >, and our support team will be able to investigate this issue further.
</p>
