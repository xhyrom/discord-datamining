<h1 id="h_01KBKA803KXKJ6Q5TFNJHSFWGM">What are Rental Decorations?</h1>
<div class="exp-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Rental Decorations are currently an experimental
  feature rolling out to select users on desktop/browser. 
</div>
<p>
  Rental Decorations let you access premium items for a fraction of the cost.
  These Rental Decorations can only be claimed with
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30593690165783"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Orbs</span></a
  >. Instead of purchasing decorations permanently, you can rent them for a
  limited time—making it easier to try new styles, enjoy seasonal looks, or use
  decorations for fewer Orbs.
</p>
<p><strong>Here's how Rental Decorations work:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee079a6e68294d5544487283514082dd9">
    Claim them with Orbs
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e980f05c06e2ac46025281efb81d913c5">
    Wear them like any other decoration while they're active
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2c7c9ca9286a84881aedd77ca1e5406d">
    When the rental period ends, the decoration will automatically unequip from
    your profile
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e33d56d6c8fd67e2ea3c638f87fcbc0c1">
    After your rental period ends, you can either rent the decoration again or
    purchase it. 
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01KBKA803QSRFGR5ERDJZF2P6Z">How to Access Rental Decorations</h1>
<div class="exp-box">

  7 day rental periods.
</div>

  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17162747936663"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Shop</span></a
  >.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KBKA804QN1894HAVV72PQMSC">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I collect a Rental Decoration but don't equip it right
    away?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Rental Decorations are available to equip as soon as they are claimed. The
  rental timer begins immediately, whether or not the item is equipped, and it
  will continue to count down even if you are offline or have unequipped the
  decoration.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Can I have multiple Rental Decorations at the same time?</strong>
</p>
<p>A: Yes! You can own multiple rentals of different items simultaneously.</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Will I get a refund if I don't use my full rental period?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Rental purchases are non-refundable, so any Orbs spent cannot be returned.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Can I purchase a version of a Rental Decoration to keep?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Absolutely! Versions to keep are available for purchase if you'd like to
  own a decoration indefinitely. You can purchase these using Orbs or through
  your preferred payment method from the Shop home page or via the Shop search.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What if I don't see the Rental decorations? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: Rental Decorations are currently an experimental feature rolling out to
  select users on desktop/browser. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Are Rental Decorations available on mobile? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Rental Decorations are currently only available to select users on
  desktop/browser.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why can't my friend gift me an item from the Shop that I'm currently
    renting?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you're currently renting a decoration, your friend won't be able to gift
  you that same item until your rental period expires. 
</p>
<p>
  If your friend has already sent you a gift of an item you're renting, you
  won't be able to claim it until your rental expires. Once the rental period
  ends, you'll be able to accept the gift normally.
</p>
