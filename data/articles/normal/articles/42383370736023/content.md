<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF63KAQ0CV81EVY1HEAT">New Themes: Ash and Onyx</h1>
<p>
  The <strong>Ash</strong> theme, the original dark theme longtime users know,
  is back on mobile with improved contrast to meet modern accessibility
  standards. All four base themes from desktop (<strong
    >Light, Ash, Dark, </strong
  >and<strong> Onyx</strong>) are now available on mobile.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Onyx</strong>, previously known as <strong>Midnight</strong> on
  mobile, uses a true black background to match the desktop version. On OLED
  screens, this can help save battery. You can fine-tune contrast and saturation
  in your Accessibility settings.
</p>
<p>1. Select <strong>Settings</strong>.</p>
<p>2. Select <strong>Appearance</strong>.</p>
<p>3. Go to <strong>Theme</strong> and select your desired theme.</p>

<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF67N1Y2XXW47W82YDZD">Updated Device Theme Syncing</h1>
<p>
  You can now choose which <strong>Light</strong> theme and which
  <strong>Dark</strong> theme are used when your device switches between Light
  and Dark mode, instead of being limited to the built-in Light and Dark themes.
</p>
<p>1. Select <strong>Settings</strong>.</p>
<p>2. Select <strong>Appearance</strong>.</p>
<p>3. Turn on <strong>Same as Device Theme</strong>.</p>
<p>
  4. Choose the <strong>Light</strong> theme and <strong>Dark</strong> theme
  you'd like to use with each device mode.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF6B1GFDFD86604XSCN3">Nitro Video Call Tile Colors</h1>
<p>
  Nitro members' voice and video call tiles now match their profile color on
  both desktop and mobile.
</p>
<p>
  To change your call tile color, select the <strong>You</strong> tab, select
  <strong>Edit</strong> <strong>Profile</strong>, and update your
  <strong>Profile</strong> <strong>Theme</strong>.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF6D570TXTES1WW4WFC8">New Shapes: Circles and Squircles</h1>
<p>
  Shapes across the mobile app now follow a consistent shape rule: people are
  circles, and things are squircles (rounded-corner shapes).
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0cbdcf189f45a00f6dac549e51e1a5ae">
    You, your friends, and bots in your direct messages (<strong>DMs</strong>)
    appear as circles.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb67cc87b0f0d457a2f8a430ea4cf6439">
    DMs and group DMs stay as circles.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e203b03fee795a96f4d57286ba938e743">
    Servers and apps appear as squircles, including in your server list.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF6FCXFZFYFVFV8W3SVW">Updated Chat Bar</h1>
<p>
  The chat bar has been decluttered to make more room for writing a message.
  Some actions have moved:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e836a6d7794abaf0d8836ad5dc65974cf">
    Still in the chat bar: Emoji, Gift, Voice Message, and other quick actions.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4dc999b1aabce58698e05223da037a80">
    Moved into the + menu: Threads, using an App,
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efde95a5a70cc03b39998f6660305e497">
    New shortcut: long-press the + icon to jump directly to an action without
    opening the full menu.

<h1 id="h_01KYX2AF6H4WC3J69SN3HV5QHQ">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I still quickly access Threads, Polls, Apps, or file
    attachments?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes! Select the <strong>+</strong> icon to find them, or long-press the
  <strong>+</strong> icon to jump straight to your most-used action.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Will my theme change automatically based on my device settings?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Only if you turn on <strong>Same as Device Theme</strong> in your
  <strong>Appearance</strong> settings. If it's off, your selected theme stays
  the same, no matter what mode your device is in.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Does the Onyx/Midnight theme still help save battery?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes! <strong>Onyx</strong> (previously called <strong>Midnight</strong> on
  mobile) uses a true black background on OLED-compatible devices, which can
  help save battery.
</p>
