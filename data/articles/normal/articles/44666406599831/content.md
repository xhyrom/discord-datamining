<p>
  <strong>Server Hub</strong> gives your server a shared place where members can
  keep up with the server’s top games, compete on a gaming leaderboard, and draw
  on the Whiteboard at the same time. Server Hub only shows activity members
  choose to share. Here’s how Server Hub works, how admins can set it up, and
  how members can join in.
</p>
<div class="exp-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Server Hub is currently an experiment on desktop
  and is not widely available at this time. It's only available in servers with
  fewer than 1,000 members.
</div>

<h1 id="h_what_is_server_hub">What Is Server Hub?</h1>
<p>
  Server Hub is a dedicated page for your server that appears as the
  <strong>Server Hub</strong> tab at the top of your server's channel list. It's
  designed to feel lively rather than like an information dashboard. Widgets
  refresh automatically with your server's latest activity, so the page looks a
  little different each time you visit.
</p>
<p>
  At the top of every Server Hub, you'll see your server's identity: your server
  icon, name, and banner, along with how many members are online, your total
  member count, and how many <strong>Server Boosts</strong> your server has.
  This section updates automatically when you change your server's icon, name,
  or banner in <strong>Server Settings</strong>, and it can't be removed.
</p>

<h1 id="h_server_hub_widgets">Server Hub Widgets</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Widgets</strong> are the building blocks of Server Hub. Some widgets
  fill in automatically using activity from members who choose to share it, and
  others are customized by admins.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Custom Banner Image:</strong> Lets admins use a different banner on
  Server Hub than the server's main banner. Server Hub banners support PNG, JPG,
  and WebP files.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Gaming Leaderboard:</strong> A weekly competition based on members'
  game activity. Each week features one of three challenges:
  <strong>Most Game Time</strong>, <strong>Most Game Days</strong>, or
  <strong>Most Unique Games</strong>. The leaderboard shows the current week's
  standings, which update once a day rather than in real time. The top three
  players are highlighted, and your own spot is marked with "(You)." When a
  competition ends, the winner is announced and the final results stay up for
  one day while a new competition begins.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Popular Games:</strong> Shows the games your server has been playing
  most this week, using each game's cover art and number of players. The bottom
  of the widget shows how many members played how many games that week.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Text &amp; Image:</strong> Lets admins add a short text block and an
  optional image, such as a welcome message, server description, or featured
  content. This widget stays hidden until an admin adds content.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Whiteboard:</strong> A shared, persistent canvas where members can
  draw, add shapes, upload images and GIFs, and leave sticky notes that stay on
  the board for others to see. Multiple members can use the Whiteboard at the
  same time, and everyone sees changes live.
</p>
<h1 id="h_find_server_hub">How to Find Server Hub as a Member</h1>
<p>
  When a server you're in has a live Server Hub, you'll see a
  <strong>Server Hub</strong> tab at the top of the channel list.
</p>
<p>1. Open the server.</p>
<p>2. Select <strong>Server Hub</strong> at the top of the channel list.</p>

<p>
  Server Hub also shows up in chat. When something changes, such as a new leader
  on the Gaming Leaderboard or a weekly winner, a system message appears in a
  channel chosen by your admins. Select <strong>See the leaderboard</strong> on
  the message to jump straight to Server Hub.
</p>
<h2 id="h_leaderboard_badges">Leaderboard Badges</h2>
<p>
  The Gaming Leaderboard gives badges to the top players in your server's weekly
  competition.
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea657589e540548ad2664429207b6b789">
    <strong>Current Leader:</strong> Goes to the member in first place in the
    current week's competition. Since standings update once a day, this badge
    can move to a different member from day to day.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ecafa9e05d3081a092f297a6230186215">
    <strong>Leaderboard Winner:</strong> Goes to the winner of each weekly
    competition and appears next to their username in that server until the next
    competition ends. If someone wins multiple weeks in a row, the badge shows
    their streak. Select the badge, then select
    <strong>View Leaderboard</strong>, to open the server's leaderboard in
    Server Hub.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  A Leaderboard Winner badge is removed if the member leaves the server or turns
  off activity sharing, or if an admin removes the Gaming Leaderboard widget or
  turns off Server Hub.
</p>
<h1 id="h_share_activity">How to Share Your Activity with Server Hub</h1>
<p>
  The Gaming Leaderboard and Popular Games widgets only include members who
  choose to share their activity. If your activity sharing is off, you won't
  appear in these widgets, and you'll see a prompt in the widget to turn sharing
  on.
</p>
<p>
  1. In Server Hub, select <strong>Share Activity</strong> on the widget you'd
  like to join (or <strong>Settings</strong>, if the widget doesn't have enough
  data yet).
</p>
<p>
  2. In the <strong>Share my activity</strong> window, turn on the settings that
  widget needs:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e88dd68379f159b362b41bd95cc5262c4">
    <strong>Share my activity:</strong> Shares activity from games and connected
    apps you use, including when and how you engage.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e854ef505cda807738f5272b954a3bf73">
    <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience:</strong> Uses data,
    such as who you talk to and what games you play, to personalize Discord for
    you.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  3. Close the window. Your activity will appear the next time the widget
  updates.
</p>

<h1 id="h_set_up_server_hub">
  How to Set Up Server Hub as a Server Owner/Admin
</h1>
<p>
  To set up and manage Server Hub, you need the
  <strong>Administrator</strong> permission in your server. This is the same
  permission needed to manage your server's Server Boosts.
</p>
<p>There are two ways Server Hub becomes available in a server:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2de4972c78bddc6be5ba5a65c539dd75">
    <strong>Published automatically:</strong> If your server already has enough
    gaming activity available, Server Hub is published to all members right
    away, with the Gaming Leaderboard and Popular Games widgets filled in.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee09d0e72b802de0d2cba37d628d77e9a">
    <strong>Draft until you publish:</strong> If your server doesn't have enough
    activity yet, Server Hub starts as a draft. Only server admins can see it
    until it's published.
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_publishing_server_hub">Publishing Your Server Hub</h2>
<p>
  1. Select <strong>Server Hub</strong> at the top of your server's channel
  list.
</p>
<p>
  2. Review the default widgets, and select <strong>Edit Server Hub</strong> if
  you'd like to make changes first (see
  <a href="#h_edit_server_hub">How to Edit Server Hub</a> below).
</p>
<p>
  3. When you're ready, select <strong>Publish</strong> in the draft banner at
  the top of the page. Everyone in the server can now see your Server Hub.
</p>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Once Server Hub is published, it can't be
  unpublished or changed back to a draft. If you need to take it down later, you
  can hide it from members instead (see
  <a href="#h_turn_off_on">How to Turn Server Hub Off or Back On</a>).
</div>

<h1 id="h_edit_server_hub">How to Edit Server Hub</h1>
<p>You can add, remove, rearrange, and customize widgets at any time.</p>
<p>
  1. Select <strong>Server Hub</strong> at the top of your server's channel
  list.
</p>
<p>2. Select <strong>Edit Server Hub</strong> in the top banner.</p>
<p>3. Make your changes:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4a07f9e26a7b03444b57462a1740d940">
    To add a widget, select <strong>Add Widget</strong> at the bottom of the
    page, then choose a widget.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e970e70bffc7ca9e9717ea4fccb105276">
    To move a widget, drag it by its handle to a new spot.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebe2dbb430ec1ad1fd92beda6a9ef937e">
    To edit a widget, select the <strong>pencil</strong> icon on that widget.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efc461634f98bc6530f80c1c2333c1d6b">
    To remove a widget, select the <strong>trash</strong> icon on that widget.
    You can add it back anytime.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  4. Select <strong>Save Changes</strong>. To undo your edits before saving,
  select <strong>Reset</strong>.
</p>

<h2 id="h_restricting_whiteboard">Restricting Who Can Use Whiteboard</h2>
<p>
  By default, everyone in the server can use Whiteboard. You can limit it to
  specific roles.
</p>
<p>1. Select <strong>Edit Server Hub</strong>.</p>
<p>2. Select the <strong>pencil</strong> icon on the Whiteboard widget.</p>
<p>
  3. Under <strong>Restrict access by role</strong>, select the roles that can
  use Whiteboard.
</p>

<h2 id="h_whiteboard_safety">Whiteboard Safety and Reporting</h2>
<p>
  Whiteboard content follows Discord's
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/guidelines"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Community Guidelines</a
  >, just like messages and images shared anywhere else on Discord. If you see
  content on a Whiteboard that breaks our rules, you can report it.
</p>
<p>1. Select the item on the Whiteboard you'd like to report.</p>
<p>
  2. Select the <strong>ellipsis</strong> on the toolbar at the bottom of the
  Whiteboard.
</p>
<p>3. Select <strong>Report</strong>.</p>
<p>
  4. In the <strong>Report Object</strong> window, select the option that best
  describes the problem.
</p>
<p>5. Follow the prompts to finish your report.</p>

<h1 id="h_system_messages">How to Manage Server Hub System Messages</h1>
<p>
  Server Hub can post updates in a channel you choose, like the start of a new
  weekly leaderboard, a new leader, or a weekly winner. Each widget can send up
  to 1 system message per day.
</p>
<p>
  1. Open <strong>Server Settings</strong>, then select
  <strong>Engagement</strong>.
</p>
<p>2. Select <strong>Show Server Hub Settings</strong>.</p>
<p>3. Choose the channel where Server Hub messages will be posted.</p>
<p>
  4. Under <strong>Server Hub System Messages</strong>, turn each widget's
  messages on or off.
</p>

<h1 id="h_turn_off_on">How to Turn Server Hub Off or Back On</h1>
<p>
  Once Server Hub is published, it can't be unpublished. Instead, you can turn
  it off to hide the Server Hub tab from all members right away. Your widgets,
  layout, and settings are saved, so you can bring it back anytime.
</p>
<h2 id="h_from_channel_list">From the Channel List</h2>
<p>1. Hover over <strong>Server Hub</strong> at the top of the channel list.</p>
<p>2. Select the <strong>X</strong> next to Server Hub.</p>
<p>3. Select <strong>Disable</strong> to confirm.</p>
<p>
  To show Server Hub again, turn it back on from
  <strong>Server Settings</strong>.
</p>
<h2 id="h_from_server_settings">From Server Settings</h2>
<p>
  1. Open <strong>Server Settings</strong>, then select
  <strong>Engagement</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  2. Under <strong>Server Hub</strong>, turn
  <strong>Show Server Hub tab in channel list</strong> off or on.
</p>
<p>
  When you turn Server Hub back on, it returns to the state it was in before,
  either published or draft.
</p>

<h1 id="h_faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Does Server Hub cost anything?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Server Hub is free for every server, and all of its widgets, including
  Whiteboard, are free to use. You don't need Server Boosts to set up or use
  Server Hub.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Who can see Server Hub?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Once Server Hub is published, all server members can see it. While it's a
  draft, only the server owner and members with the Administrator permission can
  see it.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see Server Hub in my server?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Server Hub is currently an experiment and is only available in some servers
  with fewer than 1,000 members. If your server does have it, an admin may not
  have published it yet or may have turned it off.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why am I not on the Gaming Leaderboard?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The leaderboard only includes members who share their game activity. Make
  sure <strong>Share my activity</strong> and
  <strong>Use data to personalize my Discord experience</strong> are turned on,
  and that your status isn't set to Invisible.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How often does the Gaming Leaderboard change?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Each competition runs for one week. Standings update once a day, so your
  most recent activity may take up to a day to appear. When a competition ends,
  the winner is announced and the final results stay up for one day while a new
  competition with a new challenge begins.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Is my activity shared outside of the server?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Your activity in Server Hub widgets is only shown to members of that
  server.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I use Server Hub on mobile?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Server Hub is currently only available on desktop. This includes viewing
  Server Hub, sharing your activity with its widgets, and setting up, editing,
  or turning off Server Hub as a server owner or admin.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I unpublish Server Hub?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Once Server Hub is published, it can't be unpublished or changed back
  to a draft. You can turn it off anytime to hide it from all members, then turn
  it back on from <strong>Server Settings</strong> under
  <strong>Engagement</strong>.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: What happens to our Server Hub if we turn it off?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Everything is saved, including your widgets, layout, and Whiteboard content
  (for up to 90 days after the last activity). When you turn Server Hub back on,
  it returns the way you left it.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I just joined a server. Why isn’t my earlier activity on the
    leaderboard?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The Gaming Leaderboard only counts activity from the time you joined the
  server. Anything you played before joining isn’t included, even if it happened
  during the current week. This also applies if you leave a server and rejoin.
  Your activity from before you rejoined won’t count, and your standing starts
  fresh from the day you came back.
</p>
