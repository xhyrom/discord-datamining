<h1 id="h_01JAZQ9T3V3CCFGYYHFFHB9F4C">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: How long will this sound pack be available?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The Halloween ringtone will be available from
  <strong>October 7, 2026 - November 2, 2026.</strong>
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Where can I enable this sound pack?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The Halloween sound pack should be available automatically at
  <strong>10:00AM PST.</strong>
</p>
<p>
  If you've disabled the Halloween sound pack, you can re-enable it. Go to
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Notifications.</strong> Then, under
  <strong>Sounds</strong>, toggle on
  <strong>Enable Halloween Sound Pack.</strong>
</p>

  <strong
    >Q: Can I disable this ringtone and revert back to the default
    ringtone?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: For desktop and browser users, you'll be able to go to
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Notifications.</strong> Then, under
  <strong>Sounds</strong>, toggle off
  <strong>Enable Halloween Sound Pack</strong>. Your sounds will return to
  Discord's default.
