<p>
  Message Requests are turned on for everyone. If you're in the teen age group
  or are unconfirmed, all direct messages from people you're not friends with go
  to a separate message requests inbox, so you can review them before they
  appear in your main DM list, and you can't turn this setting off until you
  confirm you're an adult.
</p>
<p>
  Once you’re in the adult age group, you can turn off Message Requests. If a
  user in the adult age group has Message Requests turned on, they will still
  have messages from non-friends routed to the message requests inbox, unless
  they share a mutual friend or a small server with the sender.
</p>
<p>
  DM spam filters are set to “"<strong>filter all</strong>”". Spam filtering is
  applied to all incoming DMs. This is a default setting that can be adjusted at
  any time.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H7M4BTEPTSX428HQGD1B46">
  Message Requests for the Teen Age Group
</h1>
<p>
  As part of our additional
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42178056096663"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">teen safety protections</span></a
  >, Message Requests are turned on for teens automatically. This helps screen
  out unwanted DMs by routing messages from people teens don't share a server or
  friend connection with into a separate inbox, so they can decide whether to
  accept them.
</p>
<p>
  Users need to be in the adult age group to turn Message Requests off. You can
  check your age group status under
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Account Status</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  If your age group is incorrect or you are unconfirmed, you can confirm your
  age group. Find more detailed instructions
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note: </strong>Teen safety protections may vary slightly depending on
  where you live, as some regions have specific requirements under local
  regulation. For region-specific information, see our directory
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42653320919447"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</div>

<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M2H7M4BXEZ3QM9J5MMQ9JWM6">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Managing Direct Messages: Controlling Who Can Contact You</span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  When a teen receives direct messages (DMs) from a sender for the first time,
  Discord will detect if a safety alert should be sent to the teen for this DM.
  If detected, Discord will notify the teen with a safety alert encouraging them
  to double check if they want to reply, and will provide links to block the
  user or view more safety tips to safeguard themselves.
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/18210977897239/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >Learn more about this experience here</span
    ></a
  >.
</p>
