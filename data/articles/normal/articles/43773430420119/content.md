<p>
  The <strong>Online Safety Act (OSA)</strong> is a UK law that applies to
  companies like Discord and carries certain obligations regarding illegal
  content, including non-consensual intimate imagery (<strong>NCII</strong>),
  sometimes referred to as "revenge porn" or "image-based sexual abuse." Under
  the OSA and relevant codes of practice, covered platforms, like Discord, must
  provide a clear reporting process, review qualifying removal requests, remove
  NCII content, and use tools such as hash-matching to help prevent that content
  from being shared or re-uploaded.
</p>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important</strong>: Need immediate help? If you have concerns about
  your personal safety, or the safety of someone you know, please contact local
  law enforcement or social services.
</div>

<h1 id="h_01M3DDN306NT5S1SBN0F11BP19">
  <strong>Our Responsibilities Under the OSA</strong>
</h1>
<p>If you are affected, you, or a representative acting on your behalf, may:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb52a13b5866c0a25381940c48771eb85">
    Notify Discord of any non-consensual intimate image published on Discord
    that depicts you and was shared without your consent; and,
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee3b3b5689212fae34587f220ff21de9a">
    Submit a
    <a
      href="https://discord.com/safety/360044103651-reporting-abusive-behavior-to-discord"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">request</span></a
    >
    for Discord to remove the content. For more details about how to submit a
    removal request, you can refer to our
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/38675715591831-How-to-Report-Non-Consensual-Intimate-Imagery-Under-The-Take-It-Down-Act"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">help article</span></a
    >.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  So that we can help you, please make sure your removal request meets these
  requirements:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4f18f3265e9a6d58e9e2ea7ef9d13a9c">
    You are identifiable in the content or via the context in which it is
    displayed (for example, your face is visible or your name is mentioned);
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5d3e609548dafb1ee47db85ef160aebd">
    The content shows you nude, partially nude, or engaged in sexual activity;
    and,
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e28b55d3ece1a913dbe5c22c16eba88a7">
    The content was shared without your consent.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  Discord may also action content submitted for removal through this process
  even if it does not meet the criteria above, such as for violation of our
  Community Guidelines, or where other UK laws may apply. Discord also reports
  content that it reasonably believes depicts persons under the age of 18 years
  old to the
  <a
    href="https://report.cybertip.org/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >National Center for Missing &amp; Exploited Children</span
    ></a
  >, and takes enforcement action against users who post such content. 
</p>
<p>Under the OSA, Discord, as a regulated service, is required to:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="edf1750352e13b767c5c53aebabcb6446">
    Maintain a clear and accessible process that allows an affected individual
    (or their authorised representative) to report non-consensual intimate
    imagery;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e367aa8018e11adc28c900ecc2d998a12">
    Review each report and assess it against illegal content requirements;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3725da6053a202e53e399bbd08f23022">
    Remove the reported content confirmed to be illegal NCII within the 48 hour
    time period required by law; and,
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="edc0f81c5e5e0681bd133b710e801e37c">
    Make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known copies of the same
    non-consensual intimate visual depiction.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01M3DDN309BG9QY2GDWP9RTQ6S">
  <strong
    >What to Expect After You Submit a Removal Request Under the OSA</strong
  >
</h1>
<p>
  We will promptly review your report to assess whether the content meets the
  legal requirements for removal. If your report is complete and the content is
  confirmed to be non-consensual intimate imagery, we will remove it and take
  reasonable steps to prevent known copies from being re-shared, including
  through hash-matching where applicable.
</p>
<p>
  If your submission is incomplete, we may be unable to process your report
  until sufficient information is provided.
</p>
