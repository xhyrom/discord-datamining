<h1 id="h_01KMRJW7Z7SG1DR6BZJ0FYJGJ3">How do I apply my Nameplate?</h1>
<h2 id="h_01KMRJWCJZ2KHM8XKSVHGBVE30">Desktop/Browser</h2>
<p>
  1. Once you have claimed your exclusive Nameplate, you can head over to your
  <strong>User Settings</strong> by pressing the <strong>cogwheel</strong> [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/39348722299543"
    alt="cogwheel.png"
    width="23"
    height="24"
  />] icon in the bottom left corner. 
</p>
<p>
  2. Then, select <strong>Edit Profiles</strong> where you will find all of your
  decorations. 
</p>
<p>
  3. On this page, press <strong>Change Nameplate</strong>. Select the Nameplate
  and press <strong>Apply</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  4. Be sure to confirm your changes by pressing
  <strong>Save Changes</strong> and you should be all set!
</p>
<h2 id="h_01KMRJZ79CX2H7WXDZVR4MPW59">Mobile</h2>
<p>
  1. Once you have claimed your exclusive Nameplate, head back to your profile
  by tapping your <strong>avatar &gt; Edit Profile</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  2. Then, tap the Nameplate box, and select your claimed Nameplate. Tap
  <strong>Apply &gt; Save</strong> to confirm. 
</p>
<p>
  3. Be sure to confirm your changes by pressing <strong>Save</strong> in the
  upper-right corner and you should be all set
</p>
