<h1 id="h_01M3SFHJ76JB71PQNB6RZT47N2">What Do I Get?</h1>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e141e70edb7b5782909617cd3ee6f992f">
    <strong>Offer:</strong> Gift a Nitro subscription (monthly or annual) to
    another user during the promotion window, and get one free familiar Avatar
    Decoration of your choice.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7d0d4114608748d9cf60a87eb6456d02">
    <strong>Who's Eligible:</strong> Anyone who gifts an eligible Nitro
    subscription during the promotion period. You don't need an active Nitro
    subscription yourself to take part.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed72a7ee1fec22a19094a1168559137a5">
    <strong>Promotion Period:</strong> October 1, 2026 through November 2, 2026.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e63feb92890d0abf1b74e2c14113950f9">
    <strong>How Many You Can Earn:</strong> You can gift Nitro up to 3 times
    during the promotion to collect all 3 familiars. After you've claimed all 3,
    you won't be eligible for additional rewards through this promotion.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01M3SFHJ7DF4WQZ4G15AC4W401">Promotion Details</h2>
<p><strong>Q: How long does this promotion run for?</strong></p>
<p>A: From October 1, 2026 through November 2, 2026.</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I choose more than one familiar?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes. You can gift Nitro up to 3 times during the promotion to collect all 3
  familiars.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M3SFHJ7E6QN7JHFHBZJKH55H">Eligibility</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Do I need my own Nitro subscription to earn a familiar?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: No, you don't need an active Nitro subscription yourself. You just need to
  gift one to another user.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Does gifting Nitro Basic or Server Boosts count toward this
    promotion?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>A: No, only Nitro (monthly or annual) gifts count toward this promotion.</p>
<p><strong>Q: What if my recipient already has Nitro?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If they have an active subscription, or a different subscription tier than
  the one you gifted, the new subscription will be added to their account as
  Account Credit instead. See
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500001829622-Claiming-a-Nitro-Gift-FAQ"
    >Claiming a Nitro Gift FAQ</a
  >
  for more detail.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M3SFHJ7F39YARXEB6CZJJBQ3">Redemption Issues</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I gifted Nitro but didn't get a reward prompt. What should I do?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: This usually means your gift doesn't qualify for the promotion. You'll see
  a banner during the Nitro gifting flow letting you know if your gift is
  eligible.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I already claimed a familiar during this promotion. Can I claim
    another?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, you can gift Nitro up to 3 times during the promotion to collect all 3
  familiars. After you've claimed all 3, you won't be eligible for additional
  rewards through this promotion.
</p>
