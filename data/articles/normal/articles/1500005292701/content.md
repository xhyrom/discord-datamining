<p>
  Age-restricted servers are Discord communities marked as 18+ to ensure adult
  content is only accessible to users who meet age requirements. These servers
  can be designated by server owners or admins.

<h1 id="h_01M2H89RBQ49CZTJM05Y440SDJ">
  Why Is A Server Labeled As Age-Restricted?
</h1>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338">Our </span
  ><a
    href="https://discord.com/guidelines"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Community Guidelines</span></strong
    ></a
  ><span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><strong> </strong>explain that adult content posted to Discord must be
    shared in age-restricted spaces. For servers, this can be done in two
    ways:</span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0f71bd6917541647658021cac5ae8e3a">
    <span style="color: #2e3338"
      >Server owners or users with the “Manage Server” permission can </span
    ><a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500005389362-Age-restricted-Server-Designation"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">self-designate</span></a
    ><span style="color: #2e3338"> </span
    ><a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500005389362-Age-restricted-Server-Designation"
      ><span style="color: #2e3338"><strong>entire s</strong></span></a
    ><span style="color: #2e3338"
      ><strong>ervers as being 18+</strong> by adding an
      age-restriction.. </span
    >
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e356abef6b025f1f582bf08ffdc612896">
    <span style="color: #2e3338"
      >Discord can classify an entire servers as 18+ through the use of an </span
    ><a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500005389362"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><strong
        ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
          >age-restricted server designation</span
        ></strong
      ></a
    ><span style="color: #2e3338">.</span>
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >Servers must be classified as age-restricted if the community is organized
    around age-restricted themes or if the majority of the server’s content is
    focused on 18+ content.</span
  >
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M2H89RBSZG66RCS3B8295E7W">
  I Can’t View or Join Age-Restricted servers. What Should I Do?
</h1>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2f3941"
    >Only users in the adult age group can enter or join servers marked as
    age-restricted by their server owner. This applies to the server owners and
    moderators as well. If you are in the teen age group, you will not be able
    to join or view the content in age-restricted servers.</span
  >
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2f3941"

      ><img
        src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516248537879"
        alt="Example of an Age-Restricted Server Confirmation Window"
        width="474"
        height="423" /></strong
  ></span>
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M2H89RBTMWEABBT5NV1FX0QM">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Example of an Age-Restricted Server Confirmation Window</span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2f3941"
    >You can check what age group you are in at
    <strong>User Settings &gt; Account Status</strong>. If you are an adult and
    aren’t able to access age-restricted servers, you can confirm your age group
    by following the instructions </span
  ><a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/30326565624343"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  ><span style="color: #2f3941">.</span>
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note: </strong>Teen safety protections may vary slightly depending on
  where you live, as some regions have specific requirements under local
  regulation. For region-specific information, see our directory
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42653320919447"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</div>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note</strong>:
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >For adults, age-restricted servers can only be joined on desktop or Android
    devices. </span
  >
</div>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >Those with iOS devices are required to "<strong>opt-in on desktop</strong>"
    in order to view age-restricted servers from their iOS device.</span
  >
</p>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"><strong>How to opt in for iOS:</strong></span>
</p>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >1. Sign in to Discord on desktop and navigate to your
    <strong>User Settings</strong> by selecting the
    <strong>cogwheel</strong> icon [<img
      src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516248539671"
      width="20"
      height="20"
    />]  in the bottom left corner of the app.</span
  >
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><img
      src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516240805271"
      alt="Cogwheel Icon: Access User Settings"
      width="315"
      height="71"
  /></span>
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M2H89RBYZFW8HRHSHYF58YJK">
  <span style="color: #666666">Cogwheel Icon: Access User Settings</span>
</h4>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >2. In User Settings, select  <strong>Messaging Permissions</strong>.</span
  >
</p>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >3. Enable <strong>Allow access to age-restricted content on iOS</strong> by
    pressing the toggle. This will let you view age-restricted servers you are
    already a member of from your iOS device.</span
  >
</p>

<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M2H89RBZVEDYW9WK9B1FZB9C">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Allowing Access to Age-restricted Servers on iOS Permission</span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >Additionally, a subset of age-restricted servers that are specifically
    focused on explicit content will be blocked entirely on iOS. </span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li
    class="wysiwyg-list-color"
    style="--wysiwyg-list-marker-color: #2e3338"
    data-list-item-id="e3c9ecfcafd4d30649f0fa09c5d4fe1fc"
  >
    <span style="color: #2e3338"
      >iOS users aged 18+ will only be able to join and access servers dedicated
      to this content  on the desktop and web versions of Discord.</span
    >
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note: </strong>Only age-restricted server owners and moderators will
  be allowed to view and manage this specific subset of age-restricted servers
  on iOS.
</div>
