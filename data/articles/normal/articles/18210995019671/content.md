<h1 id="h_01M2H36AR38Q80DHJD5QAS6SKY">Types of Filters</h1>
<p>
  We currently offer image-based media filters to help reduce exposure to
  certain categories of potentially sensitive content:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eedb5ee9735dbf63bfdf624b3e965f31f">
    <strong>Mature Sexual Media Filter</strong>: Helps detect image-based media
    that may contain sexually explicit or suggestive material involving adults
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e931fb5d0701f3a687f29dcd367b47960">
    <strong>Graphic Media Filter</strong>: Helps detect image-based media that
    may contain violent or potentially potentially disturbing visual material
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  These filters currently can detect sensitive content in image and video-based
  media. No automated system is perfect, and some content that you consider
  sexually explicit or graphic in nature may not be caught by the filters.
</p>
<p>
  You can report media that you think should have been blocked by selecting
  “<strong>Report Message</strong>”. This will help us improve our filters over
  time. You can learn more about reporting abusive content
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/22582288274071-Reporting-Abusive-Behavior-to-Discord"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note</strong>: Your default content filter settings and ability to
    change your settings may depend on your region.</span
  >
</div>
<h1 id="h_01M2H36AR67DBGRE2QTSEEG654">
  Sensitive Content Filters for the Adult Age Group
</h1>
<p>
  For adults, you have full control over your sensitive content filter
  preferences. You can choose to <strong>show</strong>, <strong>blur</strong>,
  or <strong>block </strong>potentially sensitive image-based media across both
  filter categories:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eee04727596aac1d21805c14ca6b3d2fb">
    <strong>Mature Sexual Media Filter</strong>: Detects image-based media that
    may contain sexually explicit or suggestive material involving adults
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e33b2533ade9cf2ff07dbec7197474a32">
    <strong>Graphic Media Filter</strong>: Detects image-based media that may
    contain violent or potentially disturbing visual content
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  Adults can customize their sensitive content filters by changing their
  preferences in Content &amp; Social settings. There are options to either
  <strong>show</strong>, <strong>blur,</strong> or <strong>block</strong> image
  based-media detected by the filters, and each filter (Mature Sexual Media and
  Graphic Media) has separate controls:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea65f89f2150509a9d5e0ffe7db84c679">
    Direct messages from friends
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef5519bdca98f022a4fb6637c17e1eab3">
    Direct messages from others
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e85b26f02db085ae72355f787971ab7a7">
    Messages in server channels
  </li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Default settings for Adults:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e90ee305b1299622200fd9983b1687d7c">
    DMs from friends: Show sensitive image-based media
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef322902fd3e71de03a07ab689ddd7498">
    DMs from non-friends: Block sensitive image-based media
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2f66d84f3d81081577d99c6580aa3975">
    Server channels: Show sensitive image-based media
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note</strong>: In servers, you can only choose to
    "<strong>Show</strong>" or "<strong>Blur</strong>" image-based media
    detected by the filters. Blocking is controlled by each server's individual
    safety settings.</span
  >
</div>
<p>
  If "<strong>Blur</strong>" is selected, image-based media detected by the
  filters where you are chatting will not be shown to you unless you choose to
  unblur it. 
</p>
<p>
  Adults can press on the<strong> eye icon</strong> to unblur the image-based
  media, or press the <strong>eye icon </strong>one more time to hide the media
  behind a blur again.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H3KEV49ZKFRWHC1BBE6GEP">Why We Hide Some Media?</h1>
<p>
  All users with a filter enabled can press on
  <strong>Why we hide some media</strong> below the media. Pressing this will
  show more detail about our filters and the types of media they might detect.
</p>
<p>
  Adults will have the option to go to <strong>User Settings</strong> to see
  their sensitive media preferences. Users can press on
  <strong>Mark As Not Sensitive</strong> if the media we blurred should not be
  considered sensitive. Pressing this will forward that media to our team to
  help us improve our content detection across both filter categories.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H3KEV5KN57YANW73QWM4P5">What If Media is Incorrectly Flagged?</h1>
<p>
  Another way to let us know if the media was incorrectly flagged is to
  right-click or long press the media and find the option to
  <strong>Mark as Not Sensitive</strong>. By selecting this, adults will see the
  media again with the ability to confirm "<strong
    >Mark as not sensitive.</strong
  >"
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H36ARNHGTBNYDKV2BSYW5S">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What is the difference between “blur”, “block”, and “show” when it comes
    to sensitive media?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>A: There are a few important differences between these options, namely:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef67e78a693010a0e88428fe6d58a3d07">
    <strong>Block: </strong>This option prevents potentially sensitive content
    from appearing in your Discord experience.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efde6d4965679cf014a35fad4b62695ba">
    <strong>Blur: </strong>This option conceals content behind a cover that
    adult users and teen users in some countries can press to reveal when they
    choose to view it.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2910abbe4f9c8c437a39196af240ac7b">
    <strong>Show:</strong> This option displays potentially sensitive content. 
  </li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Q: Can teens turn off the filters completely?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: No. Sensitive content is filtered by default. Images and videos flagged by
  Discord's content filters are blurred or blocked for teens depending on where
  they appear:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee55f637ee6c58b8c30b18170f1f5d977">
    Direct messages from friends: blurred
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee26cd1d4a0dfcadfb4b7d799c8b737ba">
    Direct messages from strangers: blocked
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef2c5bf1b2216a78043c2392a602ea889">
    Server channel messages: blurred
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  These filters apply only to images and videos. They do not scan text messages,
  voice, or calls. Viewing blurred content, or changing these settings to
  "<strong>Show</strong>," requires confirming you're an adult.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What's the difference between the Mature Sexual Media and Graphic Media
    filters?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The <strong>Mature Sexual Media</strong> filter detects content that may
  contain sexually explicit or suggestive material involving adults.
</p>
<p>
  The <strong>Graphic Media</strong> filter detects content that may contain
  violent or potentially disturbing visual content. 
</p>
<p>
  Both filters work together to help reduce exposure to potentially distressing
  image-based media.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How does Discord detect sensitive media?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: When these filters are enabled, messages containing image-based media are
  processed by our automated systems to identify content that may fall under
  either filter category. While our technology continues to improve, no
  automated system is perfect, which is why we provide ways for users to report
  incorrect detections.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Does Discord review all the content detected by the filters?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A:<strong> </strong>No. These filters have been developed to help improve the
  user experience on Discord by limiting exposure to image-based media that
  users may find sensitive or inappropriate. 
</p>
