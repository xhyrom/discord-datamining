<h1 id="h_01M3DEDDQBHRX5ZH4G18ANY365">How do I unlock the free Nameplate?</h1>
<p>
  To unlock the Nameplate, you must collect 4 Cauldron Chaos items between
  <strong>October 1, 2026 (9AM PT)</strong> and
  <strong>October 14, 2026 (9AM PT)</strong>. Once you collect 4 qualifying
  items, the free Nameplate will unlock and you'll need to claim it on your
  desktop app in the <strong>Shop home</strong> or in the Cauldron Chaos
  collection.
</p>

<h2 id="h_01M3DEDDQGD0PSV2X7VXAT3MJ6">Promotion Details</h2>
<p><strong>Q: What exclusive nameplate will I receive?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you collect 4 qualifying items, you will receive an exclusive Hex Eyes
  nameplate from the Cauldron Chaos collection with an estimated value of $5.99.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If I gift a friend a Cauldron Chaos item, does it count toward my
    unlock?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. It counts toward your friend's unlock if they are eligible for the
  promotion, since they're the one who ends up owning the item.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If a friend gifts me a Cauldron Chaos item, does it count toward my
    unlock?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Any qualifying item you own, whether purchased or received as a gift,
  counts toward your 4-item total.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Do bundles count toward my unlock?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you purchase a bundle, each item in it counts toward your unlock. For
  example, a 3-piece bundle counts as 3 of your 4 items.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do all Cauldron Chaos items count, or only certain categories?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: All Cauldron Chaos items count, including bundles (e.g. 3pc bundle counts
  as 3 items).
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I unlock more than one free Nameplate if I collect more than 4
    items?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>A: No, you can only unlock 1 free Nameplate as part of this promotion.</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What if I return or refund one of my 4 qualifying items after unlocking
    the Nameplate?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Shop items are not eligible for refunds per our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012668071-Refund-Policy"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Refund Policy.</a
  >
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M3DEDDQH8ZPKK8J78RN3G9BW">Eligibility</h2>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see this offer in Shop?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: This promotion is unavailable on some platforms and regions. Not every user
  is eligible for this promotion. Only users that can see the promotion on their
  Shop are eligible to participate and collect the reward item.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M3DEDDQHEQ1WV6DTJYBTY3FM">Redemption</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q. What happens if I don't redeem my free Nameplate by October 21, 2026
    (9AM PT)?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A. You'll forfeit the Nameplate. It can't be claimed after the redemption
  window closes. Our Support team is unable to make exceptions.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I redeem my Nameplate on mobile?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, redemption is only available on your desktop client, in Shop Home or
  the Cauldron Chaos collection page.
</p>
<p><strong>Q. Why don't I see my free Nameplate?</strong></p>
<p>A: There are a few possible reasons:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4499bd1b823f01864fbd070360854dce">
    <strong>Item count:</strong> Make sure you collect 4 eligible items from the
    Cauldron Chaos collection
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e17aec547977ba6534f32f5618b2661c0">
    <strong>Redemption window:</strong> The Nameplate must be redeemed before
    October 21, 2026 (9AM PT). If you owned 4 qualifying items but didn't redeem
    prior to this, the Nameplate can't be manually granted.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec01a20d00e2be0041ecfaa0866831cbf">
    <strong>Manual claim required:</strong> This Nameplate is not automatically
    granted. You must claim it yourself on your desktop client in the Shop home
    or in the Cauldron Chaos collection.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>

    >Support team</a

</p>
