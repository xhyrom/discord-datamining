<h1 id="h_01KZXWE7BZ47RC2DSWG73DKD9H">What is Discord's Beta Program?</h1>
<p>
  Discord’s Beta program is how we test new features to help us improve Discord.
  A small, random group of users gets access to a beta feature so we can gather
  feedback and see how it performs in the real world. The feature may move
  toward a wider release. If it doesn't, it may change significantly or be
  discontinued.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KZXWE7BZJBP457EQ22BXR2Y1">How Beta Eligibility Works</h1>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec4cd4e11998429a60f84e20ccef1216b">
    Beta features are given to a small, randomly selected group of users for
    testing. It's not something you can request or sign up for.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efc639a3435c09f57c1f8ecd8e3dcbb1b">
    Support can't manually add users to a beta or override targeting criteria.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e304a333620e37d20d0e64a9067333b52">
    Having access to one beta doesn't mean you'll have access to others.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3e4e353b338b6ea19dbdcc5adbf0609d">
    Beta access can be removed at any time, including if the experiment ends or
    the feature doesn't move forward.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01KZXWE7C0V336W1HMJJ0A3Q4V">
  How to Check if You Have a Beta feature
</h1>
<p>
  If you're one of the users who has access to a beta, you'll see a label
  indicating that the feature is in beta. For example, you may see a
  <strong>popover</strong> in Discord, along with a <strong>beta</strong>
  <strong>tag</strong> next to the feature.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KZXWE7C1E793Z8T6ZE93CJF8">Active Beta Features</h1>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e87ae00d445174a7dcd51e594abded481">
    <strong>Favorites:</strong> users can favorite certain channels on Discord
    and keep the conversations that matter most at their fingertips.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eddd2139162d542716f05e99fcff0b28a">
    <strong>Bookmarks:</strong> users can bookmark the messages they care about
    most and return to them later.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01KZXWE7C2K0083HBA6ZGVDVB2">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I have access to this Beta?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Users in a Beta experiment are chosen at random. If you don't have access
  to the Beta, you're not currently in the experiment group.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: How do I get access to a certain Beta feature? Can support add
    me?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: There's no way to be manually added to a Beta experiment group. If the Beta
  goes well and the feature is well received, it may be rolled out more broadly
  — at which point everyone will have access.
</p>
