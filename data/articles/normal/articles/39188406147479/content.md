<h2 id="h_01KZ4VYSJ8GFB0CBP8RP4HC988">
  <strong>Nitro Rewards Program Terms</strong> 
</h2>
<p>
  Nitro Rewards is included with eligible paid Nitro subscriptions at no
  additional cost, giving you access to perks from partners we think you'll
  actually use. The program is designed to grow and evolve — we'll be updating
  the partners and offers over time, and what's available may change.
</p>
<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>Note</strong>: If you are an eligible Xbox Game Pass subscriber, you
  get access to 3 Discord Nitro perks at no extra cost. For more info, check out
  this Help Center article:
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/40387273783831"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Taste of Nitro for Xbox Game Pass Subscribers</a
  >.
</div>
<p>
  Individual perks have their own eligibility conditions, redemption
  requirements, and availability limitations, which you'll find described in
  connection with each perk. Not all perks are available in all regions or to
  all subscription types. Nitro Basic and Nitro Classic members are not eligible
  for Nitro Rewards.
</p>
<p>
  Perks provided through Nitro Rewards are supplementary to your core Nitro
  benefits and are offered in partnership with third parties. Discord isn't
  responsible for third-party products or services accessed through the program,
  and the third parties may impose their own terms, restrictions, and
  limitations.
</p>
<p>
  Access to Nitro Rewards perks requires an active Nitro subscription. Some
  perks, once claimed, may remain valid after your subscription ends per the
  terms applicable to that perk.
</p>

  <strong
    >Q: What happens to my discount code if I cancel my Nitro
    subscription?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Any codes you've already claimed will remain valid until their expiration
  date, but you won't receive new codes unless you resubscribe to Nitro.

<p><strong>Q: When do the discount codes expire?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: It depends on the type of code. <strong>Monthly codes</strong> are valid
  through the end of the current month. <strong>One-time codes</strong> (such as
  for Logitech’s new products and the Pro Series sim line) do not expire and can
  be redeemed at any time.
</p>

  <strong
    >Q: Is there a limit to how many times I can use my discount code?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Each discount code can be used once, and you can only use one code per
  transaction on each website. However, you'll receive a new code each month
  your Nitro subscription is active.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: When exactly do new codes become available?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: New discount codes become available at the beginning of each calendar month
  for active Nitro members.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I use my code on any device?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes! Once you receive your code through the Discord desktop app, you can
  copy it and redeem it at the appropriate webstore on any device of your
  choice.
</p>

  your code!
