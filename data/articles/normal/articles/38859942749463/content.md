<h1 id="h_01M2H44H2X5228RK0DVBEDG1S2">What is Profile Privacy setting?</h1>
<p>
  Profile Privacy setting lets you control who sees your full profile. You can
  choose from options like just your friends to friends and everyone in your
  servers. This setting can be accessed from desktop, browser, and mobile
  devices.

<h1 id="h_01M2H44H31422QQ53MT5QKD8Z0">
  How to Update Your Profile Privacy setting
</h1>
<h2 id="h_01M2H44H317PQAQ5ZRDJ46BFB2">Desktop/Browser</h2>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338">1. Select the </span
  ><strong>cogwheel</strong> [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516499390743"
    width="20"
    height="20"
  />] in the bottom-left corner to access your
  <span style="color: #2e3338"><strong>User Settings</strong>.</span>
</p>
<p>
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    >2. Navigate to the <strong>Data &amp; Privacy</strong> tab and scroll down
    to the <strong>Profile Privacy setting </strong>section.</span
  >
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #2e3338"
    ><img
      class="wysiwyg-image-resized"
      style="aspect-ratio: 624/337; width: 100%"
      src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516525851031"
      alt="Profile Privacy Settings in Data &amp; Privacy Settings Page"
      width="624"
      height="337"
  /></span>
</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01M2H4CH377NHX1WVMCGF6G54T">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Profile Privacy Settings in Data &amp; Privacy Settings Page</span
  >
</h4>
<p>3. Here you can choose between the following options:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0e0e37f66fcdceff1ea355c51c4d9068">
    <strong>Friends &amp; All Servers: </strong>Your full profile will be
    visible to your friends and members of any servers you join.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed36957232d16e52b3d56132160431986">
    <strong>Friends &amp; Small Servers Only: </strong>Your full profile will be
    visible to your friends and members of any servers you join with 200 or
    fewer members.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="edec0b6c8e135d4e40e90ea05b4e08182">
    <strong>Friends Only:</strong> Your profile will be visible to your friends
    only.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  4. After selecting your preferred setting from the list above, your preference
  will be automatically saved.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M2H44H336NB9SSF4AAYKZX5K">Mobile</h2>
<p>
  1. Tap
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/40953848660759-You-Bar-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">You Bar</span></a
  >
  at the bottom of the screen.
</p>
<p>2. Your profile will open, showing your avatar, username, and status.</p>
<p>
  3. To access <strong>Settings</strong>, tap the
  <strong>cogwheel </strong>[<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/43516499392151"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />] in the upper-right corner of your profile. 
</p>
<p>
  4. Navigate to <strong>Data &amp; Privacy</strong> and then scroll down until
  you see <strong>Profile Privacy setting</strong>.
</p>
<p>5. Here you can choose between the following options:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5e1bbc7c2a91e9065a04ce01f9077380">
    <strong>Friends &amp; All Servers: </strong>Your full profile will be
    visible to your friends and members of any servers you join.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e946b42a88b51389780402ffbfa90e3ed">
    <strong>Friends &amp; Small Servers Only: </strong>Your full profile will be
    visible to your friends and members of any servers you join with 200 or
    fewer members.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="efca50bb06586a94fde7e0c33bbece804">
    <strong>Friends Only:</strong> Your profile will be visible to your friends
    only.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  6. Tap on the setting you’d like to choose and then navigate out of your
  <strong>Settings</strong> to save.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H44H36SGGN4PBD0HK0HG8F">
  Profile Privacy Setting for the Teen Age Group
</h1>
<p>
  As part of our additional
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42178056096663"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">teen safety protections</span></a
  >, we have standard profile privacy settings for users in the teen age group.
  You can change this setting at any time, you don’t have to be in the adult age
  group.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Users in the teen age group:</strong> Start with their profile set
  <strong>Friends &amp; Small Servers</strong>. This means their full profile
  details will only be visible to their friends and members of any small servers
  (&lt; 200 members) they join, not members of all shared servers. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Users in the adult age group or users who are unconfirmed:</strong>
  Start with their profile set to
  <strong>Friends &amp; All Servers</strong> everywhere.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note: </strong>Teen safety protections may vary slightly depending
    on where you live, as some regions have specific requirements under local
    regulation. For region-specific information, see our directory </span
  ><a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42653320919447"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  ><span style="color: #434343">.</span>
</div>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343"
    ><strong>Note: </strong>You're always in control. You can update your
    Profile Privacy setting whenever you like in
    <strong>User Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy &gt; Profile Privacy</strong>.
    You don’t have to be an adult to make a change.</span
  >
</div>
<h1 id="h_01M2H44H386Y1K95DX51YNZCPC">
  How Does This Affect Activity Sharing Setting
</h1>
<p>
  Profile Privacy setting and Activity Sharing are separate settings, but they
  work together to give you consistent control over your visibility on Discord.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>When you change your Profile Privacy setting</strong>, you'll be
  prompted to update your Activity Sharing setting to match. For example, if you
  set your Profile Privacy setting to Friends Only, Discord will ask if you'd
  like to restrict your Activity Sharing to Friends Only as well. You can choose
  to update both settings at once, or decline and manage them independently.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M2H44H3AKC01SBXT1MHD7NYY">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Am I required to age assure in order to change this setting?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. You can change this setting at any time in your
  <strong>Data &amp; Privacy</strong> &gt; <strong>Profile Privacy</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If my profile is private, can people still send me friend
    requests? </strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A:<strong> </strong>Yes. Making your profile private does not affect your
  ability to receive friend requests. Others can still send you a friend request
  even if they can't see your full profile details.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: If my profile is private, can people still send me DMs? </strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. You may control who can DM you through your
  <strong>Content &amp; Social</strong> <strong>settings</strong>. Your profile
  privacy does not affect this setting.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: How does the Profile Privacy setting work for server moderators?</strong
  ><br />A:<strong> </strong>Moderators see the same profile information as any
  other member of your shared server — they don't get special access to details
  you've made private. 
</p>
<p>
  Moderators keep all their existing community management tools that they need
  to keep their communities safe. Username, display name, account age, and
  mutual server info stay visible.
</p>
