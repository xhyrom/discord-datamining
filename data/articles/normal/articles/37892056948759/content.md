<p><strong>Section 1: Scope and Purpose</strong></p>
<p>
  The parties acknowledge that both Discord and you have independently
  contracted with the third party attribution or measurement service provider
  ("Attribution Partner") for attribution and/or measurement services which you
  and Discord have mutually agreed in writing to use for the Purpose specified
  herein. Discord may use and combine Event Data (defined below) with Discord's
  own data and other information to create attribution analytics, insights,
  measurement and reporting ("Attribution Results") and for Discord’s own
  internal business purposes, including: (i) providing the advertising services;
  (ii) developing and providing campaign attribution, conversion tracking,
  analytics, insights and metrics; (iii) creating custom audience for targeting,
  targeting and optimizing campaigns; (iv) optimizing Discord's systems and
  services; and (v) developing Discord’s advertising products and services,
  collectively the “Purpose.”  
</p>
<p><strong>Section 2: Provision of Event Data</strong></p>
<p>
  You will (or will cause your Attribution Partner to) provide Discord with the
  mutually agreed upon event data related to the Ad campaign (inclusive of
  Quests) where attribution will be conducted, including: (i) conversion events
  (e.g., app installations, in-app purchases, registrations) and their metadata;
  (ii) campaign identifiers and their metadata; and/or (iii) other attribution
  data as agreed by the parties and in support of the Purpose ("Event Data") for
  Discord's use in connection with Ad campaign(s). 
</p>
<p>
  You agree that you or your Attribution Partner are authorized and permitted
  to: (i) transmit to Discord all agreed Event Data related to the campaign; and
  (ii) permit Discord to measure, analyze, match against, and report on
  conversions or actions of end users regarding your application (e.g., online
  or mobile game), website, digital or physical products or services (e.g.,
  purchases, bookings, subscriptions, registrations, or ticket sales), or other
  offerings. You agree that Discord may collect, reproduce, compile, match
  against, combine with other data, use, and take all necessary actions with the
  Event Data for the Purpose outlined herein. 
</p>
<p><strong>Section 3:. Use of Attribution Results</strong></p>
<p>
  Discord may, in its sole discretion, share certain Attribution Results with
  you in anonymized and/or aggregated form. Attribution Results shared with you
  may include aggregated campaign performance metrics, conversion insights, or
  other analytics derived from the Event Data, but will not include individual
  user data or non-aggregated information. You may use Attribution Results
  solely to assess the performance of Ad campaign(s) on Discord's platform. You
  will not: (i) share any Attribution Results with any third party; (ii) attempt
  to reverse engineer or de-aggregate any Attribution Results; or (iii) use
  Attribution Results for any purpose other than evaluating campaign performance
  on Discord. 
</p>
<p>
  For the avoidance of doubt, Discord owns all right, title, and interest in:
  (i) any Attribution Results created by Discord; (ii) any insights, analytics,
  or reports derived from Event Data; and (iii) any improvements or enhancements
  to Discord's systems or services resulting from the use of Event Data. Other
  than the limited right to use Attribution Results as set forth in the
  paragraph above (if shared by Discord), you have no rights in Discord's
  Attribution Results, analytics, insights, or related intellectual property.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">
  In addition to the foregoing, and with respect to any data Discord makes

  purpose, and you will not sell, share, or otherwise disclose or make that data

  or service provider solely to carry out that purpose on your behalf.
</p>

<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">

</p>
<p><strong>b. Controller Status</strong></p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">
  Each Party shall act as an independent Controller with respect to any Personal
  Data it processes under this Agreement, including the Event Data shared by you

</p>
<p><strong>c. Security Measures</strong></p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">
  Each Party shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures
  to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk presented by processing
  the Event Data and Attribution Results, including, as appropriate: (i)
  pseudonymization and encryption of Personal Data; (ii) the ability to ensure

  processing systems and services; (iii) the ability to restore the availability
  and access to Personal Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or
  technical incident; and (iv) a process for regularly testing, assessing, and
  evaluating the effectiveness of technical and organizational measures for
  ensuring the security of the processing.
</p>
<p><strong>d. Personnel</strong></p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">
  Each Party shall ensure that its personnel authorized to process the Event

<p><strong>e. Data Transfer Compliance</strong></p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">

<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">

<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">

  notification shall include, to the extent then known: (i) a description of the
  nature of the Personal Data breach; (ii) the categories and approximate number
  of Data Subjects concerned; (iii) the categories and approximate number of
  Personal Data records concerned; (iv) the likely consequences of the Personal
  Data breach; and (v) measures taken or proposed to be taken to address the
  Personal Data breach and to mitigate its potential adverse effects. Each Party
  shall reasonably cooperate with the other Party in investigating, containing,
  and remediating any Personal Data breach.
</p>

<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-justify">

  Controller. If a Party receives a Data Subject request relating to Personal
  Data for which the other Party is the Controller, the receiving Party shall
  promptly notify and forward such request to the appropriate Party.
</p>
