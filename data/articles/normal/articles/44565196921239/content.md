<h1 id="h_faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<h2 id="h_promotion_details">Promotion Details</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If I gift a friend a Deadpool/Wolverine item, does it count toward my
    unlock?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. It counts toward your friend's unlock if they are eligible for the
  promotion, since they're the one who ends up owning the item.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If a friend gifts me a Deadpool/Wolverine item, does it count toward my
    unlock?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Any qualifying item you own, whether purchased or received as a gift,
  counts toward your 4-item total.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Do all Deadpool/Wolverine items count, including bundles?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Every item in the collection counts, and each item in a bundle counts
  separately. For example, a 3-piece bundle counts as 3 of your 4 items.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I unlock more than one free Avatar Decoration if I collect more than
    4 items?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No, you can only unlock 1 free Avatar Decoration as part of this promotion.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: What if I return or refund one of my 4 qualifying items?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Shop items are not eligible for refunds per our
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012668071-Refund-Policy"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Refund Policy</a
  >.
</p>
<h2 id="h_eligibility">Eligibility</h2>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see this offer in Shop?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: This promotion isn't available on all platforms or in all regions. Not
  every user is eligible for this promotion. Only users that can see the
  promotion on their Shop are eligible to participate and collect the reward
  item.
</p>
<h2 id="h_redemption">Redemption</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I don't redeem my free Avatar Decoration by November 23,
    2026 (9AM PT)?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: You'll forfeit the Avatar Decoration. It can't be claimed after the
  redemption window closes. Our Support team is unable to make exceptions.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I redeem my Avatar Decoration on mobile?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, redemption is only available on your desktop client, in Shop Home or
  the Deadpool/Wolverine collection page.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why don't I see my free Avatar Decoration?</strong></p>
<p>A: There are a few possible reasons:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0c64d6aef4349629665dc07ce846902d">
    <strong>Item count:</strong> Make sure you've collected 4 items from the
    Deadpool/Wolverine collection.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e16729a5eb0ade590e7917386e917dc88">
    <strong>Manual claim required:</strong> The Avatar Decoration isn't granted
    automatically. You need to claim it on desktop or browser.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9530970ccc2c77006e5b3879e131350f">
    <strong>Redemption window:</strong> If you didn't claim it by November 23,
    2026 (9AM PT), the Avatar Decoration is forfeited. Our Support team can't
    grant it manually or make exceptions.
  </li>
</ul>
