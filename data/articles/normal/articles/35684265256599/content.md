<p>
  Frequent Friends is a feature available in the DM list on desktop and browser
  versions of Discord. It lets you quickly access chats with friends you talk to
  the most.
</p>
<div class="exp-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Frequent Friends is an experiment available to a
  limited number of users at this time.
</div>

<p>
  The users shown in Frequent Friends are those you message most often and/or
  spend the most time in voice channels with.
</p>
<p>
  The Frequent Friends feature is only visible to you and no other user can see
  who your Frequent Friends are
</p>
<p>
  Frequent Friends is not limited to users you have added as a friend on
  Discord.<br /> 
</p>
