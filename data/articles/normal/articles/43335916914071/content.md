<p>
  Sponsored content is paid for by our partners, such as game developers, who
  want to offer our users a reward for engaging with their game or related
  content via <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/22225719947543"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Quests</a
  > while using Discord.
</p>
<p>
  Discord lets you choose topics you don't want to see in Sponsored Content.
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/25516720403223-Ads-Policy"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >These topics are defined in Discord's Ad Policy</a
  >. When you select a topic, Discord will stop showing you Sponsored Content
  associated with that topic.
</p>
<p>
  This is a separate control from your Sponsored Content personalization
  settings. For information about how Discord uses data to personalize Sponsored
  Content, see
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/22225542459415-Data-Used-for-Sponsored-Content-on-Discord"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Data Used for Sponsored Content on Discord</a
  >.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M20YSJNMFSX5HSFZ36W8KHSZ"><strong>Desktop/Browser</strong></h1>
<p>
  1. Go to
  <strong
    >User Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy &gt; Sponsored Content &gt; Manage
    Sponsored Content</strong
  >.
</p>
<p>
  2. Select any topic you don't want to see. When a topic is selected, you won't
  see any Sponsored Content associated with that topic.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01M20YXSNX2D3C997GEAZT2ZDA"><strong>Mobile</strong></h1>
<p>
  1. Go to
  <strong
    >Settings &gt; Data &amp; Privacy &gt; Sponsored Content &gt; Manage
    Sponsored Content</strong
  >.
</p>
<p>
  2. Hide any topic you don't want to see. When a topic is hidden, you won't see
  any Sponsored Content associated with that topic.
</p>

<div class="info-box">
  <p>Important Notes:</p>
  <ul>
    <li data-list-item-id="e0192ee191ec229240747367df4aeb0e3">
      This setting doesn't turn off all Sponsored Content. It only affects
      Sponsored Content associated with the topics you select.
    </li>
    <li data-list-item-id="e0a6eb9e19dd2780eb38c3ac74892494f">
      Discord will control certain content you see regardless of these
      selections to ensure a relevant and trustworthy ad experience.
    </li>

      Availability of topics may vary by region.
