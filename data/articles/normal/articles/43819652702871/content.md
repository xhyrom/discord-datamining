<p>
  Decree No. 12,976/2026 is a Brazilian federal regulation issued under Brazil's
  Marco Civil da Internet that sets out guidelines for protecting women online
  and addressing violence against women in digital environments. It requires
  covered platforms like Discord to: 
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5c5690055ba3eb120c44ed67a3a158ea">
    provide a clear reporting channel;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3142a83f1e97990f3040b6f47bd7253b">
    review removal requests, including notifications about crimes or unlawful
    acts or coordinated harassment committed against women because of their
    gender;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3a19f75e0464342c549185808f5bb43f">
    remove non-consensual intimate content within a defined timeframe; 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e1df760d06947c9b6c0dbaaba2402e388">
    take reasonable steps to limit the spread of that content; and 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3767e055e8a6d0cb3f177aa79e9b746b">
    block artificial intelligence (AI) tools from generating or altering
    intimate images of real people without their consent. 
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important</strong>: Need immediate help? If you have concerns about
  your personal safety, or the safety of someone you know, please contact local
  law enforcement or social services.
</div>

<h1 id="h_01M4BPT2DQAAJHJSE5EYY7RFNF">
  Your Rights and Our Responsibilities Under Decree No. 12,976/2026
</h1>
<p>
  If Decree No. 12,976/2026 applies to you, you or a representative acting on
  your behalf may:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4a0a4b5c669fb28397b54a07aa4fd5fd">
    <a
      href="http://discord.com/report"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Submit a request</span></a
    >
    for Discord to remove the content that was shared without your consent. That
    includes images, video, audio, text, or any combination of these that
    depicts nudity, partial nudity, a sexual act, or a sexualized context,
    including content wholly or partly generated or manipulated by AI.
  </li>
</ul>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e20984cbd762fd6cd9bdba0a0594e0074">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/38675715591831-How-to-Report-Non-Consensual-Intimate-Imagery-Under-The-Take-It-Down-Act"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Learn more</span></a
    >
    on how to report this content to Discord for removal via our dedicated
    reporting channel.  
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  If you're outside Brazil, or you're reporting images of someone else that you
  don’t have legal authority to represent, you can report content under our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery policy</span
    ></a
  >
  via our standard
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/360044103651-reporting-abusive-behavior-to-discord"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">reporting process</span></a
  >
  using the in-app report menu.
</p>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important</strong>: Please note that intimate imagery of a person
  under the age of 18 constitutes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We report
  illegal child sexual abuse material and grooming to the National Center for
  Missing &amp; Exploited Children (NCMEC). Discord takes enforcement action
  against users who post such content.
</div>
<p>
  Discord’s obligations as a covered provider under Decree 12,976/2026 include:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed94d954b89fab8cc7356f03b038e713b">
    Maintain a permanent, free, and easily accessible space for receiving
    reports of non-consensual intimate content and other forms of gender-based
    violence, with confirmation of receipt and a way for the reporting party to
    track their case;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e29b0555f1525b073ce828f4287f5250a">
    <a
      href="https://discord.findahelpline.com/organizations/ligue-180"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Display information</span></a
    >
    about Brazil's Women’s Assistance Center (Call 180) helpline;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e589844f274eb35776d3f674de99fa918">
    Take swift action when Discord receives a valid report, making reasonable
    efforts to identify and remove known copies of the same visual depiction,
    and applying measures to help block re-uploads of the same content;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec761ff51516e760ee3868e64a325679c">
    Take steps to reduce the reach and visibility of coordinated attacks or
    harassment against women.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01M4BPXR0W2DSEX2NHZB7PQADD">
  What to Expect After You Submit a Removal Request Under Decree No. 12,976/2026
</h1>
<p>
  We will confirm receipt of your report and review it to determine whether the
  reported content falls within the scope of the Decree.
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e36c766ec6e30502d26f2d24051811fb9">
    We will promptly review your request to confirm that it meets the
    requirements of the Decree. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3f99a508c0854c8a58a56635ccb0b70f">
    If your report concerns non-consensual intimate content, and the request is
    valid and complete, we will remove the reported content and we will make
    reasonable efforts to identify and remove known copies to prevent it from
    being re-uploaded.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e011835669e72706151121398add0d216">
    If your report concerns another form of gender-based violence covered by the
    Decree, we will evaluate it and communicate our decision — including the
    reasoning — to you and, where relevant, to the user who posted the content.
    We will not share the information in your report with users who posted the
    content to Discord. They will only be notified that the content has been
    removed, not who reported it.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  If your submission is incomplete, we may be unable to process it until you
  provide the missing information.
</p>
