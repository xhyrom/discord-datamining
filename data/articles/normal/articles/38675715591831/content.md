<p>
  Discord prohibits non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and works to
  identify, remove, and prevent its spread across the platform. This article
  covers how to submit a report to remove NCII under specific laws that might be
  available to you in your region.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> You can also submit reports of NCII through our
  standard
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/360044103651-reporting-abusive-behavior-to-discord"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">in-app reporting system</span></a
  >. Our NCII
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">policy</span></a

<h1 id="h_01M3DFVAKFYCGFX5EK9NMCT82E">What Can Be Reported</h1>
<p>
  Discord’s NCII
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">policy</span></a
  >
  applies globally. In addition, you may have legal rights concerning NCII
  reporting depending on where you live.
</p>
<p>
  Depending on the specific law available to people in those regions, Discord’s
  obligations and what happens after you submit a report will vary.<br /><br />For
  people based in the US:
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/39404618931223-What-is-the-Take-It-Down-Act-TIDA"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >What is the Take It Down Act (TIDA)</span
    ></a
  ><br />For people based in the UK:<a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/43773430420119"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">
      Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery Reporting Under the UK Online Safety Act
      (OSA</span
    ></a
  >)
</p>
<p>
  Where a legal removal process applies to you, you can report content that:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0089ca29a79cf386f9714c51c6d0d582">
    Shows you (or someone you're authorized to represent) nude or engaged in
    sexually explicit conduct
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e38d5799da71d00f42af1d99824c73d6a">
    Was shared without your consent, or was captured when you had a reasonable
    expectation of privacy
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea7992249bc53b1dcfa7f2f4ed83a1c6b">
    Is either authentic, AI-generated, or digitally manipulated depicting you
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  This includes images or videos you may have originally consented to create but
  did not consent to have shared. There's no time limit—you can report content
  regardless of when it was created or posted.
</p>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Please note that you can still submit your report
  but that intimate imagery of a person under the age of 18 constitutes Child
  Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We report illegal child sexual abuse material
  and grooming to the relevant authorities. Discord takes enforcement action
  against users who post such content.
</div>
<h1 id="h_01M3DFVAKKQJXWDYF5NKW6QAWV">How to Submit a Report</h1>
<p>
  Reports must be submitted through our public report webform found at
  <a href="http://discord.com/report" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">discord.com/report</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  We'll review your report and take appropriate action within the timeframe
  required under the specific law where you live. You will be notified via email
  on the status of your report.
</p>
<p>
  Reports may be submitted by the depicted individual or an authorized
  representative (e.g. a parent, guardian, attorney) of the depicted individual.
  Multiple reports are allowed - please create one report for each piece of
  media.
</p>

<h2 id="h_01M3DFVAKNEZATPYA50VTDFYAZ">Required Information</h2>
<p>
  We recognize that reporting NCII can be difficult.
  <strong
    >Your report will remain confidential, and we will not disclose your
    information to the person who uploaded the imagery of you.</strong
  >
</p>
<p>To file a report, you will be asked for information such as:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7abc57ad1cc32aefec5a81b649182b0c">
    Legal name of the person that is depicted in the reported content
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3509ec1b3ed269dbac8d55e90120ebfa">
    Email address where you wish to receive updates on your report
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2dec57d6ab8e9cc7c3b0de002baa033b">
    Country of residence
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ecae1c158da404d65ea439ab2319dd76f">
    Link to the content you are reporting (see the instructions in the next
    section on how to get the content link)
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e59bd250dc6e493de65a1c0dbd57dcc54">
    Your relationship to the content
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eeac41be04761036a863e5a99a6a4955d">
    Content type (what is depicted and the age of the depicted individual)
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb03eb01a77eb508fd8e11e52611afbb9">
    A good faith statement that the content was distributed without consent
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec1b1e3231e41e78d82da54d3d16d5110">
    Electronic signature, where required
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01M3DFVAMM296XKTQDDHB05K3W">
  What to Expect After You Submit a Removal Request
</h1>
<p>
  We will promptly review your removal request to confirm that it meets the
  requirements of the law that applies to you. If the request is complete and
  valid, we will remove the reported content. We will also make reasonable
  efforts to identify and remove known copies of the content.
</p>
<p>
  If your submission is incomplete, we may be unable to process your request
  until sufficient information is provided.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01M3DFVAMNSPYKZVMF2KVXZ951">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Q: When should I report NCII using the webform at </strong
  ><a href="http://discord.com/report" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">discord.com/report</span></strong
    ></a
  ><strong>
    and when should I report NCII using the standard in-app reporting
    process?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The right process depends on your situation: If a legal removal process is
  available where you live (refer to “<a href="#h_01M3DFVAKFYCGFX5EK9NMCT82E"
    >What Can Be Reported</a
  >” section above), and the images are of you or someone for whom you have
  legal authority to represent, the webform may be your best option—we also take
  steps to remove other copies of the same content, not just the one you report.
</p>
<p>
  If a legal removal process isn't available where you live, or you're reporting
  images of someone else that you don't have legal authority to represent, you
  can report content under our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline"
      >Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery policy</span
    ></a
  >
  via our standard reporting process using the in-app report menu.
</p>
<p>
  We review your reports and take action where our policies apply. Whichever
  approach you use, your report will be reviewed.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: How quickly will the content be taken down after I report it?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: We work to review your report and remove the content, if it meets the legal
  criteria for removal, within the timeframe required by the law that applies to
  you. For more information about the specific law that applies in your region,
  refer to the “<a href="https://What%20Can%20Be%20Reported"
    >What Can Be Reported</a
  >” section above. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If an image is found to meet the criteria for removal and is removed,
    can Discord prevent the same image from being uploaded again?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, once an image is found to meet the criteria for removal, it will be
  tagged in our systems and we will prevent it from being uploaded again to the
  best of our ability.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Does Discord disclose my information to the person who shared the
    content that I report?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. We will not share the information you provide to us via the report form
  with users who posted the content to Discord. They will only be notified that
  the content has been removed, but not who reported it.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How will I know what happened to my report?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: After submitting your report, you'll hear from us twice. First, a
  confirmation email so you know your report reached us. Then, we work to remove
  the content within the timeframe required by the specific law that applies in
  your region. We'll send a follow-up email with an update on whether the
  content was removed.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I submit an appeal if action was not taken on my report?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: You have the option to submit another report if you believe we did not take
  appropriate action on your initial report.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How can I appeal if action is taken on my account?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you posted content that was found to meet the criteria for removal under
  applicable law or our Community Guidelines, you may be entitled to an appeal
  under your local laws. See our blog post with instructions on how to appeal
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/360043712172-how-you-can-appeal-our-actions"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What resources are available for me if my sensitive photos have been
    shared or published online?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If your sensitive photos have been shared or published online, there is
  help available, including the following resources:
</p>
<p>
  <a href="https://stopncii.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">StopNCII</span></a
  >
  is a free tool designed to support adult victims and survivors of NCII abuse.
</p>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Take It Down</span></a
  >
  is a free tool designed to support removal of online nude, partially nude, or
  sexually explicit imagery taken before the depicted person was 18.
</p>
<p>
  More resources are also available in our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety-wellbeing"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">Wellbeing Hub</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Is there a limit to how many reports I can submit?</strong></p>
<p>
  <strong>A:</strong> We have rate limits in place to prevent mass reports of
  the same content, and submitting too many reports in a short timeframe can
  result in your reports being rejected. If you encounter an error message after
  submitting several reports in a row, please wait 24 hours before submitting
  additional reports.
</p>
