<p>
  Discord strictly prohibits non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and works to
  identify, remove, and prevent its spread across the platform. This article
  covers how people in the United States can file a non-consensual intimate
  imagery report under the
  <a
    href="https://www.congress.gov/119/plaws/publ12/PLAW-119publ12.pdf"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Take It Down Act</a
  >
  (TIDA), one of several tools available for the removal of NCII on Discord.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Individuals who are not able to make a report under
  TIDA may submit reports of NCII through our standard
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/360044103651-reporting-abusive-behavior-to-discord"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >in-app reporting system</a
  >. Our non-consensual intimate imagery
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >policy</a
  >
  applies globally in addition to other legal rights you may have where you
  live.
</div>

<h1 id="h_01KN36BPD89BP2W555FFP1P8CX">What Can Be Reported</h1>
<p>If you are in the United States you can report content under TIDA that:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e1e9b41ab1f6073bf6cc1851b392f1873">
    Shows you (or someone you're authorized to represent) nude or engaged in
    sexually explicit conduct
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3da7c78d050574aeb16a92b02d30889f">
    Was shared without your consent, or was captured when you had a reasonable
    expectation of privacy
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0c8e0ed2cbc9dfe5203e6cc73f4f55b1">
    Is either authentic, AI-generated, or digitally manipulated depicting you
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  This includes images or videos you may have originally consented to create but
  did not consent to have shared. There's no time limit—you can report content
  regardless of when it was created or posted.
</p>
<div class="warn-box">
  <strong>Important:</strong> Please note that intimate imagery of a person
  under the age of 18 constitutes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). We report
  illegal child sexual abuse material and grooming to the National Center for
  Missing &amp; Exploited Children (NCMEC). Users who post this content are
  permanently banned from Discord.
</div>
<h1 id="h_01KN36EW7CHQ1AA0YCMZCCMH7B">How to Submit a Report</h1>
<p>
  Reports under TIDA must be submitted through our public report webform found
  at
  <a href="http://discord.com/report" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >discord.com/report</a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  Within 48 hours of a report being submitted, we’ll review the report and take
  appropriate action. You will be notified via email on the status of your
  report.
</p>
<p>
  Reports may be submitted by the depicted individual or an authorized
  representative (e.g. a parent, guardian, attorney) of the depicted individual.
  Multiple reports are allowed - please create one report for each piece of
  media.
</p>

</p>
<p>
  2. Select the “<strong>Report Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery</strong>” report

<h2 id="h_01KN36N6A2A5B1Z2KJWYN8GX7R">Required Information</h2>
<p>
  We recognize that reporting non-consensual intimate imagery can be difficult.
  <strong
    >Your report will remain confidential, and we will not disclose your
    information to the person who uploaded the imagery of you.</strong
  >
   
</p>
<p>
  To file a report under the Take It Down Act, the following information is
  required by law:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e13f77cc5c4799235ceca0030d7735afd">
    Legal name of the person that is depicted in the reported content
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e6347579c3957968e620c528eefb52466">
    Email address where you wish to receive updates on your report
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8a97f78a5993bd4395c21ce5cf74c614">
    Country of residence
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e1aae749276ff517c3f65cfe835807970">
    Link to the content you are reporting (see the instructions in the next
    section on how to get the content link)
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec1a93dad712aaf3cd0009b210fdd40cc">
    Your relationship to the content
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e312942e2183f982b95f34877aea87c66">
    Content type (what is depicted and the age of the depicted individual)
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef89cedce4ddf8dcf689987a38f1fbe85">
    A good faith statement that the content was distributed without consent
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebc26ab77d011bbac5e9d803fe41b7cd6">
    Electronic signature 
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01KN37ZQX2S6E1HJGYV600X91T">
  Server-related Images (Avatars, Banners)
</h2>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Links for both server avatars and banners can be found
  in the server details menu by pressing on server name in the top left of the
  server.
</div>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large"><strong>Desktop</strong></span>
</p>
<p>
  1. Enable <strong>Developer Mode</strong> by pressing the
  <strong>cogwheel icon</strong> and navigating to
  <strong>Settings</strong> &gt; <strong>Advanced</strong> &gt;
  <strong>Developer</strong>.
</p>
<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>Tip:</strong> You can also type “developer” in the Settings search
  bar.
</div>
<p>
  2. You can either right-click on the server icon in the server list or if you
  are in the server, press on the drop-down arrow next to the server name.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KN38EBRRGZ66F96RYPKGFCE3">
  What to Expect After You Submit a Removal Request Under TIDA
</h1>
<p>
  We will promptly review your removal request to confirm that it meets the
  requirements of TIDA. If the request is complete and valid, we will remove the
  reported content within 48 hours. We will also make reasonable efforts to
  identify and remove known copies of the content.
</p>
<p>
  If your submission is incomplete, we may be unable to process your request
  until sufficient information is provided.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01KN38F71KQ5X44B9A8CYNTDY3">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong>Q: When should I report NCII using the webform at </strong
  ><a href="http://discord.com/report" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong>discord.com/report</strong></a
  ><strong>
    and when should I report NCII using the standard in-app reporting
    process?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The right process depends on your situation: <br />If you're in the United
  States and the images are of you or someone for whom you have legal authority
  to represent, the webform may be your best option—we also take steps to remove
  other copies of the same content, not just the one you report.
</p>
<p>
  If you're outside the US, or you're reporting images of someone else that you
  don’t have legal authority to represent, you can report content under our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/non-consensual-intimate-media-policy-explainer"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Non-Consensual Intimate Imagery policy</a
  >
  via our standard reporting process using the in-app report menu.
</p>
<p>
  We review your reports and take action where our policies apply. Whichever
  approach you use, your report will be reviewed.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: How quickly will the content be taken down after I report it?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Within 48 hours of a report being submitted, we will review the report and
  will remove the content if it meets the criteria for removal under the Take It
  Down Act.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: If an image is found to meet the criteria for removal under the Take It
    Down Act and is removed, can Discord prevent the same image from being
    uploaded again?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes, once an image is found to meet the criteria for removal under the Take
  It Down Act, it will be tagged in our systems and we will prevent it from
  being uploaded again to the best of our ability.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Does Discord disclose my information to the person who shared the
    content that I report?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. We will not share the information you provide to us via the report form
  with users who posted the content to Discord. They will only be notified that
  the content has been removed, but not who reported it.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How will I know what happened to my report?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: After submitting your report, you'll hear from us twice. First, a
  confirmation email so you know your report reached us. Then, within 48 hours,
  we'll send a follow-up email with an update on whether the content was removed
  based on whether it met the criteria under the Take It Down Act.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Can I submit an appeal if action was not taken on my report?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: You have the option to submit another report if you believe we did not take
  appropriate action on your initial report. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How can I appeal if action is taken on my account?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: If you posted content that was found to meet the criteria for removal under
  the Take It Down Act or our Community Guidelines, you may be entitled to an
  appeal under your local laws. See our blog post with instructions on how to
  appeal
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/360043712172-how-you-can-appeal-our-actions"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >here</a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What resources are available for me if my sensitive photos have been
    shared or published online?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If your sensitive photos have been shared or published online, there is
  help available, including the following resources:
</p>
<p>
  <a href="https://stopncii.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >StopNCII</a
  >
  is a free tool designed to support adult victims and survivors of NCII abuse.
</p>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Take It Down</a
  >
  is a free tool designed to support removal of online nude, partially nude, or
  sexually explicit imagery taken before the depicted person was 18. 
</p>
<p>
  More resources are also available in our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety-wellbeing"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Wellbeing Hub</a
  >.
</p>
