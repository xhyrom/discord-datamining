<h1 id="h_01JPR4VE8AKNPT1S6735SEJ2WQ">How to Change your Discord Theme</h1>
<h2 id="h_01JPR51MJ41MNRY57T54AYH0JF">Desktop</h2>
<p>
  First, head into your <strong>User Settings</strong> by selecting the
  <strong>cogwheel </strong>[<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/30777700886039"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />], then select <strong>Appearance</strong>.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/34331397660695"
    width="843"
    height="573"
  />
</p>
<h4 id="h_01JPR49B3618RTDM25XRB3WV73" class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Access Theme Settings on Desktop: Open User Settings &gt; Appearance</span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  Discord offers several default theme options:<strong> Light</strong>,
  <strong>Ash</strong>,<strong> Dark</strong>,<strong> Onyx</strong>, and
  <strong>Sync with computer.</strong>
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    The <strong>Light theme </strong>features a white background with dark grey
    text and includes an optional <strong>Dark Sidebar</strong>.
  </li>
  <li>
    The <strong>Ash theme </strong>uses a mid-gray background with white text. 
  </li>
  <li>
    The<strong> Dark theme </strong>displays a dark gray background with white
    text.
  </li>
  <li>
    The <strong>Onyx theme </strong>presents a gray and black background with
    white text.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong
      style="
        font-family: -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, &quot;Segoe UI&quot;,
          Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif;
      "
      >Sync with computer </strong
    >(known as <strong>Automatic</strong> on mobile) matches your Discord theme
    to your device’s appearance setting.
  </li>
</ul>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img

    width="857"

</h4>
<h2 id="h_01JPR51B5D5D9JC31RHW3RXDDX">Mobile</h2>
<p>
  Tap your <strong>avatar</strong> in the bottom-right corner of the mobile
  Discord app, then tap the <strong>cogwheel </strong>[<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/30777700890775"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />] in the upper-right corner. Then, scroll down and tap on
  <strong>Appearance</strong>.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/34331397664023"

</p>
<h4 id="h_01JPR4CA8D9HYHHB385A5SGA2K" class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    >Access Theme Settings on Mobile: Open User Settings &gt; Appearance</span
  >
</h4>
<p>
  At the top, you'll find these <strong>theme</strong> options:
  <strong>Light</strong>, <strong>Dark</strong>, <strong>Midnight</strong>, and
  <strong>Automatic</strong> (which syncs with your device settings).
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    The <strong>Light</strong> <strong>theme</strong> uses a white background
    with dark grey text. You can also enable the
    <strong>Dark Sidebar</strong> option.
  </li>
  <li>
    The <strong>Dark</strong> <strong>theme</strong> uses a dark grey/charcoal
    background with white text.
  </li>
  <li>
    The <strong>Midnight theme </strong>presents a gray and black background
    with white text.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Automatic</strong> matches your Discord theme to your mobile
    device's appearance settings.
  </li>
</ul>
<div class="info-box">
  When you enable "<strong>Sync across clients</strong>" on desktop, your theme
  syncs across all devices (desktop, mobile, and browser). However, themes may
  display slightly differently on mobile.<br />For instance, if you select Ash
  on desktop, it will appear as Dark on mobile.
</div>

<h1 id="h_01JPR4VE8AZ96PBKN8VCWFJ41G">How to Change the UI Density</h1>
<p>
  In the <strong>Appearance</strong> menu, scroll to
  <strong>UI Density</strong>. Choose between <strong>Compact</strong>,
  <strong>Default</strong>, or <strong>Spacious</strong> to adjust the spacing
  around content (and navigation spacing in the desktop app). UI density doesn’t
  affect message density.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">

<h1 id="h_01JPR4GVRS0578ZNJD7ZB21KRS">How to Change the Message Display</h1>
<p>
  In the <strong>Appearance</strong> menu, scroll to
  <strong>Message Spacing &gt; Chat Message Display</strong>.  You can choose
  between either <strong>Default</strong> or <strong>Compact</strong> to adjust
  the spacing specifically between message elements.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01JXZWY4ZQVKSDMSVG2RMJMFKF">How to Customize Your Theme</h1>
<div class="info-box">
  Make Discord match your unique vibe with custom themes! Nitro subscribers can
  choose custom themes with endless color combinations to customize their app’s
  appearance.<br /><br />When the “<strong>Sync across clients</strong>" toggle
  is enabled, custom themes applied on desktop may affect your mobile
  experience.
</div>
<p>
  Make Discord match your unique vibe with custom themes! Nitro subscribers can
  choose custom themes with endless color combinations to customize their app’s
  appearance.
</p>
<p>
  1. To get started, navigate to your <strong>User Settings</strong> by
  selecting the <strong>cogwheel </strong>icon [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/32883966902167"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />] located to the right of your <strong>avatar</strong> in the bottom-left
  corner.  
</p>
<p>
  2. Press <strong>Appearance</strong> where you will find the
  <strong>Theme</strong> category. 
</p>
<p>
  3. Under <strong>Color Themes</strong>, select the
  <strong>Custom Theme</strong> icon [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/32883966904343"
    width="24"
    height="24"
  />].
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">

    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/34331397666967"
    width="1020"

</h4>
<p>
  4. In the <strong>Customize your theme</strong> sidebar to the right of your
  app, you can select the following: 
</p>

  <li>
    Select between <strong>Dark</strong> or <strong>Light</strong> mode color
    palettes
  </li>
