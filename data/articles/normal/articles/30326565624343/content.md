<p>
  Helping to keep teens safer on Discord means making sure the right protections
  apply to the right accounts. When Discord knows your account belongs to a teen
  (13-17), we apply additional safety protections automatically, things like
  filtering sensitive content and restricting access to age-restricted spaces.
  You can read more about what those protections cover
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/42178056096663"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >. 
</p>
<p>
  For most users, our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/how-discord-estimates-age-without-collecting-your-identity"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age estimation model</span></a
  >
  determines your age group automatically and you would not need to confirm your
  age group. If you do want to confirm your age group, it's likely for one of
  these reasons:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e656126354528eea74bd3565f7f3195af">
    <strong
      >We don't have enough information to place your account in an age group
      yet and you want access to age-restricted content, spaces, or settings
      now.</strong
    >
    Additional safety protections apply in the meantime, while core features
    like messaging friends, joining voice calls or servers that are not
    age-restricted stay the same. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e76a27d16b6dc392e884ef08a839fe97c">
    <strong>Your account was placed in the wrong age group. </strong>Age
    estimation works by looking at patterns, so a small percent of accounts will
    land in the wrong group. You can fix this in a few minutes without needing
    to contact support.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01M2GWFE615TDE3D4D2ZWSR9GQ">Before You Start</h1>
<p>A few things worth knowing:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eacbb9acf014edabee03a870d037c6c41">
    <strong>Discord does not directly handle your documents or images. </strong
    >To preserve your privacy, Discord doesn’t receive or access any images, ID
    documents, credit card details, or other info you might provide when you go
    through an age assurance process. We partner with age assurance vendors who
    handle this process and only pass back an age signal. We just need to know
    whether you're an adult. It works both ways: A vendor has no way to
    associate any of your information back to your Discord account. You can
    review the vendors we partner with and the privacy standards we have in
    place
    <a
      href="https://discord.com/safety/age-assurance-on-discord-vendors-methods-and-your-data"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e3be04d85887a63b9e0127abc0850729f">
    <strong>Facial age estimation happens on your device.</strong> Processing
    happens entirely on your device and your selfie is not shared with Discord
    or our vendors.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef4876732b5c53960a23dffe3161151be">
    <strong>Documents are deleted.</strong> Any images or ID documents during
    age assurance are permanently deleted as soon as your age group is
    confirmed. They are never stored or linked to your account.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec901adedcf1cb93dfc99e021bc390388">
    <strong>Your age group is saved to your Account Status.</strong> Once we’ve
    determined your age group, it’ll be saved and you can check it at
    <strong>User Settings &gt; Account Status</strong>. Your age group isn't
    shown on your profile or visible to other members in a server.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e452172460690f17c4a1fc86bb9cd58be">
    <strong
      >We will never ask you to confirm your age group by email or text.</strong
    >
    Age assurance only happens inside the Discord app. If you receive a message
    outside of Discord, do not select any links. It is not from Discord.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01M2GWFE6DSPQBZWE44Q032HH6">Credit Card</h2>
<p>
  k-ID determines you're in the adult age group by checking ownership of a
  credit card. Your card details are processed through k-ID who uses Stripe's
  secure payment infrastructure. Discord only receives your age group or a note
  that the result was inconclusive. We never see your card details.
</p>
<p>
  A small temporary charge (under $0.50 USD) may be placed to confirm your card
  is valid and will be fully refunded within 14 business days. Stripe retains
  limited financial records, as required by financial regulations, but this
  information isn't shared with Discord.

<h2 id="h_01M2GWFE6RNZ3W0AF0NW8VQATQ">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Will I need to confirm my age group to keep using Discord?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: No. You don’t need to confirm your age group just to access Discord. The
  vast majority of users are placed into an age group automatically by Discord’s
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/how-discord-estimates-age-without-collecting-your-identity"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age estimation model</span></a
  >
  and don’t need to do a thing. You will only need to confirm you’re an adult if
  you want to access age-restricted content, spaces, or settings and have not
  been placed into the adult age group yet. This doesn't affect core Discord
  features like messaging friends, joining voice calls, or servers that are not
  age-restricted.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is my personal data stored during the age assurance process?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord only ever receives your age signal. Not your name, not your ID, not
  your selfie. We work with vendors that handle the age checks on their end to
  preserve your privacy because the goal is only to establish your age group,
  not your identity. Once the age check is complete, the underlying data is
  deleted, and Discord gets an age signal to place you into your age group (teen
  or adult). Your age group isn't shown on your profile or visible to other
  members in a server.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: When would I be asked to confirm my age group?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: In most cases, this should only happen if you try to access age-restricted
  content, spaces, or settings and are not in the adult age group. There may
  also be times where you need to try another method if we need more
  confidence. 
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How many times will I need to do this?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: Usually once. After your age group is confirmed, it's saved to your account
  and you typically won't be asked again. In some cases, for example where we
  need more confidence, you may be asked to try another method.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Which age assurance method should I use?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: You can choose whichever you're most comfortable with. All age assurance
  methods and vendors are described
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/safety/age-assurance-on-discord-vendors-methods-and-your-data"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >. We evaluate methods and providers for data security, and we don't use your
  data for anything beyond confirming your age group.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Does this affect my account if I switch devices? </strong></p>
<p>
  A: Your age group is saved to your account once it's confirmed, so it stays
  the same across devices. If you haven't yet confirmed your age group, the
  methods available to you may change depending on your device or the region
  where you’re accessing Discord. Legal obligations in some regions may impact
  what methods you can use or if you need to confirm your age group through a
  different age assurance option.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can other users see my age group?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: Your age group isn't shown on your profile or visible to other members in a
  server.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I got an email or text asking me to confirm my age. Is that from
    Discord?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: No. Discord will never ask you to confirm your age by email or text. Age
  assurance only happens inside the app. If you received a message like this, do
  not select any links, it is not from Discord.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: My account was banned after I confirmed my age. What should I
    do?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: If your account was incorrectly banned  for being under the minimum age
  required to use Discord, you can appeal and correct your age using the
  instructions
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why do I see different method options when I try to confirm my
    age?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Methods vary depending on factors such as your region or device. The
  methods you see might also be different based on previous attempts. If you
  don't see a method right now, it may become available again later.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Are age signals received from age assurance used for
    advertising?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The age signals we receive through age assurance can only be used for
  safety purposes. Those signals are used to ensure users in the teen age group
  don't see ads meant for adults, and are not used to target ads. 
</p>
<p>
  Separately, advertisers on Discord can choose to reach people within broad age
  ranges that are based on signals (such as self-declared age) that are
  different and separate from the age signals that are collected through our age
  assurance process.
</p>
