<p>
  Age assurance helps Discord provide you with an age-appropriate experience,
  such as managing default settings and controlling what sensitive content you
  see on our platform(more on when age assurance is required below). This is a
  one-time process, so once we have your verified age group, you don’t need to
  verify again.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01KBKGJ94K9TJ3RPZ82G9JR1EE">How to Complete Age Assurance</h1>
<div class="info-box">
  Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or your
  video selfies. Images of your identity documents and ID match selfies are
  deleted directly after your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used
  for facial age estimation never leaves your device.
</div>
<p>The age assurance process is the same, regardless of how it's triggered:</p>

<p>
  You'll see a prompt asking you to verify your age if you haven't done so
  already.

<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ94P37NG33SC7PAFTVKS">
  Step 2: Choose Your Verification Method
</h2>
<p>
  A "<strong>We need to verify your age</strong>" window will appear with two
  options: 
</p>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large"
    ><strong>Option 1: Face Scan</strong></span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e43b6896282582142b54148ec50ac0a4d">
    Select <strong>Take a selfie </strong>on the verification page. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec803c6387c9327f5065107d0e1d08211">
    Allow access to your camera when prompted.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5094b10d39af5e5f45affd6ceaf50f20">
    Follow the on-screen instructions to take a video selfie
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e437ac4a4f6cdd790721d2895aed4229b">
    Press <strong>Done</strong> to submit.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-large"
    ><strong>Option 2: Scan ID</strong></span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9a7351fb5bcf625de55cf276691ac805">
    Select <strong>Use your ID</strong> on the verification page. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eae5856396ffdffd8dfa0517a9302411d">
    Use your mobile device to scan the QR code.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee4b85ca0c416feeb87ce31f0910b5c42">
    Take a clear photo of your government-issued ID following the guidelines on
    screen:
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e5036f2246df756894f576ada089193d4">
        Make sure you are in a well-lit area
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e7b4b4adb83c271ce50e10f196c27556c">
        Place ID on flat dark surface
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="ece36b3ef7c42c88324edb7fa6a7f6448">
        Make sure all corners of the ID are visible
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e46855d19dfb69ac794b8d1da4f48264e">
    Take a selfie to match your ID document by following the guidelines on
    screen:
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e3231c670723973edb726233ac199da4a">
        Make sure you are in a well-lit area
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="eafea98e4926e2ebafc457f72ca19553f">
        Remove any headwear
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e6737b2a1ced90621a884a3b70bda5652">
        Keep your glasses on
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eaf01eefce8345b13a0afc61d21ddf1e3">
    Press <strong>Done</strong> to submit.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ952JJA2H0A6W9NR2V7C">If Age Assurance Fails</h2>
<p>
  If the age assurance fails, you can retry through the ID scan option at any
  time. 
</p>

  want to appeal, please follow instructions

  to go through the ID scan option to verify your date of birth.

<h2 id="h_01KBKGZ257XG53B7EQSCKG9PWR">Privacy-Forward Age Assurance Process</h2>
<p>
  Age assurance verification is the backbone of this experience for all new and
  existing UK and Australian users.  
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eae1ac9a00b196c96d4f3536b0d5bd06e">
    <strong>Privacy-protecting process. </strong>Discord and k-ID do not
    permanently store personal identity documents or your video selfies. Images
    of your identity documents and ID match selfies are deleted directly after
    your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie used for facial age
    estimation never leaves your device.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5868fe5a703b82cbccb0f9e7246bb363">
    <strong>Verify once.</strong> After completing the age assurance process,
    your Discord experience is tailored to your verified age group, and you
    won't be prompted again.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0c0624bc60ed1c1501f2f4df9230ba13">
    <strong>You can always re-verify. </strong>If you believe you’ve been
    incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you can attempt to re-verify. 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8c49c9a40b379a614473946f158343c3">
    <strong>Visible only to you.</strong> Other users can’t see your
    verification status or verified age group. That information is private to
    you.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01KBKGJ956VESBGSEF3NS13M0P">How to See Your Verified Age Group</h1>
<p>
  If you’ve already completed age assurance, you can go to your
  <strong>My Account</strong> page in your <strong>User Settings</strong> to see
  your verified age group. If you believe you’ve been incorrectly verified into
  the teen age group, you can choose to re-verify in this settings page.
</p>
<p>
  If you haven’t completed age assurance, you will see
  <strong>Unverified </strong>under <strong>Age Group</strong>. You can then
  complete the age assurance process by pressing the
  <strong>Verify </strong>button. 
</p>

<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ95KGYRBN11B92Q29YM9">
  <strong>Getting Started with Age Assurance</strong>
</h2>
<p><strong>Q: When do I need to verify my age on Discord?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you have not verified your age group yet, you may be asked to do so the
  first time you encounter any of these situations: 
</p>
<p>
  1. When attempting to unblur sensitive content blocked by our Sensitive Media
  Filter. 
</p>
<p>
  2. When attempting to change the default settings for the Sensitive Media
  Filter. 
</p>
<p>3. When attempting to change your Message Request settings. </p>
<p>4. When trying to access an age-restricted (18+) channel.</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note for UK and Australian users</strong>: <br />All new and existing
  UK and Australian accounts will experience new default settings designed to
  create a more age-appropriate experience by default. That means all UK and
  Australian users will be asked to verify their age when they encounter these
  above situations.
</div>
<p><strong>Q: How many times will I need to verify my age?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Age assurance is a one-time process. Once your age group is confirmed, you
  won't need to verify again when accessing age-restricted features. 
</p>
<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ95NBKA9TYFGMPWCTSTA">
  <strong>Content Filtering and Age Assurance</strong>
</h2>
<p><strong>Q: What happens when I encounter sensitive content?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you come across blurred content that was flagged by our sensitive media
  filter, you will need to age-verify as an adult before viewing and unblurring
  it.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age multiple times to turn off the Sensitive
    Media Filter?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. Age assurance is a one-time process. Once your age group is confirmed,
  you won't need to verify again when you attempt to access an age-restricted
  feature. However, if you believe your age group is incorrectly verified, you
  may retry the age assurance process again.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age to change content filter settings?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>A: Yes. Sensitive content filter settings are set by default to:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ef40fa8e3be89ece63eeb831af22288a9">
    Direct Messages From Friends: <strong>Blur</strong>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e6e6aa026832e47c9b2ecd4b3401e92bf">
    Direct Messages from Others: <strong>Block</strong>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eae4d3f8aa0d79a1087b9f6968b127a72">
    Direct Messages in Server Channels: <strong>Blur</strong>
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  Changing any of these to “<strong>Show</strong>” will require age assurance.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age to adjust Message Request settings?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Attempting to turn off Message Requests may trigger age assurance.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is age assurance required for age-restricted (18+) channels?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Age assurance is required before entering channels marked as
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">age-restricted</span></a
  >.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ95RC306NFRAZMM427EM">Privacy and Data Security</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is my data stored when I use Face Scan or Scan ID verification?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or
  your video selfies. The image of your identity document and the ID face match
  selfie are deleted directly after  your age group is confirmed, and the video
  selfie used for facial age estimation never leaves your device.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01KBKGJ95SYC3YAS45CA02ZCC2">Troubleshooting and Support</h2>
<p><strong>Q: What if my age group is incorrect?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you believe you were incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you
  may retry the age assurance process.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if my age group is verified incorrectly or it fails to
    verify?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you believe you were incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you
  may retry verification through your chat with Discord system messages where
  you can select <strong>Retry </strong>to enter the verification flow again.
  You can also go to your <strong>User Settings &gt; My Account</strong> and
  press <strong>Retry</strong> located to the right of the
  <strong>Age Group </strong>field.
</p>
<p>
  If you were banned after being incorrectly verified as under the
  <a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040724612"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">minimum age requirement</span></a
  >
  and want to appeal, please follow instructions
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360041820932-Help-I-m-old-enough-to-use-Discord-in-my-country-but-I-got-locked-out"
    ><span class="wysiwyg-underline">here</span></a
  >
  to use the ID scan option to verify your date of birth.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: How long does the age assurance process take?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The age assurance process typically takes just a few minutes. You'll
  receive notifications about the status and a DM from Discord's official
  profile once completed.
</p>
