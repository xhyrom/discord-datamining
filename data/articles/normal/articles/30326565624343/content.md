<p>
  <strong>Age verification</strong> helps us provide you with an age-appropriate
  experience, such as managing default settings and controlling what sensitive
  content you see on Discord (more on when age verification is required below).
  This is a one-time process, so once we have your verified age group, you don’t
  need to verify again. 
</p>

  <strong>Important: UK Online Safety Act Implementation<br /></strong>The age
  verification features described in this article are currently available only
  to users in the United Kingdom and apply to all new and existing UK
  accounts.<br />To learn more about changes for UK users due to the UK Online
  Safety Act requirements, please visit our detailed
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/33362401287959/"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >help center article</a
  >.

<h1 id="h_01K0FTZNQP54390T6Z7FPCSPF6">How to Complete Age Verification</h1>
<div class="exp-box">
  <strong>Important</strong>: Age verification is currently part of an ongoing
  experiment across various regions.
</div>
<div class="info-box">
  <span style="color: #434343" data-darkreader-inline-color=""
    >Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or
    your video selfies. Images of your identity documents and ID match selfies
    are deleted directly after your age group is confirmed, and the video selfie
    used for facial age estimation never leaves your device.</span
  >
</div>
<p>
  The verification process is the same, regardless of
  <a href="#h_01K0FVFH1KWK3TDGFXBBSDQBM0">how it's triggered</a>:
</p>
<h2 id="h_01K0FV1BTH7SC4GC0RERPXTB5H">Step 1: Start Verification</h2>
<p>
  You'll see a prompt asking you to verify your age if you haven't done so
  already.
</p>

<h2 id="h_01K0FV2KATHST9R5JKG8MF5GB1">
  Step 2: Choose Your Verification Method
</h2>
<p>
  A "<strong>We need to verify your age</strong>" window will appear with two
  options:
</p>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>Option 1: Face Scan</strong></span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Select <strong>Face Scan</strong> on the verification page. </li>
  <li>Allow access to your camera when prompted.</li>
  <li>Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the scan. </li>
  <li>Press <strong>Done</strong> to submit.</li>
</ul>
<p>
  <span class="wysiwyg-font-size-x-large"
    ><strong>Option 2: Scan ID</strong></span
  >
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <ul>
      <li>Select <strong>Scan ID</strong> on the verification page. </li>
      <li>Use your mobile device to scan the QR code.</li>
      <li>
        Take a clear photo of your ID following the guidelines on screen:
        <ul>
          <li>Make sure you are in a well-lit area</li>
          <li>Place ID on flat dark surface</li>
          <li>Make sure all corners of the ID are visible</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
      <li>
        Complete selfie to match the ID document by following the guidelines on
        screen:
        <ul>
          <li>Make sure you are in a well-lit area</li>
          <li>Remove any headwear</li>
          <li>Keep your glasses on</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
    </ul>
    Press <strong>Done</strong> to submit.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01K0FV7GVZZV6G4F3TX1QDKDD5">How to See Your Verified Age Group</h1>
<p>
  If you’ve already completed age verification, you can go to your
  <strong>My Account</strong> page in your <strong>User Settings</strong> to see
  your verified age group. If you believe you’ve been incorrectly verified into
  the teen age group, you can choose to re-verify here.
</p>
<p>
  If you haven’t completed age verification, you will see
  <strong>Unverified </strong>under <strong>Age Group</strong>. You can then
  complete age verification by pressing the <strong>Verify </strong>button. 
</p>

<h2 id="h_01K0FVFH1KWK3TDGFXBBSDQBM0">
  <strong>Getting Started with Age Verification</strong>
</h2>
<p><strong>Q: When do I need to verify my age on Discord?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you have not verified your age group yet, you may be asked to do so the
  first time you encounter any of these situations: 
</p>
<p>
  1.  When you attempt to unblur sensitive content blocked by our Sensitive
  Media Filter. 
</p>
<p>
  2. When attempting to change the default settings for the Sensitive Media
  Filter. 
</p>
<p>3.  When attempting to change your Message Request settings. </p>
<p>4.  When trying to access an age-restricted (18+) channel.</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note for UK users</strong>: All new and existing UK accounts will
  experience new default settings designed to create an age-appropriate
  experience by default. That means all UK users will be asked to verify their
  age when they encounter these situations. Check out our
  <a
    href="http://discord.com/safety/adapting-discord-for-the-uk-online-safety-act"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Safety Center article</a
  >
  to learn more about why we are making these changes.
</div>
<p><strong>Q: How many times will I need to verify my age?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Age verification is a one-time process. Once your age group is confirmed,
  you won't need to verify again when accessing age-restricted features. 
</p>
<h2 id="h_01K0FVFWCZSB00VPNFDCXKZ1Y0">
  <strong>Content Filtering and Age Verification</strong>
</h2>
<p><strong>Q: What happens when I encounter sensitive content?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you come across blurred content that was flagged by our sensitive media
  filter, you will need to age-verify as an adult before viewing and unblurring
  it.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age multiple times to turn off the Sensitive
    Media Filter?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: No. Age verification is a one-time process. Once your age group is
  confirmed, you won't need to verify again when you attempt to access an
  age-restricted feature. However, if you believe your age group is incorrect,
  you may retry the age verification process again.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age to change content filter settings?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>A: Yes. Sensitive content filter settings are set by default to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Direct Messages From Friends: <strong>Blur</strong></li>
  <li>Direct Messages from Others: <strong>Block</strong></li>
  <li>Direct Messages in Server Channels: <strong>Blur</strong></li>
</ul>
<p>
  Changing any of these to “<strong>Show</strong>” will require age
  verification.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Do I need to verify my age to adjust Message Request settings?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Attempting to turn off Message Requests may trigger age verification.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is age verification required for age-restricted (18+) channels?</strong
  > 
</p>
<p>
  A: Yes. Age verification is required before entering channels marked as
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/115000084051-Age-Restricted-Channels-and-Content"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >age-restricted</a
  >.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01K0FVFWCZ5VY3BDA2FK9HKAMX">Privacy and Data Security</h2>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Is my data stored when I use Face Scan or Scan ID verification?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord and k-ID do not permanently store personal identity documents or
  your video selfies. The image of your identity document and the ID face match
  selfie are deleted directly after  your age group is confirmed, and the video
  selfie used for facial age estimation never leaves your device.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01K0FVFWCZ2RPRVJYGYETMVVSY">Troubleshooting and Support</h2>
<p><strong>Q: What if my age group is incorrect?</strong> </p>
<p>
  A: If you believe you were incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you
  may retry the verification process.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if my age group is verified incorrectly or it fails to
    verify?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>A: You have two options:</p>
<ol>
  <li>
    If you believe you were incorrectly verified into the teen age group, you
    may retry verification through your chat with Discord system messages where
    you can select <strong>Retry </strong>to enter the verification flow again.
    You can also go to your <strong>User Settings &gt; My Account</strong> and
    press <strong>Retry</strong> located to the right of the
    <strong>Age Group </strong>field.
  </li>
  <li>
    You can retry by opting for a <strong>manual review </strong
    ><a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/requests/new?ticket_form_id=360000029731"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >here</a
    ><strong>. </strong>
  </li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Q: How long does verification take?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: The verification process typically takes just a few minutes. You'll receive
  notifications about the status and a DM from Discord's official profile once
  completed.
</p>
