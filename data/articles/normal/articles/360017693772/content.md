<h1 id="h_01J1FZH9BCJZ73NWBB9RTMESMG">
  Where to Manage all of Your Active Subscriptions 
</h1>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span id="docs-internal-guid-a5096c0b-7fff-beb8-851d-dbeacf840f60"

<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="h_01K81NXBQX6M8YTXDAHFH7C00B">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Subscriptions Tab in User Settings on Desktop</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p id="docs-internal-guid-68cbc2d3-7fff-f602-1743-01972b2b8085">
  When you’ve purchased a Nitro subscription, or maybe added some Server Boosts
  to your account, you can manage those subscriptions and find information about
  them in your <strong>Subscriptions</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  1. Head into your <strong>User Settings</strong> by pressing the
  <strong>cogwheel</strong> [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/24509110845975"
    width="23"
    height="24"
  />] <strong>icon</strong>. 
</p>
<p>2. Then, select the <strong>Subscriptions tab.</strong></p>
<p>Once there, you can find information about:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4143d87bf70f31d513f1e4ed6f66bc71">
    Any active subscriptions;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5343889b58170a949b4fcb80f3108048">
    The renewal dates and amounts you'll be charged;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7bcce61745c32edefc41f79c5e2354f3">
    Options to manage your subscriptions, including
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/19580873036695-How-to-Cancel-your-Nitro-Server-Boosting-or-other-Discord-Subscriptions"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >canceling</a
    >
    or switching plans; 
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eecbbe17d5eeaea7d3483f11e0fc7d05d">
    Any gift subscription credits you’ve received.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01K81NM0KY4S4KCEZB008EFBA5">How do I add or edit payment methods?</h2>
<p>
  You can add a new payment method or edit any pre-existing payment methods in
  the<strong> Billing tab</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  To add a payment method, select the <strong>Add Payment Method</strong> button
  and complete the prompts.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span id="docs-internal-guid-3692f165-7fff-0c35-b613-330c68eab92a"
    ><strong
      ><img
        src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/24509110852759"
        width="739"
        height="547" /></strong

</p>
<h4 class="wysiwyg-text-align-center" id="01K81P1Q0TAXGBTSXQYT9NH3QX">
  <span style="color: #666666"
    ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
      >Example of Adding a Payment Method</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p id="docs-internal-guid-199acb8c-7fff-7019-e2d2-0eed05830428">
  If you would like to make changes to a pre-existing payment method, select the
  <strong>Edit </strong>button to the right of the payment method. From there,
  you will be able to view the payment method information.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <span id="docs-internal-guid-d5e4812c-7fff-f88b-82a3-15896bddb956"

  <span style="color: #666666"

      >Default Tag and Edit Button for Payment Method</span
    ></span
  >
</h4>
<p id="docs-internal-guid-c0be2227-7fff-15e9-ecfd-40e1f8582ac8">
  You can also set a payment method as <strong>default</strong>. The default
  payment method will be the first payment method that appears when making new
  purchases. To select which payment method shows as default:
</p>
<p>
  1. Select <strong>Edit </strong>next to the payment method you’d like to set
  as the default, or <strong>Add a new payment method.</strong>
</p>
<p>
  2. Select <strong>Make this my default payment method</strong> at the bottom.
</p>
<p>3. Press <strong>Save.</strong> </p>

<h2 id="h_01K81NNQ1FDGM4RT8NNPJKFWEM">
  How do I change my payment method for my subscription?
</h2>
<p id="docs-internal-guid-ebcdc320-7fff-6031-df95-646b5efe3299">
  You can find the payment method associated with any subscriptions in
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Subscriptions</strong>. This section will show the
  payment method you first purchased a subscription with. 
</p>
<p id="docs-internal-guid-0a04a1a0-7fff-baa0-a2e7-c77107221a3c">
  If you’d like to change the payment method for your ongoing subscription
  payments:
</p>
<p>1. Open User Settings &gt; Subscriptions.</p>
<p>2. Find the <strong>Pay for it With</strong> section.</p>
<p>3. Select a payment method.</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <p>
    <span style="color: #434343"
      ><span data-darkreader-inline-color=""
        ><strong>Note:</strong> Updating your subscription payment method does
        not automatically change which payment method is set as your default
        payment method. If you’d like to set one card as both your default and
        subscription payment methods, you will need to update both settings to
        the same card. </span
      ></span
    >
  </p>

<h2 class="wysiwyg-text-align-left" id="h_01K81NPZY2MMP8P9BBK26CDRQD">
  How do I remove a payment method?
</h2>
<p>
  1. To remove a payment method, head over to your
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Billing. </strong>
</p>
<p>
  2. Then, select <strong>Edit</strong> located to the right of the payment
  method. <br />3. While reviewing your payment method details, select
  <strong>Delete Payment Method</strong> to remove it.
</p>

<p><strong>Q: What does “proration” mean?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Proration for a subscription means adjusting the cost based on how much of
  the subscription period you've used. Discord uses this to ensure you’re being
  charged the correct amount when adding subscriptions or removing subscriptions
  on your account, and in situations like when accepting a special offer or
  discount. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I have a Nitro Monthly subscription, but I just purchased a Nitro Yearly
    subscription. Can I get a refund? </strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Your original Nitro Monthly subscription was used as proration credit
  toward your Nitro Yearly subscription upgrade. This means the amount you paid
  for Nitro Yearly was not the full amount as you paid the prorated charge. When
  the subscription renews, you will be charged the full price of the
  subscription. To check your receipt, check User Settings &gt; Billing. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What’s the difference between the default payment method and my
    subscription payment method? </strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: The default payment method determines which payment will show first when
  making new purchases on Discord. However, your subscription payment method is
  what’s used to pay for your recurring Nitro and Boost subscriptions.<br />You
  can set the same payment method for both by following the steps to update your
  <a href="#docs-internal-guid-1731ba7c-7fff-87a1-c976-f62cc1cd2618"
    >default payment method</a
  >
  and
  <a href="#docs-internal-guid-1ae109ec-7fff-410f-aa54-931f058a395d"
    >updating your subscription payment method</a
  >.   
</p>
<p>
  Please note that the payment method used to pay for server and app
  subscriptions specifically will not show a Subscription tag within the Billing
  tab, and these subscriptions will charge from either the original payment
  method selected or the payment method set for the specific App subscription in
  the Premium App Subscriptions list.<br /> 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens if I previously purchased a Server Boost subscription, and
    now I’m buying an additional Nitro Yearly subscription?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you have purchased any additional subscription with a different billing
  cycle,
  <strong>Discord will unify all of your subscription payment dates</strong>.
  This means that your subscription type may change from a
  <em>monthly</em> cycle to a <em>yearly</em> cycle if you have purchased
  additional subscriptions with a <em>yearly</em> cycle.
</p>
<p>
  Additionally, if you purchase an application or server subscription, your
  Server Boost subscription cycle won't be affected.
</p>
<p>
  For example, if you initially had a Server Boost Monthly subscription,
  purchasing an additional  Nitro Yearly subscription will convert the current
  Server Boost Monthly subscription to a Server Boost Yearly subscription in
  order to simplify all subscription charges to one payment date.
</p>

<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I’m receiving a “Billing address is invalid” error when making purchase.
    How do I fix this?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If you see this error while attempting to make a purchase, this means that
  the billing address associated with the card you are trying to use does not
  match the address filed with your bank. Please check
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Billing &gt; Select the card &gt; Edit</strong> and
  update your billing address. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: Why do I still have server boosts when I canceled my Nitro
    subscription? </strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Server Boosts are a separate monthly subscription, and it's possible that
  you have one or more Server Boost subscriptions active. To manage the
  subscription, you would need to go to your
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Subscriptions</strong>. 
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I have a new Discord account, can I transfer my Nitro subscription to
    another account? </strong
  ><br />A: Unfortunately, Discord doesn't currently support transferring Nitro
  subscriptions between accounts. We understand this might be a bit
  inconvenient, and we appreciate your understanding.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I got a notice that my Nitro price is decreasing, why is that?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Discord regularly reviews and updates prices based on factors like your
  location, local market conditions, currency exchange rates, and regional
  operating costs. We also test different pricing approaches to better
  understand what works best for our community.<br />As part of this process, we
  lowered the price of the Nitro plan for a small number of subscribers in the
  US and Canada on <strong>November 12, 2025</strong>.
</p>
