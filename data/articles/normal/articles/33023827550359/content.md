<h1 id="h_01JYHHBT96YTN7JTNWCM2R587M">What is a Discord Server?</h1>
<p>
  A Discord server is a customizable digital space where communities can gather,
  communicate, and share content. Think of servers as virtual meeting places
  that bring people together around shared interests, hobbies, or relationships.
  Each server is organized with different channels dedicated to specific
  purposes, whether that's text conversations, voice chats, or media sharing,
  making it easy to keep discussions organized and accessible.
</p>
<p>
  Discord servers are free to create and can accommodate anywhere from a small
  group of friends to massive communities with thousands of members. They offer
  flexibility in organization, moderation tools, and customization options that
  make them suitable for virtually any type of group or community.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JYHHBT96AZHQ9EJVPG49MNJ2">
  Understanding Different Types of Servers
</h1>
<p>
  Discord offers three main server types, each designed for different community
  needs and scales:
</p>
<h2 id="h_01JYHHBT96THW43XKZDGD1FRBX">Friend Servers</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Friend servers</strong> are private, invite-only spaces perfect for
  staying connected with your close friends or smaller groups. These servers
  provide an intimate environment where you can chat, share memories, coordinate
  activities, and maintain relationships with people you know personally. The
  smaller scale makes them ideal for casual conversations and personal
  connections.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01JYHHBT970XC7203923YRMKY4">Community Servers</h2>
<p>
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360047132851-Enabling-Your-Community-Server"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong>Community servers</strong></a
  >
  are larger, invite-only spaces designed to bring together people from around
  the world who share common interests, hobbies, or goals. These servers focus
  on fostering learning, discussion, and connection within specific communities,
  whether that's gaming, art, technology, or countless other topics.
</p>
<p>
  Community servers come with enhanced moderation tools to help manage larger
  groups effectively, including:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2c2cf4273fd0b61b638d457b5240fba7">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032807371-Server-Insights-FAQ"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Server Insights</a
    >: Analytics to understand your community's activity, growth patterns, and
    member engagement
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e02365cf2ba31c65f5a4cb0b7873fc25d">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/10989121220631-How-to-Protect-Your-Server-from-Raids-101"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Raid Protection</a
    >: Advanced security features to protect against spam, bot attacks, and
    disruptive behavior
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea44f8d0a0f19bf3330000807b1255577">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/1500000466882-Rules-Screening-FAQ"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Rules</a
    >
    and
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/11074987197975-Community-Onboarding-FAQ"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Onboarding</a
    >: Structured systems to welcome new members, establish community
    guidelines, and create screening processes
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee286d469a17d73142bdd9daa6cdfa761">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360043913591-Community-Server-Welcome-Screen"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Welcome Screen</a
    >: Customizable introduction for new members
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed98db8c590b8394744c962eb39cc02c4">
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360023968311-Server-Discovery"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Server Discovery</a
    >: A path to becoming a Discoverable server, which can be found and joined
    without an invite (eligibility requirements apply)
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  These tools make Community servers well-suited for building thriving,
  organized communities that can scale while maintaining a positive environment
  for all members.
</p>
<h2 id="discoverable-servers">Discoverable Servers</h2>
<p>
  <strong>Discoverable servers</strong> are Community servers that meet extra
  requirements for size, safety, and activity, and whose admins have chosen to
  list them in Server Discovery. Anyone can find and join them without an
  invite, through Server Discovery,
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/servers"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >discord.com/servers</a
  >, and web search. They also have their own Server Web Page, so people can
  learn about them before joining.
</p>

<h1 id="h_01JYHHBT9BFSEDK3NH6EJ1ADQK">Getting Started with Discord Servers</h1>
<p>
  Creating your first Discord server is a simple process that takes just a few
  minutes. Here's how to get started:
</p>
<p>
  1. On the Discord desktop app or browser client, locate the
  <strong>Add a Server</strong> icon [<img
    src="https://support.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/33023792097559"
    alt="Add a Server icon"
    width="20"
    height="20"
  />] on the left sidebar of the app.
</p>
<p>2. Select either <strong>Create My Own</strong> or choose a template.</p>
<p>3. If creating your own server, choose between:</p>
<ul>
  <li
    class="wysiwyg-list-bold"
    data-list-item-id="edd74787271c4cc6ffb2b7271df11cd27"

    class="wysiwyg-list-bold"
    data-list-item-id="e9fd8469999593f49f471e0e7bab53d3b"
  >
    <strong>For me and my friends</strong>
  </li>
</ul>
<p>4. Name your server and add an optional server icon.</p>
<p>5. When you're all set, select <strong>Create</strong>.</p>

<ul>

    number of uses
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eef61461594f16bd98099466ec489ab0f">
    <strong>Permanent Invites</strong>: Links that never expire (use with
