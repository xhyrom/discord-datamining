<p>
  <strong>Profile badges</strong> are the small icons on your profile that show
  off what you've done on Discord, from boosting servers to completing Quests.
  This article explains where to find your badges, what badge rarity means, and
  how to choose which badges appear on your profile.
</p>

<h1 id="h_badge_directory">The Badge Directory</h1>

<h2 id="h_open_badge_directory">How to Open the Badge Directory</h2>
<p><strong>Option 1: From a desktop or mobile profile</strong></p>
<p>1. Open your profile or someone else's profile.</p>
<p>
  2. Select any badge (the icons next to the username) to open the
  <strong>Badge Directory</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  If you're on your own <strong>Edit Profile</strong> screen, selecting a badge
  opens the <strong>Badge Customization</strong> panel instead. From there,
  select <strong>View Badges</strong> to go to the
  <strong>Badge Directory</strong>.
</p>

<h1 id="h_badge_rarity">Badge Rarity</h1>
<p>
  Every badge has a <strong>rarity</strong>: <strong>Common</strong>,
  <strong>Rare</strong>, <strong>Epic</strong>, or <strong>Mythic</strong>.
  Rarity shows how likely you are to come across other users with that badge on
  Discord. You'll see Common badges often, while Mythic badges are held by very
  few people.
</p>
<p>
  Some badges <strong>evolve</strong>, which means they level up as you reach
  new milestones. Each level of an evolving badge can have its own rarity, so
  the same badge might be Common at its first level and Mythic at its highest.
</p>

<h1 id="h_managing_badges">Managing Your Badges</h1>
<p>
  Once you've earned a few badges, you can choose how they appear on your
  profile. Everyone can hide badges, and Nitro subscribers can also reorder
  them.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Hiding and reordering badges is available on desktop,
  browser, and mobile.
</div>
<h2 id="h_display_slots">Display Slots</h2>
<p>
  Your profile displays up to six badges in its <strong>display slots</strong>.
  New badges that you earn and badges that you level up will be placed into your
  badge display slots by default. Additional badges that you've earned are kept
  in your Badge Directory, so visitors to your profile can still see everything
  you've unlocked.
</p>

<h2 id="h_hiding_badge">Hiding a Badge</h2>
<p>
  You can hide eligible badges you've earned so they don't appear on your
  profile.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> If you're a Nitro subscriber, your Nitro badge always
  stays visible to help others understand your profile's Nitro perks. You can
  still reorder it and hide your other badges.
</div>
<p><strong>On Desktop and Browser</strong></p>
<p>
  1. Select the <strong>You Bar</strong> at the bottom of the left sidebar.
  Then, navigate to <strong>Edit Profile</strong>.
</p>
<p>
  2. Select your <strong>Badges</strong> in the <strong>profile editor</strong>.
</p>
<p>3. Select <strong>Customize Your Badges</strong>.</p>
<p>
  4. Hover over the badge you want to hide and select the
  <strong>three-dot menu</strong>.
</p>
<p>5. Select <strong>Hide Badge</strong>.</p>
<p>6. Select <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</p>

  <img
    style="aspect-ratio: 800/596"

<p>
  Hidden badges move to the end of the row and show a small hidden icon so you
  can find and unhide them later. Other users won't see hidden badges on your

<div class="tip-box">
  <strong>Tip:</strong> You can also open the
  <strong>Badge Customization</strong> panel from the <strong>You Bar</strong>.

  <strong>Badges</strong>.
</div>

  <strong>Badge Customization</strong> panel.
</p>
<p>4. Long press the badge you want to hide.</p>
<p>5. Tap <strong>Hide Badge</strong>.</p>
<p>6. Tap <strong>Save</strong> to confirm.</p>

<h1 id="h_faq">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p><strong>Q: Can I remove a badge from my account?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Badges can't be removed from your account, but you can hide any badge so it
  doesn't appear on your profile. See
  <a href="#h_hiding_badge" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Hiding a Badge</a
  >
  above.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I move my badges to a different account?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No. Profile badges are non-transferable and stay with the account that
  earned them.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What happens to my badge order if my Nitro subscription ends?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: If your Nitro subscription ends, your custom badge order will no longer
  apply. Your badges will go back to the default order shown for users without
  Nitro.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Why don't I see a badge I just earned in my display slots?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: Newly earned badges automatically move to the front of your display slots,
  so they should appear on your profile right away. If you don't see a badge you
  expected, try restarting or refreshing your Discord app.
</p>
<p>
  If it still doesn't appear, you may not have met all the requirements to earn
  it yet. Check
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/44369363856663"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Discord Profile Badges: What Each Badge Means</a
  >
  for details on how to earn each badge.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I hide my Nitro badge?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: No, Nitro badges are always visible. Nitro badges let others know why your
  profile looks the way it does, from animated avatars to custom emojis in your
  status. You can still reorder your badges and hide other badges.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Will Discord add new badges?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: New badges may be added over time. When they are, you'll find them in your
  Badge Directory.
</p>
