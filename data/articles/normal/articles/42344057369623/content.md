<h1 id="h_01KZ4G2Z2ESXG73PKPWN8BYGWT">Offer Eligibility</h1>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note</strong>: Offers are currently available only on the Discord
  desktop app and in a web browser, unless otherwise specified.
</div>
<p>
  Discord runs Nitro offers and promotions from time to time. Eligibility
  depends on a range of factors, including your region, subscription status, and
  other criteria specific to each offer.
</p>
<p>
  To check if you're eligible for an offer, select the
  <strong>Nitro tab</strong> in the Discord desktop app (via the
  <strong>Direct Messages</strong> button in the top left, or
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Nitro</strong>). If you're eligible, you'll see one
  or more of the following:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2d2be7260ca5a150b7db6618d2152e6f">
    A banner or notification in the <strong>Nitro tab</strong>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e668e6375d91b3c3938f12a138cde8e06">
    An in-app message directing you to claim the offer
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  For most offers, if you don't see one in the <strong>Nitro tab</strong>,
  you're not currently eligible, and support isn't able to manually add offers
  or change eligibility. Some promotions, like Game Shop promotions for Nitro
  subscribers, appear on other surfaces and can have their own eligibility
  requirements; see that promotion's entry in the Available Offers section
  below, and the
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/40102783004311-Game-Shops-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Game Shops FAQ</a
  >
  for details.
</p>
<div class="info-box">
  <strong>Note:</strong> Eligibility can change while an offer is running. If
  you qualify today, check back regularly, as offers are tied to real-time
  criteria and may adjust over time. Similarly, if you don't see an offer right
  now, you may become eligible later in the offer period.
</div>

<h1 id="h_01KZ4G2Z2MVPA9VVE5D37M4Y7D">Eligible Subscription Types</h1>
<p>
  To qualify for any of the
  <a href="#h_01KZ4G2Z2S6X1QJZFSG6H4SFWP">offers below</a>,
  <strong>eligible users</strong> must purchase Nitro (Monthly or Yearly) and
  pay through the Nitro tab on Desktop or web. The following subscription types
  <strong>do not</strong> qualify to receive the offer, even if you’re eligible:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9641d056f111c8f6078db1c237e4824e">
    <strong>Nitro Credit</strong>: purchased in the Shop with Orbs.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e06987aab3411d95d49cfcc39fbe2fdcc">
    <strong>Nitro Gifts</strong>: includes both sending and receiving a Nitro
    gift.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed8d5dbc2f87880e960a8e124ca87cd0a">
    <strong>Trials</strong>: these offers require an upfront payment, and trials
    don’t qualify.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e515f9b89787b65c1976fe530e00d1d16">
    <strong>Referral or Share Nitro Trials</strong>: subscriptions started
    through a referral trial or Share Nitro trial.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e15c6721cc923a7d4c761db8be728e477">
    <strong>Nitro Classic or Basic</strong>: offers are valid only on paid Nitro
    standard subscriptions.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e331930f108a5a7294c88211b8c4d1ff5">
    <strong>Upgrades from Classic/Basic &gt; Nitro</strong>: only direct
    subscriptions to Nitro are eligible.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e215d0b4d7de57913250ea50e11c5d1d0">
    <strong>Recent subscribers</strong>: if you've had an active Nitro
    subscription within the last 30 days (including trials.)
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e95f8dab31fe97d2e57613fb888de5b59">
    <strong>Other Promotions/Discounts</strong>: if you subscribe to Nitro via
    other promotions such as discounts, you’re not eligible for these offers.
  </li>
</ul>
<h1 id="h_01KZ4G2Z2PPP1B2BWXYJV8MRSK">Offer Terms</h1>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="edca14403b17bb3b34153f00585a754c5">
    <strong>Duration:</strong> Offers are limited-time and may change or end at
    any time without notice.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0f71ac7232949468b2558839cf4fb6d4">
    <strong>Combining offers:</strong> Offers can't be combined with other
    offers unless explicitly stated in that offer's entry below.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee8e73526673606bfd3a9d5f12201b94c">
    <strong>Account eligibility:</strong> Offers are only valid on the Discord
    account where you’re eligible. Redeeming on a different account or
    transferring an offer isn’t supported.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8b9eb1950aa431048e84b6e72dc5024b">
    <strong>Regional availability:</strong> Availability varies by offer. Some
    offers aren't available in certain countries or regions; specific
    restrictions for a live offer are noted in its entry below.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee8261963f4ba0ce39f0a220da0b8b4f0">
    <strong>Discord's discretion:</strong> Discord determines eligibility and
    reserves the right to limit, modify, or cancel an offer if we detect abuse,
    fraud, or a violation of our platform terms or policies.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e24dd57f8a3bdced48798f0236aec8bc0">
    <strong>Reward terms:</strong> Rewards granted through a Nitro offer, like
    bonus Orbs or Nitro subscription time, are subject to their standard terms
    and
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/360012668071-Refund-Policy"
      target="_blank"
      rel="noopener noreferrer"
      >Refund Policy</a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e88410eee448850ebb6a97eaa728eb02a">
    <strong>Refund eligibility:</strong> If you've redeemed or received any
    promotional items from Discord or a Discord partner promotion within 5 days
    of your Nitro membership renewing or becoming a new subscriber, you won't be
    eligible for a Nitro refund.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e37e5c37386c864fd93cec1c54336970d">
    <strong>Cash value:</strong> Offers have no cash value and aren't redeemable
    for cash.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2fb7355576d1bbdac146dfa46ada799f">
    <strong>Unmet eligibility:</strong> If you don't meet the eligibility
    criteria for an offer, any associated reward won't be applied to your
    account, even if you complete the qualifying action (for example,
    subscribing to Nitro).
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e8f9edd52c9e287b4a6747b871d10fba3">
    <strong>Redemption deadlines:</strong> If a promotional benefit requires you
    to redeem or activate it rather than being applied automatically, the offer
    may include a date by which you must do so.
  </li>
</ul>

<h1 id="h_01KZ4G2Z2VK3PSEXHKDS3YSYT7">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I heard about an offer from a friend or on social media, but I don't see
    it in my app. What's wrong?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Nothing's wrong on your end. Offers are based on specific eligibility
  criteria, so they don't reach everyone at the same time, or at all. To confirm
  whether you're eligible, check the <strong>Nitro tab</strong> (via the
  <strong>Direct Messages</strong> button in the top left, or
  <strong>User Settings &gt; Nitro</strong>).
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Why did I stop seeing an offer I saw yesterday?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Offers can be time-limited or capped, and eligibility can shift while an
  offer is running. If the banner's no longer showing up, the offer isn't
  available to you anymore, and it may have been removed from the Available
  Offers section above.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can support add me to an offer or make an exception?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Eligibility is determined automatically based on each offer’s eligibility
  criteria, so support isn't able to add users to an offer or make exceptions.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: I'm eligible for an offer. Where do I redeem it?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Head to the <strong>Nitro tab</strong> where you saw the banner and follow
  the on-screen steps. The Available Offers section above has the offer type and
  duration for reference, but redemption happens directly in-app.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Does an offer I claim expire?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: It depends on the offer. Check the duration listed for that offer in the
  <a href="#h_01KZ4G2Z2S6X1QJZFSG6H4SFWP">Available Offers section above</a>.
  Orbs added to your account don't expire.
</p>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: I think I should be eligible but I'm not seeing the offer. Can you check
    my account?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Individual eligibility decisions come from each offer’s eligibility
  criteria, so our support team isn't able to review or adjust them on a
  case-by-case basis.
</p>
