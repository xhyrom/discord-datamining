<p>

</p>
<p>

  server channel or DM where you would like to launch the Activity and select

    alt="discord-apps-shapes-icons-inapp.png"

  choose the YouTube Watch Together Activity.<br />If you do not see the
  Activity, you can search for ‘YouTube Watch Together’ in the search bar of the
  App Launcher. You will need to authorize the Activity before you can use it.

<h1 id="h_01JAV6KPXXFF3G3P55PDF8H4SV" class="wysiwyg-text-align-left">
  Invite Your Friends to Join
</h1>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-left">
  Have your friends join the Activity and watch LoL Worlds with you! To join,
  they need to be in the same voice or text channel as you and join the Activity
  you started. Each person joining will need to provide authorization if they’ve
  never used it before.<br />Once you’ve started the Watch Together Activity in
  a voice channel, in the desktop/browser Discord app you can right-click on the
  voice channel in the channel list and select Invite to Join. You can copy the
  invite link and send that through a DM or in a text channel or text chat to
  invite others to the Activity.

<h1 id="h_01JAV6MBDN0ND7JBK30AC6ZX72">Host a Watch Party</h1>
<p>
  Want to make sure your friends don’t change the video at the height of the
  competition? To have more control over what’s being watched in your Watch
  Together session, here are some settings we recommend.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01JAV6MKNFNYYZF4140Q1Q10HN">Host Mode</h2>
<p>
  In Host mode, only the host can change the currently playing video. Anyone can
  add videos, but without the host taking action, those videos won’t play until
  the current video is over.
</p>
<p>
  To enable host mode, look in the top top left corner on Desktop, or in the
  Settings tab on mobile. Make sure “allow everyone to edit the playlist and
  video state” is toggled OFF.
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support-apps.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/27313467932567"
    alt="discord-watch-together-host-mode.png"
  />
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support-apps.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/27313467937431"
    alt="discord-watch-together-select-new-host.png"
  />
</p>
<h2 id="h_01JAV6NG89AH794AA84YE7DZDZ">Use a server where you are an Admin</h2>
<p>
  If you lose connection, leave the Activity, or reach your max playback time,
  you will lose your status as host, and a random participant in the Activity
  will become host.
</p>
<p>
  If this happens, any Admin watching can take over host control by clicking the
  3 dots next to the current host, then clicking “revoke host control.”
</p>
<p class="wysiwyg-text-align-center">
  <img
    src="https://support-apps.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/27313467939991"
    alt="discord-watch-together-revoke-host-control.png"
  />
</p>
<p>
  To make sure an Admin is always in control of the current video, we recommend
  having a few server Admins present in the watch party.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JAV6NMGAE7790QB7QVSCK8V7">Frequently Asked Questions</h1>
<p>
  <strong
    >Q: What if I don't see the YouTube Watch Together Activity in my
    server?</strong
  >
</p>
<p>
  A: Try searching the App Launcher for YouTube Watch Together. If that doesn’t
  work, the server owner or admin may need to enable the YouTube Watch Together
  Activity. Reach out to them to ensure it's available.
</p>
<p>
  <strong>Q: Do I need a YouTube Premium account to use Watch Together?</strong>
</p>
<p>
  A: No, you don’t need a YouTube Premium account to watch streams using Watch
  Together on Discord.
</p>
<p><strong>Q: Can I watch on my mobile device?</strong></p>
<p>
  A: Yes, you can join and watch the stream using Discord on your mobile device,
  but we recommend using the desktop version for the best experience.
</p>
