<h1 id="h_01JP341J1X47W04B8QFTM65ANB">Introduction</h1>
<p>
  Supercharge your game's social experience with the new
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/discord-social-sdk/overview"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Discord Social SDK</a
  >! This powerful toolkit lets game developers easily integrate Discord's
  social features directly into their games, creating a more engaging and
  connected player experience.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JP341J1X1RV8XMMB5PCEHB1M">
  Why should I use the Discord Social SDK in my game?
</h1>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1XV0KR6YEBS3PCPZP5">Seamless Integration</h2>
<p>
  <img
    src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/30699524966167"
    alt="UFL_GIF.gif"
  />
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <p>
      <strong>Branding integration:</strong> Use your own game branding with the
      SDK for a consistent player experience.
    </p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <p>
      <strong>Unified Friends List</strong> enables players to maintain their
      social connections across their games and gaming sessions.
    </p>
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1XEYAHNQXG8CCMCPJY">Improve Player Growth</h2>
<p>
  <img
    src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/30699524967063"
    alt="Rich Presence_GIF.gif"
  />
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Rich Presence</strong> will get your players spreading the word on
    Discord about your game simply by playing it!
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Deeplink Game Invites</strong> will allow players to invite new
    players so that they can discover and join game sessions easily.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Account Linking</strong> enables Discord-based sign in and
    authorization.
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1YE12QFHH2ST82CKMG">Limited Access Features</h2>
<p>
  <img
    src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/30699524968471"
    alt="Cross Platform Chat_GIF.gif"
  />
</p>
<p>
  The following features are only fully unlocked when joining the closed beta:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Cross-platform messaging</strong> allows users to message each other
    in game and on Discord.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Voice chat integration</strong> delivers excellent audio quality and
    lets you create individual user voice streams.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Linked Channels</strong> provide persistent group chats that allow
    conversations to flow seamlessly before, during, and after gameplay—both
    in-game and out.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  While these limited access features are available in the Discord Social SDK,
  their usage is capped with a rate limit. Please read our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/topics/rate-limits"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >documentation on rate limits</a
  >
  to learn more.
</p>
<p>
  To apply for full access to closed beta features, or to reach out to Discord
  directly to discuss your game, please fill out
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/social-sdk-closed-beta-access-form"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >this form</a
  >.
</p>
<h4 id="h_01JP3433R7K2HKS0E091KWFZA2">
  ⚠️ Please Note: We cannot guarantee that full access will be granted. However,
  we encourage you to explore these limited access features already available to
  all developers in the Discord Social SDK.
</h4>
<h1 id="h_01JP341J1YA62C7392FC1BTBDZ">What Platforms are supported?</h1>
<p>The Discord Social SDK is available for these platforms:</p>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1YS4GQ813T7YA56PK3">Desktop</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Standalone C++ (Windows and macOS)</li>
  <li>Unreal Engine (Windows Only)</li>
  <li>Unity Engine (Windows and macOS)</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1YK86JW34FPVETFVAY">Console</h2>
<h4 id="h_01JP343V3KWVFA76XXPKSNXZQT">
  ⚠️ To use the Discord Social SDK in your console games, you will need to
  request middleware approval and be an approved developer for the target
  console. Check out
  <a
    href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/30209074764183"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >this article</a
  >
  to learn more.
</h4>
<p><strong>Xbox Series X|S</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Standalone C++</li>
  <li>Unreal Engine</li>
  <li>Unity Engine</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>PlayStation™ 5</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Standalone C++</li>
  <li>Unreal Engine</li>
  <li>Unity Engine</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01JP341J1YVCCDS17MFGCBK4Q1">
  Android/iOS (full mobile support coming soon)
</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Standalone C++</li>
  <li>Unity Engine</li>
</ul>
