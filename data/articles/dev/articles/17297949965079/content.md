<p>⚠️ Currently, Premium Apps are available to developers based in the US but check back here and in the Discord Developers server for future announcements</p>
<p><a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17709085688727">Premium Apps</a> are the key to taking your app to the next level. In this article, we’ll go step by step to help you tap into our end-to-end solution for monetizing your application and offering your app’s most loyal users new features.</p>
<h2>
    <a id="_x0x8p2ue6ns6"></a><strong>Prerequisites</strong>
</h2>
<p>Before considering monetization for your app, here are some fundamental requirements that will need to be met:</p>
<ul>
    <li>App is owned by a Team</li>
    <li>App uses Slash Commands or has Message Content Intent</li>
    <li>App is Verified</li>
    <li>Supported Countries</li>
</ul>
<h3>
    <a id="_tdz2rp3smqap"></a><strong>App Is Owned By A Team</strong>
</h3>
<p>If you don’t have a Team and/or an App you can create them from the<a href="https://discord.com/developers/teams"> Developer Portal</a>. If your app is Verified but not yet owned by a Team, you can<a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support/teams-and-ownership"> request an ownership transfer by submitting a ticket to the Developer Support team</a>.</p>
<h3>
    <a id="_389j3auqz6ir"></a><strong>App Uses Slash Commands Or Has Message Content Intent</strong>
</h3>
<p>If your Team owns an App but it doesn’t yet use Slash Commands, you can review our documentation on<a href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/tutorials/upgrading-to-application-commands#slash-commands"> Upgrading Apps to Use Application Commands</a>. Otherwise, your app will need to be granted the<a href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/topics/gateway#message-content-intent"> Message Content Intent</a>.</p>
<h3>
    <a id="_8lxsjxr9jar9"></a><strong>App Is Verified</strong>
</h3>
<p>If your Team owns an App and uses Slash Commands but it isn’t verified, check out this article for more<a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/6206007597207-How-do-I-get-my-bot-verified-"> information on applying for app verification</a>.</p>
<p>If your app does not currently meet these initial requirements, work to accomplish them and you will gain access to the Monetization Onboarding flow.</p>
<h3>
    <a id="_k0ozmbo2f27f"></a><strong>Supported Countries</strong>
</h3>
<ul>
    <li>US</li>
</ul>
<h2>
    <a id="_2nb9dog23epa"></a><strong>Next Steps</strong>
</h2>
<p>When your app meets the above requirements the “Getting Started” button under the Monetization section should now lead you to the onboarding flow.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/18059778310935"></p>
<h3>
    <a id="_pvimk63vpvjs"></a><strong>Onboard</strong>
</h3>
<p>The onboarding flow involves a series of steps and checks, providing payment information, and finally agreeing to the<a href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/5330075836311"> Monetization Terms</a> and<a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17442400631959"> Premium Apps Policy</a> which must be completed by the Team Owner.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/18059795072535"></p>
<p>For more guidance please visit the<a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17708927296663"> Premium Apps Onboarding</a> article.</p>
<h3>
    <a id="_td7p8bgv5x5k"></a><strong>Monetize</strong>
</h3>
<p>Finally, once you have completed onboarding successfully, you are ready to begin monetizing! You can do this by adding SKUs (stock-keeping units) to your app and integrating them into your code.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/18059788040215"></p>
<p>For more guidance on setting up SKUs check out the<a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/17298449675927"> Premium Apps SKU Setup</a> article.</p>
<p>Keep in mind that you, your App, and your Team are always required to adhere to the<a href="http://dis.gd/terms"> Terms of Service</a>,<a href="http://dis.gd/guidelines"> Community Guidelines</a>,<a href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/developer-terms-of-service"> Developer Terms</a>, and<a href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/developer-policy"> Developer Policy</a>.</p>
<p>After reviewing this and other linked articles, if you have any questions about Premium Apps and App Subscriptions, please<a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support/premium-apps"> reach out to our Developer Support team</a>!</p>
