<h1 id="h_01M1HE66E21WNNVVWKDR87TKGN">
  How to Transfer Ownership of a Verified App
</h1>
<p>
  Verified app owners can now transfer an individually owned app to a developer
  team directly in the Developer Portal themselves! This flow does not apply to
  apps that are already owned by a team.
</p>
<p>
  For information about creating a team and managing team-member roles, see
  <a
    href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/articles/34905563063703-Creating-and-Managing-a-Developer-Team"
    >Creating and Managing a Developer Team</a
  >.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M1HE66E3VXS6VFKEV181Q5NE">Before You Start</h2>
<p>Be sure to confirm each of the following before starting a transfer:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e409a58083ed03d7154d00fab596b3b23">
    <strong>You own the app.</strong> Open the Applications section in the
    Developer Portal and confirm that it is individually owned by your account.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed7a6b617313c539f2adadb7cc0fe5792">
    <strong>You own the receiving team.</strong> Open the Developer Portal’s
    Teams section and confirm that you are the team owner.
    <strong
      >Being an administrator, developer, or read-only member is not sufficient
      to transfer the app.</strong
    >
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebdc28281ecaf2c6c87ebfe3ba23d084e">
    <strong>Your account is in good standing.</strong> Review your account
    standing in Discord’s Warning System. For more information, see
    <a
      href="https://support.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/18210965981847-Discord-Warning-System#h_01HD4SVGAF2KT4AG7NH8BJBFKV"
      >Discord Warning System</a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ea4a20144763811f2fdde356469402987">
    <strong>Multi-factor authentication is enabled.</strong> Check your Discord
    account’s 2FA security settings before beginning the transfer.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec84ef428a7864f3f582aa1981ec8925a">
    <strong>The app is eligible.</strong> Monetized apps and apps that Discord
    identifies as requiring additional review must reach out to Developer
    Support instead of self-service in the Developer Portal.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9c7f22798430cfc3ccd61de4023fd1cb">
    <strong>The cooldown has ended.</strong> At least 30 days must have passed
    since the app’s last completed ownership transfer before attempting another.
    This applies in case you mistakenly made an incorrect transfer and need to
    return it as a personal app again.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9c1d896b4e337c610f8ac63eebb1deb8">
    <strong>The annual limit has not been reached.</strong> An individual app
    can complete no more than 3 ownership transfers in a rolling 365-day period.
    After this cap is met, please reach out to Developer Support for any further
    issues.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e264d3bf5ccb5445154ae43dec79b489d">
    <p>
      <strong>You understand the verification outcome.</strong> If the receiving
      team already has a verified identity, the app remains verified. Otherwise,
      the app becomes unverified and must complete the applicable verification
      process under the team’s ownership. Please see
      <a
        href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/23926564536471-How-Do-I-Get-My-App-Verified"
        >How Do I Get My App Verified?</a
      >
      for more information.
    </p>

</ul>
<h2 id="h_01M1HE66E63G6TA7KYST0DR33S">Transfer a Verified App to a Team</h2>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0255b1a9a05587f7f43e11887f092e54">
    In the Developer Portal, select the individually owned bot you want to
    transfer.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eee2a327254d98cbbec5f9f46c5025b59">
    Under the ‘<strong>General Information</strong>’ section, click on the
    ‘<strong>Transfer App to Team</strong>’ button
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e65089b98c5b8963efa19c88cc4285a12">
    Within the modal, select the developer team that should receive your app.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e612ef25fe91b468706516f7170dd9a3f">
    Be sure to review the destination team, verification outcome, and transfer
    limits before confirming transfer.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e720232cbb4c5420a3998bc4132e93f5e">
    Click the button to complete the transfer.
  </li>
</ol>

<p>
  Once complete, the <span data-token-index="1">Transfer to Team</span> button
  disappears and your app appears under the team on the

</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE6Q7YAR70BQWJMR34VMTR">
  ⚠️ If your <strong>app</strong> is monetized, already belongs to a team, or is
  not eligible for this self-service flow,
  <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/requests/new"
    >submit a ticket to Developer Support</a
  >
  for assistance.
</h3>
<h2 id="h_01M1HE66E98JR09DS2790GY8E0">What Happens After the Transfer?</h2>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e5325f7c8714ba8f4546fa7f9a92aa8ca">
    The app moves from individual ownership to the selected developer team.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e10d51eb5fc886185b236a587f0e4135d">
    The receiving team’s existing roles determine what each member can view or
    change. The team owner retains owner-only permissions, including
    ownership-transfer decisions.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e814ab8007436b6512dbc29aa09a917cb">
    The app’s bot token does not change.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e81b5dd821c5b0104a4a75be88b1715c3">
    Existing OAuth2 authorizations &amp; privileged intents are not affected.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9fe44c976d212b1c87d048f4560e1bf5">
    <p>
      If the receiving team already has a verified identity, the app remains
      verified. Otherwise, the app becomes unverified and must complete the
      applicable verification process under the team’s ownership. For
      Stripe-related questions, please see our <a
        href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/6226051178775"
        >Stripe FAQ</a
      >.
    </p>
    <figure class="wysiwyg-image wysiwyg-image-resized" style="width: 69%">

  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e04a5fe340f2a0088a3cb2d446e003887">
    A 30-day cooldown begins, and the completed transfer counts toward the limit
    of 3 transfers in a rolling 365-day period for the app.
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01M1HE66E9P4NHK00AC83JNDFN">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66E9425CW43DEGGFXH5C">
  Will my app stay verified after the transfer?
</h3>
<p>
  It stays verified when the receiving team already has a verified identity.
  Otherwise, it becomes unverified and must complete the verification process
  again under the new team’s ownership.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EA6J1BEWV508JJD68F">
  What happens to my identity verification?
</h3>
<p>
  Discord may clear the verification associated with the previous owner unless
  another submitted or approved app still depends on it.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EAGR2QD2HNXNZ8NZJS">
  Do the bot token and existing OAuth2 authorizations change?
</h3>
<p>
  No. The bot token and existing OAuth2 authorizations are not affected by the
  ownership transfer.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EA98PQ9NB7E7D75Q8V">
  Who can manage the app after the transfer?
</h3>
<p>
  Access is controlled by the receiving team’s member roles. The team owner and
  administrators can make broader changes than developers and read-only members,
  but owner-only decisions—including transferring ownership—remain restricted to
  the team owner.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EAVBA06DXR3C5QNTZ9">
  What if I transfer the app to the wrong team?
</h3>
<p>
  Review the receiving team carefully before confirming. A completed transfer
  starts the 30-day cooldown and cannot be immediately reversed through the
  self-service flow.
  <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/requests/new"
    >Submit a ticket to Developer Support</a
  >
  if a transfer was accidental or unauthorized.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EAZGCJ92K77MYMD3BJ">Why can’t I transfer my app?</h3>
<p>The app may:</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2ce62528d2d3c447ce20ea5e889ddd58">
    already belong to a team;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ebf0cba0c11bf50d9f4c540c539e20f02">
    be within its 30-day cooldown;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eca9d991d092330671ed5ee7c04728b3e">
    have reached the limit of 3 completed transfers in a rolling 365-day period;
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ed58a33e26a96b6bde88cceda5535a454">
    be monetized or require additional review
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  Follow the instructions shown in the Developer Portal or
  <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/requests/new"
    >submit a ticket to Developer Support</a
  >.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EB1YKFGV7T3JPG7PT1">
  Can I transfer a verified app to any team?
</h3>
<p>
  You must own the receiving developer team. Being an administrator, developer,
  or read-only member is not sufficient.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M1HE66EBT0EVSG13BKXE34D7">Where can I get help?</h3>
<p>
  If the self-service option is unavailable or the Developer Portal directs you
  to support,
  <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/requests/new"
    >submit a ticket to Developer Support</a
  >.
</p>
