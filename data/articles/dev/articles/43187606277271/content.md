<h1 id="h_01M1HF5REYHB34DHKD0VR12D6H">
  How to Change the Name of a Verified App
</h1>
<p>
  Eligible verified app owners can change an app’s name, and its bot’s username,
  directly in the Developer Portal.
</p>
<hr />
<h2 id="h_01M1HF5REZCB9WMPBP5XS5XKM8">Before You Start</h2>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="ee855ef85a05e6f413630b193308a634d">
    New names must comply with Discord’s policies. Before choosing a new name,
    review
    <a
      href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/developer-policy"
      >Discord’s Developer Policy</a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e82309947609f12402a87aa8381659a88">
    The app name and the bot username each have their own
    <strong>180-day cooldown</strong>. Changing one does not affect when you can
    change the other, unless you change both at once.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e2042af07dbf74ac4729e452b330b2f89">
    The Developer Portal shows the next date on which each can be changed again.
  </li>
</ul>
<h3 id="h_01M1HF7J8A4ZP63TYE187HSA03">
  ⚠️ <strong>Important:</strong> Review the proposed names carefully before
  confirming. A successful change takes effect immediately and starts the
  180-day cooldown for each name you changed.
</h3>
<h2 id="h_01M1HF7EXRK416CHYVAYEZNVVV">Change Your Verified App’s Name</h2>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb66f905230b309a5743fc2a2adc72bce">
    In the Developer Portal, select the verified app you want to rename.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e4a36a829cc0940c98f0824fe39c12349">
    Under the <strong>General Information</strong> section, edit the app name to
    enter the new name.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e0acf8ff9e4faa88bdda2036265fe2476">
    <span data-token-index="0">(Optional)</span> To give your bot the same name,
    select <span data-token-index="2">Also update the bot username</span>. The
    confirmation modal shows the exact bot username that will be used.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7d0e2472d12a358f3036dc1c00cb940b">
    Review the confirmation modal. It shows the current name, the proposed new
    name, the 180-day cooldown, and a reminder that the name must follow
    Discord’s policies.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec30adcfc25b06cfdaceec8a35e0fe998">
    <p>
      Click <strong>Confirm Change</strong> to complete the change. If accepted,
      the new name or names take effect immediately.
    </p>
    <p>
      If you leave the option unselected, only the app name changes and your bot
      keeps its current username.
    </p>
  </li>
</ol>

<h2 id="h_01M3STX48FP7HE5GV62T951CEP">Change Your Bot’s Username Only</h2>
<p>You can also change your bot’s username without changing the app name.</p>
<ol>
  <li data-list-item-id="e9f0c62e655bc11e377799f174341400c">
    In the Developer Portal, select your verified app.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e94621264cb72b6a4f31f612e8f2284e4">
    Open the <strong>Bot</strong> section.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e7dd68402b6d6757180518bf63722251d">
    Edit the bot’s username.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e115e2376b56f782f78be1ec6d4fd9c55">
    Review the confirmation modal (new username, 180-day cooldown, policy
    notice) and confirm.
  </li>
</ol>
<p>
  This starts only the bot username’s 180-day cooldown. Your app name and its
  cooldown are not affected.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M1HF5RF24G81DD6S9JN3K31K">Name Requirements</h2>
<p>
  App names and bot usernames must follow
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/developer-policy"
    >Discord’s Developer Policy</a
  >
  and other applicable policies. Names can be rejected if they are misleading,
  impersonate Discord or another entity, infringe intellectual property, contain
  prohibited content, or otherwise violate Discord’s rules.
</p>
<p>
  Bot usernames also follow Discord’s username rules. They are limited to 32
  characters, must be available, and cannot contain certain reserved words.
</p>
<p>
  If you believe a name was rejected by mistake, review Discord’s Developer
  Policy and contact
  <a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support">Developer Support</a>.
</p>
<h2 id="h_01M1HF5RF3ZDPCBH9PT9B1BJ3H">What Happens After the Change?</h2>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="eec9cafaab4ed990e1b2b8c084b2bcf94">
    The new name updates immediately.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e371a75e4ba5a7d74e9510afa06b29719">
    A 180-day cooldown begins for each name you changed.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="e57a904a1df35cf4222dde79dd7be93a8">
    The Developer Portal displays when each name can be changed again.
  </li>
</ul>

<h2 id="h_01M1HF5RF44HZ24DPZS7Q621KF">Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQE9786QAQ9TD9PZ25ZK">
  How often can I change my verified app’s name or bot username?
</h3>
<p>
  Each can be changed once every 180 days. The two cooldowns are tracked
  separately, so changing your bot username does not delay an app name change,
  and the other way around.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEABBW3VG30WSD8RVD9">
  Why don’t I have the option to change the name?
</h3>
<p>
  The app name or bot username may also be within its 180-day cooldown, or the
  app may be otherwise ineligible for the self-service flow.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEAZWZKS0QE295BG0CX">Why was the new name rejected?</h3>
<p>
  The proposed name may not comply with Discord’s policies, or the bot username
  may already be taken or break username rules. Review the Developer Policy and
  try a compliant name. If you believe the rejection was a mistake, contact
  <a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support">Developer Support</a>.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEA8CHZXHPVH2ZRGTKJ">
  Will changing the app name also change my bot’s username?
</h3>
<p>
  Only if you select <strong>Also update the bot username</strong> on the
  General Information page, under <strong>Name</strong>. Otherwise, your bot
  keeps its current username. You can also change the bot’s username separately
  under the <strong>Bot</strong> section.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEB57D97GKXS52Y5QPS">
  Why doesn’t my bot’s proposed username exactly match my new app name?
</h3>
<p>
  Bot usernames follow stricter rules than app names. Some characters or words
  may be removed, and names longer than 32 characters are shortened. The
  confirmation modal always shows the exact username before you confirm.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEB21QH8ZD0K7EDNTB1">
  What if the bot username changes but the app name doesn’t?
</h3>
<p>
  When you update both at once, the bot username is changed first. If the bot
  username is accepted but the app name is then rejected, the bot username
  change stays in effect and its cooldown begins. Your app name and its cooldown
  remain unchanged, so you can try a different app name.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEBG1E21CCZ7J4MXTGW">What if I change a name by mistake?</h3>
<p>
  Review the names carefully before confirming. A successful change takes effect
  immediately and starts its 180-day cooldown. The self-service flow does not
  provide an immediate undo option. Contact
  <a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support">Developer Support</a> if you need
  assistance.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEB3EV4RJ4Q4VZRE694">
  Are team members notified when a team-owned app is renamed?
</h3>
<p>
  Discord doesn't send team members a notification when a name changes. We
  recommend letting your team know.
</p>
<h3 id="h_01M3STZQEBSBPMVTG9E6BC663T">When does the new name take effect?</h3>
<p>
  New names takes effect immediately after the change is successfully confirmed.
</p>
<h4 id="h_01M1HF6951XPVTZTTXRT7ZZY5M">
  💡If you need help with a rejected name change or an accidental rename,
  contact <a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support">Developer Support</a>.
</h4>
