<h2 id="h_01J9PZD05F3APKBR95F0W8C5PH">App Directory: Content Requirements</h2>
<p>
  App Discovery gives users multiple ways to find and install your app across
  Discord, such as App Directory and App Launcher. In order for your Application
  to be eligible for discovery, certain content requirements must be met:
</p>
<ul>
  <li data-list-item-id="e1647d52e28d71cdd14ad37125637307c">
    You and your application must continue to comply with <a
      href="https://discord.com/terms"
      >Discord’s Terms of Service</a
    >, <a href="https://discord.com/guidelines">Discord Community Guidelines</a
    >, <a
      href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/terms-of-service"
      >Discord Developer Terms of Service</a
    >, and <a
      href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/policies-and-agreements/developer-policy"
      >Discord Developer Policy</a
    >.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec014ea3d7943b3a8ccf288d34e792d58">
    Your application must have a connected and publicly available Privacy Policy
    and Terms of Service.
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="ec6fbd9230ecbb34efaae0f3e6ed6fdb3">
    Your Application’s content must not contain any adult content. Adult content
    includes, but is not limited to, content and discussion related to:
    <ul>
      <li data-list-item-id="e8732c6ebe1e71e959c665a1705382c30">
        Sexually explicit material such as pornography or sexually explicit text
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e2039f69857b9a7582fb4f5056aa46894">
        Violent content
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="eab54cf1c34da3ffc76b4c1ae4390c4e8">
        Illegal, dangerous, and regulated goods such as firearms, tactical gear,
        alcohol, or drug use
      </li>
      <li data-list-item-id="e657625f34b8f2246a7b7f3f6b1fd76ea">
        Gambling-adjacent or addictive behavior
      </li>
    </ul>
  </li>
  <li data-list-item-id="eb6b4cd6310d42974ff18db17b747b660">
    Your Application's name, description, and commands must not contain any
    IP-violating content.
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01J9PZD05FQQZBY6CGF4AJBQ0F">Enabling Discovery</h2>
<p>
  After you and your application meet the eligibility requirements for discovery
  and your application is verified, you can enable discovery for your
  application in the <a href="https://discord.com/developers/applications"
    >Discord Developer Portal</a
  >. To enable discoverability, navigate to the Discovery tab within the
  Developer Portal. Then, at the bottom of the Discovery Status tab, click on
  the “Enable Discovery” button.
</p>
<p>
  If you have additional questions about App Discovery, reach out to our
  developer support team <a href="https://dis.gd/developer-support/discovery"
    >here</a
  >.
</p>
