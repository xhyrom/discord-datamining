<p id="h_01HR595C6STFKDTVMFQ3D9SSKC" class="callout">Currently, a <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21692628851351-Private-Activity-Safety">private</a> activity can only be launched in a server with 25 members or less.</p>
<h2 id="h_01HR583HECRE5TSAKETV86Q05J">Introduction</h2>
<p>Alright, so you've built an awesome app, and now wanna let folks play it, right?</p>
<p>Currently, we are allowing limited visibility for private Activities. This means that only people specifically invited by you can see your Activity.</p>
<h2 id="h_01HR583HEC3ZDTFBQ47687N9KD">How Can People Play My Activity?</h2>
<p class="callout">It is very important to only test activities from developers that you trust. For more information on Private Activity Safety, check out <a href="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/en-us/articles/21692628851351-Private-Activity-Safety">this</a> article.</p>
<p>There are 2 ways to get visibility on your Activity until it can be released to more users in the future:</p>
<h3 id="h_01HR583HECXA5653AC2NXT62YP">1. Adding Members to Your Developer Team</h3>
<p>If your application is managed by a developer team, other team members can also launch your Activity.</p>
<p class="callout">You must be friends on Discord with any users you add to the Developer Team.</p>
<p>You can include up to 100 members in your developer team. To do this, select the team that owns the application to which your Activity is connected.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/21796851808535"></p>
<p>Input the Discord username of the person you want to add, and choose the <a href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/topics/teams#team-member-roles">role</a> you want them to take on.</p>
<p class="callout">It is important to note that anyone on the Developer Team will have access to important app information in the Developer Portal. If you are adding someone strictly for play testing purposes, we suggest adding them as an App Tester instead.</p>
<p>The user will receive an email invitation, which they need to accept to officially join the team.</p>
<h3 id="h_01HR583HECF659272M43AWY6SW">2. Adding App Testers to the Application</h3>
<p>Additionally, you can add up to 50 App Testers, regardless of whether your application is owned by a developer team.</p>
<p>To do this, open the application in the Developer Portal and select 'App Testers' from the left-hand toolbar.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/21796851823639"></p>
<p>From this point, you can input the usernames of any of your Discord friends. However, they must check their email and accept the invitation to become an App Tester.</p>
<h2 id="h_01HR583HECWXA8RE8EBJ1YFXJC">Viewing Private Activities on the Activity Shelf</h2>
<p class="callout">Note: You can only see Activities that you own, are on a Developer Team for, or have been invited to play test.</p>
<p>To view private activities in the activity shelf that you or your team are developing or testing, navigate to your <strong>User Settings</strong> in Discord. Select <strong>Advanced</strong> on the left side of the app. Ensure that <strong>Developer Mode</strong> is turned <strong>ON</strong>. Head to a voice channel and click the Activity rocket icon and start playing! 🚀</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/21796826565143" alt="Screenshot 2024-03-01 at 11.16.13 AM.png"></p>
<p>If you are an App Tester, you will also need to go into Advanced settings. Toggle on “Application Test Mode”. You’ll see a pop-up where you will need to paste in the Application ID you want to test, and click Activate.</p>
<h2 id="h_01HR583HECJG2NC0GXW1D77VHV">Where can I find my Activity’s Application ID?</h2>
<p>At some point, you may need to access your Activity’s App ID.</p>
<p>You can do that in the <a href="https://discord.com/developers/applications">Discord Developer Portal</a>. You can find and copy the Application ID under the <strong>General Information</strong> section.</p>
<p><img src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/21796826573079" alt="2_.png"></p>
<p>If you received an invitation to become an app tester, you will find the application ID in the request email.</p>
