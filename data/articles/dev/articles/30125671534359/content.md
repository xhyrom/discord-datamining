<h1 id="h_01JP332YTM4DXH24G51FRDA12D">Introduction</h1>
<p>
  We are transitioning from the Legacy Discord Game SDK to the new
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/discord-social-sdk/overview"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Discord Social SDK</a
  >! Read on to learn the key differences between these SDKs and why you should
  migrate to the Discord Social SDK.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JP332YTMGZ55QAZ9JDN2J8PN">What is the Discord Game SDK?</h1>
<p>
  The Discord Game SDK was our original development kit focused on
  gaming-specific features. Since its shift to Legacy status, developers have
  primarily used this SDK for its rich presence features. While the Game SDK
  focused on basic game integration, the Discord Social SDK emphasizes social
  connectivity and player engagement.
</p>
<h1 id="h_01JP332YTMTXC0AQKQ9FMX701Q">What is the Discord Social SDK?</h1>
<p>
  The Discord Social SDK is our new cross-platform development kit that offers
  the following to all game developers:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Unified Friends List</strong> enables players to maintain their
    social connections across their games and gaming sessions.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Rich Presence</strong> will get your players spreading the word on
    Discord about your game simply by playing it!
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Deeplink Game Invites</strong> will allow players to invite players
    so that they can easily join game sessions with just one click.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Account Linking</strong> enables Discord-based sign in and
    authorization.
  </li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_01JP334YAWQ14ESKK4A19FJ2T3">
  <strong>Limited access features</strong>
</h2>
<p>
  The following features are only fully unlocked when joining the closed beta:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>
    <strong>Cross-platform messaging</strong> allows users to message each other
    in game and on Discord.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Voice chat integration</strong> delivers excellent audio quality and
    lets you create individual user voice streams.
  </li>
  <li>
    <strong>Linked Channels</strong> provide persistent group chats that allow
    conversations to flow seamlessly before, during, and after gameplay—both
    in-game and out.
  </li>
</ul>
<p>
  While these limited access features are available in the Discord Social SDK,
  their usage is capped with a rate limit. Please read our
  <a
    href="https://discord.com/developers/docs/topics/rate-limits"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >documentation on rate limits</a
  >
  to learn more.
</p>
<p>

  directly to discuss your game, please fill out

    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
