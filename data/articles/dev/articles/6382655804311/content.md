<h2 id="h_01JRE0RKMQSMWT2B4G773E0SXB">What are select menus for apps?</h2>
<p>
  Apps ask a lot of questions. What role do you want? What's your favorite ice
  cream? Which Minecraft biome is best and why is it tundra?
</p>
<p>
  Now it's even easier to ask and answer those questions with select menus!<img
    src="https://support-dev.discord.com/hc/article_attachments/31295912745751"
    alt=" select-menu.png"
  />
</p>
<p><!-- notionvc: 3b4774cc-12ff-44b1-8d90-08ba5558ef23 --></p>
<h2 id="h_01JRE0RKMQQ1X1Q59A11MS2XPW">What are they?</h2>
<p>
  Select menus let apps give you choices to pick from. On desktop, this looks
  like a dropdown menu. On iOS and Android, you'll see a pop-up sheet to pick
  your choice.
</p>
<p>
  Depending on how the app works, you can pick either one choice from the list
  or multiple (for when you just can't decide on one flavor of ice cream).
</p>

<h2 id="h_01JRE0RKMQ87YT9H7YR8KN8YJR">
  As a developer, do you have any tips for how I can make my app transition to
  select menus in a way that doesn't confuse or impact my users?
</h2>
<p>
  As with all new features, users would probably benefit from a quick heads up
  if the way they're used to interacting with your app is about to change. We
  recommend creating a private or controlled testing channel or server for a
  small group of users, making sure things work properly, and then posting to
  users in your support server that select menus have arrived. Every app is
  different, so do what works best for you!
</p>
