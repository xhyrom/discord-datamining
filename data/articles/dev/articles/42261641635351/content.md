<p>
  The current<strong> </strong
  ><a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/35344672307607-Profile-Widgets-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    ><strong>Game Stats Widgets experiment</strong></a
  >
  has allowed players to link their accounts with games like Marvel Rivals and
  Wuthering Waves to display player stats such as rank, playtime, wins, and
  character selections directly on Discord user profiles.
</p>
<p>
  Now, we're preparing to officially release
  <strong>Game Stats Widgets</strong> to game developers who have
  <a
    href="https://docs.discord.com/developers/platform/claim-your-game"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >claimed their game</a
  >
  in the <strong>Discord Developer Portal</strong>.
</p>

<p>
  During this experiment, we saw a lot of excitement around the ability to
  further customize user profiles, as many of you proactively opted in to the
  experiment to play with the feature.
</p>
<p>
  The existing experiment is now coming to an end, and new custom widgets can no
  longer be created. Also, existing custom profile widgets created as part of
  the experiment will no longer be supported. However, we're cooking up new
  profile widget features that will make it easier for users to customize their
  profiles in the future. Stay tuned!
</p>
<p>
  Official documentation and more details about Game Stats Widgets will become
  available alongside the full feature. In the meantime, we invite you to check
  out the
  <a
    href="https://docs.discord.com/developers/platform/claim-your-game"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Game Claim</a
  >
  process so you can get your game ready for
  <a
    href="https://support.discord.com/hc/articles/35344672307607-Profile-Widgets-FAQ"
    target="_blank"
    rel="noopener noreferrer"
    >Game Stats Widgets</a
  >
  the moment it lands!
</p>
