Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.We're seeking an experienced eDiscovery Lead to join our Legal team and lead our electronic discovery operations. This role will be responsible for managing all aspects of eDiscovery processes, from litigation holds through production, while ensuring compliance with legal requirements and optimizing efficiency across our legal matters. You will be working within the Litigation &amp;amp; Regulatory Compliance team, but you will often partner with our other Legal colleagues, Technical Program Management, Engineering, Data, Security, and other relevant functions. This is an individual contributor position reporting to the VP of Litigation &amp;amp; Regulatory Compliance.&amp;nbsp;
What you'll be doing

Supporting litigation, regulatory investigations, and internal investigations by managing end-to-end eDiscovery processes (e.g., identification, preservation, collection, processing, review, and production of ESI)
Working collaboratively with data, privacy, engineering, security, other XFN teams, and outside counsel on eDiscovery strategy and execution (including the implementation of litigation holds and data retention practices)&amp;nbsp;
Managing relationships with external eDiscovery vendors and experts
Developing and maintaining eDiscovery protocols, technologies, and tools (working with XFN partners as appropriate), and workflows to improve efficiency and reduce costs
Collaborating with IT teams to understand Discord's data architecture and ensure effective data collection strategies
Staying current with evolving eDiscovery law, technology, and best practices, and ensuring compliance with applicable rules and regulations governing electronic discovery

What you should have

Bachelor's degree&amp;nbsp;
10+ years of hands-on eDiscovery experience in a consumer-facing technology company’s in-house legal department
Familiarity with eDiscovery platforms (e.g., Relativity, Opentext, Casepoint, etc.) and developments in legal tech (e.g., AI or TAR)
Experience with cloud-based data sources and communication platforms, such as Discord, Google Workspace, Notion, etc.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
Knowledge of federal and state procedural/discovery rules, and relevant case law
Project management experience with ability to handle multiple matters
Exceptional analytical and communication skills that work for both Legal and Engineering
Deep understanding of complex data infrastructure, data analytics, and industry best practices

Bonus points

Experience with social media/gaming platforms
Certifications in eDiscovery tools (Relativity Certified Administrator, etc.)
Experience with international data privacy laws (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)
Experience managing vendor relationships and budgets
