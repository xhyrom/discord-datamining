**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Discord is your place to talk. Whether you're part of a school club, gaming group, worldwide art community, or just a handful of friends that want to spend time together, millions of people use Discord every day to create connections, hang out, and talk.
As a Staff Designer and leader within the organization, you’ll actively define what design quality means within Discord Design, and collaborate across teams to define and execute future evolutions of the product.
On the Platform Ecosystems team, you will partner with UXR, Product Managers, Engineers, and Data Scientists on the Platform Ecosystems team to craft intuitive and delightful experiences for two critical audiences: App developers who bring delightful activities, bots, and games to the platform, and users who need intuitive ways to discover, and enjoy them with friends. You will combine deep user empathy with a balance of UX, UI, prototyping, and visual design skills to enable frictionless experiences, and mentor more junior designers in doing the same.
If you're interested in joining a passionate team of designers that are creating fun experiences for millions of people across the world, Discord is the place to be.
What You'll Be Doing

You will craft the standard for visual caliber and quality on Platform Ecosystems (your team); owning the execution and production quality, and proactively identify opportunities to uplevel the quality of your work in production as you playtest.
You will Partner with the Platform Ecosystems Design Director to lead vision-setting initiatives on Platform Ecosystems, bringing diverse product ideas into one cohesive vision with tactical design deliverables, and regularly present and communicate your designs to peers and executives to gain alignment.
You will define and influence Platform Ecosystem’s roadmap with a deep understanding of the product space, acting as a thought leader with your XFN leads in defining the “how” of the product strategy.
You will proactively share your work with the broader design organization, developing and advocating for new ways of working, as well as creating workshops and spaces to learn new skill sets.
You will identify gaps or improvements for our design system and contribute UX and design patterns that address design debt, and are adopted across the design organization.
You will simplify the user experience of playing an Activity, using wire-frames, user journeys, prototypes to deliver intuitive product experiences that lead to higher Activity trial and re-engagement.
You will partner with the Platform Ecosystems Design Director to influence your team’s culture of communication through respectfully open and honest feedback, and encouraging others to be heard and understood.

What you should have

7+ years of experience designing consumer-facing products.
Experience in leading designers or teams at an initiative level.
Confidence in your ability to execute, and coach others in making design decisions, leading discussions, and communicating out to the wider organization.
Compelling storytelling skills across multiple mediums with consistent experience pitching and influencing design decisions across all levels (ie. executives).
Strong, pro-active communication skills, forming ideas and presenting them to your cross-functional team to develop product strategy and roadmaps.
A desire to deeply understand the "why" before defining the "how" and use it to develop a strategic product perspective, and vision.
A high level of design craft, and a commitment to maintaining a high bar for design implementation as you see work through from design iterations to detailed documentation, and QA.
A portfolio of work on which you were the primary designer leading large-scale, high impact projects for both desktop and mobile.
Experience working with, and contributing to a design system.

Bonus Points

Experience and passion for improving developer experiences
Casual or committed gamer
