**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.This position is temporary and US based only. 
The safety team is building the experiences for user privacy, safety, and trust for the company. As a senior product designer, you'll be involved with the full product development process; from the brainstorming phase, all the way to launch.&amp;nbsp;
If you're interested in joining a passionate team of designers that are crafting features to be used by millions of people across the world, come…JOIN US!
What You'll Be Doing

Designing solutions that cover everything – user needs, business goals, and all the nitty-gritty backend stuff that keeps our platform safe and secure. You take time to understand complex backend processes enough to deliver delightful user-facing experiences.
You will work cross functionally with Engineering, Product, Analytics teammates defining our Safety features.
You will create the flows, beautiful mockups, amazing prototypes, and help ensure it is implemented to pixel perfection.
You will present and communicate your designs to the team and the greater organization to build consensus around your concepts.
You will test, learn, and iterate on everything you do.
You will meaningfully contribute, maintain and commit to our Design System library.

What you should have

You have 5+ years of experience designing modern consumer-focused products.
You have a high level of skill at crafting high quality visual designs and have an eye for creating delight in every product experience.
A portfolio of work on which you were the primary designer contributing to high impact projects for both desktop and mobile.
You have meaningfully worked on, or with a design system.
You proactively form ideas and present them to your cross-functional team to develop strategy and roadmaps.
You collaboratively guide the vision for the product and achieve it through design explorations, documentation, and presentations.
