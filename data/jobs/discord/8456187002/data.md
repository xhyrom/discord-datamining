We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.We are seeking an experienced Senior MuleSoft Developer to help design, build, and scale our enterprise integration platform. This role will be instrumental in developing high-quality APIs and integrations using the MuleSoft Anypoint Platform, enabling secure and scalable connectivity across cloud and on-premise systems.
The ideal candidate brings deep expertise in API-led connectivity, enterprise integration patterns, and modern cloud architectures, along with strong collaboration skills to partner with engineering, architecture, and product teams.
What you'll be doing

Design, develop, and maintain APIs and integrations using the MuleSoft Anypoint Platform
Implement API-led connectivity architecture, including System, Process, and Experience APIs
Build reusable integration assets and frameworks to accelerate development and promote consistency
Develop integrations connecting SaaS, cloud, and on-premise systems such as CRM, ERP, data platforms, and third-party services
Ensure APIs follow best practices for security, scalability, monitoring, and governance
Implement robust error handling, logging, and performance optimization strategies
Publish and manage APIs using API Manager and Anypoint Exchange
Collaborate with architects and engineering teams to define integration patterns and standards
Support CI/CD pipelines and automated deployments for MuleSoft applications
Troubleshoot production issues and provide ongoing support for integration services
Contribute to documentation, knowledge sharing, and mentoring junior developers

What you should have

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience)
6+ years of integration development experience, including 4+ years working with MuleSoft
MuleSoft Certified Developer (MCD Level 1 or Level 2)
Strong expertise in DataWeave 2.x, Java, and API development using Mule 4
Experience building integrations using REST and SOAP services, with strong knowledge of XML and JSON data formats
Experience designing and implementing APIs using RAML or OpenAPI Specification (OAS)
Hands-on experience with tools such as Anypoint Studio, API Manager, and Anypoint Exchange
Strong understanding of API-led connectivity architecture, including System, Process, and Experience APIs
Experience implementing event-driven integrations using messaging and streaming platforms such as Apache Kafka, JMS, or other event brokers
Experience integrating enterprise platforms such as Salesforce, Oracle Fusion, Workday, or similar systems
Knowledge of API security standards such as OAuth, JWT, and policy enforcement
Experience with CI/CD pipelines, Git-based workflows, and DevOps practices
Familiarity with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP
Strong debugging, performance tuning, and troubleshooting skills
Excellent collaboration and communication skills in cross-functional environments

Preferred Qualifications

Experience designing enterprise integration architectures
Experience with event-driven architecture, messaging systems, or streaming platforms
Experience working in Agile or Scrum development environments
