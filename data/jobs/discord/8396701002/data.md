We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Data Engineer, Ads (P3)
Discord seeks a skilled data engineer to join our Data team, focusing on our advertising products. In this role, you will own the data infrastructure that powers Discord's ad identity and audience capabilities - including Identity Graph crosswalk tables, audience resolution pipelines, and targeting data that downstream systems consume. You will design and maintain sophisticated data pipelines, datasets, and analytical tools while partnering closely with cross-functional teams to deliver high-quality data solutions.
This role works heavily with Data Science, Ads Engineering, product, and external measurement partners. If you're energized by owning a critical data domain end-to-end and building infrastructure that directly powers advertising products, we encourage you to apply.
What You'll Be Doing

Own and maintain complex data pipelines and foundational datasets within the identity and audience domain, including pipelines that resolve user identities across hashed emails, device IDs, and partner identifiers
Design and build ETL processes, data models, and analytical frameworks using SQL, Python, and modern data stack technologies
Develop and maintain data quality audits, monitoring systems, and alerting infrastructure for your domain
Ingest and process advertiser-provided custom audiences, managing partner data integrations with external platforms
Collaborate with data scientists, ML engineers, and product teams to translate requirements into scalable, maintainable data solutions
Break down complex projects (e.g., IDG Phase 2, CAPI support) into phased deliverables that ship incremental value
Own prioritization within your domain, balancing roadmap commitments with operational needs and ad-hoc requests
Share learnings and support teammates encountering identity/PII data challenges

What You Should Have

3+ years of hands-on experience writing production code and architecting data pipelines with high-volume consumer data, preferably in advertising technology domains (ad delivery, targeting, identity, measurement)
3+ years of direct experience designing, coding, and maintaining data models and systems handling structured and unstructured data sources
Strong coding abilities in SQL, Python, and modern data engineering frameworks with demonstrated ability to write performant, maintainable code
Experience implementing and maintaining data quality audits and monitoring systems for large-scale datasets
Solid technical communication skills to explain implementations to stakeholders and collaborate effectively across functions
Ability to own a domain end-to-end: design, implementation, stakeholder management, and adoption
Collaborative mindset with intellectual curiosity and commitment to technical excellence

Bonus Points

Passion for Discord or gaming
Experience with identity resolution, customer data platforms, or partner data integrations
Experience working with PII in privacy-compliant architectures (hashing, encryption, compliance workflows)
Experience with modern data storage and processing technologies (BigQuery, Airflow, Dagster, dbt, or similar)
Experience with third-party advertising data—MMPs, ad platforms, or measurement partners
