If you thrive on creating seamless experiences and enabling leaders to focus on what matters most, we'd love to hear from you.What You'll Be Doing

Act as a trusted business partner, quickly adapting to the needs of different leaders and teams.
Support leadership with daily administrative and operational tasks, ensuring smooth workflows.
Manage complex calendars, resolving conflicts and optimizing time allocation across internal and external commitments
Coordinate internal projects and events, following up to ensure completion and acting as a liaison between leadership and their teams.

Collaborate closely with other administrative professionals to maintain alignment and efficiency across the organization.

2+ year(s) work experience in an administrative support role
Highly proficient in G-Suite, notably GCal and GMail
Experience and comfort with tools like Discord, Notion, Claude, Ramp, Oracle
You are a highly motivated, organized individual with strong attention to detail
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, and the ability to communicate with all levels of leadership and external partners
High adaptability working with diverse leadership styles and personalities in a hybrid environment
Strong operational instincts to anticipate needs, spot issues early, and handle multiple assignments&amp;nbsp;
