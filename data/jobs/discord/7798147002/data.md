**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Join our Discord Product Creative Team as a Creative Producer, supporting our expanding &amp;nbsp;Collectibles Content Creation. In this role, you'll oversee both internal and external projects, collaborating closely with a dynamic team of Illustrators, Graphic Designers, 3D Artists, and Animators, as well as vendors.&amp;nbsp;
As we expand our collectibles offering, we’re partnering not only with game publishers but also with music and entertainment industry leaders to bring diverse, engaging content to our platform. Your responsibilities will include managing budgets and vendors, acting as the primary point of contact for external partners, tracking progress across our team, and onboarding new partners. You’ll also be refining processes to scale operations efficiently.
This role ensures seamless asset delivery, anticipates team needs, and builds workflows to scale the Collectibles team efficiently as it grows. Working hand-in-hand with our creative minds, you'll contribute to the development of immersive content that sets Discord apart. If you excel in organizing details, thrive on collaboration with artists, and have a knack for producing captivating assets, you'll feel right at home with us.
What You'll Be Doing

You will be part of the Product Creative team responsible for all creative content within the Discord product. The team is made up of&amp;nbsp; Illustrators, Graphic Designers, 3D Artists, &amp;amp; Motion Designers.
Manage a high volume of projects in a fast-paced production environment.
Oversee the entire production process, from contracts to payments, ensuring timely and high-quality execution from start to finish.
Build timelines and ensure that all projects are completed on time and within budget from concept to completion.
Propose and implement streamlined workflows to enhance the creative production process and foster better collaboration.
Collaborate cross-functionally with marketing, product managers, product designers, engineers, legal, policy, and the creative team to scope projects, define processes and milestones, and ensure we are shipping content according to required release dates
Work autonomously on most tasks while providing valuable feedback on the work of others.
Creatively solve problems to remove any blockers and produce successful assets
Manage production and assign projects based on creative needs, artist skill sets, experience levels, bandwidth, and resources&amp;nbsp;
Stay connected to business strategy to understand business goals in order to prioritize incoming project requests&amp;nbsp;
Meet project deadlines and demonstrate adaptability in response to changing priorities.

What you should have

2+ years of proven project management experience in an agency or entertainment/gaming/in-house creative team
Experience managing different types of creative projects that involve graphic design, illustration, animation, and copy deliverables&amp;nbsp;
Basic understanding of design principles or graphic design experience is helpful.
Experience managing outsourcing of creative projects to freelancers and agencies
Superb organization and time management skills; able to prioritize a large volume of work effectively and efficiently with tight deadlines
Experience in documenting processes, strong understanding of creative briefs, and a well rounded knowledge of the creative process
Ability to effectively work with artists, designers, and other team members.
Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.
Flexibility and adaptability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
