We're looking for a Program Manager to empower our product teams to conduct effective self-service research. In this role, you'll create and maintain the infrastructure that democratizes user research across Discord, enabling designers, product managers, and engineers to gather valuable user insights independently.
Working with Design, PM, Engineering, and other XFN partners, you’ll serve as the bridge between our product teams and research tools, developing training materials, best practices, and support systems that make user research accessible to all. This is an opportunity to build a foundational program that will scale as Discord grows.

This position is temporary and US based only. 
What You'll Be Doing

Design self-serve research systems and processes for PMs and Designers that can scale efficiently as Discord grows
Manage partnerships with external UXR platforms (like UserTesting.com), serving as the primary point of contact and administrator
Create and maintain documentation, training materials, and best practices for self-service research tools
Enable product team members to conduct high-quality self-service research independently
Develop and implement a comprehensive program for democratizing user research across Discord
Track usage metrics and gather feedback to continuously improve the self-service research program
Collect feedback to ensure the self-serve tools/resources bring value to the company
Establish quality standards for self-service research to ensure consistency and reliability
Facilitate knowledge sharing across teams to maximize the value of research insights
Stay current on UX research tools and methodologies to recommend improvements to our tech stack

What you should have

8+ years of experience in program management, research operations, or a related field
Experience with various tools like survey platforms, qualitative research platforms, and knowledge management systems.
Strong documentation and process development skills
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Experience working with product design teams is preferred
Basic understanding of UX research methodologies
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment

Who You Are

An organized and detail-oriented professional with experience in program management, operations, or a related field
A natural communicator who can effectively support team members with varying levels of research experience
Someone passionate about user research and its value in product development
An independent operator with experience rolling out self-serve programs at scale
A collaborative team player with proven experience partnering with PMs, Designers, Engineers, Researchers, and Data Scientists
Someone who balances maintaining standards with making research accessible
