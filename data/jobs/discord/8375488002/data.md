Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Working at Discord is like working with an exciting and motivated family. Finding the right people to add to our unique mix is a difficult endeavor, so we're looking for a Senior Technical Recruiter to join our recruiting team and help us maintain a smooth and positive experience for remarkable candidates.&amp;nbsp;
What you'll be doing

Help execute against our hiring goals, partnering with infrastructure and product engineering departments. &amp;nbsp;
Serve as a company brand ambassador as the first point of contact for many candidates.
Understand how business goals impact the full hiring landscape and anticipate how organizational changes may impact hiring needs.
Develop strong relationships with hiring managers to understand their needs and help them meet their hiring goals.
Recruit top talent through sourcing on multiple channels, reviewing incoming applicants, sourcing candidates, and maintaining a successful offer acceptance rate.
Innovate on ways to attract and source world class engineers.
Identify process improvement areas and solutions, share and encourage best practices with the rest of the Recruiting, Recruiting Operations, and People teams to ensure every candidate has a positive experience.
Develop a deep relationship with candidates by understanding their goals, values, and interests to match them to the right openings and strengthen the offer process.
Assist on roles outside of Engineering and Product if open headcount volume warrants additional help.&amp;nbsp;

What you should have

A commitment to and track record of delivering high touch, world class service candidates and employees
Adaptability and openness to learning and incorporating new recruiting strategies, tactics, and processes
4+ years of experience sourcing for Engineering and Product roles
Proven track record sourcing and hiring passive candidates
Strong calendar management skills and attention to detail
Experience with Greenhouse, Gem, Seekout, other forms of applicant tracking systems, CRMs, or search tools
Confident, coachable and enthusiastic team player who thrives in a fast-moving environment
A sense of urgency in juggling multiple priorities
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Comfortable interfacing with all types of people
