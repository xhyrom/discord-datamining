Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.The Payroll Analyst plays an integral role by helping to ensure everyone gets paid accurately and on time. You will have a direct impact on the employee experience, system implementations, and have the opportunity to contribute to special projects. As a valued member of our team, you will play a key role in compliance, audits and assist with building sustainable processes.
What You'll Be Doing

Process end-to-end payroll for multi-state and/or international employees, including new hires, terminations, bonuses, and commissions
Audit timekeeping and attendance data while maintaining data integrity across multiple systems (e.g., Workday HCM, ADP payroll)
Partner with HR and Finance on payroll-related journal entries, reconciliations, and month-end close
Coordinate with People Operations, People Tech, and Benefit teams to ensure accurate employee data updates
Ensure payroll compliance with federal, state, and local regulations while supporting year-end processing
Generate payroll reports for audits and compliance needs
Respond to payroll-related inquiries and continuously identify areas for process improvement

What you should have

2-4 years of payroll processing experience, preferably in a multi-state environment
Strong understanding of payroll best practices, tax regulations, and wage and hour laws
High attention to detail and excellent problem-solving abilities
Strong communication skills and ability to handle sensitive information with integrity
Proficiency in various software applications (e.g., Google Suite and Microsoft Excel)

Preferred Experience

Experience with ADP Workforce Now; Workday HCM experience is a plus
Experience with international payroll and equity compensation
Background working in a fast-paced, high-growth environment
Familiarity with benefits administration and time tracking integrations
