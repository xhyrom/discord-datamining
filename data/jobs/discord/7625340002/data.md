**Job Description:**
This position is US based only.
Discord is about giving people the power to create space to find belonging in their lives. We want to make it easier for you to talk regularly with the people you care about. We want you to build genuine relationships with your friends and communities close to home or around the world. Original, reliable, playful, and relatable. These are the values that connect our users and our employees at Discord.&amp;nbsp;
As a Senior Product Marketing Manager for the Developer ecosystem, you will drive go-to-market strategy and execution from start to finish: market research, positioning, launch strategy and execution, and ongoing growth initiatives. You’ll quarterback cross-functional teams to execute on world-class product launches and campaigns that promote growth, engagement, and evangelism within the Developer ecosystem and deliver on business goals.
What You'll Be Doing

Go-to-Market Planning and Execution: Develop Go-To-Market strategy grounded in market awareness and customer empathy to acquire new Developers and improve adoption of Developer tools and products.
Messaging and Positioning: Develop clear, cohesive, differentiated messaging that lands the value proposition and the “why” with key audience segments.
Market Awareness: Gain an in-depth understanding of the Developer ecosystem, including its competitive and partnership landscape, and use this insight to inform Go-To-Market strategy and key narratives.&amp;nbsp;
Voice of the Customer: Be the expert on our target audience segments and act as the internal champion for the customer, ensuring their needs guide our marketing strategies and inform our product roadmap.
Content Strategy and Development: Know the product/solution intimately, and be well-versed in the user journey. You know how to get hands on or collaborate with others to deliver the right content to meet the user where they are at each phase in their journey, be it videos, product demos, case studies, training materials, etc.

What you should have

7+ years of product marketing experience including 3+ years of experience with a developer audience.
A deep empathy for Developers and passion for Discord’s mission and the role Developers have to play in the growth of the business.
A strategic thinker who is curious, resilient, goal-oriented with a bias for action, and can look at the big picture to connect dots and create clarity in an often ambiguous environment.
An exceptional communicator that creates clarity for internal and external customers through intentional, organized verbal and written communication.
The ability to lead effectively through influence and comfortable working cross-functionally across the entire business, catalyzing and inspiring teams to align behind a common goal and do their best work.
Stellar writing and content creation skills, including compelling video scripts, articles for user education.
A growth mindset. Someone who views learning as a lifelong journey and is constantly looking to expand their tools and knowledge to become a better version of themselves.
