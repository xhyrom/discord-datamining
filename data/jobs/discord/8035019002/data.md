As a P1 Data Scientist on Discord's Safety Analytics team, you'll play a critical role in supporting Discord's commitment to transparency, safety, and regulatory compliance. What You'll Be Doing

Respond to legal and litigation data requests with exceptional precision and speed, ensuring accurate and comprehensive data delivery that meets strict legal standards
Drive compliance-based transparency reporting including voluntary disclosures, EU Online Safety Act requirements, and emerging regulatory frameworks
Identify and build scalable solutions for recurring legal and compliance data needs, transforming manual processes into efficient, automated workflows
Collaborate closely with Adaptive Data teams on safety incident response, diving deep into complex data patterns to diagnose spam waves, abuse trends, and platform safety issues
Develop highly precise business metrics with exceptional attention to detail, providing accurate metric definitions and bulletproof SQL queries that withstand legal scrutiny
Partner with Data Engineering teams in production codebases, contributing technical solutions and ensuring data infrastructure meets compliance requirements

What you should have

Strong SQL skills with experience writing complex, performant queries against large datasets
2+ years of experience working with large, complex data sets in a production environment
Proficiency with spreadsheets and dashboarding tools (Excel, Google Sheets, Tableau, Looker, or similar)
Experience developing precise business metrics with strict accuracy requirements and clear documentation
Strong attention to detail and ability to work under pressure while maintaining exceptional quality standards
Technical curiosity and willingness to dive deep into codebases, working alongside engineering teams
Excellent documentation skills for creating clear, defensible metric definitions and processes
Experience with data governance, compliance, or legal analytics (preferred but not required)
