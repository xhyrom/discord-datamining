What You'll Be Doing

Serve as the subject matter expert on large collections of Workday integrations
Design, develop, and implement complex integrations between Workday and various external/internal systems using Workday Integration Tools (EIB, Core Connectors, Studio, Cloud Connect, etc.).
Create and maintain integrations that support data flows, ensuring accurate and efficient communication between Workday &amp;amp; internal/external systems.
Collaborate with business stakeholders and other internal teams to understand integration requirements.
Translate business requirements into technical specifications and design documents.
Conduct unit testing, integration testing, and end-to-end testing for developed integrations.
Troubleshoot and resolve production-related integration issues in a timely manner.
Create and maintain comprehensive documentation for integrations, including design documents, process flows, and technical documentation.
Ensure that integrations comply with security and data privacy standards.
Collaborate with security teams to implement and maintain security best practices for integrations.
Work closely with other teams, business analysts, and stakeholders to understand overall business objectives and ensure that integrations align with organizational goals.
Stay current with Workday releases and updates, and assess their impact on existing integrations.
Propose and implement enhancements to existing integrations, as needed, including annual events such as open enrollment &amp;amp; compensation events

What you should have

Minimum of 6+ years of experience as a Workday Integration Analyst.
Strong proficiency in Workday Integration tools and technologies such as Workday Studio, Workday Payroll Interfaces, &amp;amp; Workday’s connector library, with expertise in building and maintaining RESTful and SOAP based API integrations using OAuth2.0 authentication flows and certificate based security.&amp;nbsp;
Advanced skills in XSLT transformations, JSON manipulation and XML schema design and data mapping.
Hands on experience with iPaaS platforms, particularly Workato, including recipe development and custom connectors.&amp;nbsp;
In-depth understanding of Workday's data model, business processes, and security features.
Experience in integrating across business systems beyond the People &amp;amp; Talent team, such as Finance, Corporate Engineering, and IT
Experience with various integration patterns, protocols, and data formats.
Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
Strong communication and collaboration skills.
