**Job Description:**
Discord is about giving people the power to create space to find belonging in their lives. We want to make it easier for you to talk regularly with the people you care about. We want you to build genuine relationships with your friends and communities close to home or around the world. Original, reliable, playful, and relatable. As Discord's Senior Corporate Paralegal, your core responsibilities will be to manage corporate governance functions, collaborate on equity compensation management, and support Ethics &amp;amp; Compliance policies and programs. You will also flex your skills to creatively solve challenges that arise as the company grows. You'll work with some super inspiring people across various disciplines, including our Stock Admin, Accounting, Tax, Corporate Development, and Legal teams. Reporting to our Vice President &amp;amp; Deputy General Counsel, you'll help handle a range of important corporate law matters -- this is a special opportunity to have a significant impact on how the team and company expand and evolve. This hire must be based on the West Coast, however, there is no in-office requirement.
What you'll be doing

Manage corporate governance functions and assist with Board, Committee, and shareholder meetings, regulatory filings, and related documentation
Draft and maintain Board, Committee, and shareholder records
Collaborate with our Finance, Accounting, Tax, and People teams on management of the company’s equity compensation programs, including preparation of employee communications and close engagement in the equity grant process
Support the administration of our Code of Ethics, whistleblower hotline, and other policies and programs
Mentor and train junior team members, and collaborate to design and deliver engaging employee trainings relating to Corporate and Compliance topics
Providing corporate support for the creation and operation of corporate subsidiaries around the world
Support strategic transactions, including due diligence, document preparation, closing mechanics, and post-closing integration and administration of acquired companies
Providing project management support and working on other team, department, and cross-functional projects and priorities as needed

What you should have

6+ years of corporate paralegal experience, gained through either a law firm serving technology companies or in-house at a technology company&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
IPO experience or experience working for a publicly-listed technology company&amp;nbsp;
Previous roles that emphasized project management, prioritization, multi-tasking, and consistently meeting deadlines
Experience with designing and implementing scalable, cross-functional processes for an evolving company
Ability to operate with empathy, agility, and flexibility in a hybrid, collaborative, and fast-paced environment with team members, cross-functional, and external partners in various time zones
Effective prioritization when it comes to project management, navigating time-sensitive projects while maintaining quality and attention to detail on other important workstreams
Curiosity and ability to jump into less familiar areas and learn while doing in a fast-changing environment

Bonus points

Interest in learning (or experience in) non-Corporate legal work
Understanding of (or experience using) Discord
Appreciation for absurd hypotheticals, silly memes, and Oxford commas
