**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.We're looking for a talented Software Engineer to join our Subscriptions team. In this role, you'll help build the revenue infrastructure that powers&amp;nbsp;Nitro, Boosts, and many other Discord products. You’ll work cross-functionally with Product, Data Science, Design, and other teams to create best in class experiences for our users and accelerate product development teams. To learn more about Discord Engineering, take a look at our&amp;nbsp;engineering blog!
What You'll Be Doing

Work with other full-stack engineers on your team to build new capabilities related to subscription management, perk fulfillment, discounts, and recurring billing. You will work across Web, Android, iOS, and beyond.
Own problems end to end — through defining technical specifications, implementing changes, and launch.
Work closely with growth and product engineering teams to build an internal platform that drives user acquisition, activation, and retention.
Create novel ways for users to experience the premium Discord experience through features like Nitro Credits.
Collaborate with the Payments and Financial Platform engineering teams to build, maintain, and extend highly available and resilient infrastructure for payments and order processing.
Contribute to a fast-paced engineering team by rapidly prototyping new features and developing principled learnings through experimentation.

What you should have

3+ years of software engineering experience.&amp;nbsp;We primarily work in the web (React, TypeScript), Android (React Native, Kotlin), iOS (React Native, Objective-C, Swift), and backend (Python).
A growth mindset. You view challenges and mistakes as learning opportunities while building upon your successes. You push yourself and those around you to continuously improve.
Product and platform sense. You maintain a high degree of empathy for both your internal and external users, and seek feedback from them about your work.
Experience with billing. You understand the core concepts of payments processing, recurring billing, and integrations with the Google Play and Apple App stores.
