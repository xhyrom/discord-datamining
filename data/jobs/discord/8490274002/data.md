**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Our Nitro team is responsible for delivering the most premium version of Discord to our most loyal and engaged power users. We focus on building exciting features that provide value both on and off the platform, while developing the strategic incentives necessary to grow our subscriber base and deepen long-term retention.
We’re looking for a Sr. Product Manager to lead our Nitro Value squad. You will be responsible for defining the vision for our premium feature set, launching new high-value experiences while continuously optimizing our existing catalog. You’ll drive execution on the roadmap you create, while partnering with Engagement and other Revenue teams to unlock new monetization opportunities for Nitro.
What You'll Be Doing

Guide the product philosophy, vision and roadmap for Nitro Value, covering utility, identity, expression features that drive meaningful value to subscribers and increase subscription growth
Lead cross-functional execution with a team of Engineering, Design, and Data Science to ship impactful features
Partner with other product teams across Discord, like Engagement, Core Tech, and other Consumer Revenue teams (Boosting, Identity, Shop)
Clearly communicate roadmaps, priorities, experiments and decisions across a wide spectrum of audiences from partner teams to executive levels
Empathize and relate to our users, especially those who are the most active on Discord

What you should have

5+ years experience as a Product Manager shipping great software, driving prioritization, setting timelines, with a track record of building durable monetization infrastructure
Ability to thrive in ambiguous environments, excited about finding solutions to complex problems, and then executing on them
Curiosity and empathy for Discord users, with a deep understanding of gaming culture and online communities
Strong analytical ability to quickly organize and leverage data to make informed product decisions with cross-functional stakeholders. Experience making trade-offs related to achieving metric goals while ensuring we’re always delivering for customers
Experience with A/B testing and experimentation frameworks
Demonstrated technical proficiency and effectiveness working closely with engineers on user-facing products at high scale

Bonus Points

BA/BS in Computer Science, Engineering, or relevant technical experience
Experience building and growing consumer products, especially gaming, messaging, community, or social networking products
