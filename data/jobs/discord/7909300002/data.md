This position is a 6 month temporary role and US-based only.&amp;nbsp;
Discord's Talent team is seeking an Inbound Sourcer to proactively identify, engage, and qualify potential candidates using our ATS and CRM systems. In this role you will be the first point of contact for candidates, delivering a positive and engaging candidate experience while managing a robust candidate pipeline. Familiarity with recruiting and the ability to manage a high volume of candidates while seamlessly collaborating with Recruiters will make you successful in this role.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
What you'll be doing:

Review and evaluate high-volume incoming job applications and source our ATS for qualified leads if we don’t have enough applicants.
Conduct initial phone screens with potential candidates to assess their qualifications, experience, and fit for open positions
Provide a positive candidate experience through clear communication and timely follow-ups
Collaborate with recruiters and hiring managers to understand job requirements and qualifications
Maintain accurate records of candidate interactions in our applicant tracking system


Contribute ideas to enhance our job posting strategy and candidate matching processes

Must haves:

2+ years of experience in a recruiting function, preferably with a mix of agency and in-house experience
Experience using Greenhouse or similar ATS/CRM systems
Experience conducting candidate phone screens, or otherwise proven comfort on phone calls
Experience working in fast-paced, high-volume recruitment environments

Would like to have:

Clear written and verbal communication skills
Demonstrated ability to prioritize and manage multiple candidate pools simultaneously
Track record of building relationships with both candidates and hiring teams
Ability to identify qualified candidates quickly and efficiently
