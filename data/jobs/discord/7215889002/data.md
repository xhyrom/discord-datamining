Collaborate with leadership and senior engineers on the team to build the technical roadmap for the Revenue Growth team.
Partner with leadership on the product strategy and help support execution across the organization.
Collaborate with staff engineers and partners across the organization to ensure technical agreement to other systems, products and vision.
Use your technical expertise to constantly improve and ensure your team achieves extraordinary results at the high technical level we set.

7+ years of experience in software development, with a understanding of the development frameworks and fundamentals of one or more of our platforms: web (React, TypeScript), Android (React Native, Kotlin), iOS (React Native, Objective-C, Swift), and backend (Python and some Elixir).
Led large initiatives from inception to launch.
Experience building and shipping product features. Especially features for products at large scale or in real-time applications.
