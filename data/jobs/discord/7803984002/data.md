What you'll be doing:

Lead technical direction and strategy for Discord's client platforms, with particular emphasis on mobile performance and React Native optimization to support international growth in 2026 and beyond.

Mentor the next generation of technical leaders at Discord.

What you should have:

10+ years experience as a software engineer working on large, cross-platform software projects.
You deeply understand mobile performance and React Native and are excited by the challenge of delivering an incredible product to 200 million users.
Strong technical depth with JavaScript/TypeScript and React Native, with experience in C++ or other low-level languages for performance optimization.
Exceptional problem-solving abilities, with a track record of diagnosing and resolving complex performance issues.
Strong communication skills that foster clear technical discussions and help teams reach alignment, while integrating diverse perspectives into plans.
Experience leading technical excellence across teams, establishing best practices for performance, accessibility, and quality.

Bonus Points:

Active in the React and/or React Native communities, with contributions to open source or technical talks at conferences

Background in game development or game-adjacent technologies
