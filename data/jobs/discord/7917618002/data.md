We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Help our team members succeed every day as our IT Support Associate, where the results of your critical analysis will directly enhance the work experience of colleagues across the company. What you'll be doing

Provide advanced technical support for end-user hardware across multiple platforms (MacOS, Windows, and occasionally Linux)
Deliver expert-level software support for enterprise applications including Google Workspace, Asana, 1Password, Adobe, Microsoft 365, InTune/Entra, and JAMF
Contribute to strategic IT projects, including planning, implementation, and successful delivery of technical solutions that align with organizational objectives
Implement and maintain back-end architectural components related to Identity Providers
Identify recurring issues and implement permanent solutions to reduce support tickets
Serve as the final escalation point for resolving complex technical issues that cannot be addressed by Tier 1 or Tier 2 support

What you should have

3+ years of experience in IT support with progressive responsibilities
Experience with ChromeOS and Chrome Enterprise solutions
Demonstrated expertise with MacOS and Windows operating systems
Proven experience supporting enterprise software solutions (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, JAMF, Intune, Okta, etc.)
Strong critical thinking and analytical capabilities
Knowledge of identity management systems and SSO implementations
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
