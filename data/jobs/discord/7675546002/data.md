**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.As the Group Product Manager on Machine Learning, you'll drive the vision, strategy, and roadmap for Discord's Machine Learning Platform and its Applications — particularly in areas such as Personalization, Notifications, Advertising, and Safety.
This role is the most senior product leader for ML at Discord and reports directly to the overall head of product. It is a horizontal role, meaning that it supports many different teams, and therefore requires someone who is comfortable and fluent working with a diverse set of stakeholders with goals that are sometimes in tension.

What You'll Be Doing

Guide the product philosophy, vision, and roadmap for our ML platform, as well as the key use cases such as Advertising, Friending, Notifications, Safety, and Recommendations
Collaborate deeply with Applied ML, ML Platform, and Data Science to build our ML Roadmap
Stay up to date on the latest ML frameworks and tools to ensure that Discord keeps up with the fast moving world of ML, including LLMs and generative AI
Work across all product teams to identify and drive opportunities for how ML should be used

What you should have

The humility to do whatever is necessary to drive impact, to thrive in ambiguous environments, and to get excited about finding solutions to complex problems
10+ years of experience building and shipping great software, including responsibilities driving prioritization, trade-offs, and timelines
BA/BS in computer science, mathematics, physics, economics, or related field or equivalent years of experience
Experience in applying machine learning to consumer tech, with a strong product sense and pride in understanding the human-centric nature of products that ML touches
Familiarity with machine learning basics and trends, from traditional methods to the latest in generative AI. You don't need to be a super-expert, but you should be able to cut through the buzz and help us find practical, efficient solutions that actually work at scale
Proven ability to analyze and synthesize data, including writing SQL, Python, and/or R and using BigQuery
Demonstrated technical proficiency and effectiveness working closely with engineers
A passion for games and the gaming industry

Bonus Points

A degree in advanced quantitative subjects (e.g. statistics, engineering, finance, math)
