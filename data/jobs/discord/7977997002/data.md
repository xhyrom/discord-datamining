What You'll Be Doing (including, but not limited to)

Acting as a strategic business partner to Discord's Chief Business Officer, Jules Shumaker, and supporting 2 of her Directors
Maintaining our CBO's calendar, and making recommendations in regard to time management, prioritization, and needs of the business
Ensuring the CBO is prepared for upcoming meetings, presentations, conferences, and projects by proactively gathering the necessary materials
Contributing to team and org meetings and onsites by assisting and sometimes leading the planning, taking notes, and ensuring action items are completed
Finding new and efficient ways to help maximize our CBO's time, particularly acting as a "goalie" for their calendar who ruthlessly prioritizes their meeting requests
Working with our travel and events teams, you'll book domestic and international travel, ensuring travel policies are followed and proper documentation is acquired (visas, conference passes, etc). Occasionally, you may travel with the CBO for large conferences
Partnering with several internal departments to ensure cross-functional alignment on projects and internal events
Processing all expenses for the CBO and assisting with procurement requests as needed

What you should have

You are a highly motivated, organized individual with strong attention to detail who is able to work effectively and prioritize with minimal supervision
You have clear written and verbal communication skills, and the ability to communicate with all levels of leadership and external partners
You are a strong multi-tasker with an appreciation for the challenges of supporting a busy team with diverse personalities in a hybrid and remote working environment
You have the ability to prioritize and handle multiple assignments at any given time while anticipating the needs of others
You have strong relationship management skills, the ability to build a personal network throughout the company, and the ability to work with a variety of personalities. You also know how to push back and set boundaries with kindness
You have experience performing administrative work that requires analysis, initiative, and discretion
You are able to adapt in response to changing priorities and needs
You have a passion to learn and contribute
You have the ability to manage your bandwidth and speak up if you have too much on your plate

Qualifications

Highly proficient in G-Suite, notably GCal and GMail
5+ years of work experience in an administrative support role, particularly VP+ roles within a Business or Sales org
Experience with Asana, Notion, and Discord

Bonus Points

Prior experience working in a hyper-growth environment
