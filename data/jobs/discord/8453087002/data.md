**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.We are looking for a Design Program Manager to partner closely with our Systems teams. This is a high-impact, high-trust role that sits at the intersection of design craft, systems thinking, and cross-functional execution.
The Systems team is made up of Design Systems and Creative Systems. The Design Systems team is focused on developing the core primitives and standards that engineers and designers use to build Discord's products. The Creative Systems team is focused on defining and maintaining the graphic, illustration, and motion systems that shape Discord's visual identity.
As the DPM for this team, you will help Systems Designers and Engineers balance their two primary tracks of work: proactive, planned work led by the internal team, and reactive, incoming work originating with our partner teams. You will bring together the strategic and the operational, helping the entire Systems team deliver with clarity, pace, and intentionality.
You will report into the TPM organization, but will collaborate day-to-day with Systems leads, designers, and engineers, as well as cross-functional partners across the company. This role is for someone who loves building clarity out of complexity to accelerate the work of design and engineering teams.
What You'll Be Doing

Drive the Systems roadmaps and intake process, ensuring both Design and Engineering capacity stay aligned with incoming requests and partners have a clear path to request support
Track program health proactively, surfacing blockers early, and keeping stakeholders informed at the right level of detail
Serve as a connector between Systems and their partners, facilitating conversations that surface dependencies, align on priorities, and keep design and engineering timelines connected
Support Systems leadership in shaping operating rhythms, including planning, resourcing, and capacity decisions
Identify and implement improvements to how the team plans, shares knowledge, and operates, reducing friction and enabling better outcomes
Drive large-scale system initiatives like migrations, visual refreshes, rollouts, and platform upgrades from planning through adoption
Align Systems' efforts to broader organizational goals and contribute to longer-term planning cycles, including annual priorities and investment cases for system improvements

What you should have

5+ years of experience in design program management, design operations, or a closely related role, with a track record of building and evolving programs
A genuine understanding of how design systems work, including both the design craft and the technical dimensions of component libraries, tokens, and documentation
Experience leading complex, cross-functional programs with multiple workstreams and stakeholders at various levels of an organization
A builder's mentality: you create processes because you understand the people who will use them, not because process is the goal
An instinct for collaboration over control: you influence through trust, and you know when to lead and when to listen
The ability to thrive in ambiguity and bring structure to situations where none previously existed
Experience managing vendor relationships or budgets
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including a talent for translating complex topics into clear updates for leadership and partners

Bonus if you have

Experience at a consumer tech company with a Design Systems team
Familiarity with illustration systems, motion, or other creative system primitives
Hands-on experience experimenting with AI tools to solve real workflow problems, and a point of view on where they add genuine value versus noise
Background working within or alongside a TPM organization
