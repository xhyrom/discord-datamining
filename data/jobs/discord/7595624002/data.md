**Job Description:**
Discord is about giving people the power to create space to find belonging in their lives. We want to make it easier for you to talk regularly with the people you care about. We want you to build genuine relationships with your friends and communities close to home or around the world. Original, reliable, playful, and relatable. These are the values that connect our users and our employees at Discord.&amp;nbsp;
We're looking for a highly organized, detail-oriented Events Manager to join the Discord team. Reporting to the Head of Events, you’ll be joining our production and creative operations team where you’ll collaborate with the product marketing, business development, PR &amp;amp; Communications and creative departments to help execute our marcom initiatives across B2C, B2B, and press related events. You’ll have a hand in producing Discord events big and small - from small sales dinners all the way to tradeshows and conferences.
What you'll be doing

Manage and develop comprehensive work plans, including schedules, important milestones, and deliverables to help coordinate cross-functional teams
Develop and manage event budgets
Align on departmental success metrics and build an event-specific action plan to achieve their goals &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp;

Support events presence in major US and global events such as GDC, PAX, MAU, Gamescom, Tokyo Game Show, and Advertising Week
Develop and maintain relationships with vendors/partners &amp;nbsp; &amp;nbsp;
On-site booth management and event support
Coordinate event-related logistics like staff travel and lodging, business meeting room coordination, and programming schedules
Travel to event locations around the world

What you should have

3+ years experience in events field or equivalent
Strong documentation and organizational skills
Strong project and program management skills
Experience with producing sales dinners, happy hours, and smaller sales focused events
Experience developing and executing tradeshow and conference booths and experiences
Passion for events as a tool for contributing to overall business goals
Passion for designing experiences and a hunger to excel in a fast-paced environment
A no-fear attitude about new projects and experiments
Creative problem solving skills
Flexibility and agility to execute on last-minute urgent requests
Ability to work a flexible schedule and travel up to 20%
