**Job Description:**
This position is US based only.
The Audio/Video Infrastructure team oversees every voice call, video call, and Go Live session. You'll lead the team responsible for writing the software that routes all data and manages thousands of machines to create a seamless user experience. Your responsibilities will include tackling scalability challenges, managing the control plane and developing the new data plane in Rust.
If you're passionate about optimizing hardware through software to reduce costs while enhancing user quality, this team offers an exciting opportunity to do that at scale.
What you'll be doing

Lead engineering execution within the AV Infrastructure team.
Transition our infrastructure from static scaling to auto-scaling based on demand.
Develop and implement the team's roadmap to enhance reliability and efficiency.
Spearhead the re-architecting of our infrastructure primitives, shifting from bare metal to cloud.
Ensure Discord "just works" by building and maintaining reliable, efficient infrastructure.

What you should have

You have 3+ years of experience as an Engineering Manager.
You've worked with large-scale distributed systems serving tens or hundreds of millions of users.
You're a hands-on Engineering Manager who embraces technical complexity and understands the entire stack.
You have experience managing networked video infrastructure or real-time packet-based applications.
You've worked with cloud environments (e.g., GCP, AWS).
You can effectively balance cost considerations against product quality.

Bonus Points

You have experience working with WebRTC.
You've explored io_uring based approaches for problems before with your team.
You have experience with Rust or C++.
You've used Discord personally. If you've played games and used Go Live with friends, you understand the product's capabilities.
You're familiar with DevOps tools such as Salt or Terraform.
