As a crucial part of our scaling Ads business, you'll improve the efficiency and scale of Quest campaign execution while contributing to deep-dive analyses that drive business growth and inform go-to-market strategy. This person will report to the Senior Manager, Advertising Ops &amp;amp; Analytics.
What you'll be doing:

Working closely with Account Executives and Account Managers to understand client needs and objectives, and synthesize them into effective campaigns
Ensuring accurate implementation of campaign parameters – including dates, targeting, tracking, impression/reach goals, geographies and budget level&amp;nbsp;
Working cross-functionally with Account Managers and technical teams to determine best way to optimize campaign outcomes and maximize ROI for clients – including technical troubleshooting and problem solving as well A/B testing of messaging, creative, targeting
Synthesizing campaign performance insights for internal stakeholders and clients that summarize campaign performance, trends, ROI, as well as actionable recommendations.
Serving as the point of contact and expert for Account Executives and Account Managers on best campaign practices and maximize likelihood of client success on Discord&amp;nbsp;

What you should have:

3 to 5+ years of experience in digital advertising, ad operations, or related roles.
Demonstrated knowledge and experience with a broad variety of ad technologies, campaign operation methodologies, and ad tracking / measurement&amp;nbsp;
Successful track record of working with clients, understanding their needs and delivering value&amp;nbsp;
Proficiency in all aspects of campaign operations including setup, creative coordination, pacing, optimization and troubleshooting
Strong analytical proficiency with the ability to query and interpret data, generate insights, and make data-driven decisions
Familiarity and insight into market trends and KPIs such as performance viewability, brand safety, yield analysis and audience extension
Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively in fast changing environment with cross functional teams
