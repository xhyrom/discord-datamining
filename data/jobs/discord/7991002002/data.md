Discord's Talent team is seeking a proactive Technical Sourcer to lead our top-of-funnel recruitment efforts for technical positions. What you'll be doing:

Own the entire top-of-funnel recruitment process for assigned technical roles
Develop and implement creative sourcing strategies to identify and engage passive candidates
Review and screen incoming applications to identify qualified candidates
Drive employee referral initiatives to maximize internal network potential
Partner with hiring managers to understand role requirements and ideal candidate profiles
Track, analyze, and report on recruiting metrics to optimize sourcing strategies

What you should have:

3+ years of experience in technical sourcing or recruitment, preferably in a high-growth environment
Demonstrated success in building candidate pipelines for technical roles
Strong understanding of technical roles, skills, and qualifications
Proficiency with applicant tracking systems and sourcing tools (LinkedIn Recruiter and Seekout)
Experience with boolean search techniques and advanced sourcing methods
Excellent communication and relationship-building skills
Strong project management capabilities with the ability to prioritize multiple requisitions
Data-driven approach to sourcing with the ability to analyze and report on key metrics
