This role will be responsible for analyzing business needs, documenting requirements, and collaborating with cross-functional teams to support various business system initiatives. What you'll be doing:

Business System Support &amp;amp; Enhancements: Assist in supporting and improving business processes related to Order to Cash (O2C), Procure to Pay (P2P), and Record to Report (R2R) functions.
Jira Service Management (JSM) Administration: Track and manage business system requests, incidents, and enhancements in Jira Service Management to ensure timely resolution.
SOX Compliance &amp;amp; Audit Support: Ensure all system changes and enhancements adhere to SOX compliance and financial controls.
Testing &amp;amp; Validation: Assist in UAT (User Acceptance Testing) and system validation for new implementations and enhancements

What you should have:

Bachelor’s degree in business, Finance, Information Systems, or a related field and ability to work in a fast-paced, collaborative environment and experience working in the business systems department
2+ years of experience in a Business Analyst role and have experience with Oracle Fusion ERP.
High-level understanding of enterprise business processes and their impact on financial and operational workflows.
Experience with Jira Service Management or similar ticketing systems and strong problem-solving and analytical abilities, with attention to detail.
Excellent writing and communication skills, with the ability to document business requirements clearly.
Familiarity with SOX compliance and audit processes.
