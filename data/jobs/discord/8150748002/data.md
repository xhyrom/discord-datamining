We are looking for a Senior Oracle Technical Business Systems Analyst to join our team and support the continued optimization of Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP—particularly in the areas of Revenue Automation, Procure-to-Pay (P2P) and Quote-to-Cash (Q2C).
As a member of the Business Systems team, you will play a key role in supporting Oracle Fusion ERP initiatives, helping to drive process improvements, support day-to-day operations, and ensure the system meets both business and compliance needs. This role requires strong functional and technical Oracle experience, a solid understanding of accounting processes, and familiarity with internal control frameworks like SOX and IT General Controls.&amp;nbsp; Experience with revenue automation solutions is also highly valuable, as we scale and automate our Quote-to-Cash cycle.
What you'll be doing

Support configuration, optimization, and troubleshooting within Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, focusing on Procure-to-Pay (P2P), Quote-to-Cash (Q2C), and revenue recognition workflows.
Collaborate with Finance, Procurement, Sales Operations, and IT to understand business needs and help implement scalable, compliant solutions.
Contribute to the design and execution of system enhancements, integrations, and custom reporting, including those related to automated revenue recognition and revenue event processing.
Assist in the development and documentation of controls and procedures in alignment with SOX compliance and IT General Controls (ITGCs).
Participate in testing, validation, and deployment of ERP changes, working alongside other team members and external partners.
Resolve end-user issues promptly,&amp;nbsp; and implement long-term solutions to enhance system reliability and performance to enhance System Optimization, Collaboration, Compliance and Support.
Provide day-to-day support to end users by troubleshooting system issues and recommending long-term fixes.
Support financial close activities by ensuring data integrity and timely availability of key reports and reconciliations.
Collaborate with external Oracle support and consultants to resolve escalated issues as needed.
Help maintain detailed system documentation, test cases, and training materials.

What you should have

7+ years of experience in Oracle ERP, business systems analysis, or similar roles at an enterprise-level company..
Strong functional and technical experience with Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP, including GL, AP, AR, Procurement, and Revenue Management.
Hands-on experience with revenue automation solutions, such as Oracle Revenue Management Cloud (RMCS), Zuora Revenue or custom automated revenue workflows.
Solid understanding of P2P and Q2C business cycles and their cross-functional dependencies.
Familiarity with accounting principles, revenue recognition standards (e.g., ASC 606), and finance operations.
Experience supporting SOX compliance and maintaining effective IT General Controls.
Skilled in Oracle tools such as SQL, OTBI, FBDI, and Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC).
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
Ability to work cross-functionally in a fast-paced, growth-focused environment.

Bonus Points
