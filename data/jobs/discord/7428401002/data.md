This position is US based only.Discord is the place for all things gaming. We are looking for a Director of Sales, Marketing Solutions to join as a founding member of the sales team focused on our eastern region. We are seeking a sales leader who is passionate about games, the game industry and helping game companies market to the players who are most passionate about their games. This is a role where you will be working in a zero to 1 environment to build and grow a new ads business line at Discord for game publishers and developers in our eastern region. &amp;nbsp;You will harness your prior experience in advertising/marketing/media spaces and bring a combination of strategic business thinking, game publisher and agency relationships, product insights and an ability to build a significant, recurring book of business with clients.&amp;nbsp;
This is a senior leadership role where you will be joining the ads business at the ground level and play a significant role in shaping Discord’s product and business strategy in the media and marketing space. &amp;nbsp;This role will report into the Head of Gaming Partnerships &amp;amp; Sales and will be a management role, with a team that grows as the marketing solutions line grows.
This is a remote based position, candidates must be be willing to work Eastern Time Zone (ET) hours and regularly travel to meet with customers.
What You'll Be Doing

You will be responsible for hitting revenue and account goals for the east coast territory by

prospecting and sourcing media buying &amp;nbsp;opportunities with game publishers, studios, and their media agencies
building &amp;amp; deepening relationships with key game publishers and their media agencies
negotiating &amp;amp; closing opportunities
helping clients achieve business and marketing goals via Discord’s solutions


You will define and execute effective and repeatable sales motions and operational processes. You will apply an account planning process to prioritize accounts, grow revenue, educate clients, lead strategic client conversations, and navigate complex relationships.
You will create and convey compelling sales presentations and proposals utilizing internal and external data.
You will become an expert in Discord solutions and adapt recommendations to suit varying client needs
You will be responsible for consulting with clients and contributing to marketing and media plans that enables them to reach their target audiences on Discord.
You will demonstrate expertise around the gaming, media, and marketing spaces to clients, within Discord, and in industry forums.
Over time you may also be called upon to manage teams.

What You Should Have

10+ years of direct experience in digital advertising and ad sales in the gaming industry, preferably focused on the eastern region of the US (Knowledge of the European market is a plus)
Proven experience in delivering against account and revenue goals
Proven experience in overseeing and delivering on goals for significant territories / books of business / verticals
Experience in managing gaming clients throughout the funnel with a deep understanding of their challenges and objectives
Experience in building relationships with top marketing decision-makers at game publishers and their agencies in the eastern region of the US and possibly Europe&amp;nbsp;
Ability to analyze data and identify insights to assess campaign performance
Ability to proactively troubleshoot and problem solve quickly and through the proper escalation channels
Experience with marketing and ad technology ecosystem including ability to collect and convey product needs as well as working cross-functionally to diagnose and solve technical problems for clients
Experience in negotiating and structuring standard and non-standard ad buying agreements
Experience in growing, mentoring, and managing sales teams
