As the Embedded Data Specialist, you will be a trusted locus of data knowledge by stakeholders, capable of fielding requests from diverse groups of stakeholders. You balance emergent requests with creating more longterm tooling, and are conscious of the maintenance investments of the tools you build, with coaching. You expect QA of your code, but very rarely would corrections be approach-based, rather than accidents of function.&amp;nbsp;
What you'll be doing

Analyzing data to uncover patterns, insights, and trends, as well as answering complex legal and comms based Safety questions for external communication
Leveraging analysis to design operational response solutions in the Safety landscape
Writing retros of Safety incidents with a focus on guardrail solutions and clear runbooks
Iterating, improving, and socializing reporting to the needs of an emergent safety landscape across teams and efforts with a focus on stable or self-help solutions
Delivering long-term project work in the Safety operations landscape with some oversight
Documenting — in detail — your work, its impact, and the guardrails present in your design
Assisting in developing scalable, clean data infrastructure
Partnering with teams across Safety to increase the impact of your work&amp;nbsp;

What you should have

Fluent SQL, BigQuery dialect preferred
Experience updating data architecture in version control systems such as Git
Data Reporting experience, with an understanding of new feature design and guardrails
Statistical analysis training
Can determine path for code/data review with limited direction
Maintain high levels of confidentiality and accuracy while performing legal requests
Beyond just fielding requests, able to discuss data availability and limitations to help craft a request with a stakeholder
Excellent written communication and documentation skills
Ability to foster relationships and communicate complex concepts to stakeholders
Ability to prioritize tasks and work independently, but oversight and coaching is expected
Familiarity with Claude Code and leveraging AI tools to expand impact
