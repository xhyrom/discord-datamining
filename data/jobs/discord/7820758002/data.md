Generate Revenue: You will drive new partnerships with game publishers, studios, and their media agencies to build new and repeatable revenue streams for Discord’s emerging mobile ads business. You will prospect and source new opportunities, navigate client needs against Discord’s offerings, and negotiate and close business deals.&amp;nbsp;
Internal Strategic Insights: You will work collaboratively and cross functionally with internal teams to bring strategic recommendations for the product road map. You will work with teammates within the sales function to build and enhance scalable processes for the growing ads business.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
Consultative Client Partnerships: You will work with clients to identify their marketing needs, and develop strategic proposals that deliver value against those needs. You will build and deepen longterm, strategic relationships with senior decision makers across the gaming segment.&amp;nbsp;

What you should have

6+ years of direct experience in ad sales in the gaming industry, specifically in building new business from scratch with major players in the gaming sector.
Proven experience in delivering against account and revenue goals, specifically with building new mobile business.
Experience in building relationships with top marketing decision-makers at game publishers and their agencies.&amp;nbsp;
Ability to analyze data and identify insights to assess campaign performance, and proactively troubleshoot and problem-solve quickly and through the proper escalation channels.
Experience working internally with cross-functional partners to influence product development and processes.&amp;nbsp;
Willingness to travel for regional in-person client meetings
