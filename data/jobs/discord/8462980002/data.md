**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Discord is looking for an exceptional Trust &amp;amp; Safety Counsel to join our growing legal team.&amp;nbsp; This is a high-impact role at one of the most interesting companies on the planet!&amp;nbsp; As our second Trust &amp;amp; Safety counsel, you will support our Trust &amp;amp; Safety organiztion as it seeks to respond to law enforcement data requests, identify and remove harmful content and actors from the Discord platform, and comply with obligations under global laws and regulations.&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
What you’ll be doing

Provide legal guidance to our Law Enforcement Engagement and Response (LEER) team relating to user data and content takedown requests from governmental entities.
Engage with law enforcement agencies concerning responses to legal process and proactive disclosures, where appropriate.
Respond to and resolve escalations relating to law enforcement matters generally.
Manage Discord’s response to third-party civil and criminal defense subpoenas.
Develop and improve guidance documents, workflows, training materials, and other resources for use by LEER and other Trust &amp;amp; Safety teams.
Proactively identify areas of risk and compliance gaps in Trust &amp;amp; Safety operations and participate in developing policy, process, and/or product enhancements to address them.
Provide legal guidance to Trust &amp;amp; Safety staff responding to takedown requests submitted under the Digital Millennium Copyright Act and engage with rightsholders as necessary.
Provide guidance to Trust &amp;amp; Safety teams in response to escalations in light of applicable law and Discord policies.
Support Discord’s engagement with regulators and policymakers on Trust &amp;amp; Safety issues including law enforcement matters.
Collaborate with cross-functional partners to translate internal policies into actionable processes and tell Discord’s Trust &amp;amp; Safety story.

What you should have

A JD from an accredited law school and admission to the bar of a relevant jurisdiction
7+ years of experience as a prosecutor with the U.S. Department of Justice or at in-house tech legal department (consumer-facing platform preferred) supporting law enforcement and/or supporting law enforcement response operations
Strong knowledge of laws regarding user data disclosure, including the Stored Communications Act, Pen Register Act, Wiretap Act, CLOUD Act.
Experience working on multiple projects to deliver practical advice under pressure and comfort operating within ambiguity, prioritizing, and consistently achieving results
Excellent written and oral communication skills
Expert relationship builder with an attitude of open-mindedness, collaboration, flexibility, empathy, a growth-mindset, kindness, and being humble

Nice to have

You are familiar with Discord and the video game industry and/or online communication industry
Knowledge of global laws and regulations implicating trust and safety, such as 18 U.S.C. §&amp;nbsp;2258A, the Take It Down Act, the EU’s Digital Services Act, the EU’s Terrorist Content Online Regulation, UK and Australia Online Safety Acts, Brazil’s Digital ECA, etc.
Experience dealing with foreign governments and/or foreign legal process
Experience delivering training to law enforcement or governmental entities
Experience handling sensitive and/or confidential matters
A previous or current security clearance
Familiarity with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act and takedown notices and/or working with rightsholders
