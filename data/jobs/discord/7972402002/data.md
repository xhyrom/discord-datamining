**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Gaming is at the heart of Discord's origin and remains central to our mission of creating spaces where people can genuinely connect, have fun hanging out, and build communities around shared interests. Our Player Engagement team builds features that bring gaming experiences directly into Discord, creating deeper connections between players and their favorite games. We believe integrating gaming experiences more deeply into Discord will drive the next generation of platform growth. Come join us and help build innovative gaming experiences for our users!
What you'll do

Develop features that integrate gaming data and experiences directly into the Discord platform
Collaborate with design, product, data science and other engineers to introduce new features used by our +180M monthly active users
Work on systems that help users discover and engage with their gaming communities
Participate at all levels of ideation, implementation and release

What you should have

3+ years experience as a software engineer with familiarity working in web and backend stacks
Experience building user-facing features that drive engagement
A focus on impact. Gaming is central to Discord's identity, but we have exciting opportunities to deepen these connections! You want to work with engineers, product managers, designers, and gaming partners to define great solutions to ambiguous problems.
Attention to detail and an eye for user experience. You care about shipping high quality features that delight our users and keep them engaged.
A growth mindset. You view mistakes as learning opportunities and build upon your successes. You challenge yourself and those around you to improve.
A strong sense of shared ownership. You don't believe that anything is someone else's problem and are willing to explore unfamiliar parts of the tech stack to understand how it works and develop new expertise.

Bonus Points

Personal experience using Discord for gaming communities
Knowledge of gaming APIs or experience working with gaming data
Familiarity with React, TypeScript, Python, or Rust
Interest in gaming and gaming communities
