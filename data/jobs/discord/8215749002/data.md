What you'll be doing:

You will be responsible for hitting revenue and account goals by:

Prospecting and sourcing media buying opportunities with game publishers, developers, &amp;amp; platforms as well as their respective media agencies across EMEA
Building &amp;amp; deepening relationships within the gaming industry with executive stakeholders &amp;amp; key decision makers at the brand direct and agency levels
