What You'll Be Doing
Commercial &amp;amp; Marketing Support

Respond to or escalate requests from internal partners in product, business, and marketing who need Legal support.
Train clients on the contracting process and tools, and ensure that the correct process and approvals are in place for each deal.
Independently negotiate non-disclosure agreements with external partners using well-defined processes, standard templates, and established playbooks.&amp;nbsp;
Advise clients on basic commercial, marketing, and business legal concepts; review and edit marketing materials and external communications with oversight.
Negotiate and redline marketing, influencer, and consulting agreements using established playbooks with minimal oversight.
Support the IP team across various areas, including trademark portfolio research, IP clearances, and brand enforcement workflows.

AI Innovation &amp;amp; Process Improvement

Leverage your prior work experience to help drive implementation of a new contracts management system to ensure smooth adoption across different functions.
Maintain and improve legal and cross functional resources, including directories, playbooks, policies, and other key documentation.
Serve as the day-to-day administrator and point of contact for the team's CLM/contract management system, including troubleshooting, user support, workflow configuration, and creation of training materials.
Identify process improvements or efficiencies across the team's work and drive related projects to completion.
Evaluate emerging AI tools for legal work — assessing fit, reliability, and risk — and make recommendations to the team on what's worth adopting.
Design and implement AI-powered workflows that make legal work more efficient and scalable (e.g., contract review assistance, self-serve resources, automated intake).
