**Job Description:**
Discord is about giving people the power to create space to find belonging in their lives. We want to make it easier for you to talk regularly with the people you care about. We want you to build genuine relationships with your friends and communities close to home or around the world. Original, reliable, playful, and relatable. These are the values that connect our users and our employees at Discord.&amp;nbsp;
Discord is on the lookout for a Senior Media and Sales Analyst to join our Sales team. Reporting to the Head of Sales &amp;amp; Strategy, this individual will play a pivotal role in building and scaling Discord’s digital media sales efforts.&amp;nbsp;
As a Senior Media and Sales Analyst, you will provide analytics and media planning support to the sales team throughout the entire client lifecycles. &amp;nbsp;This can include providing audience insights that informs an account team’s pitch and sales strategy; conducting performance analysis to support account manager optimization recommendations during campaign flights, and developing benchmark insights that frame campaign outcomes for clients.&amp;nbsp;
You will leverage your expertise in digital advertising and media planning along with your technical, data and analytics to provide the sales team with powerful insights that help them offer clients innovative campaign solutions, compelling proposals, and actionable wrap-reports. As a trusted partner to the sales teams, your contributions to the sales process (pre and post) will support Discord in differentiating itself and successfully capture media spend from high-profile brands and advertisers.&amp;nbsp;
What You'll Be Doing

Collaborate with the sales team as well as liaise with clients to understand their strategic goals and objectives by analyzing RPFs and client briefs.
Leverage multiple data sources to develop insight regarding audience sizing, profiles, and behaviors and work with sales to team to weave those insights into high-impact proposals.
Monitor and analyze online ad campaigns including tracking and reporting on key performance indicators such as impressions, clicks, conversions.
Deploy statistical analysis, benchmarketing, A/B testing and other optimization techniques to improve ad performance and achieve campaign goals.
Collect and aggregate media performance data from multiple sources, including ad platforms and analytics tools.
Create and maintain regular performance reports and dashboards to provide insights on campaign effectiveness, audience engagement, and media spend.
Work closely with internal XFN teams (product, marketing, engineer) to continually improve and update media and data analysis efforts
Contribute to preparing proposals &amp;amp; reports to clients in a clear and compelling manner, articulating the value and benefits of Discord’s advertising solutions.
Contribute to analysis that assists the sales team in price negotiations and campaign sizing
Build templates, establish best practices and document learnings that empowers the sales team with self-service analysis capabilities.
Participate in market research &amp;amp; analysis to understand industry trends, consumer behavior, and competitive landscape including competitor performance benchmarks to identify opportunities and threats.
Maintain up-to-date knowledge of digital media trends, technologies, and competitive landscape.

What you should have

8+ years of proven experience in digital media analysis, sales planning, ad operations or related roles within the online advertising industry.
Strong understanding of the digital advertising ecosystem, metrics and analytics.
Proficiency in data analysis tools, advanced analytic methods, and platforms such as Google Analytics, Excel, and data visualization software (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
Familiarity with online advertising platforms, ad measurement platforms, and media planning tools
Strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret data and generate actionable insights.
Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to articulate complex ideas effectively.
Ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously and meet deadlines.
Creative thinking and problem-solving skills, with a focus on driving results for clients.
Superb attention to detail and organizational skills.
