**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.As Director of Corporate Development, you'll lead Discord's M&amp;amp;A engine—sourcing, structuring, closing, and integrating strategic acquisitions that accelerate our growth. Reporting to the VP of Corporate Strategy and working directly with our C-suite and product teams, you'll drive deals from thesis through integration while contributing to the strategic thinking that shapes our pipeline.
What you'll be doing

M&amp;amp;A Leadership

Source and maintain active pipeline of strategic acquisition targets across our business priorities
Lead end-to-end deal execution from initial outreach through diligence, negotiation, and closing
Develop comprehensive deal theses that secure C-suite conviction for transactions
Structure deals that balance strategic value, financial returns, and integration complexity—from capability tuck-ins to transformative acquisitions
Direct technical, commercial, financial, and strategic diligence workstreams
Negotiate term sheets and definitive agreements with target companies and advisors
Partner with Product and Engineering to drive successful post-acquisition integration
Build and refine repeatable M&amp;amp;A playbooks, templates, and processes


Strategic Contribution

Provide M&amp;amp;A perspective on strategic questions and build-vs-buy decisions
Contribute to market analyses that inform acquisition pipeline development
Support quarterly strategic reviews, annual planning, and long-range financial planning
Maintain pulse on M&amp;amp;A landscape, competitive dynamics, and emerging opportunities


Partnership Support

Advise on complex partnership structures with M&amp;amp;A-like characteristics
Provide deal structuring expertise on equity investments, joint ventures, and strategic alliances
Help evaluate acquire-vs-partner trade-offs on strategic opportunities



What you should have

10-12+ years of experience in investment banking (TMT/gaming coverage), venture capital, private equity, or corporate development at high-growth tech/media companies
5+ years people management experience
Proven deal closer: Led or co-led 5+ M&amp;amp;A transactions from thesis through close, including at least 2 deals &amp;gt;$50M
Full-stack M&amp;amp;A expertise: Sourcing, valuation, diligence, negotiation, and integration
Financial modeling mastery: DCF, LBO, accretion/dilution analysis, and returns evaluation
Gaming or digital media fluency: Understanding of business models, unit economics, and value drivers
Executive presence: Can present effectively with C-suite executives, founders, and board members
