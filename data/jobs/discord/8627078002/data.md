What you'll be doing

Provide L2 (Incident and Problem Management) and L3 (Bug Fix and Enhancement) technical support across the Oracle Fusion ERP environment.
Develop, maintain, and enhance BI Publisher (BIP) and OTBI reports, dashboards, and data extracts across GL, AP, AR and Procurement modules
Own Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC) break-fix support, monitoring, troubleshooting, and new integration development.
Partner with functional analysts and business stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable technical solutions.
Create and maintain technical design documents, integration specifications, and deployment documentation.
Support month-end, quarter-end, and year-end financial operations by resolving technical issues and performance bottlenecks.
Execute root cause analysis , technical testing, deployment validation, and regression testing for quarterly Oracle releases.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams on enterprise integrations and long-term solution architecture.
Follow and support ITGC, SOX, and change management requirements for all technical changes.

What you should have

5+ years of hands-on Oracle Fusion Cloud ERP technical development experience.
Strong expertise in Oracle BI Publisher (BIP), OTBI, and Oracle Integration Cloud (OIC).
Experience developing integrations, extensions, reports, extracts, and technical solutions within Oracle Fusion.
Experience with Oracle Visual Builder, REST/SOAP APIs, and FBDI and HCM Data loader processes.
Experience using ATP or PaaS DB for storing and managing integration payloads, audit logs, or custom reporting data layers that extend Oracle Fusion's native capabilities
Experience supporting L2/L3 production environments with documented incident and problem management processes.
Ability to create technical design documentation and integration specifications.
Experience troubleshooting web services, APIs, file-based integrations, and data transformation processes and reading failure logs.
Familiarity with SOX compliance, ITGC controls, and ERP change management practices.
Familiarity with Oracle AI Agent Studio within Oracle Fusion Cloud
Strong communication skills with the ability to work directly across functional technical team members.&amp;nbsp;

Oracle Fusion technical certification or related Oracle Cloud certifications.

Experience supporting SaaS finance systems in a high-growth technology company.
