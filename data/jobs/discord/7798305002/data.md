**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.The Discord Product Creative Team is seeking an exceptional animator with expertise in frame-by-frame animation and motion design. The ideal candidate is a master of their craft, bringing a combination of technical precision and artistic flair to everything they create. You'll be a vital part of our growing Collectibles Content Creation team, responsible for creating engaging, high-quality animations that enhance the user experience. We're looking for someone who can independently craft compelling motion pieces, collaborate with other creatives, and provide clear direction and feedback to vendors. Exceptional conceptual and illustration skills are essential for this role.
What You'll Be Doing

Collaborate with the Collectibles design team to produce diverse collectible animations, including avatar decorations, profile effects, and new collectible types.
Conceptualize, storyboard, and create animations that bring concepts to life with energy and style.
Execute various animation styles (frame-by-frame, motion graphics, etc.), showcasing your keen eye for detail and impeccable timing.
Design and animate using motion graphics principles to enhance the appeal of collectibles and user interactions.
Partner closely with game publishers, designers, and vendors to produce polished, high-quality animations.
Work within technical product constraints to ensure animations are both visually stunning and performant.
Manage multiple projects simultaneously, adapting to shifting priorities and deadlines while maintaining quality.
Provide thoughtful feedback on the work of peers and vendors to elevate creative output.

What you should have

You have a portfolio showcasing advanced animation skills, including frame-by-frame work, motion graphics, and a variety of styles.
You have 6+ years of animation experience, ideally in the tech or gaming industry.
You are an expert in 2D animation techniques, with a strong understanding of motion principles, composition, and timing.
You are proficient in industry-standard animation software such as Adobe After Effects, Toon Boom Harmony, Adobe Animate, and TV Paint. Bonus: Experience with Lottie, Rive, or Figma.
You work effectively with artists, designers, and other team members.
You can present creative concepts confidently and articulate the reasoning behind design decisions.
You are receptive to constructive feedback and can iterate on designs to achieve the desired outcome.
You are team-oriented, humble, and bring a positive attitude to your work.

Bonus Points

Proficiency with 3D software such as Cinema 4D, Maya, or Blender.
Experience with UX animation tools like Rive and Lottie.
Familiarity with illustration tools and techniques
