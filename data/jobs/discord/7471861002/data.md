**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.This position is US based only.
The focus of Safety Platform is to make Discord’s users safer by creating scalable and proactive systems to limit bad actors across the platform. Keeping users safe is an essential contributor to growth at Discord which is why the output of this team is so important. We are looking for a technical, metrics-driven and hands-on Engineering manager to lead this team. This team is the glue that holds Safety features together, building product infra that powers frontend, user-facing features including settings, NUX, block/mute and reports as well as gathering signals for ML models to detect harm and taking interventions to keep Discord safe for everyone.
What You'll Be Doing

Supporting a team of talented, 8-10 Engineers including a Staff Engineer
Owning our in-house, real-time abuse detection platform written in Python and Rust
Working with Product, Data Science, and ML to develop reusable services or architectures
Building out the signals, datasets, and databases to support our informational needs
Expanding and deploying internal APIs to combat abusive content
Maintain operations for our infrastructure on Kubernetes
Move quickly to respond to the moving target of abuse on Discord

What you should have

5+ years experience building scalable backend or real-time data systems
2+ years experience as an Engineering Manager
Experience operating and deploying large-scale distributed systems, including Kubernetes experience.
Strong communication skills and the ability to work well cross-functionally
Ability to work with others to create pragmatic solutions — and then evolve and generalize them.
Confidence to dive deep into the technical details and challenge designs or solutions.

Bonus Points

Experience in touching the whole cloud infrastructure stack - from disks to networking to permissions.
Experience with data warehouses like Druid, BigQuery, or Redshift
Prior experience working in the Safety domain
You have a strong moral compass that navigates and motivates you
