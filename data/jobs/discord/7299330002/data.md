This position is US based only.&amp;nbsp;
Discord is about giving people the power to create space to find belonging in their lives. We want to make it easier for you to talk regularly with the people you care about. We want you to build genuine relationships with your friends and communities close to home or around the world. Original, reliable, playful, and relatable. These are the values that connect our users and our employees at Discord.
The Discord Activities team is looking for a passionate and organized Partner Producer to foster collaboration with external Activity developers &amp;amp; partners, champion their success, and promote smooth integration of their products on our platform. This person will report to the team's Lead Producer.
What you’ll be doing:


Develop and manage a comprehensive workflow that supports developers in integrating Activities into the Discord platform, complete with well-maintained and clear documentation.


Provide ongoing project status reports to leadership to guarantee that development efforts are completed within budget, adhere to predetermined timelines, and stay true to the originally approved concept.


Collaborate closely with developers to ensure key project milestones and deadlines are met, while rigorously upholding industry standards and satisfying all compliance requirements.


Actively monitor and evaluate game builds, ensuring consistent progress across design, creative, and development phases, while maintaining a keen focus on the overall production quality.


Develop and maintain relationships with external partners, advocating for the platform's and developers' success.


What you should have:


4+ years of experience in partner relationship management, games publishing, or platform development


Advanced proficiency in managing partner relationships and product evangelism


Expert level skill in managing external projects, ensuring delivery within set parameters


Strong communication skills to articulate the value of resource sharing and collaboration


A proactive approach to identifying and resolving impediments in publishing and release processes


A track record of improving partner experience and optimizing product support




Experience developing games as a designer, artist or engineer


Passion for online gaming and shared experiences


Experience in Web or Mobile platform design or management&amp;nbsp;
