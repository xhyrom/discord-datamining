Consumer Revenue and Revenue Infra are the teams at the heart of premium experiences at Discord. You’ll be working closely with the teams responsible for products such as Nitro, Shop, and Orbs. What You'll Be Doing

Leverage your strong customer-centric mindset to help build and run our Revenue compliance program
Develop a holistic and enduring ownership model for our code, products, and programs that allows us to quickly meet compliance needs
Work closely Engineering, Product, and Legal as well as other cross-functional engineering teams to implement solutions to support our Orbs economy
Partner with Revenue leadership to identify, develop, and run programs that mature our Revenue products and systems while staying compliant
Work with external third-party providers to understand requirements and constraints for systems integration
Manage multiple complex projects, often with competing timelines and resource need

What you should have

10+ years of Technical Program Management experience working with fast paced product or infrastructure engineering teams
Experience working on programs related to e-commerce platforms and monetizable products (e.g. payments, wallets, check-out, billing infra, etc.) at a similarly-sized company
Worked with Engineering, Product, and Legal leadership to implement compliance requirements or meet regulatory needs, especially related to payment processing
Knowledge and deep experience in technical program management methodologies, working in ambiguous spaces, and bringing structure to work
Experience moving technical or engineering programs from concept to completion and promoting the impact using metrics, growth examples, value etc
Can break down complex challenges into workable components and make continuous progress towards solving a problem vs. trying to boil the ocean

Bonus Points

A love for helping &amp;amp; empowering others
BA/BS in Computer Science, Engineering, or relevant technical experience
Strong customer-empathy that ensures your preferred solutions take into account both Safety and company needs
