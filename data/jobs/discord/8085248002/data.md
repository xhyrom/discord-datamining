**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.We’re seeking an experienced Litigation Counsel, Intellectual Property &amp;amp; Licensing to develop and execute on our strategy regarding patent disputes and IP litigation. This role will be responsible for managing complex patent licensing negotiations, pre-litigation matters, and patent litigation, while also handling third-party trademark and copyright claims. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in technology software patents and a proven track record of successfully managing high-stakes IP matters for technology companies.
What You Will Be Doing

Lead patent litigation matters globally from initial assessment through resolution, including coordinating with outside counsel and managing litigation budgets
Develop and refine comprehensive patent litigation framework and strategies aligned with business objectives, develop and refine patent licensing strategy, together with outside licensing counsel and, as needed, Discord’s IP team.
Handle patent pre-litigation activities including assessments of demands, prior art searches, invalidity analyses, infringement analyses and settlement negotiations
Manage, together with outside counsel, incoming patent licensing demands and assertions, including initial assessment, response strategy, and negotiations of patent licensing and, in collaboration with Discord’s IP team, cross-licensing agreements.
Handle trade secret litigation and misappropriation claims
Handle copyright infringement matters, including Digital Millennium Copyright Act compliance and enforcement actions, and provide guidance regarding and collaborate with cross-functional partners on workflows and policies relating to Discord’s compliance with the DMCA and enforcement of the Copyright and IP Policy applicable to Discord users.
Collaborate with Discord’s IP team colleagues responsible for setting and executing on Discord’s IP portfolio and brand protection strategy .

Required Qualifications

J.D. from an accredited law school with strong academic credentials and active bar admission in at least one U.S. jurisdiction
7+ years of intellectual property litigation experience, with significant focus on patent matters, including familiarity with international patent litigation&amp;nbsp;
In-house counsel experience at a technology company
Experience with technology patents covering software, networking, real-time communications including video technologies, or gaming&amp;nbsp;
Strategic mindset that balances proactive protection with pragmatic risk management within resource constraints
Proven ability to translate complex IP concepts into concise, actionable advice for engineering teams and cross-functional executive leadership

Preferred Qualifications

Patent bar admission (USPTO registration)
Experience with standard essential patents (SEPs) and FRAND licensing
Background in gaming, social media, or communications technologies
Experience with IP aspects of open source software and developer platforms
Experience in patent licensing negotiations&amp;nbsp;&amp;nbsp;
