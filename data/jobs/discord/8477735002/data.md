**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.As an Administrative Business Partner, you will serve as a strategic and tactical partner to some of our leaders within our People/HR Organization. This role requires adaptability, strong organizational skills, and the ability to seamlessly integrate into various teams, ensuring continuity and efficiency in leadership support.
You’ll be supporting up to 5 members of our People Leadership Team (PLT) - all Directors or Sr. Directors reporting into the Chief People Officer (CPO). This role will also work very closely with the ABP to the CPO, Serena Szwanek on various initiatives and projects.
What You'll Be Doing

Act as a trusted business partner, quickly adapting to the needs of different leaders and teams.
Support leaders with daily administrative and operational tasks, ensuring smooth workflows.
Manage complex calendars, resolving conflicts and optimizing time allocation across internal and external commitments
Contribute to internal projects and initiatives, following up to ensure completion and acting as a liaison between leadership and their teams.
Schedule and attend key meetings, capturing clear notes, tracking action items, and ensuring timely follow-ups.
Coordinate domestic and international travel arrangements, including flights, lodging, transportation, and documentation.
Aggregate and submit expense reports in a timely manner.
Collaborate closely with other administrative professionals to maintain alignment and efficiency across leadership teams.

What you should have

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to engage effectively across all levels of leadership.
High adaptability working with diverse leadership styles and personalities in a hybrid environment.
Strong problem-solving and critical thinking skills, with the ability to context switch frequently, prioritize workflows, and help leaders optimize their time.
Ability to contribute to and support projects in a fast-paced environment with strong follow-through and attention to detail.
High level of confidentiality, discretion, and judgment when managing sensitive information.
Comfortable navigating ambiguity and quickly ramping up to understand evolving team needs.
Strong operational instincts to anticipate needs, spot issues early, and handle multiple assignments simultaneously.
Self-awareness about capacity and ability to communicate bandwidth limits proactively.
Proficiency in G-Suite, particularly Google Calendar and Gmail.
2+ year(s) of administrative support experience, bonus points for supporting HR or Operations teams.&amp;nbsp;
Experience and comfort with tools like Discord, Notion, Claude, Ramp
