**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.Developer Compliance is dedicated to safeguarding Discord's platform, ensuring that applications from third-party developers meet our safety standards and policies. This role is crucial in maintaining a trustworthy environment where our users can confidently explore and enjoy diverse offerings from external creators. The Developer Compliance Analyst will report to the Developer Compliance Manager, and will serve as an expert in the detection of abusive applications and content violations in the Discord Developer Ecosystem. Discord is looking for a candidate with a deep understanding of the technical underpinnings of how APIs function, experience in detecting and assessing abusive behavior and fraudulent practices, and confidence in understanding and explaining highly complex issues.
You will be responsible for detecting and reporting on Developer Policy violations and recommending scaled approaches to ensuring that 3rd-party apps are not being developed or used in abusive or non-compliant ways. We're looking for someone who is highly collaborative and versatile in their approaches, has experience interpreting internal policies and regulatory mandates, and be able to communicate technical complexity and policy considerations with a range of technical and non-technical stakeholders.

What You'll Be Doing

Leverage technical expertise and sound judgment to review and evaluate bots and user generated content for compliance with our Community Guidelines and Developer Policy
Develop internal products for detecting and assessing violations in our developer ecosystem
Write reports and analyze trends on emerging issues within the platform ecosystem, for a cross-functional audience
Work with Product, Legal, Trust &amp;amp; Safety, and Communications teams in responding to sensitive issues
Analyze new and potential features and propose plans to help reduce or remove 3rd-Party abuse before it happens

What You Should Have

Bachelor’s degree or equivalent education/experience
Experience with utilizing APIs and/or SDKs.
Experience composing and understanding advanced SQL queries, as well as the ability to compose and understand Javascript and Python based internal tools
Familiarity with standard software development practices, such as spec creation, code review, version control, and writing technical documentation
Passion for supporting our users and developers with unwavering patience and empathy
You thrive in ambiguous situations and get excited about figuring out solutions to complex problems, and then executing on them
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate complicated findings or technical approaches in easy to-understand ways
A desire to work with amazing, passionate people who care deeply about solving challenging problems to keep Discord a safe place for users
Last but not least - a collaborative attitude and a healthy dose of natural curiosity

Bonus Points

Previous experience enforcing and/or interpreting developer policies
Previous experience with cyber-security, fraud detection, and Trust and Safety work
