**Job Description:**
Discord is used by over 200 million people every month for many different reasons, but there’s one thing that nearly everyone does on our platform: play video games. Over 90% of our users play games, spending a combined 1.5 billion hours playing thousands of unique titles on Discord each month. Discord plays a uniquely important role in the future of gaming. We are focused on making it easier and more fun for people to talk and hang out before, during, and after playing games.The Design Systems team is a cross-functional team that ensures design quality across all facets of UI, content, and graphics. As a part of the team, you will be responsible for the creation and maintenance of the design system that all product designers, engineers, and product managers use to design &amp;amp; develop at Discord. Using your full range of interaction, visual, and prototyping skills, you will own creating a coherent and ownable look &amp;amp; feel for our desktop and mobile experiences.
As a qualified candidate, you have experience contributing to systems projects that deliver value to users and the business. You collaborate effectively with cross-functional partners, including engineers, and communicate ideas clearly. You approach problems with a systems mindset, considering how solutions fit into a cohesive whole. You can articulate design decisions and rationale to a variety of stakeholders and work closely with product teams to adapt and apply systems to meet specific product needs.
What You'll Be Doing

Contributing to and maintaining components, patterns, and templates
Collaborating with product designers &amp;amp; engineers to ensure successful contributions
Supporting the adoption of system elements by sharing your work and presenting it cross-functionally

Assist in migrating and deprecating legacy elements


Developing clear, accessible documentation and tutorials to support and educate users of the system

Provide support and guidance in customer support channels


Creating detailed mocks and prototypes to communicate your ideas effectively, leveraging user feedback and data to guide decisions
Contributing fresh ideas and supporting the team in evolving the system towards next-generation experiences

What you should have

Minimum of 2 years experience designing and shipping a web/app product for a variety of devices and platforms.
Experience working on design systems (creation, documentation &amp;amp; support)
Highly proficient using Figma, using styles, variables and components
Proficiency with technical UX writing
Experience with inclusive design / WCAG
Ownership mindset with a bias towards action, helping to drive projects and decisions forward.
High craft bar and attention to detail in user experience, visual design, interaction design, and prototyping.
Ability communicate complex problems and experiences simply, and to know when a user journey, wireframe, deck, prototype, or design specification will be most effective in doing so.
Experience designing for mature products — against visions, and through incremental improvements.
Ability to work with a low-ego, highly collaborative, cross-functional team

Bonus Points

Experience with version control and Github
Technical writing experience, familiarity with markdown
Experience creating Figma Tooling
Casual or committed gamer
