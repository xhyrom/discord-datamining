            <p>Today, we’d like to share some design tips with everyone on Discord, on a place that anyone can use: your profile! How do you make people feel when you show up? What do people say when you leave the room? That’s your personal brand. Your personal brand can be linked to your online persona.<br></p>

Whether you’re a gamer, artist, fan, or anything in between, there are multiple places on Discord where you can customize your appearance—including your avatar, banner, and bio—in a way that represents who you truly are.&nbsp;<br></p>

            <h2>Your Profile Avatar</h2>
            <p>Your avatar is shown in your servers, the conversations you have with your friends, and when you’re in a Voice call. On Discord, you aren’t limited to how others may perceive you — your presence on the platform is whatever you make of it, and in turn, who you truly are. Choose imagery that represents the real you, whatever that may be!&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Some pointers on uploading the best avatar: while you may use a square photo, it’ll be cropped to a circle when displayed on Discord. When uploading, it's best to use a simple image or GIF (file formats JPG, PNG, or GIF) that’s around 200x200 pixels at minimum, and under 10 MB. There are plenty of tools available to create your avatar with — Photoshop, Procreate, Figma, Spline, Blender, your own camera, and plenty more!&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Now let’s hop into designing!<br></p>
            <h3>First, Brainstorm Your Idea!&nbsp;&nbsp;</h3>
            <ul role="list">
                <li>What things do you like? What inspires you?</li>
                <li>What colors best represent you? Or, what’s your favorite color?</li>
                <li>What do you do on Discord? What kind of communities are you in and what are your roles there, if any?</li>
                <li>How would you like your banner to look along with your avatar? Is there a common theme you’d like to carry across the two of them?</li>
                <li>What imagery would you like to use to bring out your themes?&nbsp;<br></li>
            </ul>
            <p>If it helps, you may also create a moodboard to help you think of your theme and imagery. Lay everything out and listen to what jumps out at you!&nbsp;&nbsp;<br></p>

You may experiment with different colors and visual styles to bring out different moods, convey a different message, and more!&nbsp;</p>

            <h2>Visuals &amp; Tone: The Fundamentals of Profile Design</h2>
            <p>There are many elements you can use to describe yourself - colors, imagery, tone of voice, and more. We’ll break them down and connect them to your Discord profile!<br></p>
            <p><strong>Color&nbsp;</strong></p>
            <p>Color is one of the first things people notice when they look at your profile and show your overall aesthetic. Within the professional art community, color symbolism is how different hues of color help perceive meaning in life driven by the viewer’s cultural associations and personal experiences. <br><br>For example, <a href="https://99designs.com/blog/tips/color-meanings/">red represents good luck in China but it means morning in South Africa</a>. Red is also associated with passion, energy, and danger. It’s important to keep your audience in mind when you’re choosing colors.&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Each color also has a different hue, shade, tint, and tone that affect its perception. For example, neon colors have different meanings than pastel colors.&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Combining multiple colors can also enhance or take away from your message from your design. <a href="https://99designs.com/blog/tips/the-7-step-guide-to-understanding-color-theory/">Color theory</a> explains the relationship between colors using art and science.&nbsp;</p>
            <figure style="max-width:1600pxpx" class="w-richtext-align-fullwidth w-richtext-figure-type-image">
                <div><img src="https://assets-global.website-files.com/5f9072399b2640f14d6a2bf4/61b3a26096095825418dd9f3_mppsp3CIgI9ZZ-OwxbiY0L1ilTMgx91tW7KUYLAKiZWtRhH18lWWmg15xm5Xn7wcQO4oVNmwkmoXkOVzG--qHKKexCVgzLf0048t1L-zyxheDEKkOARnN6yYKF81H1fzZQhYZ_Zw.png" alt=""></div>
            </figure>
            <p><strong>Imagery&nbsp;</strong></p>
            <p>Imagery is the visual language and aesthetic appearance that helps others recognize you. They can help tell your story and communicate your message beyond color. Imagery could be your own art or photos in the case for your profile.&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p><strong>Tone of voice</strong></p>
            <p>What do you sound like? Playful, formal, or funny? Your tone of voice is how you and your brand communicate with the audience. It influences how people perceive your message. It helps build connection, trust, and memorability.&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Your tone of voice can be reflected through your bio, username, and the way you communicate with others online. You can reference our bios section of this blog post for some examples.&nbsp;<br></p>
            <p>Lastly, there is no right or wrong way to approach your online identity and personal brand. It’s uniquely you. You and your online identity, and personal brand are all a constantly evolving process! Don’t be afraid to try new things, grow, and change.&nbsp;</p>
